FAQS

Free Trial Questions, Answered

How long is Abby’s free trial?

The free trial runs 14 days or 500 Abby Minutes, whichever comes first. It starts when your account is set up and your calls are forwarded to Abby.

Does the free trial cost anything?

No. $0 is due today and there is no setup fee. You choose a plan and enter a payment method, and nothing is charged until the trial ends. To avoid the first charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial expires, as the terms describe.

Is the AI Receptionist part of the free trial?

Yes. Every plan includes both human receptionists and the AI Receptionist, and the trial does too. A minute with the AI Receptionist uses half an Abby Minute, so you can see how far the plan stretches when routine calls go to the AI.

Do real receptionists answer during the trial?

Yes. Your calls are answered by Abby’s U.S.-based receptionists, who all work in the office in Las Vegas (none from home) and are trained on your call handling instructions before the first call. The trial is the full service, not a recorded demo.

What do I need to start the free trial?

A business phone number to forward and a few minutes to tell us how you want calls greeted, transferred, messaged and scheduled. There is nothing to install.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Your plan continues month-to-month at the price you chose and you can move up or down a plan at any time. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial expires and nothing is charged. New clients also have a 22-day money-back guarantee on their initial purchase, counted from the day the subscription starts.

Can I try 24/7 answering during the trial?

Yes. Nights, weekends and holidays are included on every plan with no upcharge, and you decide whether those hours go to human receptionists, to the AI Receptionist, or to both.

Is bilingual answering included in the free trial?

Yes. English and Spanish answering is included for both human and AI receptionists on every Abby plan, including during the trial.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. You forward your existing number to Abby and keep it. Forwarding can be changed or switched off at any time, so nothing about your number changes.

How does Abby’s free trial compare with Ruby, Smith.ai and AnswerConnect?

As published on September 2, 2026, Ruby and AnswerConnect list no free trial on their sites and Smith.ai offers a 30-day money-back guarantee after you pay. Abby’s trial is free before you pay, with human and AI receptionists on your real calls. See the best virtual receptionist services compared.
