Featured news

Shepherd communication writebacks are here

AllyDVM now writes communication activities back to Shepherd, making it easier for practices to keep client communication records aligned across systems. Instead of relying on staff to manually re-enter activity into the PIMS, teams can reduce duplicate work, save time, and maintain a more complete view of client engagement within Shepherd. For Shepherd users, that means a more connected workflow and fewer manual steps for busy veterinary teams.
