How do you compare car insurance quotes?

To compare car insurance quotes, enter your details into Insurify’s comparison tool. We’ll show you side-by-side quotes from multiple insurers. Check that each quote offers the same coverage before you choose one.

Here’s how to compare car insurance quotes, step by step:

Gather your information. To get accurate car insurance quotes, you’ll need to share the driver’s license numbers, birth dates, address, and any accidents, tickets, or claims in the last three to five years of everyone who’ll be on the policy. You’ll also need to share the VIN or year, make, and model and the annual mileage for every vehicle the policy will cover. It’s also a good idea to have the declarations page of your current policy, since it lists the limits and deductibles you already have. Quotes can change after an insurer checks your driving record and credit, where allowed, so it’s important to share accurate information.

Get quotes from multiple auto insurers at once. You can do this by visiting each insurer’s site, by calling an independent agent, or by using a car insurance comparison site. A comparison site can be the fastest way to get multiple quotes. For example, Insurify partners with over 120+ insurers and doesn’t require you to provide a phone number to get quotes.

Make sure coverage matches what you already have. It’s a good idea to look at car insurance quotes for coverage that matches your current policy. Compare the same bodily injury and property damage liability limits, comprehensive and collision deductibles, add-ons, and policy terms (six or 12 months). For example, a quote with a $1,000 deductible will likely be lower than one with a $500 deductible. But your out-of-pocket costs will be higher with the first quote if you get into an accident. Insurify’s policy upload tool lets you upload a photo of your declarations page and compare your current policy side by side with new quotes.

Compare more than price. An affordable car insurance premium is important, but you should also consider each insurer’s customer service and claims ratings, financial strength ratings from AM Best, fees, and available discounts, such as bundling, telematics, or paying in full. Insurify has a library of more than 85,000 verified user reviews to help you evaluate insurers.

Compare again at renewal. Car insurance rates can change significantly, so it’s a good idea to compare quotes when your policy comes up for renewal, typically every six or 12 months.

What affects car insurance rates?

Nationwide, the average cost of car insurance is $187 per month for full coverage and $99 for liability-only insurance, according to Insurify data. But your costs may be more or less than average, based on factors like:

Your driving record (past car accidents, speeding tickets, DUIs, and citations): Highest impact

Age and gender: High impact

Vehicle make and model: Moderate impact

Credit history (in most states): Moderate impact

Where you live: High impact

Deductible amount: Moderate impact
