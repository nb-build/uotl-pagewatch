Frequently Asked Questions

What does DockMaster's sales management cover?

The full boat sales cycle: prospect and lead tracking, opportunity pipeline, quotes and sales contracts, trade-in valuation, F&I products, and commission calculation — connected to the same inventory and accounting your whole dealership runs on.

How does DockMaster handle prospects and leads?

A CRM-style pipeline tracks prospects from first contact to close, with activity logging, follow-up scheduling, automated letter generation, and conversion to customer records when the deal lands.

Can DockMaster generate sales contracts?

Yes. Generate firm and quick quotes, build sales contracts with terms and trade-ins, and send documents for eSignature — so deals close without paper shuffling.

Does DockMaster track sales commissions?

Yes. Commissions calculate by salesperson with sales history and performance tracking, and everything posts to the integrated accounting suite.

How does F&I work in DockMaster?

Finance and insurance products attach to the deal, with CoreLogic Credco integration for credit reports to support lender decisions.
