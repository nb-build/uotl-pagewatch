Make your book of business work for you

Opportunity Mining watches your entire database: credit pulls, refinance signals, home-equity changes, and new MLS listings from your clients and partners. The moment a past client's situation changes, BNTouch tells you, with the context to act.

Credit Check Alerts
When a past client starts shopping for a mortgage, you know, and can respond with a firm offer of credit.

Equity Alerts
BNTouch monitors past clients' property values and generates alerts based on current equity and the additional parameters you set.

Rate Refinance Miner
Watches your book against current rates and flags past clients who would now benefit from a refinance.

Strike Rate Alerts
Set the rate gap, loan type, and timing that matter to you; the alert fires during a borrower's crossroad.

Just Listed Alerts
A past borrower lists their home, which means they need their next mortgage. You hear about it while the listing is fresh.

Listed Alerts by Partner
When an agent in your network lists a property, the alert arrives the day it goes live, keeping you current on your network's activity and your conversations well timed.

Three Questions Every Loan Officer Asks

I already have a CRM. How painful is switching?
Less painful than staying. Your contacts, partners, and pipeline come over during onboarding, and setup, training, and support are included with every subscription, so you are never left to figure it out alone. Our team also helps you rebuild your current setup: campaigns, pipelines, and automations included.

Will it work with my LOS and my lead sources?
Yes. BNTouch syncs with the major LOS platforms and plugs into your existing lead sources, so the file you start in the CRM is the file your ops team finishes.

Is there a free trial?
No, and it's deliberate. A trial hands you an empty box and wishes you luck. Every BNTouch subscription includes onboarding, training, and setup, and White Glove puts a team of specialists on it who build the entire system around your business. Why test-drive an empty CRM when day one can start with everything already working? And if you prepay 12 months, the 13th is free.
