Banyan Software Expands Print MIS Footprint with DocketManager Acquisition

Banyan Software, a leading acquirer and operator of mission-critical software businesses, is pleased to announce its acquisition of DocketManager, a cloud-based Print MIS and Web-to-Print platform serving print shops across North America.

Founded in 2006 and headquartered in London, Ontario, DocketManager provides an all-in-one solution for quoting, order intake, production, and job tracking. The company is known for its ease of use, affordability, and deep industry alignment, with a customer base of over 300 small and mid-sized print shops.

John Alden, CEO of PrintIQ, will also serve as CEO of DocketManager, bringing shared strategic oversight while each company continues to operate independently.

“Joining the Banyan family is a natural next step for DocketManager. It allows us to continue supporting our customers while investing more deeply in innovation and growth. We’re excited to be part of a long-term vision that respects our history and values our independence.”

Brent Darling • General Manager of DocketManager

“DocketManager has built a loyal customer base by focusing on simplicity and value. We’re excited to collaborate as sister companies under Banyan, sharing knowledge while preserving each platform’s unique strengths. Banyan’s permanent ownership model ensures DocketManager has the resources and stability to thrive for years to come.”

John Alden • CEO of PrintIQ and DocketManager

“DocketManager is a fantastic addition to our portfolio. Its commitment to customer success and track record of thoughtful growth aligns closely with our values. We look forward to supporting the team as they continue expanding their reach and delivering essential solutions to the print industry.”

Arun Srinivasan • Operating Partner at Banyan Software

About DocketManager. Founded in 2006, DocketManager is a cloud-based Print MIS and Web-to-Print platform that helps print shops streamline their operations from quote to production. For more information, visit docketmanager.com

About Banyan Software, Inc. Banyan Software provides the best permanent home for successful enterprise software companies, their employees, and customers. Banyan’s mission is to acquire, build, and grow great enterprise software businesses all over the world with dominant positions in niche verticals. Founded in 2016, the company follows a buy-and-hold-for-life strategy, and is set up with a permanent capital base to preserve the legacy of founders. As a purpose-driven company, Banyan is committed to making an enduring, positive impact on the world.
