Construction Estimating Software FAQs

+What Is The Best Construction Estimating Software?

McCormick is the premier construction estimating software for specialty trades, providing a unified platform for preconstruction takeoff and cost analysis. It is specifically engineered for the electrical, plumbing and mechanical industries to ensure bid accuracy and project profitability.

Key features include:

Patented Auto Home Run and auto-count for electrical takeoff

Trade-specific databases with 100,000+ plumbing and mechanical SKUs

Cloud-based multi-user collaboration for remote estimating teams

Customizable reporting tools for generating detailed bid proposals and budget forecasts

McCormick's features enable precise budget forecasting, risk management and team-wide visibility — helping contractors improve project profitability and operational efficiency.

+Who Uses Preconstruction Estimating Software?

Contractors and specialty trades use preconstruction estimating software to analyze costs, assess risks and create winning bids before work begins. Accurate estimating capabilities are essential for specialty trades to secure profitable work while maintaining competitive edges in their markets.

McCormick delivers comprehensive solutions tailored for electrical, plumbing and mechanical contractors.

The platform covers every aspect of preconstruction, from digital takeoff and material procurement to labor scheduling and subcontractor management. Its integrated approach helps teams develop precise cost estimates, optimize profit margins and create compelling proposals.

+What Are The Benefits Of Using McCormick Estimating Software?

McCormick transforms how electrical, plumbing and mechanical contractors approach project planning and bid management. Its advanced takeoff capabilities and supplier pricing features help contractors generate precise estimates that improve accuracy and bid success rates.

Construction professionals gain a competitive edge through McCormick’s cloud-based accessibility and full-suite preconstruction workflow. As part of the Foundation Software portfolio, McCormick is backed by deep construction industry expertise — giving estimating teams the tools and support they need from bid to job cost.

+Which Software Is Most Commonly Used For Creating Plumbing Estimates?

McCormick is the leading choice for plumbing estimating software because plumbing professionals love how it handles commercial projects without breaking a sweat.

The software includes:

Advanced tools for calculating pipe sizing, pressure loss and flow rates across complex systems

A fixture database that covers over 100,000 SKUs from major manufacturers that updates prices weekly

Supplier pricing options updated directly from popular supply houses

These features help plumbing contractors develop accurate bids faster while maintaining the precision needed to deliver projects profitably.

+What's The Best Estimating Software For Electrical Projects?

McCormick electrical estimating software is built to handle the unique demands of commercial electrical projects.

Specialized electrical features include:

A customizable database with 55,000+ items and 25,000 prebuilt assemblies for fast, accurate material estimating

Pricing service compatibility with major suppliers to keep material costs current to expedite the takeoff process

Auto-count and patented Auto Home Run features to streamline measurements and home run calculations

Electrical databases specialized for both USA and Canada

Cost tracking and data analysis features that support everything from material procurement to labor scheduling

The software's advanced bid management capabilities, combined with seamless integration options for accounting and project management systems, create an all-in-one solution that transforms complex electrical estimating into a streamlined, profitable process.

+How Will McCormick Estimating Software Help Me Improve My Business?

McCormick estimating software helps contractors improve business performance by combining built-in takeoff, cost tracking and real-time reporting in one platform.

It integrates seamlessly with digital plans, enabling contractors to rapidly quantify and price project components while maintaining precise accuracy in their estimates.

Key features driving contractor success include:

Advanced built-in takeoff tools that automatically sync with material databases

Comprehensive change order management for tracking cost impacts and scope adjustments

Detailed job breakout capabilities that enable budget analysis by phase or trade

Automated markup calculations that optimize profit margins

McCormick's comprehensive reporting capabilities enable teams to make data-driven decisions that protect profitability while delivering projects on time and within budget. As a leading estimating software solution built exclusively for specialty contractors, McCormick gives you the tools to bid smarter and grow your business.
