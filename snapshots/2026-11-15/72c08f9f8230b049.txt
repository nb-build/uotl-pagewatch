Merchant Central FAQs

What is NMI Merchant Central?

Merchant Central is NMI’s merchant management platform, designed to help payment providers manage the full merchant lifecycle in one place. It is a purpose-built merchant CRM for ISOs, payment facilitators and financial institutions.

Within Merchant Central, teams can manage merchant onboarding and underwriting, track their sales pipeline, monitor performance, handle reporting, manage residuals and provide ongoing support. By bringing these capabilities together, it provides a single system for managing merchant relationships and day-to-day operations.

Is NMI Merchant Central the same as IRIS CRM?

Yes! Merchant Central builds on the foundation of IRIS CRM following NMI’s acquisition of IRIS. The core capabilities users associate with IRIS CRM are still part of the platform.

Merchant Central is the next stage in its development, expanding beyond a traditional merchant CRM to offer a broader merchant management system. It brings together direct-to-processor onboarding, pipeline management, underwriting support, reporting, residuals, a white-label merchant portal and embedded lending within a single platform.

For partners familiar with IRIS CRM, Merchant Central is the evolution of that product into a more integrated merchant management solution.

How is Merchant Central different from other merchant management platforms and CRMs?

Merchant Central is built specifically for the payments industry, which sets it apart from general merchant management software or traditional CRM systems.

While many merchant management platforms focus on basic customer tracking, Merchant Central combines CRM functionality with tools tailored to payment providers. This includes direct-to-processor onboarding, built-in KYC, KYC and AML checks, residuals tracking, reporting as well as underwriting and risk management. It also includes a white-label merchant portal, giving partners a way to deliver a consistent, branded experience to their merchants.

Merchant Central is designed to be modular, allowing partners to use the full platform or select the components that fit their business without replacing their existing stack. By bringing these capabilities together within a single system, it reduces the need to manage multiple tools and helps teams operate more efficiently.

Because Merchant Central is designed for use alongside a payment gateway, it aligns more closely with how ISOs and payment providers operate compared to a standard CRM.
