What’s included in every account

Scheduling & Dispatch

Four schedule views (Timeline, Map, Combined, Calendar), drag-and-drop assignment across technicians, recurring appointments with flexible patterns, route optimization, and the Unassigned queue for flexible dispatching.

Customer Management

Complete profiles with service history, bodies of water with gallons and equipment (pump, filter, heater, cleaner, salt system, sanitizer), gate codes and property notes, tags and custom fields, and search by name, address, status, tags, balance, or service type.

Water Chemistry Tracking

Full chemistry captured per visit: pH, chlorine, alkalinity, calcium, cyanuric acid, salt, phosphorus and more, each reading shown beside the previous visit’s value. Built-in saturation index (LSI) and dosing suggestions computed from the actual gallons of each body of water. Chemistry reports and history by customer.

Invoicing & Billing

Create and send invoices with custom line items, taxes, and notes. Invoices build themselves from completed service visits: labor, service call, and chemicals used. Batch invoicing, online payment collection through Stripe (credit card and ACH), and status tracking from Draft through Paid.

QuickBooks Online Integration (+$25/mo add-on)

Two-way sync of customers, items, and invoices. Status syncs back automatically, with sync running throughout the day. Optional: POM’s own invoicing works independently if you don’t use QuickBooks.

Mobile App (iOS & Android)

The whole field day in one app: clock in, see the route, start jobs, work the checklist with photos, record chemistry, log chemicals used, and submit. The office sees the finished visit as it syncs.

GPS & Activity Tracking

Real-time technician location, activity dashboard, historical location data, and service completion verification.

Inventory Management

Multi-location tracking across warehouse, trucks, and trailers; barcode scanning; auto-deduction as items are used during service; transfers between locations; usage reports.

Quotes & Estimates

Quotes with selectable line items, e-signature approval, one-click conversion to invoices, and status tracking.

Time Tracking

Clock in and out from mobile and desktop, GPS-verified, with daily, weekly, pay-period, and overtime reports exportable for payroll.

Reporting

Custom reports with flexible rows and columns, grouping by customer, technician, service type, or date, CSV/PDF export, and scheduled email delivery.

FAQ

Pricing questions

How does pricing work?

As of October 2026, $125/month for your first user unlocks the full platform. Each additional active user is $25/month. Deactivate a user anytime and stop paying for that seat the next month.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. 30 days with access to every feature. You enter a card at signup, nothing is charged until day 30, and you can cancel anytime before then and pay nothing.

Are there any setup fees?

No setup fees, no onboarding fees, no implementation charges.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Month to month, cancel anytime.

How does payment processing work for my customers?

POM integrates with Stripe for credit card and ACH payments, at Stripe’s standard processing rates. QuickBooks Online payments and Biller Genie with Fortis are alternative processors.

Do I need QuickBooks to use POM?

No. The QuickBooks Online integration is an optional $25/month add-on. POM’s own invoicing works independently.

Can I import my existing customer data?

Yes. Data migration is free, including from competitors. Connect QuickBooks Online, or email your customer file to files@poolofficemanager.com; imports usually land within 1 business day.

How many users can I add?

No limit. $25/month per active user.

What if my team size changes seasonally?

Turn users on and off month to month; pay only for active users.

Is my data secure?

Your data is encrypted and stored securely, and you can export it anytime.

What kind of support do you offer?

US-based phone support, 614-710-0074, from a team that understands pool service.
