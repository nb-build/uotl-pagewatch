Local Services Ads vs Google Ads: What's the difference?

add remove

Standard Google Ads are customizable, pay-per-click text ads open to any business. In contrast, Local Services Ads are pay-per-lead listings for verified local service providers, which appear at the top of the page and feature a Google Verified badge. Learn how to choose between LSA and standard Google Ads.

What are Google Local Services Ads?

add remove

Based on the services you offer, Local Services Ads gives your business the opportunity to feature prominently in Google Search results in the areas you choose. Potential customers can click or tap on your ad to either call you, send you a message request, or schedule a booking directly. Learn more about Local Services Ads by visiting the Getting started with Local Services Ads help page.

What types of local service businesses are eligible for Local Services Ads?

add remove

Local Service businesses include businesses within the service categories of home, business, health, learning, care, wellness, beauty, and more. To check eligibility or to see a list of business types, visit the Getting started with Local Services Ads help page.

How much does Google Local Services Ads cost?

add remove

You choose the budget that works for your business based on the number of new leads you want every week. You can adjust your monthly budget whenever you need to. You only pay when potential customers get in touch through your ads on Local Services Ads. Estimate your ad budget by visiting the calculator tool found on the Local Services Ads homepage.

How do I set up Google Local Services Ads?

add remove

When you sign up for Local Services Ads, you’ll provide your business information, such as your business hours, service area, and services offered.

To advertise your business on Google and receive leads directly from potential customers, all businesses must pass Google’s screening and verification process. Once successfully verified, your business will earn the Google Verified badge. This badge is a signal of your business’s credibility, helping you build a trusted reputation online and instill confidence in customers ready to take action. It will show alongside details of the specific verification checks your business has passed, adding an extra layer of transparency.

Learn more about Local Services Ads by visiting the Getting started with Local Services Ads help page.
