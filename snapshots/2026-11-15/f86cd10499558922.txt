PestPac FAQs

What Is PestPac?

PestPac is business software built specifically for pest control companies. It brings customer management, scheduling, routing, mobile field tools, billing, payments, compliance documentation and reporting together on one platform, so pest control businesses can run their entire operation from a single system. PestPac is part of WorkWave.

Who Is PestPac For?

PestPac is built for residential and commercial pest control businesses with growing teams, from established local companies to multi-location enterprises. It supports companies that need to automate scheduling, routing and billing across multiple technicians, as well as franchisors and consolidators that need multi-branch management, advanced reporting and the ability to bring acquired companies onto one system.

What Features Does PestPac Include?

PestPac includes customer relationship management, scheduling, route optimization, a mobile app for technicians, billing, payment processing, automated customer communications and compliance tools. Reporting is handled through Report Writer, which lets teams build and schedule reports on the metrics that matter most. Because every feature shares the same data, field updates flow directly into billing and reporting.

How Does PestPac Help With Regulatory Compliance?

PestPac helps pest control companies stay compliant by capturing service and product application details in the field and storing them with each job. That creates audit-ready documentation across every technician and branch, which makes it easier to meet state and industry requirements and respond quickly to customer or regulatory requests.

How Much Does PestPac Cost?

PestPac pricing is based on the size and needs of your business, including the number of users and the features you need. To get a quote tailored to your operation, request a demo and a PestPac specialist will walk you through options that fit your goals.
