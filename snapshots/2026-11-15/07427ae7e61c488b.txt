What is Applied Epic?

Applied Epic is an insurance agency management system, the platform an agency runs its business on. It brings client servicing, new business and financial operations into one record across P&C and benefits.

Producers, account managers and finance teams work in the same system, and AI built into it takes on routine work such as reading documents and reconciling statements.

Is Applied Epic right for my agency?

Applied Epic fits established, growth-minded agencies running multi-line books across P&C and benefits, typically 10 to 500+ staff. Smaller or single-line personal lines agencies focused on quoting speed are usually a better fit for EZLynx®.

If you’re unsure which fits, an Applied specialist can review your book and lines of business with you.

Does Applied Epic work with the other software my agency uses?

Applied Epic offers APIs that let the other tools your agency relies on read and write client and policy data. Those tools stay connected to the same record your team works in, so nothing is rekeyed between systems.

The tools you want to keep stay in place, while Applied Epic remains the system of record.

How does Applied Epic integrate with carriers?

Applied Epic connects to carriers through Applied’s own carrier platforms. Ivans handles download and data exchange with more than 700 carrier markets, and Cytora helps carriers read incoming submissions and return quotes faster.

Applied works on both sides of that exchange, so policy, submission and quote data moves between your agency and its markets from inside Applied Epic.

How does Applied Epic compare to Vertafore?

The clearest difference is where the AI runs. In Applied Epic, Epic Conductor works inside the management system, on the same client and policy records and under the same roles and permissions your team uses.

New AI capabilities build on the data and controls you already have.

How long does it take to move to Applied Epic?

Timing depends on book size, lines of business and how much historical data comes across. Applied’s implementation team plans the conversion with you and maps your existing data before go-live.

Training runs alongside, so your team is ready at go-live.
