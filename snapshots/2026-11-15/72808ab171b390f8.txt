Platform overview

The AI platform built exclusively for distributors

Proton connects the systems distributors already run, from your ERP to your web store, into one data layer. Then AI gets to work across your business.

Book a call→Explore the platform ↓

Watch the overview: Benj Cohen, Founder & CEO · 3:12

Start with the work slowing your team down

→Reps guess who to call.Proton CRM ranks every account by what's at stake and builds each rep's call plan.→Half the catalog is missing specs.Proton PIM finds the missing specs, cites every source, and keeps one product record.

→The web store doesn't know the customer.eCommerce AI recommends products from an account's full order history, across ERP and online clicks.→Emailed POs get retyped into the ERP.Quote & Order Entry Automation reads each emailed PO, matches every line, and drafts the order for your team to approve.

Early access→Discounts are more guesswork than data-backed.Pricing AI recommends a price for every customer and SKU, based on margin and buying history.Early access→Split orders get lost between ERPs.Order Management sends each line to the ERP and branch that will fill it, and shows you when it’s confirmed.

Early access→One missing item holds up the quote.Proton Sourcing finds the item across a network of distributors and puts the options right in the quote.Early access→Cash sits in stock that doesn't move.Inventory AI forecasts demand for every SKU, so you stock what sells.
