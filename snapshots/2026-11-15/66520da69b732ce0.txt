Meet Granum

Learn about our company.

What is Granum?

Granum is the new name for the combined company behind LMN, SingleOps, and Greenius. We’re a software partner purpose-built for landscapers and arborists – combining deep industry experience with leading-edge software expertise.

Why did you choose the name Granum?

We spent a lot of time thinking about a name that truly speaks to our purpose and what we seek to provide to our customers. We kept coming back to one central idea: growth. The origin of the word Granum is ‘seed.’ It’s the foundation of everything. You plant a seed and it grows into something incredible. We strive to help our customers do just that.

Why did LMN, SingleOps, and Greenius come together?

Our companies merged in late 2024. By joining forces, we can invest more into the tools landscapers and arborists rely on every day. Together, we now have industry specific expertise working alongside technology expertise. We’re sharing best practices, learning from past efforts, combining resources, and building smarter, more intentional software and customer experiences.

What does the future look like for Granum?

With thousands of collaborative customer relationships and deep technology expertise, Granum is already the leading provider of software in this industry.

We’re thinking big for the next chapter of our company. We have the data and resources to evolve how landscapers and arborists use new technologies to improve outcomes on a daily basis. We see a future where our products surface any type of information you need and take action in seconds, wherever you are. As we innovate our goal will remain clear: move faster, integrate our solutions more seamlessly, and build new tools that serve your business at every turn.

What is LMN?

Learn about our software dedicated to landscapers.

What is LMN?

LMN has been a pioneer in landscaping software since 2009. It was designed to help landscaping businesses estimate profitably using a budget, schedule work, track crews, and invoice jobs, all in one platform. LMN is now part of the Granum suite of software products.

Who is LMN best for?

LMN supports landscaping businesses of all sizes, with tools for estimating, budgeting, job tracking, and invoicing. Whether you’re just starting out or scaling across multiple crews and services, LMN helps you stay organized and profitable.

If I sign up for LMN, do I have to sign up for other products too?

No, you can subscribe to LMN alone though you have the option to add Greenius if you’re interested.

What is SingleOps?

Learn about our software dedicated to arborists.

What is SingleOps?

SingleOps has been the #1 software platform built specifically for tree care and arborist businesses since 2013. Its easy-to-use workflow helps businesses manage their client and job lifecycle, from proposals and scheduling to billing and customer communications.

Who is SingleOps best for?

General tree and plant health care companies who have a desire to improve or grow their business by using tools to get organized, save time, and impress clients for life.

What is Greenius?

Learn about our training and development platform for landscapers and arborists.

What is Greenius?

Greenius is online training and development software built for landscaping and arborist crews. It helps crews learn job skills, safety standards, and company processes, in a formal way that ensures consistency across team members.

Do I have to subscribe to LMN or SingleOps to subscribe to Greenius?

No, you can sign-up for Greenius as a stand-alone product to help level-up your crews this season.

Customer FAQs

Find answers to common questions about our company merger.

Are my login URLs or product experience changing?

No. You can continue to use the same login and product URLs you’ve always used. You’ll start seeing the Granum name on our website and materials, but your day-to-day experience won’t change.

Are these products going to be changing?

LMN, SingleOps, and Greenius will be getting even better with the combined Granum team behind them.

Will support or billing change?

No changes are planned. You’ll continue to receive support and billing through the same product teams you’ve always worked with.

What’s the biggest benefit of this change for me?

More resources, better tools, and a stronger team backing your success. We bring together decades of landscaping and arborist experience with software know-how to build the best possible products for your business.
