The 5 SMART Steps™

Our exclusive 5 SMART Steps™ program is the key to maximizing your profitability with RETAILvantage retail management system software. There are only three ways to increase net profit: increase sales, increase gross margin and reduce expenses. With our 5 SMART Steps™ you will do all three. The first three steps improve inventory management, the fourth motivates your sales force to earn better margins, and the fifth motivates your customers to come back for more.

Industry Specific Metrics and Analysis

RETAILvantage was designed by experts with over 100 years of experience in the retail furniture industry, and strengthened by decades of feedback from furniture retailers. It provides industry specific reporting and analysis to help your business reach its goals and increase your bottom line profitability. Since every business is different from the next we offer you choices. Choose which reports and features from the multitude in RETAILvantage, which will run your business most efficiently. Choose the installation that works best for your business as well. We offer cloud-based hosted solutions in addition to traditional On-Premise installations. Running on a variety of Windows-based devices, you can access the information you need from nearly anywhere in the world. Utilizing our exclusive 5 SMART Steps™ program, RETAILvantage systematically reduces your inventory while increasing the bottom line and raising your gross margin percentage. No other furniture store software system features the industry specific functionality and metrics needed for you achieve your goals of higher profit and increased efficiency. These tools have been developed over the years from listening to our client’s feedback and incorporate it into our retail management system software.
