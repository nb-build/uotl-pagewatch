What is a cloud phone system?

A cloud phone system is a business phone service that runs over the internet from the provider's data centers, so there is no PBX hardware in your office and staff can take calls on a desk phone, laptop or mobile app. Cloud VoIP and business VoIP are other names for the same thing.

Every provider in this list covers the basics: unlimited domestic calling, an auto-attendant, call routing, voicemail transcription and mobile apps. The differences that matter are price per user per month, how easy it is to port your existing numbers, which CRMs it connects to, and how well it works for remote teams spread across locations.

Most providers in this list now sell their own AI receptionist add-on. Retell AI ranks first because it adds a full AI voice agent on top of any of them, over SIP or call forwarding, without changing your phone system.

Business VoIP provider reviews

1. Retell AI: Best AI Layer for Any Cloud Phone System

What does it do? Retell AI is an AI voice agent platform that answers calls on top of the phone system you already have. It is not a dial-tone replacement: your numbers stay with your VoIP provider or carrier, and calls reach the agent over SIP.

Who is it for? Teams that already have a VoIP provider but lose calls to voicemail during lunch, after hours or at peak times.

Retell connects to the carrier you already use through elastic SIP trunking, and you can import existing numbers from Twilio, Telnyx, Vonage or any provider that supports SIP trunks (docs). The agent answers questions from your knowledge base, books appointments, and transfers to a person with a private summary of the call first. After every call it logs a transcript, summary and the fields you choose, and can write them to HubSpot or Salesforce. One senior-care provider raised its scheduling NPS by 38% after moving booking calls to Retell.

How we compared these providers

Total Cost of Ownership, Not Sticker Price

I looked at real monthly cost, including taxes, porting fees, and required add-ons. Sticker prices on landing pages rarely match invoice reality.

AI Call Handling Depth

I looked at whether each provider actually answers calls conversationally or just transcribes voicemails after the fact.

SIP Flexibility and Carrier Choice

I weighted heavily whether a provider locks you into its own telephony or lets you bring a SIP trunk. Bring-your-own-carrier is the difference between a 30-minute migration and a 30-day cutover when you need to switch.

Integration Ecosystem Depth

CRM, helpdesk, and calendar integrations matter more than raw count. I checked specifically for Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Microsoft 365, and Google Workspace native connectors, since those cover most buyer stacks.

What do businesses use VoIP for in 2026?

After-hours inbound answering. Pairing a VoIP provider with an AI answering service closes the coverage gap without paying for a night-shift receptionist.

Appointment booking during peak hours. Dental, medical, and service businesses lose bookings when three front-desk lines are all busy. An AI appointment setter handles overflow calls, checks a shared calendar in real time, and books slots without putting callers on hold.

Inbound lead qualification for sales teams. Routing inbound calls through lead qualification before they reach a rep ensures only qualified prospects hit the calendar. Every call is logged into the CRM.

Multi-location front-desk coverage. Franchises and clinics with 3+ sites struggle with inconsistent call handling across locations. AI-powered receptionists run the same script at every branch while GoTo Connect or Nextiva handles the dial tone.

High-volume outbound campaigns. Collections, reminders, and survey calls scale poorly on human-seat pricing.

International support coverage. Teams supporting customers across regions benefit from providers like 8x8 for international VoIP minutes and AI platforms for 24/7 multi-language coverage.

What can go wrong with business VoIP?

Network quality is the ceiling. VoIP performance depends on internet stability. Jitter and packet loss turn into dropped calls and robotic audio, and AI layers amplify the problem because context gets lost when a sentence stutters. Upgrading to a dedicated business internet circuit is often step one.

Hidden taxes and regulatory fees. Published pricing rarely includes E911 fees, universal service fund surcharges, and state-level telecom taxes. Ask each provider for a sample invoice before you sign.

Number porting timelines: Porting a business number from a legacy carrier is not instant. Plan the migration around low-volume periods and keep the old line active as backup.

Compliance complexity in regulated industries: Healthcare and financial services require specific BAA contracts and call-recording consent flows. See the HHS HIPAA telehealth guidance for current rules on recorded calls and PHI.

Annual contracts and auto-renewal traps: Most providers default to annual billing with auto-renewal. Month-to-month billing usually costs more per user than annual. Read cancellation terms carefully before signing.

Business VoIP FAQ

What is the best VoIP for small business?

For a small business under 20 seats, the best VoIP provider is usually the one with no contract and every feature on the base plan; in this list that is Ooma Office. If you want AI transcription on every call, Dialpad's entry plan includes it. When comparing cloud phone systems for small business, check three things before price: whether you can keep your number, whether the plan is month to month, and whether the auto-attendant is included or an add-on.

Which VoIP providers work best for remote teams?

Remote teams need a cloud phone system that works the same on a laptop, a mobile app and a desk phone, with one number per person that follows them anywhere. Zoom Phone suits teams already meeting in Zoom, and 8x8 suits teams spread across countries. For any provider, test call quality on home internet, since VoIP quality depends on the connection.

Which business VoIP provider offers the best price-to-feature ratio for small teams?

For teams under 20 seats with basic calling needs, Ooma Office at $19.95/user/month with no contract delivers the most features per dollar. For teams that need modern AI transcription baked in, Dialpad Standard at $15/user/month is the better pick since AI is included on the base plan instead of a $15 add-on.

How does a business VoIP provider compare to an AI voice agent platform?

A business VoIP provider gives you dial tone, numbers and routing. It moves voice packets. An AI voice agent platform like Retell AI answers calls conversationally and executes tasks like booking or call transfer. Most 2026 stacks use both: a VoIP carrier for the SIP trunk and an AI layer for inbound handling.

Can any business VoIP provider handle 24/7 inbound calls without hiring more staff?

Some can now, through paid AI receptionist add-ons such as RingCentral AIR, Nextiva XBert and GoTo AI Receptionist. Without one, a VoIP provider sends after-hours calls to voicemail. An AI voice agent like Retell AI answers those calls around the clock on top of any provider.

What is the real cost of a business VoIP provider beyond the per-seat price?

Expect E911 fees, federal and state telecom taxes, and SMS registration fees on top of the per-user price. Ask each provider for a sample invoice before you sign.

Which business VoIP provider is best for healthcare practices under HIPAA?

RingCentral and Nextiva both sign HIPAA BAAs for covered services. RingCentral lists RingEX and its AI Receptionist as covered, and Nextiva limits some features on HIPAA accounts. 8x8 and GoTo Connect also support HIPAA workflows. If the use case includes AI-handled patient calls, verify the BAA covers the AI layer specifically. Retell AI offers a self-service HIPAA BAA portal, while some AI add-ons from traditional VoIP vendors require custom contracts.

Does a business VoIP provider lock you into its telephony carrier?

It depends. RingCentral and Nextiva bundle their own carrier on standard plans. Zoom Phone supports bring your own carrier (BYOC), 8x8 sets up SIP trunks through its professional services team, and Ooma includes SIP trunking on its Enterprise plan. Pure AI voice platforms like Retell AI are carrier-agnostic and work over any SIP trunk. That is useful if you want to keep an existing carrier while upgrading the intelligence layer.

How does number porting work when you switch VoIP providers?

Number porting moves your existing business number from your old carrier to the new VoIP provider, so customers keep calling the same number. You submit a port request with a recent bill from the old carrier and a letter of authorization; the new provider handles the transfer with the old carrier. Keep the old service active until the port completes, then test inbound calls before you cancel it.

How do I connect an AI voice agent to my business VoIP provider?

You have two options: forward a number, queue or IVR branch to the AI agent, or point a SIP trunk at it. Retell AI supports elastic SIP trunking and dialing to a SIP URI, and does not charge for telephony on your own trunk.
