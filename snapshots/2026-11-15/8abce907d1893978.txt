Marketing & Patient Experience

Drive Patient Engagement

Keep patients connected and your schedule full with automated reminders, two-way texting, and targeted campaigns. Dentrix centralizes communication to reduce no-shows, save staff time, and build long-term patient loyalty.

Stay connected. Build loyalty.

Reach patients at the right time and in the right way—boosting confirmations, reducing no-shows, and driving more visits with integrated and automated calls, email campaigns and two-way texting. All communication is managed through a central hub, giving front-office teams more control with less effort.

How It Works

Reach patients how they prefer

Easily send updates, reminders, and promotions by email, text, or phone from one dashboard, keeping patients informed in their preferred manner.

Automate your communications

Boost patient engagement with automated reminders and targeted email or text campaigns that reduce no-shows, promote services, and keep patients returning.

Ensure timely communications

Communicate in real time with two-way texting and automated calls, ensuring patients get timely updates in their preferred format.
