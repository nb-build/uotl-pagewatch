How the CareSmartz360-Activated Insights Integration Works

CareSmartz360 automatically sends relevant client and caregiver data to Activated Insights for the experience management company to launch personalized surveys or send invites within our software.

Clients and caregivers can then complete surveys directly in CareSmartz360. Consequently, our platform displays Activated Insights feedback reports, allowing agencies to identify and address satisfaction issues.

Have a question?

What is the main benefit of the integration?

The integration helps agencies import client satisfaction survey data, benchmarking insights, and other relevant metrics directly into CareSmartz360. Thus, they can access feedback and performance data within their existing workflow.

Are there any further steps needed to activate the integration and unlock its benefits?

This integration is all about ease of use. You won’t need to worry about any configuration steps – it’s ready to go when you are!

What aspects of the user journey will we be gathering feedback on?

Agencies can leverage Activated Insights’ pre-designed surveys on client and caregiver experience, along with the ability to customize questions for targeted feedback.

Could you elaborate on the improvements this integration brings to caregiver management?

The Activated Insights and CareSmartz360 integration empowers agencies to personalize training and support for caregivers. Client feedback and satisfaction scores pinpoint areas for growth, leading to better care delivery.

Does the integration offer customization options to fit different agency workflows?

Yes. The integration is built to bend, not break! Agencies can choose the data that matters most and set up automatic reports and alerts. This ensures a smooth fit with your current processes.

Is there anywhere I can check out more details about how these two systems work together?

Have questions? Our support teams at CareSmartz360 and Activated Insights are happy to help. Contact us for more details on the integration’s features and benefits.
