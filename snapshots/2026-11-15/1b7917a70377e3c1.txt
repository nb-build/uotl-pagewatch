Client Re-Engagement Report

2 min readUpdated October 29, 2026

Overview

With over 20 built-in reports, you’ll know exactly how your business is doing. Learn more.

The client re-engagement report shows the clients who have not had a job closed in the last 12 months. You can use this report as a tool to flag which clients fall into this category so they can be re-engaged so you can win more work.

To find the client re-engagement report:

Select Insights from the side navigation.

Select Reports.

Under Client reports, select Client re-engagement.

Columns

This report has six columns:

Name: This is the client’s name.

Phone #: This is the client’s primary phone number.

Billing Address: This is the billing address for the client including street address, city, state, and zip code.

Last Closed Job: This is the date of the client’s last completed job. It’s possible that they might have an ongoing recurring job that has yet to be closed.

Open: Clicking the icon in this column will open the client’s profile.

Re-engaging your clients

After identifying the clients that an be re-engaged with this report, the next step is to follow-up with them. You can follow-up with your clients individually or the follow-up can be automated with our Mailchimp integration. Using Mailchimp will allow you to set up an email campaign that will target these clients.
