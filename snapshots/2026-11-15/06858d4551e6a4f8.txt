What is Numa?

Numa is the AI Operating System that increases CSI by improving how dealerships engage with customers and coordinate across departments. It integrates with your CRM and DMS, flags at-risk customers immediately, captures every call and text, and books appointments with timely follow-up. We free up employees’ time and orchestrate their work better.

How is Numa different from a regular AI Voice or phone-answering service?

Numa Voice AI connects to your DMS in real time and answers with full context instead of reading a script. Because it already knows the caller's vehicle and open repair order, it can tell a customer their car is waiting on a part and ready Thursday, or book them into a genuinely open service bay without double-booking. Most AI voice only greets and transfers; Numa resolves the request and writes it back to the DMS.

What results do dealerships see with Numa?

Dealerships use Numa to recover service revenue lost to missed calls and slow replies. Stores running Numa respond to customers in 13 minutes, compared with an industry average of nearly 23 hours, and lift repair-order dollars per advisor by over 35%.

How long does it take to get Numa up and running?

Most dealerships go live in 2-3 weeks. A dedicated Numa team handles DMS integration, phone configuration, and team training, with check-ins in the first weeks to catch issues early. Because Numa connects to systems you already run, setup doesn’t disrupt daily operations, and most teams are proficient within 90 days.

Is Numa a good fit for my dealership or dealer group?

Numa is built for franchise dealerships and multi-rooftop groups running high call and text volume through fixed ops, where missed communication directly costs service revenue and retention. It fits single stores wanting to stop missing calls and large groups standardizing operations across rooftops alike.
