Register my warranty

Thank you for purchasing a GAF roof! Find your warranty below to register.

Register or transfer a GAF warranty

Register your shingles & accessories with GAF

It is not necessary to register your shingles & accessories with GAF in order to access the protection available in our base warranties, but you may elect to do so anyway. To get started, click the link below.

Register now

Register EverGuard® TPO products here

If you had EverGuard® TPO installed on your single family home, you can complete the warranty registration process through the link below.

Register now

Transfer your limited warranty

If you've recently purchased a property with GAF shingles and/or accessories installed on the roof, you need to provide GAF with notice of the property transfer within the time frame specified in your Limited Warranty in order to ensure continued protection. In order to request a transfer, email warrantytransfers@gaf.com or click the link below. Please note that in order to complete the warranty transfer, you will need to provide proof that you own the property. If you have any questions, call 1-877-423-7663.

Request a warranty transfer

Register enhanced warranties (certified contractors only)

GAF enhanced warranties can be registered only by contractors certified by GAF using their Partner Portal account
