FAQs

What’s an online Booking Page?​

A Booking Page showcases your service menu, availability, and prices online. It has a custom URL and can function as a standalone website. Visitors can self-schedule and pay for appointments 24/7 without having to call, email, or travel to your premises.

Sign up for your free appointment scheduler and create a Booking Page in minutes.

What’s the difference between my Setmore calendar and Booking Page?​

Your calendar is only visible to you and permitted staff members. Add appointments, edit your working hours, and get a real-time view of team schedules. When you update your availability, it reflects on your Booking Page.

Your Booking Page is accessible by anyone. It can function as a free booking website or connect to your existing website. Share its link directly, through emails and DMs, and connect it to your social media pages. Customers can schedule appointments online when it suits them. Once a booking is confirmed, the details land in your calendar.

Your calendar and Booking Page work in harmony, syncing instantly.

How do I take online payments in advance?

Simply enable a payment integration and opt to accept Booking Page payments in your app’s settings. Learn more about accepting payments online and in-person with your free appointment booking app.

Can I connect Setmore with other calendars?​

Yes, 1-way and 2-way sync options are available to connect other calendars. 2-way syncs are particularly popular as they send appointment details to and from Setmore.

Learn more about reducing scheduling conflicts with Google and Office 365 calendar syncs.

How can I get more customers with free appointment scheduling software?

Free appointment software offers new leads and loyal customers an instant route to book online. There’s no need to contact you directly to find a time that works.

Your Booking Page connects to your existing website, Facebook, and Instagram, enabling people to learn about your services and self-schedule appointments 24/7. Every online channel becomes an all-new conversion machine. You can also add your Booking Page URL and QR code to emails, business cards, and promo items, helping to capitalize on interest without any wait times.

What do I get with my Setmore account?​

Every Setmore account comes with an online Booking Page and all-in-one calendar. You can create unlimited appointments, automate confirmations and reminders, add staff, host video meetings, and store customer details.
