Growth

Built-in analytics with WellSky Personal Care software tracks key performance metrics and helpful data. This provides a competitive edge when marketing your private duty agency to new clients and referral sources. Track changes in condition to reduce hospitalizations and access easy-to-use dashboards, reports, and marketing tools to optimize business performance and prove value using WellSky’s private-duty software.

Staff management

Whether you are a franchise agency or independently owned, our comprehensive personal care software can help you recruit, manage, and retain top caregiver talent. Applicant tracking, screening, and training tools keep hiring efforts on track, and our built-in safety program fosters a safe work environment for all staff. The easy-to-use WellSky Personal Care solution empowers caregivers to spend less time on administrative tasks and more time providing high-quality private-duty home care.

AI workflows

WellSky Personal Care, powered by WellSky SkySense AI™, uses AI-driven tools, including summarization and ambient documentation, to boost talent retention, recruitment, time management, client experience, and operational efficiency — enabling staff to focus more on meaningful human interactions instead of technology.

Caregiver engagement

Caregiver satisfaction is critical to retention and engagement. WellSky Personal Care is not only the leading solution for documenting home-based visits, it also provides innovative tools to help agencies with engagement, recruitment, and retention of caregiver talent.

Family communication

Keep families connected and eliminate the struggle of paper logs with the Family Room feature in WellSky’s Personal Care software. A free and secure access portal, the Family Room gives loved ones access to the client care calendar and shift notes, plus the ability to pay or split a portion of the bill.

Mobile app

Minimize the day-to-day burden for caregivers and administrators with the convenient, user-friendly, WellSky Personal Care mobile app. Easily manage schedules, shifts, tasks, and profiles from the convenience of your mobile device. Apple Store Google Play store

All-in-one platform

Our end-to-end personal care software combines in-home and virtual care solutions across care delivery, caregiver management, and back-office operations, with easy-to-use interfaces for desktop and mobile. Reduce operational burden, optimize care, and watch your private-duty home care business grow.

HITRUST certified

WellSky Personal Care software has obtained HITRUST CSF certification, an independent third-party verification of HIPAA compliance and security and privacy best practices. The HITRUST Alliance requires compliance on over 300 controls in 19 areas such as information protection, password management, and access control.

WellSky Recruitment Fraud Alert

WellSky has been made aware of fraudulent recruiting activities by individuals who are falsely claiming to represent WellSky. Specifically, there are bad actors who are contacting people through various web platforms (e.g., LinkedIn, Indeed, Instant Messaging Accounts, and text messaging) and conducting fake interviews and often providing fake offers of employment. Their goal is to persuade victims to pay money or to divulge sensitive personal information.

Please be careful and consider the following information as you progress your job search with WellSky:

The WellSky recruiting process will always begin with candidates submitting a formal job application directly through our official WellSky Careers website at www.WellSky.com.

Any communication you receive from WellSky will come from a WellSky recruiter with an @wellsky.com email address.

WellSky recruiters will never ask you to provide any personal or financial information via email or text messages.

Please click here to review our current active postings.

If you believe you have been a victim of a crime, please contact your local authorities. In addition, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) offers a reporting service for those who have been targeted by a recruiting scam. For more information on job scams, visit the FTC at https://consumer.ftc.gov/all-scams/job-scams.
