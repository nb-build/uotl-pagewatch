Everything the job needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Teravo Pro was built for the people who actually run the jobs, whether one owner wears every hat or a team of estimators, schedulers, and office staff each own a piece. Simple enough to learn in a day, complete enough to run the whole company on, with no spreadsheets in between.

Draw the fence, price the job

Sketch the line on screen, drop gates where they go, and the bid prices itself from your own material and labor numbers — priced the way you actually sell.

Proposals customers can sign

A clean proposal with your options, photos, and terms — signed electronically from the customer’s phone, deposit invoice sent through QuickBooks, no printing and no chasing.

Follow-ups that run themselves

Every proposal kicks off your follow-up sequence automatically — sent during business hours, stopped the moment the customer signs. The quiet leads stop slipping away.

QuickBooks, actually integrated

Estimates, deposits, invoices, and job costs flow into QuickBooks on their own. Not an export button — a real connection, because patched-together integrations you have to micromanage are why this product exists.

Scheduling built around crews

Install days, crew assignments, and calendar blocks in one view, with work orders and material lists generated straight from the bid. The week of the job, each crew’s packet goes out to them automatically, with the work order, pull list, tools checklist, and walk-through all in one place.

A portal your customers can check

Every customer gets a no-login link with their project status, signed documents, job-site photos, payments, and the warranty papers for their exact fence — fewer "just checking in" calls.

Cash flow you can actually see

Estimates, deposits, invoices, and job costs land in QuickBooks correctly from day one — so your books are clean enough for real cash-flow and job-profit reporting, not guesswork.

One place for the whole job

Lead to bid to signed proposal to scheduled crew to paid invoice — every customer, photo, file, and callback on one screen instead of multiple apps.

KPIs beyond the sales board

Sales numbers are the easy part. Teravo Pro also tracks what happens after the signature: estimated vs actual hours, margin on every job, and which crews and fence types make you money, all gathered automatically as the work gets done.

New: Teravo Pro on your phone

The Teravo Pro app is now in the App Store and Google Play. Draw and price a job in the yard, add photos to the job, scan paperwork, and get an alert the minute a lead comes in or a proposal gets signed. Included with every plan.
