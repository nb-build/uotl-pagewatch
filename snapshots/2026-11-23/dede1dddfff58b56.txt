Technology

We are building the future of healthcare operations.

Healthcare organizations are built on thousands of repetitive operational tasks. Yet the industry has largely addressed them with disconnected point solutions and more administrative staff. The next generation of providers will be powered by intelligent software that handles the operational burden, allowing teams to focus on caring for patients.

We mapped the entire operational journey. Every module we build removes another manual process, and every new capability makes the whole platform stronger.

Below is our not-so-secret master plan.

Lead Generation

Patients no longer just search Google. They ask ChatGPT, browse maps, click ads, and discover providers across dozens of channels. Understand where demand comes from, optimize marketing spend, and make sure patients can find you wherever they’re looking.

Schedule Appointment

Patients call, text, email, and visit your website. Meet them where they are instead of forcing them into another platform. Intelligent scheduling should optimize both the patient experience and the practice schedule.

Insurance Verification

Insurance mistakes create denials, write-offs, and frustrated patients. Verification should be fast, reliable, and consistent across every visit.

Confirm Appointment

Reduce no-shows with intelligent outreach. Call, text, or email patients at the right time, stop once they confirm, and automatically help fill open slots.

The Visit

Great patient experiences don’t happen by accident. Coordinate schedules, optimize staffing, and use procurement intelligence to ensure every provider has the right supplies at the right time.

Claims & Compliance

From claim submission to EOB posting and denial management, automate the operational work that turns completed care into collected revenue.

Reactivation & Recall

The easiest patient to schedule is one that already trusts your practice. Most practices don’t have time to follow up with every inactive patient. Keep patients engaged, reactivate unfinished treatment, and make sure no one falls through the cracks.

Provider Readiness

Ensure providers are credentialed, compliant, and ready to serve patients.
