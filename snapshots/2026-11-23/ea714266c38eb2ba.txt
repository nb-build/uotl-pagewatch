Frequently Asked Questions

See how CleanGuru bid templates help cleaning businesses start faster and build more consistent bids and proposals.

What is a bid template in cleaning software?

A bid template is a pre-built starting point for a cleaning bid. In CleanGuru, it helps load the right setup for the type of work and building you are bidding, so you do not have to build every bid from scratch.

What cleaning services do CleanGuru bid templates support?

CleanGuru supports bid templates for janitorial and office cleaning, post-construction cleaning, maid service, tile and grout cleaning, window cleaning, disinfecting and sanitizing, carpet cleaning, and move-in / move-out cleaning.

Can I use CleanGuru bid templates for commercial and residential cleaning?

Yes. Some CleanGuru bid templates go straight to building selection, while others let you choose commercial or residential first. That helps you start with the right setup for the type of work you are bidding.

Do CleanGuru bid templates include areas, tasks, and frequencies?

Yes. CleanGuru bid templates are designed to load the right areas, tasks, and cleaning frequencies for the job, so your team can build bids faster and stay consistent.

Can I view sample proposals before building my own?

Yes. You can browse sample proposals to see how CleanGuru templates turn into polished proposals across different cleaning services and building types before building your own.
