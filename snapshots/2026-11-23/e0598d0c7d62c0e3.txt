Frequently asked questions

What is tl;dv?

tl;dv is an AI meeting assistant and AI note taker. It records your meetings, writes the notes, transcript and action items, and sends them to the tools your team already uses, from your CRM to any LLM over MCP.

How is an AI meeting assistant different from an AI notetaker?

An AI notetaker records the call and writes the notes. An AI meeting assistant also does the work that follows: it updates your CRM, drafts the follow-up, briefs you before the next call and answers questions across all your meetings. tl;dv does both.

Which meeting platforms does the tl;dv notetaker work with?

The notetaker joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, plus Slack huddles and WhatsApp calls. For in-person meetings there’s the mobile app, and the desktop app records without a bot in the call.

Is tl;dv free?

Yes. You can start for free and keep using tl;dv on the free plan. Paid plans add more AI features, integrations and admin controls.

Do participants have to consent to being recorded?

You decide. tl;dv can ask every participant for consent on screen before recording, with a message written in your own words, or record without asking when you don’t need it.

Is my meeting data used to train AI?

No. Your meetings are never used to train AI models. You choose how long recordings are kept and can delete them at any time.

How does tl;dv work with ChatGPT, Claude and other LLMs?

tl;dv connects to any MCP-compatible assistant. Ask questions across all your meetings, or let your agent combine meeting data with your other tools and run whole workflows.

Which CRMs and tools does tl;dv integrate with?

tl;dv syncs notes, next steps and deal fields to HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive, and sends meeting data to Slack, Notion, Jira and thousands of other apps through Zapier, the API and webhooks.

How many languages does tl;dv support?

tl;dv transcribes and writes notes in 100+ languages, so international teams get the same quality in every meeting.
