FAQ

Does Smart Service work with QuickBooks?

Yes — Smart Service is built around QuickBooks, not bolted onto it. Smart Service Cloud syncs automatically with QuickBooks Online and connects to QuickBooks Desktop through import and export, and Smart Service Desktop installs as a dedicated QuickBooks Desktop add-on. Either way, jobs, invoices, customers, and payments get into QuickBooks without being keyed in twice.

Does Smart Service have AI?

Yes, in Smart Service Cloud. Mission Control is an AI dispatch board with five assistants — Intelligence, Optimization, Night Vision, Forecasting, and Wingman. The Oracle answers reporting questions in plain English. The AI Writing Assistant rewrites tech notes for customers, and the AI Knowledge Center answers questions from your uploaded manuals. All of it ships inside Cloud with no add-on module or per-seat AI fee; features built on Claude or ChatGPT use your own account at the provider's rates. AI is not available in Smart Service Desktop.

How do custom digital forms work in Smart Service?

Smart Service replicates your work orders, inspections, and compliance forms as fully interactive, branded forms on the tech's device — drop-downs, checkboxes, signature capture, photo capture — saving real-time data straight into the system, ready for dashboards and reports the moment the form closes.

Is support really US-based?

Yes. Support is in-house in Dublin, Ohio — never outsourced to a third-party call center. We answer roughly 90% of calls live during business hours. Call 888-518-0818 and you'll reach someone who actually knows the product.

Should I choose Smart Service Cloud or Smart Service Desktop?

Smart Service Cloud is cloud-native — it runs in any browser, syncs across devices, and includes a mobile app for techs. Smart Service Desktop installs alongside QuickBooks Desktop on the same machine, with iFleet as its mobile companion. Most businesses moving to the cloud or running QuickBooks Online pick Cloud; long-time QuickBooks Desktop shops often stay on Desktop. A demo will settle it in about five minutes.

What does a demo involve?

A free, live 30-minute walkthrough with our team — your workflow, your questions, no canned video. Call 888-518-0818 or book online, and you'll see scheduling, dispatch, mobile work orders, and QuickBooks sync working together before you commit to anything.

What kinds of service businesses use Smart Service?

Hundreds of trade businesses across the US and Canada run on Smart Service — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest control, garage door, septic, fire protection, chimney sweep, and dozens more, from single-truck operators to multi-location operations. If your work happens at the customer's property, it fits.
