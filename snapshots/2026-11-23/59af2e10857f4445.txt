What is manufacturing ERP software?

Manufacturing ERP software is an integrated business management system that connects the processes used to make products with the wider functions needed to run the company. It brings manufacturing resource planning together with inventory, procurement, sales, shop floor management, workforce planning, costing, and finances.

Instead of entering the same information in separate spreadsheets and applications, teams work with shared, up-to-date data. A customer order can create demand for materials and capacity, production can update stock and actual costs, and fulfillment can feed invoicing and financial reporting.

MRPeasy gives small manufacturers this connected ERP coverage without the complexity of an oversized enterprise system.

One manufacturing ERP system. Six connected workflows

Order to cash

Estimate costs and lead times, create quotations and customer orders, book available stock, launch production, prepare shipments, issue invoices, and manage returns without re-entering the same order data.

Plan to produce

Turn demand into material and capacity plans. Use BOMs, routings, the master production schedule, and detailed production schedules to decide what to make, how much, and when.

Source to receive

Calculate purchase requirements, compare vendor terms and lead times, create purchase orders, receive materials, and connect supply dates directly with production demand.

Inventory and traceability

See what is on hand, available, booked, and expected. Track materials and products by location, lot, batch, or serial number from receipt through production and delivery.

Shop floor and workforce

Give workers simple interfaces for viewing tasks and reporting operations, labor, and material use. Monitor progress, workload, performance, quality, and bottlenecks in real time.

Costing and finance

Compare estimated and actual manufacturing costs, including materials, labor, and overhead. Track inventory value and profitability, then use built-in accounting or connect QuickBooks Online or Xero.

Manufacturing ERP built for the way you produce

Make-to-order manufacturers and job shops

Connect each customer order with its BOM, routing, production schedule, material requirements, delivery date, and actual cost. Quote custom work more reliably and understand the margin on each job.

Make-to-stock and batch manufacturers

Use forecasts and the master production schedule to balance expected demand with inventory and capacity. Plan batches, manage reorder points, and track lots, expiry dates, and quality records.

Engineer-to-order and configure-to-order businesses

Manage complex and configurable products with multi-level and Matrix BOMs, custom fields, and product version and revision control. Keep order-specific requirements connected with purchasing and production.

Discrete, process, and subcontract manufacturers

Manage routings and assembly operations for discrete products, recipes and co-products for process manufacturing, and purchase orders and free-issue materials for subcontracted operations.
