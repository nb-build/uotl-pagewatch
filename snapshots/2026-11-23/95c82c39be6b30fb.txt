Do we have to use every module?

No. Turn on what your team needs. A crew that doesn’t run marketing campaigns can ignore Marketing Tools entirely without it cluttering their view.

What actually happens to the call after it ends?

The caller is saved as a contact, a lead job is created in your pipeline with the source attached, and the whole call lands on that homeowner’s communication timeline next to any texts or other calls. You get a transcript summary, the call duration so you can triage the morning’s leads in a couple of minutes.

Can we bring our existing data over?

Yes. Import by CSV or the lead ingestion API, or run a guided migration from Salesforce, MarketSharp, or Go High Level during onboarding.

Does every team member see the same screen?

Access is role-based, so a scheduler runs the board and a CSR manages communication. Crews open app.oblysspro.com on their phone and work from the work order’s checklist.

How is this different from a general contractor CRM?

General CRMs are built for same-day service calls. Oblyss is built around engineering holds, permit delays, per-pier pricing, and milestone billing, the parts of the job most platforms don’t account for.
