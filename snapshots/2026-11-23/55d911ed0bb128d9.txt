The End-to-End Orchestration Loop

Here is what that looks like end to end: IVA picks up a missed call and the context behind it. ChatKit books the appointment, creates the follow-up task, and drafts the email that should go out next. For something routine – a quote confirmation, a document request – it can send on its own. For anything with a personal edge – a birthday note, a check-in after a hard renewal conversation – it drafts and waits.

Expect Outcomes, Not More Management

Every action traces back to where it came from and what it is for – nothing here is a black box. And it all runs inside the controls you already have: turn any piece off, fine-tune what it is allowed to do, adjust access at the CRM level. It is not a new feature. It is a shift from managing a system to expecting an outcome.
