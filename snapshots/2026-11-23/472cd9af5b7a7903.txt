Application submissions shouldn't feel so painful

Insurance applications still move by email, phone call and PDF, and every handoff with your client and in your agency loses detail. New business starts from a blank form, renewals ask clients to retype what they said last year, and your team rekeys all of it.

The Collaboration Portal in Applied Epic - the tool many agencies still know as Indio – gets you and the client working together in one application, and the finished answers go seamlessly to carriers and onto the client account.

Key benefits

What collaborative digital applications change for your agency

Applications back faster

A client fills the application online with your team alongside them, so the paperwork stops being the reason a bind waits.

Renewals clients don't dread

Last year's answers come back for the client to confirm, so a renewal takes minutes instead of an evening.

Staff time off data entry

Your team stops retyping what the client already wrote, and the finished answers land on the account without a second pass.

A record of what changed

If a client later says they disclosed something, the application shows what they answered and when they signed it.

Run a more collaborative and automated application process

How it works

Automatically fill past risk detail into the application for a head start

Upload dec pages or last year's application, and AI will read the documents and fill out the ACORD application. Eliminate the rekeying and provide the human approval before you get started.

Work the application online with your insured

Answer questions alongside multiple stakeholders from your client in one collaborative online form via a secure portal, so comments and changes are tracked. Stop the emailing and chasing phone calls.

Maintain a clean schedules view

Upload existing schedule workbook data so your clients can collaborate on the same live documents. Stop sharing large Excel files and documents and better manage version control.

Turn every renewal into a verification step instead of a full rebuild

How it works

Carry last year's information forward

Autopopulate last year's risk details into the new year’s forms and applications, so the client edits only what has actually changed. No full restarts required.

Automatically log every change as it happens

Timestamp each client made update, verification, and comment, so you can intelligently track what the client confirmed and when. Stop searching line by line for changes.

Update the policy details without rekeying information

Keep client details from the online collaboration portal synced with the policy details in Applied Epic once it's final and they sign. Maintain your records with ease.

Part of Applied Epic

Applications sync to the management system

A signed application updates the policy, attaches documents and logs its history in the same place your team already works. Because the form lives inside Applied Epic instead of a separate application tool you have to keep synced, there's one record of the account, not two.

Frequently asked about the Collaboration Portal

Collapse All

What is collaborative insurance application software?

Collaborative insurance application software is how you and your client complete and sign applications online instead of trading paper forms, PDFs and email. You both work the same application, your client signs it digitally, and you send the finished packet to carriers without printing anything. The Collaboration Portal is Applied Epic's collaborative insurance application software.

Is Collaboration Portal the same as Indio?

Yes. The Collaboration Portal is a module for insurance forms and applications that is part of the Applied Epic platform, formerly known as Indio®. If you have used or are using Indio for application collection, eSignature or ACORD form-fill, you are the Applied Epic Collaboration Portal.

Does the Collaboration Portal integrate with Applied Epic?

Yes, policy header and ACORD form data exchanges both ways between the core Applied Epic management system and the Collaboration Portal, so a finished application updates the policy without manual re-entry.

Can AI fill insurance applications automatically?

Yes. AutoFill in the Collaboration portal leverages AI to read carrier documents (dec pages, prior-year applications, schedules, carrier supplemental forms) and write the answers into the corresponding fields of the application. It flags anything you need to review and confirm before the data writes to the fields in the form, with a change log that records which fields got filled.

How does the Collaboration Portal reduce E&O exposure?

Every client verification, edit, comment and schedule change is logged automatically with a timestamp, so the file shows what was collected, what the client confirmed, and when. That's the record you can point to if a client or carrier ever disputes what was submitted and when.
