D-Tools and Portal.io
are now one company

Two long-standing partners have merged, bringing together the complementary strengths of D-Tools and Portal.io. Portal.io founder Kirk Chisholm becomes Chief Product Officer of the combined company. One team, one product vision, and three purpose-built products that scale with your business, from your first proposal through enterprise deployment.

This is not two companies meeting for the first time.

D-Tools and Portal.io have been partners for more than a decade, since Portal.io's managed product catalog was first integrated into D-Tools SI, and neither company has grown by targeting the other's customers. That partnership worked because both companies have always been aligned on the same thing: building solutions that meet the needs of today's integrator. Merging is the natural next step in the evolution of that relationship.

Integrators have long had to assemble their software from different vendors, with one tool for proposals, another for system design and project management, and a third for service and the back office. Every transition costs time and every seam introduces error. Integrators are better served by one team investing in the tools they depend on every day than by two teams solving the same problems twice.

The merger also brings together the largest network of integrators in the industry, running from single-operator shops to enterprise teams with hundreds of users. That scale gives the combined company a broader and more representative view of how integrators work, where they struggle and what they need from software, creating a stronger foundation for what it builds next.

One company, three purpose-built products

Rather than force a single product onto every customer, the combined company offers three purpose-built products and works with each integrator to match the right one to their business and stage of growth. For customers of every platform, nothing changes overnight. Both teams share a deliberate, customer-led approach to how the products evolve from here, guided by what integrators tell them they want rather than by how quickly anything can be brought together.

Speed, simplicity, and a client-facing proposal experience that wins the job.

Accurate, consistent, and current product data.

D-Tools operates the industry's most comprehensive product data catalog, with complete specifications and pricing from thousands of manufacturer and distributor suppliers. Portal.io brings its own extensive managed catalog and established relationships with industry manufacturers. Accurate, consistent, and current product information is critical to an integrator's day-to-day business, and bringing the two catalogs together improves that accuracy, delivers more up-to-date product and pricing information, and strengthens specification workflows across the combined portfolio.

Your project and customer data belongs to you. Market intelligence delivered to manufacturers uses aggregated and anonymized data, and integrators choose whether to participate. Our policies are published at d-tools.com/privacy and portal.io/privacy.

Built product-first, for an industry AI is about to reshape.

The combined organization is built product-first and integrator-first, earning every dealer's business through the quality of the experience rather than the length of a feature list.Both companies share a conviction that AI will reshape how integrators sell, design, manage, and execute their work. Portal.io was the first in the industry to introduce an AI proposal builder, and D-Tools has been investing in AI capability across its platforms. Together the teams intend to apply AI throughout the integrator workflow as a foundation that makes complex business processes faster, easier, and more intelligent, while using AI internally to build and ship for customers at a pace neither company could reach alone.Anyone can prototype software quickly today, but a software partner takes it further, building fast, scalable, secure solutions backed by real support teams, a platform that stays open to dealers who want to build and integrate their own tools, and a community of integrators who rely on it.

The same commitment, a stronger foundation.

The combined company will continue to serve the CEDIA channel, residential and light commercial custom integrators, with the same commitment both companies have always brought, while continuing to expand into commercial, pro AV, security, and international integrator communities where both see growing demand. Existing relationships with distributors, buying groups, and industry associations continue and will be strengthened under the combined company.

FAQs

Is Portal.io being discontinued?

No. There are no plans to end-of-life Portal.io, D-Tools Cloud, or D-Tools SI. Portal.io continues to be developed and supported by the team that built it.

Do I have to migrate to a different product?

No. There is no forced migration. You stay on the product you signed up for, on your current terms.

Will my pricing change?

Your current pricing continues under the terms of your existing agreement. As with any subscription business, pricing evolves over time, and any future change is communicated with appropriate notice.

Will my support team change?

No. Same teams, channels, hours, and standards. Portal.io customers reach Portal.io support; D-Tools customers reach D-Tools support.

What happens to my data and my integrations?

Your data remains yours, and existing integrations keep working, including QuickBooks, Zoho, Salesforce, and Stripe.

Which product is right for my business?

For most integrators, the one you are already on. Your account team can walk you through the fit.
