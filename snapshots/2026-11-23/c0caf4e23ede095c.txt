Where the difference is

Kaseya Quote Manager does have automated quote approvals, on quote value, product price and discount. Salesbuildr can also hold a quote on markup or annual recurring revenue (ARR), so a thin margin or a large recurring change waits for a check before it goes to the client.

Kaseya Quote Manager syncs Autotask organizations about every 15 minutes, and ConnectWise into Kaseya Quote Manager takes 5 to 15 minutes. Halo is not found in their docs. Salesbuildr gets company and contact changes in real time on Autotask, ConnectWise and Halo, so reps quote the right customer and contact without re-keying or waiting for that sync.

Salesbuildr reads those PSA contracts back in. Whitespace shows what each client already has, so upsell, cross-sell and renewal proposals start from the contract instead of a spreadsheet. That read-back is not found in Kaseya Quote Manager's docs.

Why MSPs switch from Kaseya Quote Manager

Works with Halo too. Salesbuildr syncs natively with Autotask, ConnectWise and Halo. We did not find Halo support on Kaseya Quote Manager's public pages, so if you run Halo, or might later, your quoting moves with you.

Approval rules on ARR and markup. Both tools hold quotes for approval. Kaseya Quote Manager's rules use quote value, product price and discount; Salesbuildr can also hold a quote on markup or annual recurring revenue (ARR), so a thin margin or a large recurring change waits for a check before it goes to the client.

A public API on Premium. The public REST API with webhooks (Premium) lets you automate quoting. Kaseya Quote Manager's public API is read-only.

Questions about switching

Can I keep Kaseya Quote Manager running while I switch?

Yes. Salesbuildr runs alongside your current quoting tool while you set up, so you switch on a date your team picks and cancel the old tool once its last open quotes are closed.

What happens to my open quotes?

Let open quotes finish in Kaseya Quote Manager and start every new quote in Salesbuildr from a fixed date. Quotes expire, so the overlap ends by itself. Bundles and client-specific prices are rebuilt as Salesbuildr bundles and pricing books.

What happens in my PSA when a client accepts a quote?

In Halo and Autotask, your quote creates and updates the opportunity and quote. On approval, the signed PDF is uploaded, then you build the Halo sales order or the Autotask ticket from what was approved.
