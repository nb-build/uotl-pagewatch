Our story

2007

Our future CEO, Matt Gornick, helps a cleaning company set up quality control software. It’s a long, frustrating process. In the end, the staff finds it too complicated and doesn’t use it. The experience gives Matt the idea to create an alternative that would be easy to set up and simple to use.

2009

Matt starts OrangeQC as an easy-to-use quality control system for cleaning companies. He works with a team to build an intuitive app for inspections and tickets, as well as a web app capable of powerful reporting and analysis.

2010

The OrangeQC system is first deployed at the University of Chicago Medical Center.

2011

OrangeQC usage expands outside North America for the first time, to an airport in Istanbul.

2014

Our team exhibits at the ISSA North America Show, beginning an annual tradition.

2017

OrangeQC officially joins APPA as a member-partner. We’re invited by the University of Colorado at Boulder to attend the first of many APPA regional conferences.

2023

APPA invites our team to contribute a chapter on inspection methodology to the Operational Guidelines for Educational Facilities: Custodial, Fourth Edition.

Today

We serve teams across North America and the world. These include commercial cleaning companies and teams in education, healthcare, transit, and more. We’re known as the top choice for easy-to-use, reliable inspection software for top-tier facilities teams.
