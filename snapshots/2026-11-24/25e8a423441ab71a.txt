Related Articles

FAQ

Is it legal to record in-home sales appointments?

Yes, when it's done correctly. In two-party consent states, the homeowner needs to know before recording starts. See our guide to recording sales conversations for the state-by-state breakdown, and confirm your policy with your legal counsel.

How do sales reps tell homeowners about recording?

Most keep it simple: "I have my notetaker running so I don't miss anything about your project." Homeowners are used to it from their own doctor's visits, and reps get comfortable after a few appointments. Our team helps you set the wording and roll it out.

Will our sales reps feel like they're being watched?

Siro is built for reps first. They review their own appointments, get feedback right after each one and practice what they missed, so most of the coaching happens without a manager in the loop. Managers use it to help, not to audit every sit.

Our sales reps are 1099 contractors. Can we still use Siro?

Yes. Many home improvement companies with independent-contractor sales teams use Siro as a tool reps choose to get better with, not a monitoring system.

Does Siro work with our CRM?

Siro sends appointment summaries and what was promised to the systems your team already uses, so the office and production work from what was actually said. See all integrations.

Is there field sales software for small home improvement companies?

Yes. A five-rep company gets the same platform as a 500-rep one: recording, scoring, follow-ups and coaching on every appointment, with onboarding and support included.

Does Siro work for roofing, windows, bath and solar sales teams?

Yes. Siro works across in-home trades, including roofing, windows and doors, siding, bath, basements, decks and solar, because the sit is the same: one rep, one homeowner, one decision. Kanga Roof, Window World and Southwest Exteriors all use Siro. For HVAC, plumbing and other service teams, see Home Services.

Can AI coaching replace ride-alongs for in-home sales reps?

It replaces the sampling. A ride-along covers one appointment, and reps sell differently with a manager in the room. Siro covers every appointment for every rep, so managers coach the moment that decided the job and save ride-alongs for when they're most useful.

How is Siro different from other in-home sales recording tools?

Most tools record the appointment for the manager. Siro is built for the rep first: a next step during the sit, a debrief call right after, practice on what they missed, and control over which recordings they share. Reps who get that value keep recording, so managers see every appointment. Siro covers the phone calls around the sit too.

What is AI sales software for in-home sales teams?

It records and analyzes in-home appointments, then turns them into coaching, appointment notes and CRM-ready data. Sales reps get help before, during and after each sit, and managers see every appointment without riding along.
