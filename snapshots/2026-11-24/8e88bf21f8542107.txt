How it works

From dormant lead to live phone call

One coordinated team of AI agents acquires your leads, works them with AI, and hands warm, ready-to-talk prospects directly to you. Here's the relay:

01

Social & email pull them back

Dynamic listing alerts and retargeting ads keep your brand in front of every lead — and drive them back to your IDX site instead of the portals.

02

The platform spots intent

Repeat views, favorites, shares, new saved searches — real signals that someone's getting ready to move.

03

AI Text starts the conversation

It opens with context it already has, replies in under 60 seconds, and surfaces the ones who are actually ready to talk — at a 48% response rate, 3x the industry average.

04

AI Voice takes over

As the conversation heats up, Voice calls as your assistant, qualifies on timeline and budget, and arranges a warm transfer — persisting for up to 90 days per lead.

05

You take the baton and close

A ready-to-talk buyer rings your phone or lands on your calendar. That's the only step that's yours — answer the call and close the deal.

Straight answers

Ylopo FAQ: the questions everyone asks

What is Ylopo?

Ylopo is an AI-driven digital marketing platform for real estate agents and teams. It generates buyer and seller leads with social ads, Google PPC and live-transfer campaigns, builds you a branded IDX home-search website, and runs a team of AI agents (AI Text and AI Voice) that engage and qualify every lead for up to 90 days, then warm-transfer ready-to-talk buyers and sellers to you. It works alongside your existing CRM, and your leads are 100% yours.

How much does Ylopo cost?

Ylopo doesn't publish one flat price. There are three plans (Solo, Growing Team and Complete), and your monthly price depends on your market, the lead types you want, your agent seats, and the size of your database. You can get an ROI estimate in about 60 seconds or your exact price on a free, no-obligation 45-minute demo. See the full breakdown on the Ylopo pricing page.

How do I log in to Ylopo?

Current Ylopo clients sign in to their account at stars.ylopo.com. For help with your account, training or the platform, visit the Ylopo Support Center or email info@ylopo.com.

Is Ylopo legit? Where can I read Ylopo reviews?

Yes. Ylopo (YLOPO, LLC) was founded in 2015 by the team that built TigerLead. Ylopo has 2,200+ client businesses, mostly teams, which together represent 75,000+ real estate professionals. You can watch verified client stories on this page and read Ylopo reviews on our reviews page.

How does Ylopo compare to alternatives like BoldTrail (kvCORE), CINC, Lofty or Real Geeks?

Ylopo replaces the bolted-together stack of point tools with one orchestrated AI team: lead acquisition, AI Text, AI Voice, re-engagement and dynamic listing alerts working together and feeding your existing CRM. We built the platform ourselves rather than white-labeling it. See our side-by-side comparisons for CINC, Lofty, BoldTrail (kvCORE), Real Geeks, Luxury Presence, RealScout and Fello.

Is Ylopo hiring? How do I apply for Ylopo careers?

Yes. Current openings at Ylopo across engineering, sales, customer success, marketing and more are listed on our Jobs page, and you can apply directly from each posting.

Are Ylopo leads exclusive?

Yes. Every lead your campaigns generate is 100% yours, never sold, shared or recycled to another agent. Your brand is on every ad, every text and every listing alert.

I already have a CRM. Do I have to switch?

No. Keep your CRM. Ylopo's AI agents work alongside the CRM you already use and replace the stack of point tools bolted around it.

I'm a solo agent. Is Ylopo overkill?

No. Solo agents are some of Ylopo's strongest results. Johnny Apple spent $9K in his first 90 days as a new agent and returned $21K. The AI does the follow-up a team of ISAs would, which matters most when the team is just you.

What happens in the first 30, 60 and 90 days?

Campaigns launch and your site goes live in the first weeks. The AI starts working new leads immediately and can re-engage your existing database from day one. Leads typically start flowing within the first month, with appointment volume building as the AI's 90-day follow-up cycles compound.

Do I keep my leads if I cancel?

Yes. Your leads are yours, full stop. They live in your CRM and stay with you.

What does the name Ylopo mean?

Ylopo is the last five letters of the word Monopoly spelled backwards, a nod to handing the consumer relationship back to the agent instead of the portals.
