Your Leads Aren’t the Problem. The Gaps Between Your Teams Are.

Most home improvement companies don’t lose jobs because of bad leads. They lose them in the handoffs between marketing, the call center, sales reps, and production.

When teams aren’t connected, opportunities slip through the cracks, follow-ups get missed, and revenue is left on the table.

LeadPerfection connects the dots, so every team works from the same playbook, every opportunity gets the attention it deserves, and more leads turn into jobs.

Leads leak between sources.

Web forms, home shows, canvassing and call-ins land in different places. Nobody sees the whole pipeline, and good leads sit waiting for a call.

The wrong rep walks into the home.

Appointments go to whoever is free, even when another rep closes that product far more often.

Marketing dollars chase the wrong sources.

Spend gets judged on lead counts instead of jobs sold, so the budget keeps flowing to sources that never close.

Here’s what changes when everything runs through one system

Picture Monday morning. Every weekend lead is already in one queue. Setters are calling the best ones first. Each appointment goes to the rep most likely to close it. And you can see exactly which marketing paid for itself.
