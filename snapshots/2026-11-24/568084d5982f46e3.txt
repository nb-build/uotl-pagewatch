CleanTelligent Rebrands as Otuvy: Celebrating 20+ Years with a New Name and a New Product

Honoring 20+ Years of CleanTelligent as Otuvy Looks Ahead​

For more than two decades, CleanTelligent Software is a trusted name in the cleaning industry, known for its quality and janitorial management software.

In 2023, the company took its next step and rebranded as Otuvy, marking a strategic shift toward an even more innovative and efficient approach to serving on-site and field service personnel.

​

That move was never about leaving the past behind. CleanTelligent built the foundation Otuvy stands on today: 20+ years of customer relationships, hard-won industry knowledge, and a reputation for doing the work right. Otuvy carries that legacy forward with the same core values, dedication to quality, and relentless pursuit of excellence that defined CleanTelligent from the start.

​

The decision to rebrand grew out of the company’s long-standing commitment to adapting to its customers’ evolving needs and embracing a changing industry. The name Otuvy, which stands for “Outcomes to Verify,” captures that vision. It opened the door to expanding into new industries across the services sector while continuing to grow alongside the customers who made the first 20 years possible.

​

Alongside the rebrand, Otuvy launched Otuvy Frontline, a platform built to transform the way service professionals manage their work. Caden Hutchens, CEO of Otuvy, explained that the app was developed with a deep understanding of the challenges faced by on-site and field service personnel who are on the frontlines.

​

“Otuvy Frontline aims to empower employees by promoting accountability, clarity, and recognition for a job well done. By placing the power of work management in the hands of every single employee, not just management, the app minimizes the need for constant follow-ups and repetitive instructions, ultimately enhancing efficiency.”

​

Otuvy QM, the platform formerly known as CleanTelligent Software, continues to serve building service contract professionals. Otuvy Frontline extends that reach to service professionals across industries on the frontline and in the field, including cleaning, maintenance, construction, landscaping, safety and security, and more. The app is customizable to each team’s needs, and its real-time reporting gives managers clear insight into team performance so they can make data-driven decisions and optimize their operations.

​

Through the transition, customers kept uninterrupted access to their accounts and data, supported by the same team they had come to rely on.

​

Twenty-plus years in, we haven’t forgotten where we came from. The CleanTelligent story is part of every Otuvy product, every customer relationship, and every decision we make. We’re proud of what the last two decades have held, and we’re excited to see what the next 20 years have in store.
