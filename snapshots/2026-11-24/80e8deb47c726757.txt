Built for Water Treatment

Your business runs on recurring service. Your software should too.

Water treatment is different from most trades. Filter changes, salt deliveries, annual testing, equipment warranties — WorkBoss handles the complexity without adding any.

Recurring Service Plans

Track every customer’s service schedule — quarterly testing, annual filter swaps, monthly salt refills. Never miss a visit and never lose a recurring customer.

Customer & Equipment Profiles

Track what’s installed at every property — softener model, RO system, UV unit, install date, warranty status. When a customer calls, you have the full picture instantly.

Pipeline & Lead Tracking

Track new system installs, water testing leads, and service upgrades through your pipeline. See the dollar value of every opportunity and close more deals.

Estimates & Invoicing

Quote the install while you’re still testing the water.

Run the test, diagnose the issue, and build a professional estimate right on your tablet — softener, RO system, iron filter, installation labor, all itemized. Send it before you leave.

Customers approve online and the job hits your schedule automatically. No follow-up calls, no “let me think about it” that turns into a lost sale.
