Frequently asked questions

When does the Black Friday sale start and end?

The Gravity Forms and Gravity Flow deals run from Monday, November 23 through Tuesday, December 1, 2026, ending at midnight (ET). Certified Add-On deal dates may vary, so check each developer’s site for their exact timing.

What deals are included?

During the Gravity Black Friday sale, you can get 40% off both new Gravity Forms Elite and Gravity Flow Ultimate licenses. You’ll find Black Friday deals from Certified Developers, including CosmicGiant, GravityKit, Gravity PDF, JetSloth, and Gravity Wiz.

Can I use the discounts on existing licenses or renewals?

The Gravity Forms and Gravity Flow discounts apply only to new licenses. For Certified Add-Ons, terms vary by developer, so check the details on the relevant sites.

Can I use the discounts on existing licenses or renewals?

Yes! You can use our no-questions-asked 30-day money-back guarantee if you’re not satisfied with your purchase. For Certified Add-Ons, check the details on the relevant sites.

What happens when my license renews?

Gravity Forms and Gravity Flow licenses renew automatically at the regular price at the end of the 12-month license term. Certified Add-On renewal terms are set by each developer.
