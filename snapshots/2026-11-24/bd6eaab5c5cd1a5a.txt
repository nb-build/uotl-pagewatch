Contractors can reap many benefits from using home improvement CRM software. It supports sales and building customer loyalty in a central spot. Lots of contractors use Builder Prime, but it may not meet all your needs. Builder Prime alternatives could be a better fit.

Why Look for a Builder Prime Alternative?

You should look for a Builder Prime alternative if the CRM software lacks key features you need to run your business.

As a remodeling sales software, Builder Prime has many features you need in a CRM. However, gaps do exist. Some of the most common complaints include:

Greater learning curve: It’s a complex system, requiring a structured implementation and specialized training.

Customization limits: Users complain that there are restrictions in building certain workflows.

Focus on pipeline: If organizing leads and tracking them isn’t a primary function you want, you may find its features don’t align with your needs.

Pricing: There are different tiers, but the company isn’t transparent about fees.

If any of these sound familiar, it may be time to look at Builder Prime competitors.

Ease of use: This defines how easy it is to get up and running and what type of training may be necessary for adoption.

Features: This looks at all the core capabilities that a contractor CRM should include.

Customer support: This evaluates consistency and responsiveness to customer issues.

Value for money: This assesses the value you get for the cost, not just what’s the cheapest plan.

The Best Builder Prime Alternatives

What are the best CRMs for remodeling contractors that aren’t Builder Prime? We’ll review the top six on the criteria listed above as well as give you an overview of pros and cons for each.

improveit 360

improveit 360 delivers a CRM that’s best for established remodelers seeking deep, Salesforce-powered customization and enterprise-wide reporting.

Looking at reviews on G2 and Capterra reveals insights on each category:

Ease of use: Users report a clean interface and a streamlined CRM.

Features: improveit 360 has many CRM features, including lead capture, lead routing, customizable workflows, task automation, job templates, job tracking, and data cleansing.

Customer support: improveit 360 offers phone support with reps 24/7 as well as training.

Value for money: Pricing varies by services and is a subscription model. The more you add, the higher the monthly fee, which some users pointed to as unsatisfactory.

Builder Prime vs. improveit 360: Builder Prime is a native CRM, while improveit 360 is built directly on the Salesforce platform. There are differences in estimating and workflows. Additionally, Builder Prime is a better fit for small companies, and improveit 360 is an enterprise solution.

Bottom line: improveit 360 is an excellent enterprise CRM for high-volume contractors, with its standardized workflows and automation capabilities.

MarketSharp

MarketSharp is a contractor CRM that excels at lead generation management and high-volume appointment setting.

The product has reviews and information on G2 and Capterra, used for the analysis below:

Ease of use: Most reviewers cite the CRM as user-friendly. Some did note that some areas were complicated.

Features: MarketSharp CRM features include lead generation tracking, lead-to-sales conversions, and customizable workflows and reporting. Users noted that the workflow creation was helpful.

Customer support: Reviews are mixed on support, with some satisfied and others stating it was poor.

Value for money: Pricing starts at $182 per month for essentials with an additional per-user fee. For all features, the cost rises. Reviewers stated the company has competitive pricing and is affordable.

Builder Prime vs. MarketSharp: Builder Prime is an all-in-one CRM, and MarketSharp focuses more on the lead and sales side. MarketSharp simplifies lead labeling, while Builder Prime has more granularity. Builder Prime has real-time KPI (key performance indicator) tracking. MarketSharp’s reporting centers around lead generation and marketing.

Bottom line: For a connected ecosystem of sales, marketing, and project management, MarketSharp is a good option.

Leap

Leap stands out as a CRM for home improvement companies that have door-to-door sales teams, as it provides digital contracts, proposals, and financing.

Capterra and G2 reviews data highlight its performance in each category below:

Ease of use: Most users reported the CRM is easy to use and that it helps minimize manual tasks. Other reviews did suggest that training to use the platform is necessary.

Features: Leap CRM features include customizable workflows, subcontractor and customer portals, reporting, integrations with other tools, and automation. Users expressed that some features take a while to set up and optimize.

Customer support: Leap does offer support to users. Reviews for support had both positive and negative sentiment. One recurring theme was that updates to the software often caused downtime.

Value for money: Pricing starts at $79 per month for one user. Team subscriptions have a base price of $298 per month plus $99 per user. It can get expensive quickly with this model.

Builder Prime vs. Leap: Leap’s features align most with the digital sales process. Builder Prime is a more general CRM. Integrations are easier with Builder Prime, as you’ll need add-on modules to do that with Leap.

Bottom line: Leap offers workflows that account for the entire life cycle of a job within its CRM. Digital documents are a clear advantage with Leap.

JobNimbus

JobNimbus has a CRM designed for roofing and exterior crews, supported by its mobile, in-the-field app.

To evaluate JobNimbus, reviews on G2 and Capterra offer insights:

Ease of use: Reviews on both sites show high satisfaction. Reviewers called it easy, reliable, and efficient. Many said the learning curve was manageable.

Features: JobNimbus CRM features include lead tracking, scheduling, proposals, custom workflows, sales boards, insights, and automation.

Customer support: Many users explained that the onboarding and training were helpful. Support is available by phone and chat. There’s also a knowledge base for users.

Value for the money: Pricing is not available on the company website. There are four tiers available with costs varying based on the number of users and features. The Capterra rating for value for money is 4.6 out of 5.

Builder Prime vs. JobNimbus: The major difference between Builder Prime and JobNimbus is that the latter caters to roofing. Builder Prime doesn’t have specific industry features. JobNimbus is great at quoting and tracking but doesn’t have the in-depth functionality of Builder Prime.

Bottom line: JobNimbus is ideal for roofing companies but has many capabilities that apply to all home improvement companies.

Buildertrend

Buildertrend provides a CRM that works best for large remodeling or home building businesses. It combines project management with CRM functionality.

Evaluations below are based on reviews from G2 and Capterra:

Ease of use: Users reported that there are usability issues with the platform. Overall, the user experience has positive sentiment, but many reviewers noted they don’t use all features.

Features: Buildertrend CRM features include creating, capturing, and tracking leads, proposals, email marketing, document organization, and communication tools.

Customer support: The company provides training, phone support, and a knowledge base. There are mixed opinions about the quality of support.

Value for the money: Pricing is not available on the company website. You can only inquire to get a quote. Reviewers expressed that it is expensive, and G2 calculated that it takes at least 14 months for a return on investment.

Builder Prime vs. Buildertrend: Buildertrend excels at project management with a CRM, while Builder Prime has CRM features only. Buildertrend’s CRM capabilities are not as robust as Builder Prime’s, so it works best for large-scale construction projects.

Bottom line: If your home improvement company needs CRM and project management capabilities, Buildertrend is a good option.

AccuLynx

AccuLynx has a CRM that caters to restoration and insurance-related repairs for roofing. The platform supports your sales pipeline.

Reviews from G2 and Capterra offer these opinions on the platform:

Ease of use: Reviewers praise the platform for its centralized workflow organization. Some themes in these reviews are that it’s easy to set up and simple to use.

Features: AccuLynx CRM features include contact records, lead management, lead intelligence, photo and document repository, estimates, and contracts. Users highlighted that it’s great at project tracking and keeping information organized.

Customer support: Reviewers stated that the company has very responsive customer service and it’s knowledgeable and consistent. Support is available via phone or chat, and there’s also a knowledge base.

Value for the money: AccuLynx does not publish its pricing. The perceived cost, based on reviews, is that it’s one of the most expensive CRMs. The rating on Capterra is a 4.2 out of 5 for value.

Builder Prime vs. AccuLynx: AccuLynx is a specialty CRM geared toward roofing contractors. Builder Prime can work for any home improvement category. AccuLynx also has features that make it more attractive to insurance companies. Overall, Builder Prime offers more functionality around lead management than AccuLynx.

Bottom line: AccuLynx is a roofing CRM with features aligned for that industry. It does have full pipeline workflow management, which is ideal for high-volume contractors who want to automate and streamline.

How to Choose the Right CRM for Your Business

Now that you have the details on these home improvement CRM software solutions, you can begin your evaluation. Remember these tips when choosing the best one for your company:

Define your core business goals in respect to CRM features. For example, are you primarily trying to manage leads better, track jobs throughout the life cycle, or enhance your marketing efforts?

Decide the number of users. Who will need access to the CRM?

Set a budget. Calculate the total cost of the system and what you can afford.

List out the primary features that will be most beneficial to your company (e.g., lead tracking, quoting, mobile access, scheduling, automated workflows).

Determine any integrations you require to connect to other platforms. Not all CRMs easily integrate with other solutions.

Check out the support options. Look at the training and onboarding provided along with continuing support.

Review scalability. As your business grows, make sure your CRM can keep up.

Answering these questions offers you a baseline for decision-making. It can also assist with comparing Builder Prime vs. the alternatives reviewed above.

Builder Prime Alternatives FAQs

Why do contractors switch from Builder Prime?

Contractors typically make a switch because of customization limitations, lack of jobsite execution tools, and constraints on reporting. These frustrations and pain points drive companies to seek other options that best fit their CRM needs and business goals.

What are the top-rated alternatives to Builder Prime?

Some of the top-rated alternatives to Builder Prime include improveit 360, MarketSharp, Leap, JobNimbus, Buildertrend, and AccuLynx. Each of these platforms scores well on usability, features, customer support, and value for the money. Comparing each of these categories helps you make the best decision for your business.

What’s a good monthly price for Builder Prime alternatives?

A good monthly price for Builder Prime alternatives depends on the value versus just the cost. You should compare what you get for the price to determine this. Remember to include subscription fees and add-on costs to determine a total cost of ownership.

What features are most critical to select the best CRM for remodeling contractors?

The most critical features of a CRM for remodeling contractors include project estimating, lead management, job scheduling, automated email campaigns, workflow customization, analytics and reporting, and digital contracts.
