Manage Inventory Across Every Rooftop from One Dashboard

August 12, 2026

Stop managing inventory one rooftop at a time. Configure customizable account groups to monitor inventory and appraisals across every dealership from a single dashboard. Instantly locate vehicles throughout your organization, improve collaboration between stores, and help customers find exactly what they're looking for—without relying on phone calls, spreadsheets, or manual searches.

What's New

New customizable account groups provide a single dashboard view of inventory and appraisals across the entire dealer group.

Dealers can instantly locate vehicles across all rooftops from one connected inventory view.

Shared inventory visibility makes it easier for stores within the group to collaborate.

Inventory management is centralized, reducing reliance on phone calls, spreadsheets, and manual searches.

How This Helps Car Dealers

Find vehicles faster across the dealer network to better meet customer needs and save more deals.

Improve efficiency with one centralized view of inventory and appraisals across all locations.

Increase collaboration between dealerships through shared inventory access.

Maximize inventory utilization by selling vehicles from the entire dealer group, not just a single store.

Key Takeaways

Dealerships can now manage inventory and appraisals across multiple locations from a single dashboard within CDK Vehicle Inventory Suite, making it easier to locate vehicles, improve collaboration, and maximize inventory opportunities across the dealer group.

Learn More About CDK Solutions

This update is part of CDK Vehicle Inventory Suite, which helps dealers acquire, manage, and sell inventory more effectively with connected tools that improve visibility and decision-making.
