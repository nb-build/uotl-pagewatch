Ricochet360 – AI – New UI Release

AUGUST 2026 • LIVE LAUNCH

A Whole New Ricochet360 Experience

Redesigned Interface | Three Built-In AI Teammates

Ricochet360 just got its biggest upgrade ever! Completely redesigned for speed and clarity, featuring three built-in AI teammates (Rico Guru, Page Assistant, and Call Guru) engineered to accelerate lead management and boost agent performance for Allstate.

PHASE 01

New Interface

Speed, Clarity & Workflows

Streamlined Lead Management

Customizable column views with hideable filters. Trigger calls,
view history, and edit status directly from row lists.

Overlay Lead Cards

Click any lead to launch an instant overlay without navigating
away. Access complete tabbed history:

Record Tabs: Notes, Tasks, Statuses, Calls, Emails, Contacts, Docs, Timeline.

Quick SMS Bar: One-click templated texting from bottom control bar.

Multi-Lead Docking & Dark Mode

Dock active leads at screen bottom to multitask without losing
data. Full Dark Mode toggle from profile menu.

Searchable Settings

Instant search for company settings with match counters.

PHASE 02

Rico Guru

Built-In AI Assistant

Always On Call

Positioned on the right edge of every page—accessible via voice or
plain-English text.

Live Data Reporting

Ask in natural language (e.g., "Show new leads last 90 days").
Rico generates real-time charts directly from live data without
custom report builders.

Smart Diagnostics

Troubleshoots why leads aren't appearing on dialers by analyzing
campaign filters, recycling rules, and DNC statuses.

In-Chat Technical Tickets

Pre-fills and submits support tickets directly within the chat
window with screenshots attached.

PHASE 03

Page Assistant

Context-Aware AI Guide

Screen-Specific Expert

Click the orange side tab on configuration pages for step-by-step
guidance tailored to the active screen.

Conversational Upload Co-Pilot

Uploads leads through an interactive conversation:

Identifies vendor catalogs & campaign links

Configures dialing queue & lead tags

Applies optional data enrichment

Populates complex import forms automatically as you answer.

PHASE 04

Call Guru

Post-Call AI Coaching

Automated Call Scoring

Scores every recorded call automatically in the Call History Report,
evaluating agents on a standardized 100-point scale.

5 Performance Categories

Breaks down scores into Call Structure, Rapport, Product Knowledge,
Sales Effectiveness, and Professionalism.

Actionable AI Coaching

Highlights strengths, growth areas, and recommended next steps,
plus customized role-play scenarios for agent training.
