The Structure Studios Story

What does it take to develop the top 3D design software in the industry?

Our answer: A phenomenal team. A love of cutting-edge technology. And an unswerving dedication to excellence.

Ever since we designed the first 3D swimming pool in 1999, our goal has been to improve lives through 3D experiences. We innovate the most groundbreaking ways to use the latest technology to meet the unique needs of the outdoor living industry.

Making Lives Better Through 3D Experiences.

That commitment has earned us recognition as the industry standard. Now, our award-winning software is relied on by 96% of top designers and used to create more than 2,000,000 projects each year.
