Frequently asked questions

How do I start accepting payments?

Sign up for QuickBooks and get approved as a merchant to streamline accepting payments to your bank accounts.

Install the QuickBooks mobile app or sign up for a QuickBooks Online plan from your browser. Create an account, and complete a payments application. If approved, you can start accepting payments through QuickBooks Online. You’ll also have the option to apply for a business banking account.

How much does QuickBooks Payments cost?

QuickBooks Payments uses pay-as-you-go pricing with no separate monthly fee or processing minimums. You pay only per transaction and processing fees vary based on how your customer pays. Businesses processing more than $2,500 per month may qualify for discounted rates of up to 25% off standard transaction costs.

What payment methods can I accept?

You can accept credit cards, debit cards, ACH bank transfers, Apple Pay, PayPal, and Venmo. Customers can pay directly through an online invoice, payment link, in person, or over the phone.

How long does it take to get paid and have money deposited into my bank account?

Deposit timing depends on your plan and payment type, though many payments arrive within 1-2 business days. You can also choose instant deposit on eligible plans to access qualifying funds the same day.

How can I get paid faster and manage my cash flow?

Create invoices your customers can pay online instantly with a card, ACH transfer, digital wallet, or Affirm’s buy now, pay later option. Track when invoices are viewed and paid in real time, and let automatic reminders follow up with late payers for you. You can even get faster access to your funds with instant deposit or offer the option to get paid on the spot through the QuickBooks mobile app.

Do I need a card reader to accept in-person payments, or can I use my phone?

iPhone users can accept contactless payments directly on their device with Tap to Pay—no extra hardware required. Android users need a QuickBooks card reader to take in-person payments by tap, dip, or swipe.

Do I need a paid QuickBooks Online plan to use QuickBooks Payments?

No, you don't need a paid plan to get started. You can accept payments on the QuickBooks Free plan, which includes up to two invoices per month, or unlimited invoices if you qualify for and enable QuickBooks Payments

Is QuickBooks Payments secure, and how does chargeback protection work?

Yes, QuickBooks Payments is secure, and money movement services are provided by Intuit Payments Inc., a licensed Money Transmitter. With chargeback protection, we cover eligible credit and debit card disputes up to $10,000 per dispute and $25,000 per year, giving you peace of mind against unexpected disputes.

Can I set up recurring or automatic payments for repeat customers?

Yes, you can automatically charge repeat customers on a set schedule using saved payment details. You can also give customers the option to enable automatic payments themselves. This is ideal for subscriptions, retainers, or memberships, helping you make cash flow more predictable.

Are there monthly fees or long-term contracts, and can I cancel anytime?

QuickBooks Payments has no separate monthly fees, no minimums, and no long-term contracts. You only pay processing fees when you accept a payment, and you can cancel anytime.
