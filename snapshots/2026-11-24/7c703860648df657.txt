Best non-medical home care software in 2026.

Non-medical home care — personal care and companion care — is its own category, and the platforms that win here are different from the clinical heavyweights. For most agencies it’s AxisCare. If you’re already in the WellSky ecosystem, it’s WellSky Personal Care (the former ClearCare). If you’re tech-forward and growing toward multi-service, it’s AlayaCare. Here’s how they compare.

By the HeyHomeCare team. HeyHomeCare connects to AxisCare through its API today; our AlayaCare connection is built and switched on once AlayaCare’s partner agreement is in place. We don’t resell either.

Quick verdict

For most non-medical agencies, AxisCare — focused, fast, and affordable. For agencies already on WellSky, WellSky Personal Care. For tech-forward operators planning multi-service growth, AlayaCare.

01 / Methodology

How we ranked.

We focused on platforms purpose-built for, or strong at, non-medical home care — personal care and companion care — for private-pay and Medicaid-funded non-medical agencies. Rankings draw on our work connecting HeyHomeCare to AxisCare and building against AlayaCare’s published API, the platforms’ public install-base and pricing disclosures, and discovery calls with agency owners and operations leads. No platform paid for placement.

We weighted three things non-medical operators care about most: simplicity of daily use, total cost at mid-market size, and how cleanly the platform scales if the agency grows.

AxisCare

Founded 2011·Waco, TX·~5,000+ agencies

AxisCare is the default non-medical pick for a reason: it does personal and companion care well and doesn’t make you pay for clinical modules you’ll never use. Scheduling, caregiver matching, shift swaps, EVV, and a clean mobile app are all tuned for the non-medical workflow, and implementation runs 2–6 weeks. For agencies from a handful of caregivers up to 250, it’s the safe choice.

05 / Free tools

What about free or generic tools?

New agency owners often ask whether they can run on a generic scheduling app or spreadsheets. You can — briefly. But home care has compliance, billing, EVV (for Medicaid-funded visits), and caregiver-workflow requirements that generic tools don’t meet, and migrating off them later is painful. QuickBooks handles your accounting, but it isn’t home care software.

Best non-medical home care software in 2026: AxisCare for most, WellSky Personal Care for the WellSky ecosystem, AlayaCare for tech-forward growth.

Q.02

Do I need EVV for non-medical home care?

Only for Medicaid-funded non-medical visits, where EVV is required under the 21st Century Cures Act. For 100% private-pay non-medical, EVV is optional — but most agencies use it anyway for caregiver accountability and proof of service.

+

Q.03

What’s the cheapest non-medical home care software?

AxisCare typically, on a per-active-caregiver model that lands around $300–600/month for a 50-caregiver agency. Some very small agencies start on generic scheduling tools, but those lack the compliance, billing, and integration capabilities needed as you scale.

+

Q.04

Should small agencies use enterprise software?

No. Small and mid-market non-medical agencies should start on a focused platform like AxisCare, not an enterprise post-acute suite. The cost and complexity of enterprise software is mismatched to a non-medical-only operation.
