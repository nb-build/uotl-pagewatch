JobNimbus, the #1 AI-enabled CRM for roofing and exterior contractors, launches Analytics, built-in Financing, and JobNimbus Enterprise as part of its fall product Rollout.

Analytics and Financing are now live in JobNimbus accounts, helping contractors make faster, more informed business decisions and win more work. JobNimbus Enterprise brings those capabilities together with account hierarchy, financial system sync, and dedicated implementation support for multi-location operators running multiple crews across locations and trades.

Analytics: Reporting you can act on

The new Analytics tool includes prebuilt dashboards for sales, production, and workflow that show which reps are closing and at what margin, where jobs are stalling, and which crews are finishing on time. Every metric traces back to the jobs and records underneath, so an owner can go from a trend to the jobs causing it in a couple of clicks.

Analytics AI turns that into a conversation. Contractors can ask a question the way they would ask their operations manager, such as why revenue dipped in a given market, and get an answer in seconds with the data behind it. Follow-ups work the same way, so an owner can keep pulling the thread until they find the cause. For multi-location operators, Analytics Enterprise extends the same foundation with raw data access and custom dashboards built by a JobNimbus analyst.

"Every owner has a list of things they wish they knew about their business, and the hard part is knowing where to look. Analytics AI gets you there, and it shows you the data behind every answer. When you're scaling across markets, Analytics Enterprise goes further with raw data and custom dashboards." —Tyler Folkman, Chief AI Officer at JobNimbus

For sales teams, that means fewer deals lost at the price conversation and a stronger case for the full scope of work. For the office, it means one record for the roof and the money attached to it.*

JobNimbus Enterprise also connects to the accounting systems enterprise teams already rely on, starting with QuickBooks, with NetSuite and Sage integrations coming soon. A dedicated implementation specialist supports onboarding, from data migration and workflow configuration to on-site training.

JobNimbus Launches In-Estimate Homeowner Financing for Roofing Contractors

JobNimbus Financing brings the full loan lifecycle onto the job, from the first payment option on the estimate to the final payment when the work is done.

JobNimbus, the #1 AI-enabled CRM for roofing and exterior contractors, announces the general availability of JobNimbus Financing. Contractors can now present financing options directly on an estimate, see application decisions, track the loan against the job it belongs to, and get paid once the work is complete, all inside the system they already run their business on.

Price is among the most common reasons a good estimate doesn't close. For many contractors, offering financing means moving through several steps across several systems. Homeowners experience that gap as a clunky buying process, and reps experience it as a stall in the middle of a sales conversation.

Financing, built into the job itself

JobNimbus Financing removes that friction by keeping the entire process in one place. Field reps add monthly payment options directly on the estimate, so homeowners see every way to pay at the same time and can apply right there. Application decisions and loan status live on the job in JobNimbus, so the rep who sold the work and the admin who supports it are looking at the same record. Contractors use progress payments as work moves forward and collect the final payment once the job is complete, all without chasing checks or logging into a separate portal.

For sales teams, that means fewer deals lost at the price conversation and a stronger case for the full scope of work. For the office, it means one record for the roof and the money attached to it.

Getting started takes a one-time business application through the Wisetack integration. Once approved, financing is available on every estimate.

Consistent financing across every location

For contractors running multiple locations, financing tends to look different everywhere: one location offers it on every estimate, another only when a homeowner asks, and leadership has no clear view of either. JobNimbus Financing gives every branch the same process on the same platform, so owners can standardize how financing is offered, tracked, and paid out across the entire business.

Because loan status lives on the job, owners and operations leaders can see financing activity alongside the rest of the business, by branch and by rep.*

Now it’s all right there in JobNimbus, so our reps stay connected to the homeowner. Financing becomes part of the conversation instead of ‘hold on, let me get logged in.’” —Kurtis O'Connell, Sales Manager at Joyland Roofing.

JobNimbus and RepCard Now Sync Both Ways, Ending Double Entry

Contacts, appointments, and job statuses now move between both systems, so reps and the office are looking at the same job without retyping anything.

JobNimbus, the #1 AI-enabled CRM for roofing and exterior contractors, today announced a two-way integration with RepCard, the Sales Operating System for field sales teams. The sync is available now to shared customers, and setup takes minutes.

When a rep creates a customer or books an appointment in RepCard, it lands in JobNimbus as a contact or a job, in the stage the contractor mapped it to. When that record changes in JobNimbus, the update flows back into RepCard so the rep who sold it can see where it stands.

Removing duplicate data entry

Running sales and production in separate systems means entering the same customer twice and hoping the two records match. Every retype is a chance to lose a phone number or misspell a street. And the rep who sold the job has no visibility into what happened after the handoff. Reps also lose visibility once a job is handed off to production, leaving them to call the office for an inspection or scheduling update, or to give the homeowner their best guess.

The integration closes both gaps. Reps capture the customer once, at the door, and JobNimbus has it before they reach the next house. When the job moves stages in JobNimbus, that status appears in RepCard without anyone having to ask.

How it works

The rep captures it once: A rep creates a customer or books an appointment in RepCard. Contractors choose which action triggers the sync.

It lands in the right stage: Contractors pick one JobNimbus workflow and match their RepCard statuses to their JobNimbus job statuses, so records show up where they belong instead of piling into a single bucket.

Updates come back on their own: A JobNimbus automation fires whenever a contact or job is created or modified. That update flows into RepCard, so reps and managers see current job status without leaving the app.

Setup takes minutes: Once the connection is mapped, the sync runs on its own. Contractors don't manage imports, exports, or scheduled file transfers.

"Our customers are already juggling a lot between the field and the office. This integration means a rep's work doesn't disappear into a black box the second the job gets handed off. It shows up in JobNimbus in the right stage automatically, and status updates flow right back to RepCard. That's less duplicate data entry, fewer 'what's going on with this job' calls, and reps who can tell a homeowner what's next without picking up the phone." —Wade Brown, VP of Business Development, JobNimbus

"Reps hate double entry. If someone has to type the same customer into two systems, they'll either do it wrong or they won't do it at all, and then nobody trusts the numbers. Now a rep knocks the door, sets the appointment on their phone, and it's a job in JobNimbus before they get to the next house. When the crew updates that job, the rep sees it. No calling the office to find out if the deal is still alive." —Brad Mortensen, Founder and CEO, RepCard

The JobNimbus and RepCard integration is live now for shared customers and can be enabled from within RepCard.

JobNimbus and ReferPro Team Up to Turn Happy Customers Into Ready-to-Work Leads, Automatically

New native integration pushes referral leads from ReferPro directly onto the JobNimbus job board, already linked to the contact and ready to work.

JobNimbus, the #1 AI-enabled CRM for roofing and exterior contractors, and ReferPro, the AI-native referral platform for the trades, today announced a direct integration that sends referral and self-service leads from ReferPro into JobNimbus as new jobs, eliminating manual entry, CSV imports, and the need to maintain a separate referral dashboard.

Referrals rank among the highest-converting lead sources for contractors, but they are also among the most frequently lost. ReferPro data indicates that 83% of customers say they would refer a company, while only 29% do. Referral requests are commonly deprioritized during periods of high job volume, and inbound referrals often arrive through text messages, voicemails, or standalone referral tools disconnected from the contractor's primary system of record. The result is duplicate data entry, unworked leads, and limited visibility into which referral relationships generate revenue.

The integration routes referrals directly into the contractor's JobNimbus job board with source and relationship context attached, removing the need for a second system to capture leads the business has already earned.

How it works

When a customer submits or receives a referral through ReferPro, ReferPro creates a corresponding contact and job in JobNimbus, placed in the contractor's designated new lead status.

The job record includes the referring customer, their relationship to the new contact, contact information, and any notes describing the customer's needs.

When the job is marked complete in JobNimbus, ReferPro enrolls the customer in a post-service follow-up campaign inviting them to submit a referral of their own.

Once an invoice is approved, ReferPro attributes the resulting revenue to the originating referral, giving contractors automated ROI tracking across referral relationships.

"83% of customers say they'd refer a company. Only 29% ever do. That's not because contractors don't care, it's because asking is always the thing that gets bumped when the day goes sideways. ReferPro makes sure it never does, and JobNimbus is where these teams already run their business, so the best leads a contractor has finally live where the rest of the work does. We're excited about this partnership and integration providing roofers better leads and time back." — Murphy Nadauld, CEO of ReferPro.

"Referrals are the highest-trust leads our customers get, and they're the easiest ones to lose when there's no system catching them. ReferPro closes that loop. The referral becomes a job on the board the moment it happens, already carrying the context and the attribution, which means the office spends its time working the lead instead of logging it." — Wade Brown, VP of Business Development, JobNimbus

The ReferPro integration is available to JobNimbus customers now.

JobNimbus and WhatConverts Launch Native Integration Connecting Marketing Spend to Job Revenue

From lead to revenue: contractors can now see exactly which marketing is winning them jobs

LEHI, UT — September 3, 2026 — JobNimbus, the #1 AI-enabled CRM for roofing and exterior contractors, today announced a native integration with WhatConverts, a lead attribution and reporting platform. The integration automatically syncs revenue data from JobNimbus into WhatConverts, connecting every job and invoice back to the lead and marketing source that produced it.

For contractors, the workflow already lives in JobNimbus. A lead comes in, the team works it through an estimate, schedules production, and invoices the customer. What has been missing is the line back to marketing.
