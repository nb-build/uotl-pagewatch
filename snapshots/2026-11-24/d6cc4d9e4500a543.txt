Risk Management FAQ

One Quick AR Win This Month.

As the end of the year rolls in, start planning your seasonal awareness website content and email campaigns to stay relevant and add value for your clients and prospects.

Turn the most common questions you’ve been answering about weather preparedness into a blog on your Forge website—have FirstDraft help if you’re stuck on writing!

Here are some common blog topics you could leverage, depending on where you’re located in the United States:

Getting ready for policy updates with end-of-year life changes.

Getting your home winter-ready on the East coast.

Getting holiday travel squared away and knowing your coverage gaps.

FirstDraft can do all the heavy lifting of getting your blog started for you! Check out this Help Center article to learn more about how to leverage Forge’s AI writing tools to help you blog faster.

The blog topics above also make for great emails to send to clients. If you have Forge and Fuse, you can also cross-link that blog in an email to your personal lines clients.

In general, seasonal preparedness emails are some of the best candidates for a Broadcast email send. Check out this Help Center article if you have any questions about setting up this type of message.

As a bonus, consider adding in a simple “Getting Ready for Winter” checklist so readers have a valuable resource to take away from your content. With the busyness of the end of the year, your clients and prospects will appreciate you thinking of them and their safety—and you can leverage Agency Revolution’s tools to accomplish just that.

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to refresh my Fuse email campaign content every year?

It depends on the type of campaigns you’re running, but we recommend giving your anchor campaigns—renewals, account reviews, and welcome kits—a look at least twice a year for relevance and tone. Now would be a great time of year to get ready for 2027!

How many blogs should I post on my Forge website per month?

The sweet spot is between two and four blogs per month, but consistency matters over quantity. SEO and AI search care about a posting schedule and quality content over flooding your site with blogs. Posting about topics specific to your area will also help you win in search. Remember: blog content can be repurposed into newsletters, social posts, and more!

After launching a new Fuse email campaign, how long should I wait to check in on its performance?

Your campaign’s timing will depend on what you’re checking, but the Marketing Analytics dashboard built into Fuse is a great place to drill down on metrics for individual campaigns. If you’re sending a Broadcast message, checking in after 24–48 hours will show you most of your opens and clicks. For ongoing campaigns, it’s best to check the Marketing Analytics dashboard on a monthly basis to benchmark performance.
