How Should You Shortlist Med Spa Scheduling Software?

Shortlist software by the work your front desk repeats daily. Use these practice needs to narrow your options:

AestheticsPro: Forms-led practices that need online booking.

Boulevard: Practices coordinating rooms and devices.

Fresha: Solo practices comparing subscriptions and booking channels.

GlossGenius: Beauty and aesthetics practices that want booking, charting and marketing in one subscription.

Mangomint: Practices coordinating rooms and equipment.

Mindbody: Wellness businesses that offer both classes and appointments.

Portrait: Practices that want scheduling through the membership they use to order supplies.

Vagaro: Solo practices comparing starting subscription costs.

Zenoti: Multi-location groups seeking centralized control.

What Features Should Med Spa Scheduling Software Include?

Check how online booking, rooms, devices, payments and staff permissions work together. Use the same appointment example in every demo. Include a clinician, a room, a device and a cancellation so each vendor answers the same practical questions.

Online booking: Check where clients book and whether they need an account. Ask the vendor to demonstrate booking, rescheduling and cancellation on a phone.

Rooms and devices: Check whether resource scheduling blocks double-booking when appointments need both a clinician and equipment. Ask what happens when staff change the room after booking.

No-show protection: Check deposits, cards on file and cancellation fees against your practice's policy. Ask staff to demonstrate collecting, changing and refunding a charge.

Waitlists: Check how patients join, how openings reach them and how long an offer remains available. Ask what happens when several patients want the same appointment.

Reminders and confirmations: Check text and email timing, two-way confirmation and per-text charges. Ask whether a patient's reply changes the appointment or creates work for staff.

Staff schedules and permissions: Check how staff block clinician time and restrict access to records or payments. Ask who can change availability, move appointments and override booking rules.

Patient privacy: Under HIPAA, the federal health privacy law, a covered entity needs a written business associate agreement (BAA) before sharing protected health information (PHI) with its business associate. Ask every vendor, Portrait included, whether it will sign a BAA before patient records move. See HHS business associate guidance (opens in a new tab) from the federal health department.

Total cost: Check monthly charges per user, per location and per text, plus add-ons, payment processing and marketplace commissions. Request a written quote reflecting your staff, locations and expected messaging.

Scheduling is one part of a wider software decision. Compare all-in-one med spa software for broader practice needs. For follow-up and marketing, compare customer relationship management software: med spa CRMs.

How Does Each Vendor Handle Scheduling?

Boulevard coordinates provider availability, rooms and devices. Fresha connects booking to your website, social media, Google or its marketplace. Mindbody supports classes and appointments. Compare the features below with the appointments your practice needs to book.

AestheticsPro

AestheticsPro lists a client portal with online booking in every edition.

Scheduling: You get multiple resource allocation, text reminders and automatic email confirmations. Staff tools include scheduling and a time clock.

Clinical tools: You get a library of 500+ customizable forms and photo capture with alignment guides and ghosting.

Demo check: Ask how the software handles conflicting resource bookings and how client portal bookings reach the calendar.

Prices checked November 5, 2026 list these AestheticsPro subscription plans:

Pro-Plus: $160/month for 2 user seats. An additional user seat costs $85/month, with up to 3 user seats.

Executive: $285/month with unlimited staff logins and support for multiple locations.

Enterprise: $350/month with unlimited staff logins.

Add-ons: AP Texting and AP Marketing Solutions require additional fees. Request those charges alongside the subscription quote.

Boulevard

Boulevard lists online booking, resource scheduling, a waitlist, deposits, and email and text reminders across its med spa plans.

Booking: Precision Scheduling, its room and device scheduler, coordinates provider availability, rooms and devices. Self-booking stays on your practice's website.

Clinical tools: Each med spa plan lists forms and charting, plus photo markup and sign-off.

Staff controls: Boulevard lists staff scheduling plus roles and permissions.

Prices checked November 5, 2026 use monthly billing for Boulevard's med spa subscription plans:

Aesthetics Premier: Normally $455/month per location.

Aesthetics Prestige: Normally $520/month per location.

Limited-time offer: A fall offer for practices new to Boulevard showed $364/month per location for Aesthetics Premier and $416/month per location for Aesthetics Prestige. Terms apply. Confirm eligibility and expiration before comparing quotes.

Aesthetics Essentials: $199/month for one location and up to 3 professionals.

Messaging: Ask which allowance fits your appointment volume and what additional usage costs.

Fresha

Fresha's booking page works through your website, social media, Google or its marketplace listing.

Scheduling: You can schedule rooms and equipment, match waitlist openings with waiting clients, and use card confirmation.

Payments: Deposits and no-show protection come with Fresha's optional payment system. Booking and calendars work without it.

Client records: You get intake forms and before-and-after photos.

Prices checked November 5, 2026 list these Fresha charges:

Subscriptions: Independent costs $19.95/month for one person with a single calendar column. Team costs $14.95/month per bookable team member.

Notification allowance: Independent includes 100 free automated texts or WhatsApp messages a month. Team includes 100 per team member a month.

Additional notifications: After those allowances, you pay $0.02 per text or $0.04 to $0.11 per WhatsApp message. Automated email notifications are free.

Marketing: Marketing texts cost $0.08 per text.

Marketplace: New clients acquired through Fresha's marketplace incur a one-time commission. Returning clients incur no marketplace fee. Ask how these charges appear alongside your subscription.

GlossGenius

GlossGenius offers online booking without a client login.

Rooms and waitlists: Resource Management, its room and equipment scheduler, reserves both at booking to prevent double-booking. Its waitlist matches waiting clients to newly available appointments.

Payments and reminders: You can collect deposits through the booking site and set card-on-file rules by client type. GlossGenius lists unlimited confirmations and reminders.

Charting: You get photo markup and SOAP notes connected to bookings, payments and marketing.

Prices checked November 5, 2026 list these GlossGenius charges:

Practice Essentials: $168/month, billed monthly. GlossGenius describes it as providing essential EMR (electronic medical record) tools.

Practice Advanced: $298/month, billed monthly. GlossGenius describes it as supporting unlimited providers.

Additional locations: Each additional location is charged the plan price, at a discount.

Reception: The call, text and booking add-on costs an additional $50/month.

Demo check: Ask which plan includes the resource and charting tools demonstrated.

Mangomint

Mangomint offers website booking without requiring clients to create an account.

Rooms and waitlists: Resource scheduling assigns rooms and equipment to services to avoid double-booking. Intelligent Waitlist matches openings with waiting clients.

Payments: Express Booking sends clients a link to provide details, add a card and accept the cancellation policy. Bookings can require partial deposits or full payment. Express Booking, cards on file and deposits need Mangomint's own payment processing.

Records: Mangomint describes itself as an EMR.

Prices checked November 5, 2026 list Mangomint at $120/month per location plus $10/month per user.

Included messages: Unlimited text and email appointment confirmations and reminders are included at no additional cost.

Add-ons: Phone costs $70/month per line. Marketing starts at $30/month for the add-on.

Patient privacy: Mangomint says support enables its HIPAA compliance arrangement with a signed BAA. Ask support to explain that process before moving records.

Mindbody

Mindbody supports booking through desktop, mobile, its app and Reserve with Google, a Google booking service.

Scheduling: You get waitlist notifications and cancellation windows set by service category: classes, appointments and courses. Scheduling also includes late cancellation fees and no-show charges.

Clinical tools: Its med spa page describes client records, treatment plans, SOAP notes and customizable intake forms.

Mindbody's pricing page, checked November 5, 2026, lists from $99/month per location in its plan header.

Two published prices: Mindbody's FAQ page lists $79/month for North America and Asia. Plans are quoted by sales, so confirm the price in a quote.

Plan requirements: Room and resource management require Accelerate or higher. Text-message appointment confirmation also requires Accelerate or higher.

Demo check: Ask the vendor to demonstrate booking for both appointments and classes. Ask which quoted plan includes email and text-message marketing campaigns and the lead management dashboard. Features vary by region and plan. Request written confirmation of the functions your quote includes.

Vagaro

Vagaro includes calendar and resource management, waitlist options, unlimited notifications and appointment reminders with every plan.

Booking channels: You can offer online booking through Vagaro, Instagram, Facebook and Apple Maps.

Calendar controls: You can use processing gaps, optional double bookings and personal time blocks.

Clinical tools: SOAP notes appear in every plan. Vagaro's med spa page also describes charting, before-and-after photos, e-forms and consent.

Prices checked November 5, 2026 list Vagaro's Just Me at $30/month for one user at one location.

Introductory rate: For one location, $23.99/month applies for the first 6 months, with exclusions.

Team size: Pricing rises with team size. Ask for a quote that reflects your full team after the introductory period.

Demo check: Ask the vendor to demonstrate resource conflicts and explain when staff can enable double bookings.

Zenoti

Zenoti automatically checks provider, room and equipment availability when scheduling.

Booking channels: Patients can book through Webstore, its online booking channel, a branded app or SmartBot, its booking chat tool.

Appointment controls: Online booking supports deposits, prepayment, rescheduling and cancellation.

Waitlist: After a cancellation, the waitlist notifies the next eligible patient by email and text message.

Patient privacy: Zenoti says its HIPAA workflows include a BAA.

Pricing checked November 5, 2026: Zenoti does not publish prices and quotes per business.

Locations: Multi-location pricing depends on your footprint and needs.

Communications: Voice, text-message and messaging usage is billed by consumption, with optional base packs. Ask for a quote covering each location and your expected communication volume.

Demo check: Zenoti describes centralized control for multi-location groups. Ask it to demonstrate how individual locations manage availability within those controls.

What Should You Ask in a Scheduling Software Demo?

Ask the vendor to demonstrate booking, cancellations, deposits and data export with your appointment types and staff schedules. Ask each vendor these questions using your cancellation policy.

What is the total monthly cost with add-ons, per-text charges and payment processing?

Will you sign a BAA before we move patient records into your software?

How does booking reserve rooms and devices, and how does it prevent double-booking?

How does the waitlist offer openings, and what happens when a patient does not respond?

How do staff collect and refund deposits and cancellation fees?

Do clients need an account to book online?

How can we export appointment and client data if we leave?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Patients Need an Account to Book a Med Spa Appointment?

No, not with every vendor. GlossGenius and Mangomint let patients book without creating an account. In your demo, follow the booking route on a phone through to cancellation. Check what information patients must enter and ask staff to show where the booking appears on their calendar.

Can I Take Deposits Without Using the Software Vendor’s Payment System?

Not with Fresha or Mangomint. Both tie deposits to their own payment processing. Fresha's calendar and booking functions can work without its payment system. When you compare quotes, include processing charges alongside the subscription and add-ons so your comparison reflects the services your practice needs.

Are Appointment Reminders Included in the Monthly Subscription?

Some subscriptions include reminders, but your bill can still depend on message volume. Compare the allowance with the number of texts your practice expects to send. With Fresha, automated notifications have allowances and charges beyond them. With Zenoti, communication usage is billed according to consumption, with optional base packs.

How Can Med Spa Scheduling Software Reduce No-Shows?

Assess no-show tools through reminders, confirmation replies and payment rules that fit your cancellation policy. Use the feature checklist to test what happens before a missed visit. Ask about deposits, cards on file and cancellation fees, then check whether confirmation replies update the appointment or leave staff another task.

What Happens to My Scheduling Software Bill When My Team Grows?

Your bill depends on how the vendor counts staff and locations. Fresha's Team subscription charges for each bookable team member. Mangomint combines a location charge with a charge per user. Ask for a written quote based on your full staff count, then include messaging and other add-ons in your comparison.
