ISO360 is a complete ERP and CRM for ISOs and agents: digital boarding, portfolio CRM, a to-the-penny residual engine, downline payouts, equipment logistics, and tax compliance in one login. Built by EPI, on the rails your merchants already run on.

The platform

One login runs the whole office.

Most offices duct-tape a CRM, a boarding portal, residual spreadsheets, and a payroll process together. ISO360 replaces the stack: every module reads and writes the same portfolio.

BoardingDigital applications & e-signQuick App takes a merchant from lead to signed application in minutes, with three intake paths, ESIGN-compliant signatures, and in-house underwriting measured in hours.

CRMThe whole book, one recordEvery merchant, lead, call note, document, and data point, organized by division, office, and rep, with visibility that follows your org chart.

ResidualsA real residual enginePer-MID, per-rep attribution that reconciles to the penny, with adjustments, bonuses, and statements you can drill into line by line.

PayoutsDownline payouts by ACHSplits across divisions, offices, and reps computed automatically and settled weekly: residuals and bonuses netted against equipment, every Friday.

OperationsEquipment & logisticsTerminal, POS, and paper orders with automatic shipping labels, deployment tracking, ticketing, and even field-driver dispatch.

ComplianceTax season, handledW-9 collection, IRS TIN matching, and 1099 reporting on every payout, plus PCI tracking and chargeback workflows on every MID.

Boarding & underwriting

From lead to live MID in hours, not weeks.

Quick App is a six-step digital application your reps can run three ways: send the merchant a link, sit with them face to face, or email a pre-filled app for review. Signatures are ESIGN-compliant, bank details validate instantly, and EPI's in-house underwriters pick the file up the moment it lands.

The residual engine

Residuals that reconcile to the penny.

ISO360's residual engine attributes every basis point to the merchant, the rep, and the schedule that earned it. Cygma residuals pay on the 5th, the earliest in the industry, and monthly residuals on the 15th, with drill-down statements that survive an audit.

Per-merchant, per-rep attribution on every MID

Signing, conversion, and milestone bonuses computed automatically

Adjustments and true-ups applied with a paper trail

Statement exports for your accountant, or the ISO360 API

Downline payouts

Pay your whole downline without a spreadsheet.

Divisions, offices, sub-agents, reps: ISO360 models your org the way it actually splits money. Every week it nets residuals and bonuses against equipment debits per agent, then settles the whole downline by ACH on Friday. Clawbacks, holds, and collection states are handled, not hand-tracked.

Division → office → rep hierarchy with per-deal splits

Weekly net settlement: residuals + bonuses − equipment, by ACH

Commissions release when funds actually collect

Every payout visible to the agent who earned it

Operations

The unglamorous stuff, automated.

The work that eats an office manager's week (orders, paper, tickets, chargebacks, taxes) is a module, not a side hustle.

EquipmentOrders that ship themselvesTerminals, POS bundles, and paper orders with live pricing, automatic FedEx labels, and deployment tracking from warehouse to countertop.

ChargebacksA chargeback desk per MIDDisputes land as alerts with the evidence workflow built in. Respond, represent, and track outcomes without leaving the platform.

TicketingTickets & field dispatchSupport tickets tied to the merchant record, plus dispatch for field techs and delivery drivers, with a login-free day view on their phone.

Merchant billingFees, invoices & collectionsMonthly fees, ad-hoc charges, and equipment billing with retry logic and collection states. Collect before you ship, automatically.

RiskRisk that watches with youACH return handling, first-transaction flags, ticket-size anomalies, and hold workflows, surfaced on the portfolio, not buried in email.

Taxes1099s without January panicW-9s collected at onboarding, TINs matched against the IRS in real time, and year-end 1099s generated from the payout ledger you already have.
