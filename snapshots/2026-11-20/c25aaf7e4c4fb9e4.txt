Frequently asked questions

How secure is our data in Skimmer?

Your data is encrypted and securely stored. We follow best-in-class data practices and ensure your business information is safe.

How does billing and payments work at scale?

You can generate hundreds of invoices in just a few clicks. Skimmer supports batch invoicing, recurring billing, AutoPay, and client payment reminders. Payment statuses sync back to your dashboard and accounting software, cutting down on admin time.

Can Skimmer integrate with other systems we use (e.g., CRMs, QuickBooks, payment processors)?

Yes. Owners and admins can add and set up integrations right in Skimmer from Integration Marketplace, including PoolCorp, HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Nutshell, Zoho, Google Calendar, and FieldPay. Skimmer also connects to QuickBooks Online and Heritage Pool Supply, and popular payment platforms like Stripe. For more complex enterprise needs, our public REST API lets enterprise customers connect Skimmer to the rest of their tech stack.

Is there onboarding and support specifically for large teams?

Yes. Larger businesses receive white-glove onboarding, including live training sessions, route import help, and access to dedicated customer success support. We’ll work with your team to set up workflows that fit your current operation—with as little disruption as possible.

What’s the ROI for a company of our size?

Skimmer typically pays for itself quickly by reducing office workload, improving technician accountability, and helping you retain more customers through professional communication and reliable service records. Plus, businesses that use Skimmer often grow faster thanks to streamlined operations and better visibility.

What reporting and oversight tools are available for managers and owners?

Skimmer offers detailed reporting on service completion, chemical usage, billing, and more.

What if some of our team isn’t very tech-savvy?

Skimmer is known for its intuitive interface—techs typically get the hang of it in under an hour. We provide training resources, videos, and help documentation to get your entire team up to speed quickly
