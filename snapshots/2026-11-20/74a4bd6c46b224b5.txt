Aspire To Unveil Command Center at Ignite 2026, Bringing AI-Powered Insights and Action Together for Landscape Contractors

New Intelligence Layer Helps Contractors Identify Operational Challenges, Make Smarter Decisions, and Drive Profitable Growth

October 08, 2026 07:00 ET

| Source:

ServiceTitan

ServiceTitan

ORLANDO, Fla., Oct. 08, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Aspire Software, a ServiceTitan company and leading software provider for landscape businesses, today announced that it will unveil Command Center at Ignite 2026, Aspire's annual user conference, with limited access opening to a limited group of Aspire customers. A new intelligence layer across Aspire, Command Center gives landscape contractors a near real-time view of their operations, helping them identify issues and opportunities sooner, make more informed decisions, improve efficiency, and drive profitable growth.

“Landscape contractors have an incredible amount of data across their businesses, but the real value comes from being able to understand what that data is telling them and use it to make better decisions,” said Vahe Kuzoyan, Co-Founder and President of ServiceTitan. “Command Center is designed to bring intelligence to the forefront, helping contractors understand what’s happening across their operations, identify opportunities sooner, and focus their attention where it can have the greatest impact on their business.”

Rather than relying on static reports, overnight data synchronization, or third-party analytics tools, contractors can access near real-time insights across individual branches or their entire business, helping them identify issues and opportunities and make more informed decisions.

By bringing together Aspire’s foundational business data with Atlas, ServiceTitan’s conversational agent, Command Center is purpose-built for the way landscape businesses operate. Contractors can ask questions about their business, create customized dashboards, and proactively identify opportunities to improve performance, with an experience that becomes more tailored to the needs of their business over time.

Command Center will introduce new AI-powered capabilities designed to:

Turn business data into actionable insights: Give contractors a near real-time view of operational and financial performance across their business, helping them identify issues and opportunities and determine where action may be needed.

Simplify business intelligence with conversational AI: Enable users to ask Atlas questions about their business data in plain English and generate customized charts and dashboards without specialized technical expertise.

Proactively identify operational challenges: Surface issues such as declining crew productivity, overdue invoices, and eroding contract margins through automated alerts delivered via email.

Build customized alerts and dashboards: Allow users to describe the information they want to monitor in plain English, create alerts tailored to their business, and access ready-made dashboards without relying on dedicated analysts or complex reporting tools.

Command Center early access will be limited to 100 Aspire customers, with general availability expected early next year. For more information about Aspire Software, Command Center, and Ignite, follow Ignite's Live Blog here.

Aspire's cloud-based SaaS solution helps field service companies manage, create, and execute against their business opportunities and challenges.
