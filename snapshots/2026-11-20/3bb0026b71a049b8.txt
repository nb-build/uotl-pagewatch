About

PressWise from SmartSoft was designed as an alternative to purchasing independent modules (or custom development) of Web-to-Print Software, Production Workflow, Print MIS, and Mail Preparation Software. Instead, a single platform is available with the comprehensive tools needed to manage your print business:

– Web-to-print Storefronts

– Estimating and Quoting Tools

– Order Management

– Production Automation

– Mailing Software (Optional)

– Shipping and Fulfillment

PressWise is available cloud-hosted by SmartSoft or self-hosted in your own shop (Windows/Mac/Linux).

PressWise is offered on a SaaS business model with NO long term contracts.

Implementation is fast and easy to set up, with online training provided to get you started.

PressWise is accessed via a browser, giving you full visibility to your shop’s workflow, whenever and wherever you need it, even through mobile devices.

PressWise easily integrates with most third-party web storefront products, including OnPrintShop, Pressero, PageDNA and more.

Best of all, because PressWise was designed by a printer, with real employees and customers, it just plain works!
