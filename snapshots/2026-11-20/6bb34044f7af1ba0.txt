Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions about TurboRater

Common questions about TurboRater.

What is TurboRater?

TurboRater is a personal line comparative quoting platform that helps agencies quote multiple carriers in one place.

Who can benefit from TurboRater?

TurboRater is built for brokers and distribution partners selling personal lines of insurance. It’s also used by organizations like credit unions through embedded and API-based quoting experiences.

Which lines of business does TurboRater support?

TurboRater supports a wide range of personal lines, including auto, homeowners, condominium, renters, dwelling fire, and motorcycle policies, with access to accurate, carrier-approved rates.

How does TurboRater help reduce errors?

TurboRater uses single-entry quoting, real-time carrier connections, and built-in underwriting rules. This reduces repetitive data entry and helps ensure quotes meet carrier requirements.

How many carriers can TurboRater quote?

TurboRater connects to 200+ personal line carriers, allowing agencies to compare options without switching websites or systems.

Can TurboRater integrate with my agency management system?

Yes. TurboRater integrates with many leading agency management systems, so your data flows directly into your existing workflows.

Does TurboRater support website or digital quoting?

Yes. You can embed TurboRater into your agency website and digital experiences, allowing customers to shop, compare, and start quotes online while keeping agents involved in the process.
