From an idea to 15+ million leads

Blazeo, was founded in 2008 with the idea of bringing live chat to the new and upcoming smartphones. Ashhad Syed built the first version of the product but soon realized that businesses weren’t quite ready to use smartphones for live chat.

However, in the process, he discovered a largely unserved market of online advertisers who would benefit from live chat for ad conversion and lead capture. Soon thereafter Ashhad partnered with Jim Bert to lead business development and with key technology resources to build a platform that could scale.

In the first few years, the company’s focus was to continue to build the tech platform and service the key initial customers. Initial growth was primarily by word of mouth but by 2013, Blazeo hired its first salesperson, David Scheppler, and continued to branch out from the original chat product.

Today, as an ad conversion platform, Blazeo offers several other ad conversion tools and services and has expanded to 400+ employees spread out over 4 offices.

Blazeo’s mission continues to be to make advertisers and digital agencies more profitable and efficient, and we are excited to bring cutting-edge solutions to serve this mission.

Ashhad Syed

Chief Executive Officer

Ashhad has over two decades of experience as an entrepreneur and has always been interested in how businesses acquire customers and how technology can make the process more efficient. Ashhad bootstrapped Blazeo, formerly known as ApexChat, in 2009, and since then has led the company to serve over 35,000 businesses. Prior to starting Blazeo, Ashhad started two additional startups in the internet lead generation and lead management space. Prior to starting these companies, Ashhad was a principal at a professional services company focused on CRM integration.

Ashhad is a recent transplant to NYC and is always looking for restaurant recommendations to try from NYC’s massive, diverse culinary scene.

Ashhad holds a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering from Cornell University and an MBA from the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania.

Aarij Khan

Chief Product and Marketing Officer

Aarij is the Chief Product and Chief Marketing Officer at Blazeo and heads the Product Management, Engineering, UI Design, and all Marketing functions. Following a few initial years in software engineering, he moved on to SaaS product and go-to-market (GTM) areas. Aarij has held executive roles at several VC funded SaaS startups and has been instrumental in their successful exits, helping them through their high growth stages. Prior to Blazeo, Aarij was CMO at Bromium, Securonix, and RiskIQ, and has served at Tenable Network Security and ArcSight. Over the years Aarij has also advised SaaS CEOs on GTM strategy.

Aarij holds an MBA and a BS in Computer Engineering from Cornell University, as well as a M.Sc. in Economics from the Catholic University at Leuven, Belgium.

Christin MacLeod

Director of Sales

Christin MacLeod started her journey in sales with Blazeo in 2016 in our Pleasanton, CA office, focusing on assisting clients in the Legal and Education space.

In 2017, Christin helped open our second office in Jacksonville, FL and assisted with hiring, training, and developing an additional sales team. Christin currently manages our direct and partnership sales team, which spans the globe. Prior to that, Christin worked in sales in the Life Sciences Industry and had a long career in retail management with Nordstrom in both Northern and Southern California.

Christin is an avid music lover, enjoys cooking, trying new restaurants, Pickleball, and enjoying outdoor life in Florida.

Deniz Kiremitci

Director of Finance & Accounting

Deniz is the Director of Finance and Accounting at Blazeo, overseeing financial operations, reporting, and strategic planning. She started her career in commercial banking at Finansbank and Garanti Bank before transitioning into finance leadership roles in the SaaS industry.

Before Blazeo, she was the Finance Manager at MobileAction and provided financial consulting to digital agencies and B2B SaaS companies, helping them optimize financial workflows and cash flow management.

She holds a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from İzmir Dokuz Eylul University and attended Monterey Peninsula College while living in the U.S. Having lived and worked in Turkey, the UK, California (U.S.), Colombia, and now Spain, she brings a global perspective to finance.

Outside of work, she enjoys biking with her twin daughters, swimming, crocheting, and exploring books and podcasts—while always missing the Aegean Sea and mezes from her hometown, İzmir.

Ashhad Iqbal

Director of Engineering

Ashhad Iqbal, a.k.a AI, is the Director of Engineering at Blazeo. AI's journey has been filled with growth since joining the company in 2015 as a Junior Support Developer.

From mastering the intricacies of the Blazeo codebase to effortlessly resolving complex system and customer-related issues, AI made his way to become an important asset to the organization. His problem-solving abilities and dedication paved the way for his ascent to Director of Engineering.

Outside of work, AI loves long drives and is known for crafting exceptional pizza dough for family events and showcasing expertise in preparing BBQs, steaks, and beef burgers.
