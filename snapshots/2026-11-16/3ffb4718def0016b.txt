How to Use Lead Sources

Learn how to use our Lead Source field

July 24, 2026

Table of contents

Our Lead Source field allows you to track where your customers found your business without having to use tags.

Lead source tracking enables better reporting so that you can make smart marketing decisions by investing in the sources that bring you good jobs. Leads can be tracked on customers, jobs, and estimates.

Create a Lead Source

ℹ️ System lead sources are added to your list of lead sources by default.

To create a Lead Source:

Go to Settings ⚙️ > Lead Sources

Click the + Create lead source button at the top of the page

Name the lead source and click Create.

🔎 For more information on those lead sources, please see Auto-Tagging with System Lead Sources.

Edit a Lead Source

ℹ️ Editing a lead source will update all existing Leads with that name.

To edit a Lead Source:

Go to Settings ⚙️ > Lead Sources

Click the More button (⋮) to the right of the lead source

Select Edit

📌 Note: System Lead Sources cannot be edited or deleted.

Edit the lead source name

Click Save

Archive a Lead Source

ℹ️ Archiving a system lead source removes it from future use and will disable it from reporting, but it will remain on past jobs and customers.

To archive a lead source:

Go to Settings ⚙️ > Lead Sources

Click the More button (⋮) to the right of the lead source

Select Archive

Click Archive in the confirmation window

The lead source will remain in your Lead Sources list, and the status will update to Archived

ℹ️ Archived lead sources can be restored by going to the Archived tab in your Lead Sources settings.

Unarchive a Lead Source

To restore a system lead source:

Go to Settings ⚙️ > Lead Sources > Archived

Click the More button (⋮) to the right of the lead source

Select Unarchive

The lead source will be removed from the Archived list, and the status will update to Active

Delete a Lead Source

ℹ️ Deleting a custom lead source removes it from future use, but it will remain on past jobs and customers.

To delete a lead source:

Go to Settings ⚙️ > Lead Sources

Click the More button (⋮) to the right of the lead source

Select Delete

Press Delete in the confirmation window

Adding Lead Sources

Lead sources can be assigned to customers, jobs, and estimates, allowing you to easily track where each lead originated.

Add a Lead Source to a Customer Profile

When creating a new customer, you will see a field to add a lead source in the bottom right corner of the Notes section.

ℹ️ Learn more about creating a customer here

To add a lead source to an existing Customer Profile:

Open a Customer Profile

Select the Profile tab

Scroll down and click the Lead source field on the left

Select a lead source from your list or create a new one

Add a Lead source to a Job or Estimate

When creating a job or estimate:

Click the Lead source + button on the left side of the page

Select a lead source from your list or create a new one

ℹ️ Learn more about creating jobs and estimates

To add a lead source to an existing job or estimate:

Open a job or an estimate

Scroll down and click the + Lead source button on the left side of the page

Select a lead source from your list or create a new one

Bulk Updating Lead Sources

You have the flexibility to bulk-add lead sources, streamlining the process and ensuring that your lead data is efficiently categorized across multiple records at once.

Bulk Update Customer Lead Sources

On the Customers tab, you can select multiple customers and bulk-add or update lead sources to those customers:

Go to Customers

Select the customers whose lead source you need to update

Click the Update lead source button above your Customers list

Select a lead source from your list or create a new one

Click Update

Bulk Update Job and Estimate Lead Sources

Go to your Jobs or Estimates list

Select the jobs or estimates to update

Click the Update lead source button at the top of the list

Select a lead source from your list or create a new one

Click Update

Reporting on Lead Sources

You can report on lead sources using the filter functionality on the reporting tab. After applying filters, you will be able to view summary metrics for any lead source(s) and drill into job details to see the lead source for each job.

Report on Job Lead Sources

To report on Job lead sources:

Go to Reporting > Reports

Select Jobs at the top of the page

Under Type, select Job lead source

Report on Jobs by Customer Lead Source

To report on jobs by customer lead sources:

Go to Reporting > Reports

Select Jobs at the top of the page

Under Customer, select Customer lead source

Report on Estimate Lead Sources

To report on Estimate sources:

Go to Reporting > Reports

Select Estimates at the top of the page

Under Type, select Estimate conversion by estimate lead source
