Enerflo Product Announcements

MARCH 2026

Enerflo’s March 2026 Product Announcements introduce updates across Solar PV system design, solar financing, and platform workflows, built to support residential solar companies preparing for peak season amid reduced volume following the 2025 ITC repeal pull-forward. As the operating system for residential solar, Enerflo remains focused on improving operational efficiency, lowering soft costs, reducing errors and change orders, and maximizing profitability on every installation.

Enerflo Platform Updates & Feature Releases

Solar PV System Design

Enerflo introduces an update to Solar PV system design with Aurora, enabling users to archive and reuse solar design data across deals for improved efficiency and cost savings.

AURORA SOLAR INTEGRATION UPDATES

Aurora Solar Designs Now Archivable in Enerflo

Enerflo now allows users to archive Aurora Solar design requests and reuse them on other deals. This helps users move design data without creating new requests, avoiding unnecessary costs.

Solar Finance

Enerflo introduces multiple Solar Financing updates, including support for Palmetto Energy Backup—a new battery-only TPO/lease offering that enables additional revenue streams for solar installers—along with enhancements to financing applications, lender configurations, and deal workflows.

NEW! BATTERY-ONLY LEASE (TPO)

Unlock a New Revenue Stream with Your Install Base — with Enerflo + Palmetto Energy Backup

With the Enerflo + Palmetto integration, you can:

Properly size energy storage using Enerflo’s Battery Sizing Tool

Offer Battery-Only Lease (Energy Backup)

INTEGRATED FINANCE APPLICATIONS UPDATES

Streamlined Financing Application Updates

Enerflo has enhanced its integrated financing applications to simplify customer data entry and ensure information stays up to date with the Enerflo customer record.

SUNGAGE FINANCIAL INTEGRATION UPDATE

Sungage Financial User Authentication Updates

Enerflo now alerts users earlier when a sales rep does not have a matching Sungage account, helping teams resolve issues sooner and avoid delays later in the deal.

GOODLEAP TPO INTEGRATION IMPROVEMENT

Support for GoodLeap TPO Emailed Applications

Enerflo users can now email GoodLeap TPO applications and track their status in Enerflo, bringing TPO in line with existing loan workflows.

NON-INTEGRATED LENDERS UPDATE

Non-Integrated Lenders No Longer Depend on ITC

Enerflo has updated non-integrated lender configurations to remove ITC dependencies, while still allowing admins to configure voluntary prepayments for loans that adjust over time (AKA reamortize).

Enerflo Platform

Enerflo introduces four platform updates designed to improve system configuration, data accuracy, and overall workflow efficiency across the platform, giving teams more control and visibility in managing deals and operations.

ENERGY CONSUMPTION DATA CURVE FIX

More Accurate Monthly Consumption Modeling in Enerflo

Enerflo has improved how annual energy consumption data is converted into monthly usage curves, using baseline data to deliver more accurate system modeling and forecasts.

ENERFLO ADMIN CONTROLS UPDATES

Control Installer Access by Sales Team in Enerflo

Enerflo enables dealers to restrict which installers each sales team can sell for, improving deal accuracy and enforcing installer permissions.

TPO PROPOSAL SUMMARIES IMPROVEMENTS

Improved TPO Proposal Summaries in Enerflo

Enerflo enhances proposal summaries for TPO financing, ensuring users see clearer, more relevant deal information.

LICENSE MANAGEMENT UPDATES IN ENERFLO

Enerflo License Management for Organizations and Users

Enerflo introduces license management, enabling organizations and users to manage licenses by type, state, and status.

Org admins and users can add, edit, and archive license numbers, while Enerflo controls license types.

The system includes duplicate detection and expiration indicators to maintain accurate and compliant records.
