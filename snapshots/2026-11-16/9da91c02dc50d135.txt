Powerful Insurance Agency Management Software

JenesisNow is a cloud-based insurance agency management system designed to make running an independent insurance agency easier. Bring essential agency workflows together in one platform while giving your team the tools they need to stay organized, productive, and connected.

Client & Policy Management

Keep client information, policies, documents, notes, and important agency data organized and accessible.

Carrier Downloads

Bring policy data, renewals, endorsements, commissions, and other carrier information directly into your agency management system.

Communications & Automation

Connect with clients through email and texting while using automation to reduce repetitive tasks and stay engaged.

Commission Management

Track insurance commissions and access the information your agency needs to manage producer and company commissions.

Reporting & Insights

Use customizable insurance agency reports and dashboards to better understand production, renewals, agency activity, and performance.

Integrations

Connect JenesisNow with insurance rating solutions, accounting tools, VoIP systems, electronic signatures, payments, and other services your agency uses.
