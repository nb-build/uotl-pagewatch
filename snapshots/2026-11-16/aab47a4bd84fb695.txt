Sell Online Without Managing Two Businesses

You know your customers shop online. The problem is the work that comes after launch. Uploading photos, updating prices, removing items that sold in-store. For most jewelers, the website becomes a second job no one has time for.

The Edge connects your POS directly to your website so everything stays in sync.

1. Keep your website accurate without manual updates.
2. Give customers real tools, not just a digital brochure.
3. Turn online browsers into in-store buyers.

1. Sync inventory to your website automatically.

Nothing erodes trust faster than a customer clicking "buy" on a piece that sold yesterday. The Edge pushes images, descriptions, pricing, and stock levels to your site on a schedule you control.

• Automate syncs daily or multiple times per day.
• Push product images, descriptions, and pricing automatically.
• Remove sold items without anyone remembering to do it.
• Keep online product data consistent with your POS.

2. Let customers browse real inventory and manage wish lists online.

Your website should be an extension of your store, not a static brochure. The Edge connects your POS data to your website in both directions.

• Let customers view and edit their wish list from your website.
• Give customers online access to their repair job status.
• Sync customer data so online leads flow into your in-store CRM.
• Turn your website into a tool that drives foot traffic.

3. Turn online browsers into in-store buyers.

The goal of your website isn't just visibility. It's getting people through the door. The Edge tracks ecommerce activity, so your team can follow up with the people who are already interested.

• Track which online interactions are actually converting to in-store visits.
• Reach customers beyond your local market without adding staff.
• See what products a customer browsed or saved before they walk in.

Put your showcase online without doubling your workload.

Connect your in-store inventory to your website automatically. Let customers browse real products, check repair status, and save wish lists while your team focuses on the people in front of them.
