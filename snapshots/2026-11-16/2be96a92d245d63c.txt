RevenueWell Launches Standalone AI Receptionist to Deliver Always-On Front Desk Coverage for Dental Practices

Chandra Chakravarthi

April 16, 2026

News

RevenueWell today announced the launch of AI Receptionist, a standalone AI solution designed to help dental practices modernize front office operations without changing their existing systems.

Built to absorb routine administrative work, the AI Receptionist supports front desk teams by handling patient calls, scheduling, rescheduling, missing patient information, and common practice questions, 24 hours a day. The product can be used independently alongside their existing systems and integrates with their practice management system, giving them flexibility without forcing a technology switch. Practices don’t have to connect to RevenueWell’s products to use the standalone AI Receptionist.
“Dental teams are under constant pressure to do more with less and missed calls often mean missed revenue and frustrated patients,” said Katherine Shuman, CEO of RevenueWell. “The AI Receptionist ensures every patient interaction is handled, even during peak hours or after hours. It gives practices a reliable, always-on extension of their front desk reducing staff strain while improving the patient experience.”
An Always-On Front Desk, Without the Overhead

The AI Receptionist acts as a dependable extension of the dental front desk, delivering consistent patient service across the phone, SMS, and website chat, including after hours, weekends, and holidays. Whether a team member is sick, the practice is experiencing seasonal demand, or phones are overwhelmed during peak hours; the AI Receptionist never stops working.

Key Capabilities Include:

24/7 Call Coverage: Capture every patient call, eliminating missed opportunities ‍

Appointment Scheduling & Rescheduling: Books, reschedules, and cancels appointments for patients with the option for dental teams to review appointment requests or allow for instant booking ‍

Patient Inquiries: Answers common questions about hours, insurance, services, and policies ‍

Missing Information Capture: Collects incomplete patient details like demographics and insurance via text and phone securely ‍

Multi-Language Support: Communicates in English and Spanish so patients can connect with practices in the language they’re comfortable with ‍

Live Call Transfer: Seamlessly route calls to staff when patients request human assistance

By reducing missed calls and voicemail backlogs, the AI Receptionist helps practices respond faster, stay organized, and maintain a calmer, more manageable front office environment.

Built for Flexibility and Growth

AI Receptionist is designed for practices of all sizes, from single locations to large DSOs. It supports teams at every stage helping new staff ramp faster, reducing pressure on experienced team members, and providing reliable coverage when hiring is limited or demand fluctuates.

Because it operates independently of RevenueWell’s broader platform, practices can deploy the solution immediately, without disruption, and begin benefiting from AI-driven front office support right away.

Availability

The standalone AI Receptionist is now available. Practices can learn more or request a demo at https://www.revenuewell.com/dental-ai-virtual-assistant.
