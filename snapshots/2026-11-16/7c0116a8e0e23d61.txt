Est.

1984

Originally · RVSOFT

When Steve Hawkins started IDS in 1984, originally known as RVSoft, his goal was simple: to work closely with RV dealerships to give them all the technology, insights and expertise they need to steer their business toward growth and success.

Four decades later, that mandate has expanded across RV, marine and trailer, and the platform has grown from a single desktop tool into an integrated DMS with embedded payments, AI-powered VIN decoding, industry-first automated service communication, and a dedicated marine division.

What people ask about IDS.

Who is IDS Astra?

IDS (Integrated Dealer Systems) is a dealer management software company serving RV and marine dealerships across North America. Our Astra platform puts inventory, sales, F&I, service, parts, payments and analytics on a single system, and more than 1,200 dealers rely on it every day. IDS helps to connect, simplify and grow the dealerships we serve.

What is the best DMS for RV, marine and trailer dealerships?

The best DMS for RV and marine dealerships is one built around how these dealerships actually work. IDS Astra is designed for these industries and runs inventory, sales, F&I, service, parts, payments and analytics on one system. More than 1,200 dealers trust IDS to connect, simplify and grow their business.

How much does a dealer management system cost?

DMS pricing depends on the size of the dealership, the number of users, the modules needed and the number of locations. IDS provides a quote tailored to your dealership, so you only pay for what you need. Request a demo and the IDS team will give you a clear picture.

Can a DMS integrate with the other tools my dealership uses?

Yes. A strong DMS connects with the manufacturer, finance, payment and marketing tools a dealership already relies on. IDS Astra keeps every department on one system and supports integrations that cut down on double entry. Ask an IDS representative about the specific tools you use today.
