Adjustment Summary Report

The Adjustment Summary Report displays details regarding adjustments that have been made in your practice during the past three months. Next to each dollar amount on the report, either an upward-pointing, diagonal, green arrow (indicating a positive number) or a downward-pointing, diagonal, red arrow (indicating a negative number) appears for easier identification. The report groups adjustments by the production type (collection or production) and then by category (charge or credit).

Purposes: Analyze Collections, Analyze Production

Aged Receivables Report

The Aged Receivables Report displays the aged balances of accounts and the total aging of all receivables as of today or for a specified aging bracket. You can send this report to your accountant monthly.

Applied Collection Report

The Applied Collection Report displays payments based on the dates that they are applied to procedures. You can customize the default report as needed. This is the recommended report to use if you pay your providers on collections.

Collection Success Report

The Collection Success Report displays the successfulness of collections as a percentage (the collections divided by the production) over the past 12 months across multiple locations. For each location that you have permission to view, the report displays the production, collections, and success percentage by month and totals. To make it easier for you to see the relative success from month to month, the percentages are shaded: greener is better; redder is worse. The report includes prepayments and unallocated amounts as part of collections. You can customize the default report as needed.

Purpose: Analyze Collections

UDS 6B Section M Line 22a 2022 Report

The UDS 6B Section M Line 22a 2022 Report, which deals with quality of care, provides the necessary information for a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) to report to Medicaid to receive federal funding. The report includes {a} the total number of patients who were ages 6-9 and who had a moderate to high risk of caries; {b} the number of records reviewed; {c} the number of patients from (a) with sealants on first molars; and {d} the percentage of patients from (a) who received a sealant on a first permanent molar.

Purposes: Provide Clinical Information to Medicaid, Collect Money

UDS 6B Section N Line 22a for CY 2020 Report

The UDS 6B Section N Line 22a for CY 2020 Report, which deals with quality of care, provides the necessary information for a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) to report to Medicaid to receive federal funding. The report includes the total number of patients who were ages 6-9, had a moderate or higher risk of caries, had an evaluation, and had one or more first molars that qualify for a sealant; the sample size, which is all applicable patients; and the total number of patients who received sealants on first molars.

Purposes: Provide Clinical Information to Medicaid, Collect Money

UDS Table 9D Report

The UDS Table 9D Report, which deals with patient-related revenue, provides the necessary financial information for a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) to report to Medicaid to receive federal funding. The report includes the total charges, collections, retroactive settlements, allowances, self-pay sliding discounts, and self-pay bad debt write-offs by payer.

Purposes: Provide Clinical Information to Medicaid, Collect Money

Wrap Around Claims Report

The Wrap Around Claims Report provides the necessary information for a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) facility to petition Medicaid for supplemental payments. The report includes the number of encounters, rate, total amount billed, total receipts, and amount to be funded.

Purposes: Provide Clinical Information to Medicaid, Collect Money
