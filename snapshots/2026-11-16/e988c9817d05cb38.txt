Moraware Products

What products does Moraware offer?

Moraware offers three products built for countertop fabrication shops: CounterGo for drawing and quoting, Systemize for scheduling and job management, and Inventory for slab ordering and tracking. Each can be used on its own, and they connect to carry a job’s information from quote through installation.

What is the difference between CounterGo, Systemize, and Inventory?

CounterGo is used to draw, lay out, and quote a job, typically in front of the customer. Systemize takes a won quote and manages the job through scheduling, digital job packets, and shop-floor tracking. Inventory manages slab purchasing and ties material directly to the jobs that need it.

A shop can use one, two, or all three.

Is Moraware one platform or separate products?

Moraware is three specialized products — CounterGo, Systemize, and Inventory — that are designed to connect rather than a single bundled platform a shop has to buy all at once. A quote from CounterGo becomes a scheduled job in Systemize, and Inventory ties slab stock to those same jobs, so shops can start with what they need and add more as they grow.

All three products are covered by the same team with unlimited customer support.

Can I use just one Moraware product, or do I have to buy all three?

You can start with just one. Shops commonly begin with CounterGo for quoting or Systemize for scheduling, then add Inventory or the other product later as needs grow. The products are designed to connect when used together, but none requires the others.

What is CounterGo used for?

CounterGo is Moraware’s drawing and quoting software for countertop fabricators. It lets a shop draw a layout and generate an accurate quote, often in about three minutes, directly in front of the customer, with consistent pricing pulled from the shop’s own price list.

What is Systemize used for?

Systemize is Moraware’s scheduling and job management software. It gives a shop one shared calendar, keeps digital job packets (cut sheets, DXFs, signed waivers) attached to each job, and makes job status visible without walking the shop floor to check.

What is Moraware Inventory used for?

Moraware Inventory tracks slab ordering and stock for fabrication shops. It records what’s on order, what’s on hand, and which slab is assigned to which job, so shops avoid selling the same slab twice or discovering a shortage on fabrication day.
