BombBomb Unveils Engage: A Next-Generation Video Messaging Platform

MarTech360 Bureau•May 19, 2025

Advertisement

BombBomb, the trusted video messaging platform for sales professionals, has announced the launch of BombBomb Engage—a fully reimagined, enterprise-grade platform designed to help modern sales teams stand out in crowded inboxes with authentic, face-to-face video communication.

As the B2B sales landscape evolves—marked by longer sales cycles, changing buyer behaviors, and intensified digital competition—sales professionals are under increasing pressure to break through noise, reach key decision-makers, and maintain personalized outreach at scale. In response, many are turning to digital tools that not only streamline daily workflows, but also support automation and data-driven decision-making. Among these, video messaging continues to emerge as a high-impact solution—enabling sellers to foster trust, personalize engagement, and accelerate deal cycles.

BombBomb Engage: Purpose-Built for Scale, Simplicity, and Speed

BombBomb Engage represents a complete rebuild of the company’s core video messaging platform—redesigned to meet the demands of today’s enterprise sales teams. The new user interface is intuitive and frictionless, offering faster performance, streamlined navigation, and seamless access to recording, editing, sending, and tracking personalized videos. Whether used by individual sellers or large sales organizations, Engage empowers users to deliver impactful video messages quickly and efficiently.

The platform integrates natively into email clients, web browsers, and CRM systems, removing the need for platform switching or extensive training. Admins benefit from enterprise-ready tools such as Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC) and customizable team-level permissions, ensuring secure and scalable management.

“Sales pros don’t need more tools, they need better ones,” said Conor McCluskey, CEO of BombBomb. “Engage automates video outreach, so nothing falls through the cracks, and sales pros can see exactly who’s watching, clicking and engaging, in real time. It’s not about sending more videos…it’s about sending the right video at the right time to drive a real pipeline.”

Key New Capabilities Introduced with BombBomb Engage

The launch of BombBomb Engage also introduces several powerful features designed to support go-to-market teams and enterprise users:

Copilot – Automated Video Workflows

Included in BombBomb’s Core + Copilot and Enterprise plans, Copilot enables users to automate video campaigns, removing the need to send each message manually. Sales reps can record key videos once, and Copilot ensures they’re delivered at the right moment. Admins can build fallback videos into campaigns, and team members benefit from pre-written scripts and video examples to make content creation fast and simple.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Now a standard feature across all BombBomb packages, RBAC ensures compliance and governance by allowing teams to control who can view, edit, or manage content—an essential capability for large organizations.

Enterprise-Grade Security and Compliance

BombBomb Engage comes equipped with SOC 2 Type II compliance and Single Sign-On (SSO) functionality, offering the robust security enterprises require for modern software deployments.

Looking Ahead: AI-Driven Innovation on the Horizon

With the launch of Engage, BombBomb is laying the foundation for a series of AI-powered enhancements that will begin rolling out later this year.

“Incorporating generative AI into anything should never be a 'one and done' venture,” said Heather Stevens, VP of Product at BombBomb. “It needs to be incorporated in a way that it is forever retraining and learning how to be better and more effective. This is why our approach to AI is thoughtful, purposeful & not just adding AI for the sake of AI.”

BombBomb Engage sets a new standard for sales engagement tools, delivering the speed, personalization, and automation modern sellers need to build pipeline and close deals—faster and more authentically.
