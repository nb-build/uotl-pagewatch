Carrier Warranty Registration and Information

Peace of mind with Carrier warranties

Registering your Carrier HVAC warranty protects your home comfort investment by unlocking comprehensive coverage options for your heating and cooling systems. By registering your eligible equipment, you ensure long-term peace of mind, optimized energy efficiency, and reliable performance.

Carrier creates solutions that matter for people and our planet. Whether you need to register a new product, look up an existing record, or read our warranty FAQs, our homeowner resources are designed to give you confidence in everyday life.

Carrier offers dedicated tools to help you register your product, verify coverage, and get expert advice. Choose from the options below to seamlessly manage your warranty benefits.

Activate your coverage by registering your equipment online. Activate your warranty today to unlock your benefits.

Where can I find warranty FAQs?

Our comprehensive FAQ section provides quick answers to common coverage questions. Explore answers to the most frequently asked questions about your warranty.

What is the Carrier Consumer Choice Warranty?

With the Carrier Consumer Choice Warranty, you get the flexibility to choose your coverage. Upon timely registration of eligible new Carrier equipment, you can select either a 10-year parts-only limited warranty or a 5-year parts plus 3-year labor limited warranty.¹ This program puts the power in your hands, ensuring your comfort is protected in a way that best aligns with your long-term goals. Dealer participation required – to find a participating Consumer Choice dealer, please visit our dealer locator.

Find participating dealers

How does the Consumer Choice Warranty work?

To activate your preferred coverage, you must register your eligible equipment within 90 days of installation. Once your system is installed, you use our registration tool to select the warranty option that gives you the most peace of mind—either a longer overall parts timeline or added short-term labor coverage.

Which products are eligible for the Consumer Choice Warranty?

Carrier’s Consumer Choice Warranty applies to a wide range of residential heating and cooling solutions. Eligible products include: Air Conditioners, Heat Pumps, Gas and Oil Furnaces, Fan Coils, Evaporator Coils, Packaged Products, Ductless Systems (Excluding value tier, which is only eligible for a 5-year parts warranty), Humidifiers, Ventilators, Whole-Home Air Purifiers, UV Lamps, Boilers and Infinity® System Controls.

What are the rules for Consumer Choice Warranty selection?

The Consumer Choice warranty is designed specifically for existing residential applications and requires timely action. New residential construction installations are not eligible for this program.

When must I make my warranty selection?

The desired warranty option must be selected at the time of product registration, within the first 90 days after installation. You can change your selection only within this 90-day window.

How do grouped registrations work?

The option you select will apply to all eligible products registered together. If you want different coverage options for different products, they must be registered separately.

What happens if I miss the registration deadline?

If you register after 90 days, the standard 5-year parts warranty applies with no labor coverage. (Note: Jurisdictions where warranty benefits cannot be conditioned on registration will automatically receive a 10-year parts limited warranty, which can be changed to the labor option within the first 90 days.)

Is dealer participation required for the labor warranty?

Yes, the 5-year parts plus 3-year labor warranty option is available only if your installing dealer or contractor has officially elected to participate in the Consumer Choice program.

Frequently Asked Questions

What parts should be used to repair my Carrier HVAC system?

To ensure optimal performance and maintain your system's integrity, you should always insist on genuine Carrier parts for any repairs. Using approved Carrier parts helps your equipment continue to operate safely, efficiently, and stay in compliance with your warranty terms.

What happens if I forget to register my Carrier HVAC warranty?

If you do not register your Carrier equipment within 90 days of installation, the standard 5-year parts-only limited warranty will apply, and you will not be eligible for the extended 10-year parts or labor coverage options. (Note: Jurisdictions where warranty benefits cannot be conditioned on registration will automatically receive a 10-year parts limited warranty, which can be changed to the labor option within the first 90 days.)

Are new construction homes eligible for the Consumer Choice warranty?

No, new residential construction installations are not eligible for the Consumer Choice warranty. This specific program is designed exclusively for replacement residential applications.

Can I change my Carrier warranty selection later?

You can change your Consumer Choice warranty option only within the first 90 days after installation. Once the 90-day window passes, your coverage selection becomes permanent.
