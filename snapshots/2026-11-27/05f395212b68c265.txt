Referral program software FAQs

Expand all

What is Referral Rock?

Referral Rock is referral program software for businesses with teams. It's built for service businesses, B2B companies and ecommerce brands, where sales, support and field teams earn referrals by taking great care of customers.

Signing up or logging in from here is not joining a referral program. This site is for businesses who want to run one.

What is referral program software?

Referral program software, also called a referral app or referral platform, provides what a business needs to run an automated referral program: a member portal, reward rules, payout management, and much more, all to help you get more referrals.

Is Referral Rock a refer-a-friend platform?

Yes. Refer-a-friend is the customer referral program type, and Referral Rock runs it end to end: customers are enrolled automatically, every referral is tracked back to the person who sent it, and both sides can be rewarded.

The same platform also runs brand ambassador and partner or affiliate programs, so one tool covers all three.

Is a referral program right for my business?

The best sign you're ready is that customers already refer you on their own, even informally. A referral program organizes and rewards that word of mouth, so it works best when there's already some enthusiasm to build on.

Your business model matters more than your industry: a program fits best when each new customer is valuable, through a high price, repeat purchases, or both.

See the readiness check for the honest version of who a program fits, and who it doesn't.

Should I build a referral program myself or use software?

You can start with a spreadsheet and manual payouts, and many businesses do. A spreadsheet usually holds up until the program grows, then tracking who referred whom, preventing fraud, and paying rewards on time becomes real, ongoing work.

Software takes over the parts that don't scale. Our guide to building or buying referral software covers the hidden costs and when building makes sense.

How long does it take to see referrals coming in?

First referrals usually arrive within the first few weeks of launch. How fast depends on the size of your existing customer base and how you promote the program. Your advisor helps you work that out before you go live.

How can I create a referral website?

Our software makes it simple to create a referral website. We provide customizable landing and sharing pages so you can quickly set up a dedicated site for your program.

You can add sign-up forms, referral tracking, and reward information, and an advisor sets it up with you, so your referral website is live in days.

How does referral tracking integrate with my existing systems?

Our referral tracking software connects to popular CRMs, e-commerce, and marketing platforms through pre-built integrations. Data flows both ways between your referral program and your other systems, so tracking stays accurate without manual work.

Can a referral count without a link or code?

Yes. A referral counts even with no link or code. You or your team can log one from a call, a form or a CRM deal, and Referral Rock credits and rewards it like any other.

Is it difficult to set up and manage a client referral program?

Modern referral program software is built to be user-friendly. Our platform offers templates and guided setup, so a team can launch and manage a program without deep technical knowledge.

You can see your program take shape the day you start, and go live in days with an advisor setting it up with you.

What are best practices for implementing referral software?

Start with the fundamentals that separate programs that grow from programs that stall. Reward both sides, not just the referrer. In our analysis of 952 programs, 54% rewarded only the referrer, leaving the friend with no reason to act.

Send people to a personalized referral page instead of your homepage, and invite customers while they're still excited. One Referral Rock customer saw 86% of their referrals come within the first 90 days.

Promote continuously rather than relying on a single launch email, and lean on the advisor included with every plan to put these in place before you go live.

What are the most common referral program mistakes to avoid?

Across 952 programs we've analyzed over 9 years, the biggest killers are inconsistent promotion after launch (behind 82% of stalled programs), rewarding only the referrer, and sending referred friends to a generic homepage or checkout form with no context (which 42% of programs do).

Fixing those three is usually what keeps a program producing referrals month after month instead of stalling out.

How can I promote my referral program to maximize participation?

Treat referral as an ongoing system, not a one-time campaign. We recommend the 3 P's of continuous promotion.

Proactive Invites: reach out right after a customer milestone.

Passive Entry Points: make the program easy to find across your site, emails, and checkout.

Program Recruiters: sales, support and field teams enroll customers as referral program recruiters, with credit for each one.

Referral Rock automates ongoing campaigns that target only the customers who haven't joined yet, so promotion keeps running without manual effort.

What rewards and incentives work best for referrals?

Reward choice depends on your audience, but the data points in clear directions. Two-sided rewards, something for both the referrer and the friend, consistently outperform one-sided ones, yet most programs still reward only the referrer.

Among Referral Rock customers, gift cards are the most popular member reward (61%), while referred friends respond best to discounts and gift cards.

Most programs use fixed amounts (92%) rather than a percentage of the sale, which keeps budgeting simple.

How can I measure the ROI of my online referral program?

Our referral program tracking captures the metrics that matter: shares, unique people reached, referrals generated, conversion rates, and the rewards you've paid. Set those against what a new customer is worth to your business to see what the program returns.

What results can I expect from a referral program?

Results vary by business, but referral traffic is high-intent. Across all program types on our platform, referred visitors who become customers typically do so within about a day, each share reaches roughly 13 people, and programs average around 1,388 monthly website sessions from referral sources.

Manage the leading indicators (shares, then reach, then referrals) rather than waiting on the final count. Our analytics dashboard, brand-visit tracking, and data exports give you the numbers to prove ROI.

How secure is a referral system?

Security is a top priority. Our referral system includes data encryption, secure reward distribution, and full compliance with relevant data-protection regulations like GDPR and CCPA, and we update our protocols regularly to keep your data and your customers' information protected.

Does Referral Rock help prevent fraudulent referrals?

Yes. Referral Rock includes fraud detection and prevention: IP tracking, duplicate-entry detection, and suspicious-activity monitoring to protect the integrity of your program. Clients see meaningful reductions in fraudulent activity after switching to our system.

Referral Rock is one simple plan: the Operator Plan at $250 a month, with unlimited referrals and a referral expert included. You can try everything on a 30-day free trial before you commit. Add-ons cover the features not everyone needs, so you only pay for what you need.

Scale and Multi-Org plans cover higher volume and multi-location or franchise networks.
