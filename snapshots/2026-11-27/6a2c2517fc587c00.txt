Foundation & waterproofing software FAQ

What is foundation & waterproofing software?

It's the system a foundation repair or waterproofing contractor uses to document inspections with photos, build tiered estimates with financing, manage multi-day projects, and bill by milestone draw with warranties tracked — all on one platform.

Can it document inspections with photos?

Yes. Inspections are photo-documented with moisture readings and before/after records, which justify the repair and live in the customer portal for sign-off.

Does it track warranties?

Transferable lifetime warranties are tracked per property, so coverage and transfers are on file without a spreadsheet.

How does draw billing work?

Deposit, progress, and completion draws are billed automatically by pay-by-link, while labor and material costs are tracked against the bid for live margin.
