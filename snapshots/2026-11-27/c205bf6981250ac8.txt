DispatchTrack and Pistachio Software Offers a Connected Purchase to Delivery Experience

Media contact

Brian Hoey
brian@dispatchtrack.com

CAMPBELL, Calif., August 21, 2025 — DispatchTrack, the global leader in last-mile delivery and service execution technology, is excited to announce a new partnership with Pistachio, a modern all-in-one software platform trusted by furniture and mattress retailers for payment and order management. Together, the companies are streamlining the customer journey—from the moment a sale is captured in-store to the final delivery at the customer’s door.

By integrating Pistachio’s point-of-sale and order management capabilities with DispatchTrack’s delivery management platform, retailers can now bridge the gap between in-store sales and last-mile execution within a single, unified workflow.

What This Integration Delivers

End-to-end visibility and control from the point of sale to delivery fulfillment—ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.

Better operational flow, with automated order handoff to delivery scheduling—reducing delays and speeding up processing.

Improved customer experiences, powered by real-time tracking, proactive communication, and consistent service execution.

Greater efficiency and reduced complexity, giving sales teams and logistics staff a unified platform that simplifies training and minimizes errors.

Creating A Seamless Workflow

A customer makes a purchase using Pistachio’s modern, cloud-based POS interface.

The order—complete with items, preferences, and customer info—flows directly into DispatchTrack.

DispatchTrack assigns and optimizes the delivery, manages route planning, and sends ETAs and notifications to the customer.

Deliveries are executed with full visibility and performance tracking, ensuring accuracy and accountability from sale to doorstep.

Retailers gain unmatched clarity and efficiency across their operations, while customers enjoy a frictionless, connected experience that extends from checkout to delivery.

###

About Pistachio

Pistachio Software provides a robust, cloud-based platform designed for furniture and mattress retailers. It delivers end-to-end visibility—from quoting to invoicing, inventory to deliveries—all in an easy-to-use, mobile-ready interface that modern retail teams rely on.
