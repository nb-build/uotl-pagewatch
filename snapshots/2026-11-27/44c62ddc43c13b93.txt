What Lightspeed DMS Helps You Do Better

DMS

Many dealers struggle with the inefficiency of switching between different tools for sales, service, and parts. Why juggle multiple systems? Let Lightspeed eliminate that chaos by connecting your dealership’s departments into one cohesive platform.

CRM

Disconnected leads often result in lost sales and forgotten customers. Lightspeed CRM bridges that gap by tracking every interaction from initial text to final purchase allowing your team to nurture relationships and drive repeat business through automated, targeted marketing.

Sales

Closing deals is harder when your data is scattered across different tools. Lightspeed Sales combines real-time texting, competitive benchmarks, and F&I tools into one seamless workflow to maximize every deal's profitability.

Service

Track tech time, job status, and revenue with precision. With Tech Video and real-time texting, your service team closes more jobs, and keeps customers looped in every step of the way.

Accounting

No more manual data entry and disconnected books. See how Lightspeed Accounting integrates every transaction from across your departments in real-time, giving you total financial clarity with one single login.

Parts

Stocking the wrong parts or losing track of special orders drains your bottom line. Lightspeed Parts simplifies inventory with mobile barcode scanning and a built-in locator that lets you source parts from other dealers without leaving the invoice.

Mobile

Managing a dealership from behind a desk limits your team’s ability to engage with customers on the lot or in the bays. Lightspeed Mobile untethers your staff by putting powerful tools like unit walk-around videos, mobile check-ins, and inventory scanning right onto their smartphones and tablets.

Rental

Managing a rental fleet alongside unit sales can often feel like running two separate businesses. Lightspeed Rental brings everything together by integrating reservations, contract management, and maintenance tracking directly into your DMS to ensure your fleet is always profitable and ready for the next customer.

Integrations

Running a dealership means relying on a web of tools from OEM portals to F&I platforms to parts suppliers. When those systems don't talk to each other, your team pays the price in manual data entry, duplicate records, and deals that stall at the worst possible moment. Lightspeed's 550+ integrations connect the tools you already use directly into your DMS, so your data flows automatically — no re-keying, no reconciliation, no gaps.

Multi-Store

Whether you have one location or 10, our Multi-Store Premium features take efficiency even further, allowing you to transfer parts, sync service templates, and reassign sales leads between locations with just a few clicks.

Pricing

A one-size-fits-all price doesn't make sense when every dealership operates differently. Lightspeed offers modular, scalable pricing that ensures you only pay for the specific tools and integrations that drive your dealership's profit.

RevPay

Manual reconciliation and separate payment terminals create constant friction and reporting gaps. See how Lightspeed RevPay℠ embeds processing directly into your DMS to automate reconciliation, simplify multi-store funding, and offer modern "text-to-pay" options all from a single platform.

AI Command Center

Skip time-consuming custom reports and manual spreadsheet exports. Lightspeed AI Command Center lets you ask plain-language questions to instantly get data-driven answers, charts, and recommended next steps directly from your live DMS.

Highlighted Features

TextMarketing

Many dealerships lose valuable time playing phone tag or waiting for emails to be read. Lightspeed Texting meets customers where they already are, boasting a 98% open rate to ensure important service updates and sales messages are seen in minutes.

EmailMarketing

Generic, plain-text emails often get ignored or lost in crowded inboxes. Learn how EmailMarketing uses your DMS data and built-in AI tools to create stunning, branded, targeted email campaigns that reach the right customers and drive real ROI.

eSignatures

Relying on physical paperwork and manual signatures often creates bottlenecks and delays for both your sales and service teams. See how Lightspeed eSignature removes those barriers by allowing customers to sign contracts and approve repair orders remotely via text or email.

Tech Video

Customer trust is often lost when they can't see the work being recommended, leading to declined repairs and missed revenue. Lightspeed Tech Video eliminates that skepticism by allowing technicians to record and share walk-around inspections directly with clients, proving the need for service and boosting your shop's sales.

700 Credit (Credit Report) ​

Lightspeed’s 700 Credit integration helps you secure financing for your customer fast, with a convenient integration build into every Lightspeed deal. See how the integration automatically sends all the details you’ve already filled out to 700 Credit, saving you time and getting the deal closed faster.

InsightHub

Fragmented data hunts and manual refreshes slow your team down and hide critical tasks. See how InsightHub delivers real-time, role-based guidance and a modern interface that surfaces your dealership’s most important next steps the moment you sign in.
