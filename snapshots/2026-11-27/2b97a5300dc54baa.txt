Am I affected by the new limits?

You are affected if your MailerLite account is on the Free plan and exceeds any of the new limits. The most common trigger is the subscriber count: many free users sit between 250 and 500 contacts today, which was fine under the old cap but is over the new one. Run through this quick checklist now.

Are you over the new free limits?

✓More than 250 active subscribers on your list

✓Sending more than 2,500 emails a month (subscribers multiplied by campaigns)

✓Running more than 3 active automations or relying on more than 3 forms

✓Using more than one landing page or website builder site

If you ticked any box, plan ahead so a launch or a scheduled send does not get blocked. If none apply, you are fine, and you actually gain a second seat and the new features. Not sure how much room you have? It helps to understand how providers count contacts and sends in the first place, which our primer on email marketing fundamentals covers alongside list hygiene and deliverability.

What happens if you go over the limit?

If you are over 250 subscribers, MailerLite pauses your ability to send campaigns, run automations, and manually add new subscribers until you either upgrade to a paid plan that fits your list or reduce your subscriber count below 250. If you stay within the subscriber cap but exceed the 2,500 monthly email limit, campaign and automation sends stop for the rest of that month. Importantly, your account and data are not deleted, so nothing is lost while you decide.

The practical risk

The 12,000 to 2,500 email cut is the one that surprises people. A 250-contact list emailed twice a week already uses about 2,000 sends a month, so even a small, engaged list can hit the ceiling fast. If sending pauses mid-month, your newsletter or automated welcome series simply stops going out until the next cycle or an upgrade.

What are your options now?

There is no single right answer; it depends on your list size, how often you send, and whether you are ready to pay. Broadly, you have three sensible paths: trim your list to stay free, upgrade MailerLite, or switch to a more generous free plan.

1. Stay free, trim down

Clean your list to under 250 active contacts and keep automations to three. Best if your list is small and you rarely send.

2. Upgrade MailerLite

Paid plans start at $12 per month and lift the caps. Best if you like the tool and your list is growing steadily.

3. Switch free plans

Move to a more generous free tier such as Mailsoftly (500 contacts) or Kit. Best if you want room to grow without a bill yet.

Option two is genuinely reasonable if MailerLite already fits your workflow, and the entry price is fair: the official MailerLite pricing page shows paid plans starting around $12 per month. But if the appeal was the free plan in the first place, it is worth knowing that other providers still offer materially more for free. Before deciding, it helps to weigh how each tool performs on support and deliverability, which independent review platforms aggregate from verified users beyond the marketing pages. Will MailerLite delete my subscribers if I go over the new free limit?

No. If you exceed the 250-subscriber limit, MailerLite pauses your ability to send campaigns, run automations, and manually add new contacts, but your account and data are not deleted. Nothing is lost while you decide whether to trim your list under 250, upgrade to a paid plan, or switch to a more generous free provider.

When do the MailerLite free plan changes take effect?

The new Free plan limits take effect on July 1, 2026 for accounts that were on the Free plan before June 16, 2026. New signups and downgrades from paid plans get the new limits immediately. The tighter feature-use limits on automations, forms, and pages are planned for enforcement starting in August 2026.

What are the new MailerLite free plan limits?

Since July 1, 2026, the MailerLite Free plan allows 250 active subscribers, 2,500 emails per month, 2 user seats, 3 active automations, 3 active forms, and 1 landing page and website. This is down from 500 subscribers and 12,000 monthly emails, though free accounts also gain features like a custom HTML editor and more templates.

What happens to my account if I am over the limit?

If you exceed 250 subscribers, sending campaigns, running automations, and manually adding subscribers will stop working until you upgrade to a paid plan or reduce your list below 250. Exceeding 2,500 monthly emails pauses sending for the rest of that month. Your account and data are not deleted, so you can decide without losing anything.
