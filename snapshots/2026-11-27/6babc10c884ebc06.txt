How does Sana keep prices and stock accurate?

Sana takes pricing, stock and customer terms directly from SAP or Microsoft Dynamics each time a buyer loads a page. There's no middleware and no separate database to maintain, so what buyers see online always matches your ERP.

Which ERP systems does Sana work with?

SAP ECC, SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain Management, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. Your pricing and customer logic stay in your ERP, set up once, then shown accurately everywhere afterwards.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on your ERP setup, catalogues and workflows. Because Sana uses the logic already in your ERP, there's less to rebuild. JENSEN-GROUP replatformed 15 web stores in 9 months.

What happens in the demo?

A specialist looks at your ERP setup and ordering workflows, then shows you how Sana would work in your current situation. After the demo, you know exactly whether Sana fits and what implementation would involve.

How is Sana different from other B2B e-commerce platforms?

Most B2B e-commerce platforms keep their own copy of your data and sync it with your ERP on a schedule. Sana works directly with your SAP or Microsoft Dynamics data, so you get no sync errors, no extra systems and no duplicate setup.

How does Sana Commerce handle customer-specific pricing and agreements?

Customer-specific pricing, discounts and contract terms are read live from the ERP each time a buyer logs in. If a buyer has a negotiated price for a product, that is the price they see. If their payment terms are Net 60, that is what applies at checkout. Nothing is duplicated or cached.

Is Sana Commerce suitable for manufacturers?

Yes. Sana Commerce is used by manufacturers and other B2B sellers across industries including machinery, automotive, construction, electronics, medical supplies and chemicals. The platform supports the order complexity these sectors typically deal with, such as configurable products, technical documentation, large catalogues and account hierarchies.
