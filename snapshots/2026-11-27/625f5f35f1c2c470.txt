FAQs

What advantages does the combined platform create for customers?

Customers will get the industry's leading sales engagement and the industry's leading forecasting in a single system. That means fewer tools, less context-switching, and a complete picture of your revenue.

What does the combined company mean for me as a customer?

Customers now have one company accountable across the revenue lifecycle, with the capabilities they rely on today continuing as part of the Salesloft brand.

Is Clari going away?

Clari and Salesloft are now operating as one company, with Salesloft as our go-forward company and platform brand. Clari Forecast retains the Clari name, and Clari's broader capabilities and technology across revenue intelligence, deal management, and conversation intelligence continue as part of Salesloft.

Is this a rebrand?

Salesloft is unveiling a new global brand identity, but the announcement represents more than a visual rebrand. The company changed first: less than a year after the merger, Clari and Salesloft are operating as one company with one leadership team, one strategy and one brand.

Are Clari products or capabilities going away?

The brand launch is not a product discontinuation announcement. The capabilities customers rely on today continue as part of Salesloft. Clari Forecast retains the Clari name, while Clari's broader capabilities and technology across revenue intelligence, deal management, and conversation intelligence live on under the Salesloft brand.

Will my products or workflows change?

Not immediately. Product integration is ongoing, and you'll see capabilities deepening over time — not disruption to what you rely on today. We're committed to transparent updates as roadmap details are ready to share.

Where can I get updates as things evolve?

The Salesloft newsroom and the innovation hub are the best places to follow product and company news.

What is The Predictive Revenue System?

Salesloft's Predictive Revenue System connects buyer engagement, conversations, deal and pipeline signals, forecasting and execution workflows to help sales teams understand what is true now, identify risk while there is still time to change an outcome, and act on what matters next.
