Frequently asked questions

Who is Projul for?

Projul is built for construction businesses of all sizes, from 5-person crews to 1,000+ employee enterprises. Residential general contractors, commercial general contractors, and specialty contractors all use Projul to run their operations.

Our customers include mid-sized contractors coordinating multiple crews across dozens of jobs and large enterprises running hundreds of simultaneous projects. The software grows with you, offering the simplicity small teams need and the power large operations demand.

Tired of juggling multiple systems, losing leads, or struggling to track profitability? Projul brings everything into one platform. From your first employee to your thousandth, Projul is designed to help you win more work, stay organized, and maximize profit.

What makes Projul different from other construction software?

Projul is built by former construction company owners who experienced the pain of outdated, overpriced software firsthand. We combine powerful features like CRM, estimating, scheduling, invoicing, time tracking, and job costing into one platform with honest, transparent pricing.

No hidden fees, no per-user/per-project/per-revenue charges that punish growth, and no forcing you to buy multiple products. Plus, our team answers the phone because we actually understand your business.

How long does it take to get started with Projul?

Most teams start using Projul within one week. Our cloud-based platform requires zero server installation or IT involvement.

Teams of 5-20 employees often go live in 2-3 days. Larger operations (100+ employees) typically complete full implementation within two weeks, including data migration and multi-department setup. Every annual plan includes our Premium Support Package with personalized workflow analysis, data import assistance, customized training, and ongoing support via phone, email, or video chat whenever you need it.

Does Projul integrate with QuickBooks or other software?

Yes. Projul integrates seamlessly with QuickBooks Online, automatically syncing estimates, invoices, payments, time logs, and customer data to eliminate dual entry.

We also integrate with JustiFi for payment processing, allowing your customers to pay invoices via credit card or ACH directly from their phone or computer. Additionally, Projul integrates with 1build for live construction cost data, ensuring your estimates stay accurate as material prices change.

Our lead capture form embeds directly on your website to import prospects automatically, and Zapier integration lets you connect lead sources like Facebook ads, Google Ads, and thousands of other applications to flow leads directly into Projul's CRM.

What's included in Projul? Do I need multiple subscriptions?

Everything you need to run your construction business is included in one subscription. Every Projul plan includes lead management, estimating, scheduling, invoicing with payment processing, and time tracking. Core adds CRM, digital signatures, project management, photo and document management, reporting, mobile apps for iOS and Android, and unlimited projects.

You will never pay extra for storage or add-on modules. Core+ and Pro add advanced features like job costing, client portal, and geofencing, and Pro adds Projul AI Connect, so the AI assistant you already use (like ChatGPT or Claude) can add leads, draft estimates, and update your schedule. Every plan can be billed monthly or annually.

Is Projul the best construction software for small companies?

Projul is built to work for construction companies of every size, but small teams especially benefit from our flat-rate pricing model. Unlike most construction software that charges per user, Projul never penalizes you for growing your team. Add employees without watching your software bill climb.

The Starter plan covers estimating, scheduling, invoicing, and time tracking for teams of up to 3. Core and Core+ add CRM, the full mobile app, and job costing for crews of up to 20, the same tools enterprises with 1,000+ employees rely on. Most small teams are up and running within a few days.

We're the team behind your team; here to help.

By subscribing with Projul, you're partnering with people who appreciate what it takes to operate a construction company. With approximately 8 years in the construction industry and over 3,000 completed projects under our belt, we're able to ensure that every new feature and update is relevant to our industry.

Additionally, you drive our roadmap. While we have a long term understanding of where we'd like to be 1-year, 3-years and 5-years down the road, the day to day priority of new features is largely driven by the feedback received from our customers.
