Frequently Asked Questions and Resources

Do you have a question about WPForms? See the list below for our most frequently asked questions. If your question is not listed here, then please contact us.

Does WPForms have an AI form builder?

Yes, WPForms comes with a powerful AI form builder for WordPress. WPForms AI lets you describe the form you need in plain English and generates a fully working form in about 60 seconds. It handles field types, validation rules, and layout automatically.

No copy-pasting from ChatGPT, no manual field setup. Just type something like “create a quote request form for a roofing company” and you get a production-ready form with the right fields, labels, and logic already configured. You can also use AI to generate individual dropdown lists, checkbox groups, and multiple choice options for any field.

WPForms AI form builder is designed to help you save time on building any type of forms including online order form, quizzes, lead calculators, and more.

Can I build calculation forms with WPForms?

Absolutely. The Calculations addon lets you build real-time calculators that update as visitors type or select options. Use it for instant price quotes, order totals, loan calculators, ROI estimators, BMI calculators, or any formula-based form. If you can write “x 0.15” for a 15% tax rate, you can build it.

One WPForms customer eliminated $10,000 in pricing disputes in a single quarter by letting customers calculate their own quotes. Calculations work with dropdowns, number fields, radio buttons, and checkboxes, so you can build anything from a simple cost estimator to a multi-step configurator.

What integrations does WPForms support?

WPForms connects with 400+ tools across every category you need:

Payment Gateways: Stripe, PayPal Commerce, Square, Authorize.Net (plus a Coupons addon for discount codes).

CRMs: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM.

Email Marketing: Mailchimp, Constant Contact, AWeber, Brevo, ActiveCampaign, Campaign Monitor, Drip, GetResponse, MailerLite, Kit.

Cloud Storage: Google Drive, Google Sheets, Dropbox, Airtable, Notion.

Communication: Slack notifications, Twilio (SMS and WhatsApp), Google Calendar event creation.

WordPress Native: User registration, post submissions, and full compatibility with all major page builders.

All of these are native, built-in integrations that connect through a simple toggle or API key. No custom code required. And if you need something not on the list, Zapier (10,000+ apps), Make, and n8n let you connect WPForms to virtually any tool you already use.

How much does WPForms cost? Is there a free version?

WPForms Lite is 100% free and includes the drag-and-drop builder, pre-built templates, email notifications, and spam protection. No credit card required, no trial period, no feature lockout on a timer. Over 6 million websites use WPForms Lite today. When you’re ready for more, paid plans start at $49.50/year and every plan includes a 14-day money-back guarantee, so there’s zero risk to try it.

Do I need any coding skills to use WPForms?

Not at all. WPForms was built for non-technical users from day one. The drag-and-drop builder lets you add, remove, and rearrange fields visually. Or skip the builder entirely and let WPForms AI generate a complete form from a text description. With 2,100+ pre-built templates covering everything from simple contact forms to multi-page applications, you rarely need to start from scratch. WPForms works with every major page builder, including Elementor, Divi, and the native WordPress block editor. As one user put it: “As a non-programmer, I was amazed by how easy and intuitive this tool is to use.” Most people have their first form live in under 5 minutes.

How does WPForms handle spam protection?

WPForms gives you four layers of spam defense built in: Google reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha, Cloudflare Turnstile, and Akismet filtering. You can enable any combination with a single toggle. On top of that, WPForms includes its own smart anti-spam engine that blocks bot submissions without making your visitors solve puzzles.

Will WPForms slow down my website?

No. WPForms is engineered for performance. Forms load with lightweight, optimized assets, and features like form abandonment tracking run silently in the background using minimal JavaScript. There’s no bloat: WPForms only loads scripts on pages where your forms actually appear. As one user described it: “Super clean form builder. No bloat, works great with my theme.” WPForms is consistently rated among the fastest WordPress form plugins available, trusted by over 6 million websites, including high-traffic sites that can’t afford any slowdown.

How reliable are WPForms WordPress form notifications?

Unlike other WordPress form plugins, WPForms have one of the most robust built-in WordPress form notification system. WPForms sends email notifications through your WordPress site’s mail system every time someone submits a form. You can customize who gets notified, what the email says, and route different notifications based on form answers using conditional logic.

For guaranteed email delivery, WPForms works seamlessly with WP Mail SMTP (used on 4+ million sites) to send notifications through trusted providers like Gmail, Outlook, SendGrid, SendLayer, Amazon SES, MailGun and Brevo. This means your form submissions bypass the spam folder and land in the inbox.

You can also set up instant Slack notifications, SMS alerts via Twilio, and automatic sync with Google Sheets, so you never miss a submission regardless of email.

Is WPForms Secure and Privacy Compliant?

Yes, WPForms follows WordPress security best practices and includes proper proper data validation and sanitization to help protect form submissions from common security risks.

For maximum data security and privacy, all form submission data is stored in your own WordPress database, never on third-party servers. WPForms is GDPR and CCPA compliant out of the box.

For payment forms, all transactions are processed with PCI-compliant security. You can also use the Form Locker addon to password-protect forms, restrict access to logged-in members, or set submission limits.

November 5, 2026

Start Your Stripe Subscriptions With a Trial Period

A Stripe subscription sold through WPForms can now begin with a trial period: the subscriber signs up today, and their first payment waits until the trial is over.

The card is collected and checked at signup, held by Stripe, and charged automatically on the day the trial ends.

The length is a number of days, weeks, months, or years, chosen in the form builder’s Payments tab for each Stripe subscription you sell.

Each trial shows up in WPForms » Payments with a Trialing status until that first payment lands.

Payments

October 6, 2026

Paystack Addon

The Paystack addon brings card, bank transfer, and mobile money payments to your forms for buyers in Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, and Kenya.

Buyers click submit and pay in the Paystack popup in naira, cedis, rand, or Kenyan shillings, with no new field to add to your form.

Bank transfer and mobile money payments are saved as Pending, and WPForms can hold your notification emails until Paystack confirms the payment, then completes the order on its own.

Every Paystack payment shows up in WPForms » Payments, and full refunds can be issued without leaving WordPress.
