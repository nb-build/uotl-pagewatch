Get started with Google Business Profile

Manage your notifications

You can choose which notifications you get from your Business Profile. These help you stay informed about:

Account updates: Get notified about changes to your Google Account or Business Profile.

Features and tips: Announcements about new features and suggestions to help you get the most out of your profile.

Customer activity: Alerts when customers leave new reviews or add photos.

Business info alerts: Reminders to keep your business info up to date.

Bookings and requests: Get notified when customers book appointments or send requests.

You might get Business Profile notifications through email, Search, or Maps. Even if you turn off some notifications, you may still get important updates from Google.

Manage Business Profile notifications on Google Search

Go to your Business Profile.

Select More Notifications.

Find the notification you want to update.

Next to the notification, you can turn it on or off.

Find your Business Profile notifications on the Google Maps app

On your device, open the Google Maps app .

At the bottom right, tap Business.

At the top right, tap Notifications .

Tip: To manage your Google Maps app notification settings, learn how to turn Google Maps notifications on or off.

Stop notifications for unverified businesses

If you don't want to get notifications for unverified businesses, remove the claimed Business Profiles. Learn how to remove a Business Profile from your Google Account.

Tip: You'll still get notifications for your verified businesses.
