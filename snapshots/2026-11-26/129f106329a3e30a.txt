F.A.Q.s

What is a client portal?

A client portal is a secure, cloud-based platform that provides access to digital files, services, and information via the internet, available on both desktop and mobile devices.

It streamlines communication through private messaging, integrates CRM for client management, enables secure file sharing, and supports tailored customer experiences to boost retention and minimize churn.

How can I use a client portal?

Manual email chains and repetitive client responses are time-consuming and often clunky.

Enter SuiteDash: a CRM + client portal engineered for seamless, professional client interactions, eliminating the need for multiple tools or costly custom builds.

With SuiteDash, you deliver a polished, flexible experience that saves time and elevates your brand, all from one intuitive platform.

How secure is SuiteDash? Is it HIPAA compliant?

SuiteDash uses administrative, physical and technical safeguards described in its Privacy Policy. Review the Security Overview for account and access settings.

SuiteDash is HIPAA-capable, not HIPAA-by-default. Covered Entities and Business Associates must execute a BAA with SuiteDash before storing, transmitting or processing PHI. Your organization must also configure access and messaging for its workflow. Review HIPAA setup.
