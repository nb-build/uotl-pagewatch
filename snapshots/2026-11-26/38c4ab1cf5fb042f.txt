Duda FAQs

What makes Duda different from other website builders for web agencies?

Duda is built specifically for professionals managing client sites at any scale, not individual DIY users. The platform combines a fast visual editor, white-label branding, granular client and team permissions, and powerful automation tools like APIs and MCP, giving agencies the tools they need to build, launch, and manage many sites efficiently under their own brand.

How does Duda help agencies retain clients long-term?

Duda gives agencies the tools to keep client relationships close and sticky: clients can review, comment on, and request edits to their sites without gaining full ownership, while agencies retain control of the account. Combined with reliable hosting, built-in SEO/AEO optimization, and fast performance, this helps agencies deliver ongoing value that keeps clients renewing.

Does Duda allow agencies to manage multiple sites and domains from one dashboard?

Yes. Duda is designed for managing hundreds or even thousands of client sites from a single, centralized dashboard, with tools like custom templates, reusable elements, and global themes that make building and maintaining many sites efficient.

Does Duda offer white-label solutions for freelancers and agencies?

Yes. Duda supports end-to-end white labeling, letting agencies and freelancers present the entire platform as their own, from the editor interface to client-facing communications, so their brand stays front and center.

Does Duda allow full client management and collaboration?

Yes. Duda includes role-based permissions and collaboration features that let agencies invite team members and clients into the platform. Clients can comment on and review their sites directly, while agencies maintain ownership and control over the final build. Agencies may also elevate their clients’ permissions, allowing them to make edits directly to the website.

How does Duda support recurring revenue and reselling websites?

Duda is built around a recurring-service model: agencies can resell website hosting and management as an ongoing service, supported by white-label branding, client management tools, and automation that reduces the time it takes to build and maintain each site, protecting margins as a client roster grows. Integrated client billing makes this process seamless, enabling agencies to tie the publish status of their clients’ sites to their payment status, automatically.

Is Duda a good website builder for freelancers and growing agencies?

Yes. Duda scales with a business's needs, from a single freelancer managing a handful of client sites to larger agencies running hundreds, or even thousands. Features like white labeling, client permissions, and automation are useful at any size, and become more valuable as a business grows.

What is the learning curve for new users on Duda?

Duda's visual editor is designed to be approachable for new users, with drag-and-drop tools, pre-built templates, and guided workflows that get sites live quickly. Agencies managing larger client rosters can also take advantage of automation and APIs as their needs mature. On review sites, customers praise Duda’s ease-of-use, with the platform consistently leading in G2’s
“Most Implementable,” “Best Usability,” “Easiest Setup,” and “Best Support” categories.

Does Duda offer built-in eCommerce features?

Yes. Duda includes built-in eCommerce tools
for launching high-converting online stores, purpose-built for small and mid-sized businesses that agencies and SaaS platforms serve. Duda’s unique eCommerce architecture lends itself to high-performance in traditional and AI search, and is easily extended via comprehensive REST APIs.

How secure are websites built with Duda?

Duda sites are hosted on enterprise-grade, ultra-reliable AWS infrastructure with built-in security safeguards, and the platform maintains compliance with standards like GDPR, ISO 27001, and SOC2.

Is Duda suitable for SaaS platforms looking for embedded or integrated website building?

Yes. Duda offers an embeddable, integratable website builder
that SaaS companies across verticals, like property management, hospitality, and transportation, use to give their own customers a native website-building experience within their product. Duda’s comprehensive suite of REST APIs allow SaaS platforms to sync data between their systems and Duda’s platform, while editor-level features like Connected Data work together to automatically inject that data into client sites. Custom Widgets enable those same SaaS platforms to embed their core technologies into the website builder directly, as native drag-and-drop elements that can live permanently on Custom Templates, a delivery-method proven to drive product adoption and reduce churn.

Can I migrate my website from WordPress or another platform to Duda?

Duda supports migrating existing websites onto its platform, allowing agencies and businesses to move client sites over while taking advantage of Duda's performance, security, and management tools going forward.

Does Duda support REST APIs and MCP for automating site creation?

Yes. Duda offers REST APIs, MCP server support, and integrations like Zapier that let agencies and platforms create and manage sites programmatically, automating repetitive workflows at scale.

How does Duda's AI help teams launch and manage websites faster?

Duda's AI tools, including its conversational Vibe editor, let teams generate full websites and web applications from a prompt, then refine them through natural conversation. Combined with automation for content upkeep and site creation, this significantly speeds up how quickly teams can launch and maintain client sites. Within the editor, agencies can work alongside the agentic Duda Copilot to complete tasks conversationally like adding elements, redesigning sections, creating pages, analyzing website data, managing client accounts, and more.

What kind of web experiences can I create with Duda Vibe?

Duda Vibe allows users to generate complex, full-stack web applications and custom websites using a chat-first interface. It enables businesses and agencies to go beyond standard layouts, building tailored, data-driven web experiences that scale to meet specific client needs.

What differentiates Duda Vibe from other vibe coding tools?

While many AI coding tools focus solely on generating raw code, Duda Vibe is purpose-built for professionals, integrating seamlessly with Duda's robust ecosystem. Unlike standalone coding tools, Duda Vibe provides built-in client management, end-to-end white labeling to protect your agency’s brand, enterprise-grade security, and automatic SEO-readiness out of the box, ensuring every AI-generated experience is professional, scalable, and ready to perform.
