Dealertrack DMS integrates with Podium

Generate more revenue per customer with the Dealertrack DMS integration with Podium! This connection enhances post-sale engagement by allowing Jerry, your AI employee, to re-engage past customers with personalized reminders, boosting revenue through lease renewals and service reminders. Additionally, they enrich contact data with information from Dealertrack and automate post-sale follow-ups, service reminders, and review invitations, streamlining customer re-engagement.

Post-sale revenue mining: Let Jerry, your AI employee help you re-engage past customers with lease renewals, service reminders, and more.

Rich Contact Data: Contacts augmented with data directly from Dealertrack.

Automate More: Re-engage your past customers with post-sale check-in, service reminders, post-sale and service review invites, and more.

Podium and Dealertrack DMS

Compatibility

All Versions

Setup Process

Step 1: Enable Podium in Dealertrack DMS

• Open the Dealertrack DMS.

• Select “Data Exchange” from the 1st dropdown.

• Select “Opentrack” from the 2nd dropdown.

• In the “Vendor Access” screen, add 4 methods one at a time.

NOTE: Even if you select all the methods simultaneously, it will only add 1 of the 4 methods.

• Methods: Vehicles, Customers, Deals, Service

• Select “Podium” or “PDU” as the vendor.

• Select today’s date as the “Access Begins” date.

• Leave the "Access Ends" date blank.

• Click “Ok.”

• Locate and provide the dealership information (account codes).

Step 2: Connect Dealertrack to Podium by entering your enterprise code, company number, and server name.

Step 3: Select the businesses country

Step 4: Click Finish

Step 5: Enable any automation
