DX1 is the ONLY all-in-one software solution that gives you access to everything you need to manage and market your Powersports, Golf Cart, and OPE dealership. This includes a dealer management system (DMS), lead manager, website & online marketing tools, and more.

Visit DX1 Now

The Dealership Management System (DMS)

DX1’s Dealership Management System (DMS) is built on Microsoft® Azure’s secure, cloud-based servers and provides you access to your DMS from anywhere, anytime, from any internet-enabled device. Log into one location to manage your major units, inventory, accounting, parts & accessories, services, and marketing with minimal clicks. Integrations with top OEMs connect you with preloaded model specifications, photos, descriptions, and free price file updates to maximize your time efficiently.

Lead & Customer Management

The DX1 platform and its unique “Road to the Sale” provides a more efficient sales process for your team. Their color-coded sales process ensures timely follow-up to every lead, so no one falls through the cracks. Use the DX1 Sales Planner to organize each sales task, improving follow-up and your conversion ratio. Effectively track leads and convert them to customers. Integrated SMS/text messaging makes communication fast and easy while saving all transactional history to protect your dealership.

Powersports Websites

All of DX1’s websites are built with a mobile-first mindset allowing all customers to browse your virtual dealership before they buy. The Search Engine Optimization (SEO) DX1 adds into each website revs up your online brand and helps you rise to the top of search results. All websites are designed to stand out in the powersports industry and with DX1’s completely integrated platform your dealership’s website will look great and be easier to manage by simplifying day-to-day tasks so you can focus on your business.

Marketing

DX1’s full suite of marketing applications can get your dealership in front of your customers quickly and easily. Create and schedule promotions or newsletters with drag and drop technology. Upload and distribute your inventory across multiple channels and listings for greater visibility. Managed SEM services are available too.

Service

The enhanced service scheduler makes organizing work much easier. Schedule a service appointment directly from a work estimate or repair order and track your billables. Integrated Fiche, warranty repair orders, and OEM labor rates make updating orders fast. Even text with customers or the front office without leaving the computer.

Parts & Accessories

Bring a new level of insight and efficiency to your parts department through integration with the industry’s OEMs, distributors, and aftermarket companies. DX1 works with these companies and third parties to provide you with free price file updates and real-time parts availability. Order parts directly in the system and email or SMS message the customer when it arrives.

Accounting

Track and reconcile revenue and costs through a QuickBooks® or QuickBooks Online® integration, one of the nation’s most popular accounting software. Monitor accounts receivable, record deposits, and automate journal entries in Quickbooks too. Detailed reports and a time clock manager can help keep your dealership organized & efficient.
