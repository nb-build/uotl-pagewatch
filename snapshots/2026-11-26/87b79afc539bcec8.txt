Dealertrack NADA FAQs

What is Dealertrack showcasing at NADA 2027?

Dealertrack is bringing new innovation to NADA 2027. See what’s ahead with an evolving, next-generation DMS, embedded fraud intelligence to verify buyers, deeper workflow integrations, and more lenders for funding efficiency. Come see how Dealertrack is creating a more connected, tech-forward dealership experience from sales to service.

What can dealers expect from the next generation of Dealertrack DMS?

Built on the foundation of a trusted, proven DMS, Dealertrack is evolving the platform for what comes next. The vision starts with a more intuitive, modern interface and expands to deeper service and parts integrations, intelligent insights, AI-powered capabilities, and scalable enterprise technology designed to provide greater consistency and control across the dealership. At NADA, dealers can get an early look at this evolution and where Dealertrack DMS is headed.

How is Dealertrack changing the way dealerships defend against fraud?

Dealertrack is moving fraud defense inside the deal, embedding fraud detection and ID verification directly into the F&I workflow so dealers can identify risk earlier and move forward with greater confidence. At NADA, see how capabilities including AI-powered fraud-risk alerts and Driver’s License Validation with Selfie Check are helping create a more seamless approach to protecting your deals.

What’s next for Dealertrack F&I?

Dealertrack is advancing F&I around two things dealers need more of: greater intelligence and greater efficiency. Fraud-risk intelligence and identity verification are being built deeper into the deal, while aftermarket products, menu and contracts, and Vitu registration and title are becoming more seamlessly integrated into the F&I workflow. The result is fewer disconnected steps and a more efficient path from credit application through contracting and funding. At NADA, dealers can see how Dealertrack is bringing more of the deal together in one connected experience.

How do Dealertrack DMS and F&I work together?

Dealertrack connects the operational and transactional sides of the dealership so information and workflows can move more seamlessly across the business. As DMS and F&I continue to evolve, deeper connections across sales, financing, service, parts and accounting can help reduce duplicate work, improve efficiency and create a more unified experience for dealership teams.

How does Dealertrack connect with other Cox Automotive solutions?

Dealertrack’s connections across Cox Automotive help carry customer, vehicle, deal and service information farther through the retail journey. Deeper integrations with solutions including VinSolutions and Xtime are creating opportunities to reduce duplicate work and connect workflows across sales, F&I and service, while Dealertrack’s Opentrack integration platform gives dealers flexibility to connect the technology their business relies on.

I’m already a Dealertrack client. What can I discover at NADA 2027?

NADA is an opportunity to explore what’s next for the Dealertrack solutions you use today, from evolving DMS experiences and deeper integrations to new approaches to fraud defense and finance efficiency. Meet with our team to talk through your dealership’s workflows, see what’s new, and explore opportunities to get even more from Dealertrack.

How can I request a Dealertrack demo at NADA 2027?

Ready to see what’s ahead? Request a demo using the form on this page and select the products you’re interested in. Our team will focus the conversation on your dealership’s priorities and the solutions you’re most interested in exploring.
