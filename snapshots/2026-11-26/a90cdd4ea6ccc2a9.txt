Roofing Sales Software & CRM Built for Canvassers and Contractors

Roofing deals don't end at the signature—they move from a roof inspection and storm claim through EagleView takeoffs, material orders, crew scheduling, and ACV/RCV depreciation checks. Knockio connects your door-to-door roofing sales tracking directly to your back-office operations, giving storm restoration and retail replacement contractors one platform to sell, build, and collect.
Schedule a Demo

What should roofing contractors look for in a CRM and sales platform?

A true roofing project management software carries the job from the first knock to the final lien waiver. That means complete property records, automated territory cutting by ZIP and city, a pipeline matching roofing stages, EagleView, Hover, or GAF QuickMeasure numbers entered on the estimate, work orders with shingle orders, e-contracts, and deductible payment collection. Knockio handles all of it in one platform—adding live GPS tracking of canvassers and crews so your job history lives in a single record.

Quick summary

Why Roofing Teams Choose Knockio

Knockio is a roofing contractor software built to manage customer records, inspections, crews, production, work orders, inventory, and payments. From the first knock to the completed roof, it all sits in one connected platform. It is a complete roofing management software that allows owners to run sales and production without the confusion of two systems that never quite agree.

Manage Jobs

Customer & Property Records in One Place

Every job record holds the address, contacts, roof age, storm dates, insurance claim numbers, photos, notes, and full activity history. When a homeowner calls back about a leak or supplement, your team sees the whole story instead of digging through group texts.

Dynamic Job Pipeline for Roofing Stages

Move jobs through clear stages: Lead, Inspection, Contingency Signed, Claim Filed, Adjuster Meeting, Approved Scope, Build Scheduled, and RCV Released. Fully customize statuses to match your exact storm restoration or retail replacement workflow.

Crew & Roofing Production Scheduling

Assign tear-off and install crews to jobs, track production progress closely, and keep the office and the field reading one shared calendar. Everyone sees who is on which roof next without double-booking crews.

Track the Field

Auto Territory Creation by ZIP & City

Build canvassing territories automatically from ZIP codes and cities instead of drawing boundaries by hand. After a hail or wind storm, carve up an affected neighborhood and get reps knocking doors in minutes.

Live GPS Tracking of Every Roofing Rep

See where every canvasser, project manager, and crew is in real time. Pin-drop verification confirms exactly how close a rep stood to an address when marking a door worked, improving field accountability and safety.

Route Planning That Saves Daylight

Plan tighter, smarter routes so your roofing reps hit far more doors in less time each day—eliminating crisscrossing across the same blocks and saving valuable daylight hours after a storm.

Work Orders & Materials

Work Orders With Takeoffs & Budgets

Build a work order with tasks, labor budgets, and shingle/flashing takeoffs for every roof. Track the job against real numbers—not a rough estimate—so the office sees exactly what was ordered and what it cost.

Purchase Orders When Shingles Run Short

When shingles, underlayment, or ridge vents run short, fire off a purchase order for your supplier such as ABC Supply, Beacon, or SRS. Knockio tracks SKUs and flags low stock before your crew runs out mid-job.

Assign Jobs to Crews or Subcontractors

Route work orders directly to your in-house crew or a trusted 1099 subcontractor. Attach aerial measurements, roof photos, and schedules while stripping out customer financial markup.

Documents & Media

Job Photos & Roof Media in One Place

Upload timestamped before, during, and after photos straight to the job. Insurance adjusters, homeowners, and your own crew get the full visual record in one spot instead of scattered across personal phones.

E-Contracts With Legal Protection

Send contingency agreements, build contracts, and Direction to Pay forms for digital signature with IP timestamp logging. Add the mandatory FTC 3-Day Notice of Cancellation to your contract templates for door-to-door sales.

Centralized File Storage Per Job

Keep insurance scopes (Xactimate ESX files), municipal building permits, material warranties, and lien waivers attached right to the job record so nothing gets lost when an insurance supplement is filed.

Quote & Get Paid

Roof Estimates From Real Measurement Data

Build clean, accurate estimates using measurements from your EagleView, or entered from your Hover or GAF QuickMeasure report. Add optional line items like upgraded ventilation or impact-resistant shingles from a pre-saved product catalog.

Digital Proposals & Bilateral Change Orders

Send proposals that homeowners review and e-sign from their phone, and present the financing options you offer. Mid-job decking repairs or wood replacements are handled via dual-signature change orders before extra work proceeds.

Collect Deductibles on Site

Collect insurance deductibles, ACV material deposits, and final RCV draws on the spot or remotely via Text-to-Pay, card, or ACH—keeping cash flow moving without waiting on a check in the mail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Roofers Ask Before Switching to Knockio

Switching software is a real decision when your whole pipeline, crew schedule, and customer history lives inside it. Here are the questions roofing contractors ask most before making the move.

Can Knockio keep all my job history in one record?

Yes. As a complete roofing contractor CRM, Knockio ensures every job record holds the property's contacts, photos, notes, documents, and full activity history, so anyone on your team sees the whole story instead of piecing it together from texts and email.

Can Knockio create canvassing territories automatically?

Yes. Our built-in roofing canvassing app can build territories automatically from ZIP codes and cities, so you can split up a storm-hit area and get reps knocking fast, without drawing every boundary by hand.

Can Knockio build estimates from EagleView measurement reports?

Yes. Knockio lets you create roof estimates using measurements from your EagleView, or entered from your Hover or GAF QuickMeasure report, so pricing is based on accurate roof data instead of a rough guess.

Does Knockio handle work orders and materials for production?

Yes. Functioning as a full roofing project management software, Knockio ensures work orders carry tasks, a budget, and a material takeoff. You can even raise a purchase order straight from the work order when materials run short, with low-stock alerts to warn you first.

How is Knockio different from AccuLynx, JobNimbus, or Jobber for roofing companies?

AccuLynx and JobNimbus are built mainly for production and job management, while Jobber focuses on basic residential scheduling. Knockio gives you estimating, work orders, and invoicing, but natively adds live rep tracking and automated territory creation for your sales team. It is one connected system built around the way a roofing job actually runs, from the first knock to the final payment.
