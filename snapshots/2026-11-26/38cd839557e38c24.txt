Definition

What is Solar Contractor Financing?

Solar contractor financing lets solar professionals offer homeowners monthly payment options at the point of sale instead of asking for the full project cost upfront. With Hearth, the homeowner sees personalized offers from an 18-lender marketplace after a soft credit pull, and the contractor pays no per-loan dealer fee.

Solar is one of the largest tickets in home improvement, and the sale collapses the moment the homeowner has to think about the gross price instead of a monthly number. Single-lender solar programs also leave credit-marginal buyers with exactly one no.

Solar buyers expect financing by default. A marketplace with multiple lenders — rather than one captive solar fintech — means more approvals across the credit spectrum and no dealer fee baked into your bid.

Among Hearth contractors who use both, jobs won with financing offered average 15%+ larger than jobs won without (median across 801 contractors, trailing 24 months).

FAQ

Solar Contractor Financing FAQ

How much does solar contractor financing cost?

Most companies invest between $2,000–6,000/yr depending on the level of service, with no expensive per-loan dealer fees — letting you keep more of your hard-earned cash. The homeowner pays their lender directly; rates start at 7.99% APR depending on credit.

Can my customers with lower credit scores still get offers?

Hearth's lender marketplace serves credit scores down to 550 FICO. Each homeowner sees personalized offers after a soft credit pull that doesn't affect their score.

How do solar contractors get paid through Hearth?

Get paid on your schedule. One of our lending partners funds your customer — you're paid in full, and they pay over time. No waiting on a financing company's draw schedule.

What's the minimum job size for solar financing?

Hearth financing covers projects from $1,000 up to $250,000 — from a panel addition to a full system with storage.
