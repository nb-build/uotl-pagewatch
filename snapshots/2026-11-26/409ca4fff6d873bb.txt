AccuLynx’s Spring 2026 Product Updates

Publish date: March 31, 2026

Estimated reading time: 4 minutes

Product Updates

Last Updated On November 18, 2026

Key Takeaways

AccuLynx’s latest updates streamline workflows for roofing contractors by introducing custom fields, outcome-based appointment tracking, and improved estimation tools.

Users can now create custom fields for data capture and accuracy.

The new appointment tracking feature allows users to record specific outcomes for better performance insights.

Updates to the estimation tool offer more pricing options and allow for labor cost calculations based on roof pitch.

At AccuLynx, we are constantly listening to the needs of roofing contractors to ensure our software evolves with your roofing business. Our latest advancements are designed to save you more time and maximize your output. With our latest updates, you’ll be able to create and manage custom fields, track outcomes of calendar appointments, see improvements with the estimation tool, and much more.

Here’s a look at the latest enhancements in AccuLynx:

Capturing unique data points with custom fields

Tracking appointment outcomes to boost performance insights

Providing more flexibility with the estimation tool

Reducing errors and simplifying record creation with lead form redesign

Capturing unique data points with custom fields

AccuLynx is expanding the way customers can capture and manage data points. Previously, customers were limited to the system default fields. Now, you can create and manage your own custom fields for both contacts and jobs using the new Custom Fields Manager in your account settings, giving you full control over how data is structured and displayed.

Flexible placement and access: Custom fields can be added exactly where you need them, such as the contact form, lead form, job overview page, and in reports. Custom fields can be accessed and edited through your Account Settings, the AccuLynx API, and Hubspot or Spotio using our AppConnections add-on.

Data systematization: Maintain better data hygiene by storing all custom data in AccuLynx, leading to consistent data capture and more accurate reports.

Tracking appointment outcomes to boost performance insights

To help managers gain better visibility, AccuLynx now allows users to record and track specific outcomes of calendar appointments. You can create custom outcomes in your settings to easily categorize every meeting. These outcomes can be analyzed using the Appointments Report, enabling teams to easily identify and plan next steps.

Providing more flexibility with the estimation tool

AccuLynx’s estimation tools have received a series of updates enabling users to easily offer homeowners more pricing options and calculate precise labor costs.

The estimation tools have been enhanced with:

Smart(er) Docs updates: Users can now utilize Smart Fields to pull data from multiple estimates rather than just the primary one, allowing you to create “Good, Better, Best” pricing.

The ability to offer a true lump sum estimate: You can hide or remove the tax line item from displaying on an estimate for a lump sum presentation.

Pitch-based labor calculations: Users can set their templates to automatically calculate labor rates based on the pitch of a roof.

Reducing errors and simplifying record creation with lead form redesign

The lead form has been revamped to simplify the record creation process and increase data precision. These updates increase efficiency and control by allowing users to schedule appointments faster and copy select data between records.

The benefits of the lead form updates include:

A more modernized interface: Enjoy a cleaner layout for contact and project location data, along with “possible matches results” viewing to improve the cleanliness of your database.

Integrated custom data: Newly created custom fields are now integrated directly into the lead record.

Comparative scheduling: When scheduling appointments, multiple calendars can now be viewed side-by-side.

Easier data management: Transfer information between records faster with the ability to copy select data from one record to another.

Start using AccuLynx’s latest updates today

If you’d like to see how these updates can transform your roofing business, schedule a demo of AccuLynx today!
