July 2026 · reported Price increase

DeepSeek V4

DeepSeek roughly triples V4 pricing and moves to peak/off-peak billing

DeepSeek V4 Flash went from $0.14 in / $0.28 out to $0.44 in / $1.32 out per 1M at peak, and V4 Pro from $0.435 / $0.87 to $1.32 / $3.96 — output rates rose about 4.6x. DeepSeek also introduced time-of-day billing: peak is 01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC, with off-peak (the other 17 hours) at half price. Cache-hit input rose in step, taking V4 Pro's cached discount from roughly 0.8% of its input rate to about 3.3%. DeepSeek remains cheap by frontier-lab standards, but it is no longer an order of magnitude below the field.

July 2026 · reported Price cut

OpenAI GPT-5.6

OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 Luna five-fold and drops Terra by 20%

GPT-5.6 Luna fell from $1.00 in / $6.00 out to $0.20 / $1.20 per 1M — a five-fold cut that moves current-generation OpenAI quality into budget-tier pricing. GPT-5.6 Terra dropped from $2.50 / $15.00 to $2.00 / $12.00. Sol, GPT-5.4 Mini and GPT-5.4 Nano were unchanged. Price cuts are rare enough in this category to be worth noting: most 2026 movement has been upward.

June 16, 2026 Price increase

MailerLite

MailerLite raises prices across every tier and cuts its free plan to 250 subscribers

New pricing took effect June 16, 2026: paid plans rose roughly 10–30% across subscriber tiers, the free plan now covers 250 subscribers (down from 1,000), and the free sending cap dropped to 2,500 emails/month. MailerLite remains one of the cheapest full-featured platforms — but the gap narrowed.

Kit (ConvertKit)

Kit's free Newsletter plan now caps at 1,000 subscribers

Kit's pricing page now lists the free plan at up to 1,000 subscribers — many 2026 reviews still cite the older 10,000-subscriber free tier, which no longer appears for new signups. The Creator plan starts at $39/mo at 1,000 subscribers.

Early 2026 · reported Free tier cut

Mailchimp

Mailchimp's free plan shrinks to 250 contacts

The free tier now covers 250 contacts (down from 500) with 1,000 sends/month — and total contacts, including unsubscribed, count toward the limit. Mailchimp remains the priciest mainstream option as lists grow.

2026 · reported Free tier cut

GetResponse

GetResponse drops its permanent free plan

GetResponse no longer offers a free-forever plan — new users get a 14-day trial, then must pick a paid tier (Starter from $19/mo at 1,000 contacts). It stays one of the best-value paid options at larger list sizes.

December 2, 2025 Plan change

Flodesk

Flodesk retires its famous flat-rate unlimited plan

Flodesk's $38/mo unlimited-subscribers plan — its signature differentiator — closed to new customers on December 2, 2025 (existing subscribers were grandfathered). New pricing scales by subscriber count like the rest of the market: Lite from $19/mo at 1,000 subscribers, up to $149/mo at 25,000.

2025 · reported Free tier cut

SendGrid

SendGrid replaces its free plan with a 60-day trial

The long-running free tier (100 emails/day forever) was retired in favour of a 60-day trial — after it ends, you must move to a paid plan (Essentials from ~$20/mo). Developers wanting a permanent free tier now look to Brevo (300/day) or Resend (3,000/month).
