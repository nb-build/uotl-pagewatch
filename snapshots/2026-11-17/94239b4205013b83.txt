Frequently Asked Questions

Will it fit the way our business works?
+

Estimate Rocket serves residential and commercial service contractors across trades. In a demo, walk through how you estimate, schedule, manage jobs, and invoice so you can see where it fits your business.

Will our crew be able to use it in the field?
+

Estimate Rocket is a mobile-friendly web app for phones, tablets, and laptops. Team members can view schedules, add job photos and notes, and track time. Training is available to help them learn the tools they’ll use.

Do we have to replace QuickBooks?
+

No. You can keep QuickBooks Online for accounting and connect it with Estimate Rocket. Other integrations include Zapier, CompanyCam, and YouCanBookMe. Ask the team which connections fit your current setup.

How do we get set up and train the team?
+

Free setup and team training are included. Get help with your workflow and proposal templates, and work with the team on the information you’ll need, such as labor rates and material costs. Discuss your existing records during the demo to plan the next steps.

What if we handle longer, multi-phase jobs?+
Estimate Rocket handles everyday job and crew scheduling. The JobRocket add-on provides Gantt scheduling and capacity planning for longer, multi-phase work. Ask to see it if that matches the projects you take on.
