Simplify Your Fence Company Operations with TRUE

TRUE is a cloud-based construction management system built for fence contractors like you. Our customizable solution combines all the tools you need—CRM, drawing tools, estimates, jobs, schedules, inventory, invoicing, job cost accounting, and more—into one user-friendly platform.

CRM / PROPOSALS

Manage your company’s sales leads and opportunities. Assign prospects to your sales team for follow-up, and allow them to track all their activities and notes.

SCHEDULING

Simplify complex scheduling issues with auto-assignment, email and text field notifications, dispatch mapping, certification tracking and more.

PROJECTS

Fully manage all residential and commercial projects with quotes, eSign, work tickets, invoicing, expenses, equipment tracking and inventory management.

PRODUCT CATALOG

Take charge of your products with this add-ons easy to use product catalog. Add and modify products with each vendor update without having to manage multiple spreadsheets. Product updates are processed in real-time so your estimation team always has the most up-to-date product pricing.

MOBILE DRAWING

Take your estimates on the road with the ability to process a full quote while standing on the job-site. Easily draw the job-site details and instantly receive your total project measurements. Compatible with tablets and laptops, your estimation team can create drawings and quotes quickly and efficiently without having to drive back to the office.

PRODUCT COMPONENT PARTS

Increase your quote accuracy. Tie frequently associated products together within your product catalog to ensure the correct products are chosen every time.

EASY PRODUCT SELECTIONS

Once your drawing is made, painlessly select each product you would like to display on a quote. Need more quote selection options? No problem. Create multiple selections to allow your client to choose their preferred fence type.

QUOTE OPTIONS

Allow your team to provide the client with all available product options when building their fence. The Fence Estimation add-on will allow you to show potential product options, like fence color, right when a product is selected for a quote. No additional pricing checks required.

ON-SITE SALES TOOLS

Allow your client to view images and descriptions of your product options while standing on the job-site creating their new quote. Compose each product sales description so you no longer have to show the client materials provided by your vendors.

TRUE INVENTORY

Generate detailed material lists for each fence project using the TRUE Fence App’s estimating capabilities. The material lists integrate seamlessly with TRUE to create associated inventory items you can track and manage over time.

With the out-of-box functionality of Inventory where materials are received into stock, barcode and scan them into multiple warehouse locations within TRUE. Get real-time visibility into on-hand inventory amounts across your operation.

TRUE & QuickBooks

Prevent double entry and instantly push and pull data to QuickBooks with TRUE. Integration with QuickBooks Online, Pro, Premier, Contractor, Enterprise, and more. Sync customers, vendors, products, taxes, terms, down payments, invoices, pay apps, vendor bills, expenses, and payments.

WORK TICKETS

When materials need to be pulled for a fence installation job, create integrated work tickets in TRUE. As materials are used from inventory, the costs are automatically job costed to ensure accurate project accounting.

REAL-TIME BUSINESS ANALYTICS

With data integrated across inventory, purchasing, projects, and accounting, our ERP system provides real-time analytics and reports to help you run your fencing business more efficiently.
