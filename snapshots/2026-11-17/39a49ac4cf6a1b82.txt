What med spa operations actually demand

A med spa is two businesses stapled together. One half is hospitality: rebooking, memberships, packages, gift cards, a front desk that answers. The other half is clinical: injection maps and lot numbers, consents that survive a subpoena, and a record that ties money to the visit.

Six things decide whether software works here:

Memberships and packages. Monthly dues, prepaid series such as six IVs, and balances that draw down cleanly at checkout.

Recall and rebooking. Neurotoxin wears off on a schedule, so something has to surface the patient at the right week.

Consents. Per-visit, per-procedure, versioned, signed, and exportable when someone asks.

Before-and-after documentation. Standardized angles and images tied to the treatment record.

Payments. Card, HSA and FSA, deposits, payment plans, text-to-pay, and readable processing rates.

Missed-call recovery. An unanswered call at 6:40pm is usually a booking that goes to the clinic down the street.

Where each one fits

Ona. Built for practices that treat the med spa as a clinical operation. Consents are versioned, immutable once signed and exportable as an audit PDF, with per-visit or annual expiry. Memberships, prepaid series, gift cards and payment plans run out of billing, and invoices generate from the signed note. E-prescribing runs on embedded DoseSpot. The AI receptionist picks up 24/7 and escalates anything urgent to a human. More on the med spa and aesthetics page.

Boulevard. The strongest choice when front-of-house experience is the product. Pricing is public and per-location: Essentials $176/mo, Premier $293/mo, Prestige $410/mo, currently $140, $234 and $328 on a limited-time offer for new customers, with a forms add-on from $65/mo per location. Choose Boulevard if self-booking polish matters more than a deep clinical record.

Zenoti. Choose Zenoti if you run a chain. It is built for salon, spa, medspa and fitness estates, with multi-location management, marketing and an AI receptionist. It publishes no pricing, so budget for a sales cycle.

Aesthetic Record. Aesthetics-native and inexpensive per seat: $15 per user per month on Essentials or $19 on Accelerator, both with a $399 startup fee, plus ChartSmart AI from $30/mo and cloud e-prescribing $40 per prescriber, $31 on an annual commitment. If before-and-after photo and video management is the center of your chart, choose it over Ona.

PatientNow. Aimed at aesthetic practices where marketing and imaging are inseparable from care, across med spas, plastic surgery and dermatology: RxPhoto imaging, consent packets, memberships and loyalty, Recura AI for calls. Pricing is not published.

Mangomint. Clean, well-priced spa operations software at $120/mo plus $10 per user, with memberships, packages, forms and charting in the base plan and free onboarding. If your med spa is closer to a spa than a clinic, Mangomint is the better value.

Nextech. Choose Nextech if you are a larger dermatology or plastic surgery group with a med spa arm needing specialty EHR plus RCM in one place. It is heavier than a two-injector clinic needs.

Where Ona is not the right answer

Being fair here saves everyone a demo. Ona ships no dedicated before-and-after imaging module: images and documents attach to the record, but there is no side-by-side comparison or standardized capture workflow. Ona's nineteen tools do not include retail inventory, so a busy skincare shelf needs something alongside it. Scheduling has no per-room or per-device rules, and recall runs through the leads pipeline, tasks and the shared inbox rather than automated SMS cadences. If any of those three is your number one problem, buy the tool that solves it.

FAQ

What is the best med spa software?

There is no single winner. For a med spa running a medical model, meaning injectables, weight management, IV therapy or hormone care, Ona covers charting, consents, memberships, payments and an AI receptionist on one record. For multi-location chains with heavy retail, Zenoti and Nextech fit better. If before-and-after imaging is the center of the chart, Aesthetic Record and PatientNow are purpose-built for that.

Does Ona handle med spa memberships and packages?

Yes. Ona's billing supports recurring membership billing, treatment packages and series such as six IVs or four acupuncture visits, gift cards with balance tracking, and payment plans with auto-charge and automatic failed-card retries. Payments run on Stripe with pass-through rates, and Ona takes no cut of your payments.

Can med spa software answer the phone after hours?

Some of it can. Ona's AI receptionist answers calls 24/7, books against your live calendar, escalates urgent or sensitive calls to a human, and writes a transcript into the chart. It costs $100 per month for 400 included minutes, up to $400 for 1,500, with $0.20 per minute beyond the tier. Zenoti, Boulevard and PatientNow also publish AI call-answering products on their own sites.

Does Ona do before-and-after photo documentation?

Not as a dedicated imaging module. Staff and patients can upload documents and images onto the patient record, but Ona does not ship side-by-side photo comparison, standardized capture angles or an imaging gallery today. If before-and-after imaging is the core of your chart, Aesthetic Record and PatientNow, which uses RxPhoto, are built for exactly that.

How much does med spa software cost?

Published list prices vary widely. Ona charges $130 per practitioner seat and $45 per staff seat per month, with every feature on every plan. Mangomint publishes $120 per month plus $10 per user. Boulevard publishes Essentials at $176 per month per location, currently $140 on a limited-time offer for new customers. Aesthetic Record publishes $15 per user per month on Essentials plus a $399 startup fee. Zenoti, PatientNow and Nextech do not publish pricing.

How long does it take to switch med spa software?

Ona migrates your data from any EHR or practice management system at no cost, typically within one business day, with full cutover usually taking one to three weeks for smaller practices. There are no setup fees and no long-term contract, and you can run a 14-day free trial first with no credit card.
