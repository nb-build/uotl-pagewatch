Top 10 Practice Management Software

Edition: Q2 2026
Updated: May 13, 2026
Methodology: Peer vote + editorial review
Category: Software & Technology

The Index — At a Glance

The top 10 practice management software for 2026, ranked by DentalTop10's editorial review combined with peer feedback from verified dental professionals. The top three are Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft. The complete ranking covers the 10 leading products and providers in the practice management software category, with notes on positioning, manufacturer, and best-fit use case for each.

01

Server-based with cloud overlay

Dentrix

Henry Schein's flagship — the most widely deployed practice management suite in North American dentistry.

Provider: Henry Schein One
Best for: Best for established multi-doctor practices.

02

On-prem / self-hosted

Open Dental

Open-source pioneer with the strongest customization ecosystem and rapidly growing market share.

Provider: Open Dental Software
Best for: Best for tech-forward practices and DSOs.

03

Server-based

Eaglesoft

Patterson Dental's longtime PMS — deep integration with Patterson equipment and imaging.

Provider: Patterson Dental
Best for: Best for practices already in the Patterson ecosystem.

04

Fully cloud

Curve Dental

Cloud-native PMS that pioneered browser-based dentistry and remains the category leader.

Provider: Curve Dental
Best for: Best for new practices going cloud-first.

05

Cloud

Denticon

Planet DDS's multi-location platform — the dominant choice for DSOs and group practices.

Provider: Planet DDS
Best for: Best for groups with 5+ locations.

06

Server-based

Carestream PracticeWorks

Integrated PMS + imaging from Carestream Dental — strong for general practices.

Provider: Carestream Dental
Best for: Best for practices using CS imaging hardware.

07

Cloud

tab32

Cloud platform with built-in patient communication, payments, and analytics in one stack.

Provider: tab32
Best for: Best for practices wanting all-in-one.

08

Cloud

Dentrix Ascend

Cloud rebuild of Dentrix — Henry Schein's answer to browser-based PMS demand.

Provider: Henry Schein One
Best for: Best for Dentrix users wanting cloud.

09

Cloud

CareStack

Multi-location cloud PMS with built-in patient communication and online scheduling.

Provider: CareStack
Best for: Best for growing 2–20 location groups.

10

Cloud

Oryx

AI-forward cloud PMS with integrated clinical AI and modern UX, popular with newer practices.

Provider: Oryx Dental Software
Best for: Best for solo practices that prioritize design and AI.
