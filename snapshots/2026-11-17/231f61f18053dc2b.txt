What is a cloud phone system?

A cloud phone system is a business phone service that runs over the internet from the provider's data centers instead of on PBX hardware in your office, so your team can make and take calls on a desk phone, laptop or mobile app. Cloud VoIP, hosted VoIP and cloud PBX are other names for the same thing. The modern cloud phone stack covers inbound routing, outbound dialing, SMS, voicemail, and usually a video or team chat layer bundled as UCaaS (unified communications as a service).

Several tools on this list now include some form of AI call handling, which changes what a cloud phone system can do when no one is free to answer.

How does SIP trunking connect an AI voice agent to a cloud phone system?

SIP trunking is a way to carry business phone calls over the internet between a carrier, a cloud phone system and other services such as AI voice agents.

It lets you keep your numbers and your cloud phone system while an AI agent answers some or all of your calls. Your team keeps its extensions, apps and desk phones.

Retell AI connects over SIP to Twilio, Vonage, Telnyx or any SIP carrier. Retell does not charge for telephony when you use your own SIP trunk, according to its pricing page.

A typical setup takes three steps:

Route your main line, an overflow queue or after-hours calls from your cloud phone system to the agent.

Connect the agent to your calendar and CRM through function calling and webhooks.

Use warm transfer to send live calls back to an extension or queue with a summary first.

Some cloud phone systems also support bring your own carrier. Zoom, for example, offers a Zoom Phone Pro license for Bring-Your-Own-Carrier setups.

How we compared these systems

Total Cost After Hidden Fees

The per-user sticker price is almost never the real bill. I checked each platform's invoice structure for line fees, SMS overage rates, regulatory surcharges, and AI add-on pricing. RingCentral invoices typically land 20 to 30% above the advertised price once fees apply, which changes the math at scale.

Call Handling Beyond Routing

For the 2026 market, "does the phone ring the right seat" is table stakes. The real question is what the system does when nobody picks up. Missed calls rarely call back, so I weighted after-hours automation and AI call handling heavily.

SMS Caps and Messaging Limits

SMS caps burned more teams in my interviews than any other hidden cost. A 25-texts-per-user cap on a Core plan is not a theoretical limit for support teams; it is a first-week overage charge. I documented each plan's base cap and overage rate.

Integration Depth, Not Integration Count

Every platform claims Salesforce and HubSpot integration. What matters is whether the integration logs calls to the right record automatically, syncs custom fields, and triggers workflows, or whether it is a CTI click-to-dial button with nothing behind it. I tested the real sync behavior, not the logo count.

What do businesses use a cloud phone system for?

24/7 after-hours call answering: Callers who reach voicemail often hang up without leaving a message. An AI voice agent layered onto your cloud phone system as an AI answering service converts those missed calls into booked appointments without adding headcount.

Appointment booking with live calendar sync: Healthcare, home services, and legal practices lose high-value appointments to phone tag. Real-time function calling to Cal.com, Google Calendar, or an EHR lets a voice agent book appointments during the call instead of promising a callback.

Replacing legacy IVR menus: "Press 1 for billing" routes calls; it does not handle them. A modern AI IVR understands intent, holds a conversation, and routes based on what the caller actually needs. That upgrade keeps callers who would otherwise give up on a menu tree.

Outbound sales dialing at scale: Traditional cloud phones handle one call per user per minute. Batch calling infrastructure handles thousands of calls per hour with branded caller ID, lead qualification, and automatic CRM updates, the difference between a 200-dial-per-day rep and a campaign that runs overnight.

Inbound lead qualification before human handoff: Inbound leads cool fast, and routing every call to a human BDR wastes time on unqualified traffic. Lead qualification by a voice agent ahead of warm transfer routes only ready-to-buy callers to sales.

Warm transfer settings in Retell. A whisper debrief briefs the person picking up without the caller hearing it.
Multi-location receptionist coverage: Small offices and multi-site service businesses often staff a receptionist at every site. A single voice agent covers every location, routes to the right person, books appointments, and forwards urgent calls for a fraction of the cost.

What can go wrong with a cloud phone system?

Hidden fees that break budget models: Nearly every traditional UCaaS vendor publishes a per-user price that omits line fees, SMS overage, regulatory charges, and AI add-ons. Real invoices commonly land 20 to 30% above quote, and cancellation friction is the single biggest complaint across customer reviews on Trustpilot.

SMS caps disguised as unlimited plans: Base tiers from RingCentral (25 SMS/user), Ooma (250/account), and Vonage all cap text messaging in ways that are invisible until the first overage bill. Compliance and Campaign Registry approval delays can also block SMS launches for weeks.

AI features sold as separate subscriptions: Most UCaaS vendors charge $39 to $60 per user per month in add-ons for AI receptionist, conversation intelligence, or transcription. This stacks on top of the base license, doubling the per-seat cost once AI is included.

Compliance certifications that sound the same but are not: SOC 2 Type I differs materially from Type II. HIPAA BAA availability varies by plan and sometimes by add-on. Buyers in healthcare and finance need to confirm the specific certification and whether it is included on their chosen tier.

Scaling math that favors automation: Once call volume crosses a certain threshold, adding seats to a UCaaS plan costs more than automating the call itself.

Cloud phone system FAQ

Can a cloud phone system handle calls without adding more users?

Traditional UCaaS platforms (RingCentral, Nextiva, Dialpad) price per seat, so scaling call volume means scaling licenses. AI voice agents bill per minute, from $0.07 per minute, and one agent handles many concurrent calls without additional seats, which is why high-volume teams pair a cloud phone system with an AI call bot for overflow.

What is the cheapest cloud phone system in 2026?

Nextiva NEXT Core, Dialpad Standard and Quo Starter share the lowest published entry price at $15/user/mo billed annually. Zoom Phone's US and Canada Unlimited plan follows at $16/user/mo.

If most of your volume is inbound calls rather than internal calling, a pay-as-you-go AI voice agent from $0.07 per minute can cost less than adding seats.

Which cloud phone system has the best SMS coverage without overage fees?

Nextiva, Dialpad, and Quo include functional SMS without hard caps on their base plans. RingCentral Core caps SMS at 25/user/mo, low enough that most support teams hit overages within the first week, and even the Ultra plan caps at 200 per user.

Is Retell AI a replacement for RingCentral or Nextiva?

Not directly. Retell AI handles the calls; a traditional cloud phone system routes them between human users and manages internal communication. Many businesses run Retell AI alongside their existing UCaaS for call center automation and after-hours coverage, keeping the UCaaS layer for internal calling and video.

How long does number porting take between cloud phone systems?

Porting a US business number typically takes 1 to 3 weeks depending on the losing carrier. Some providers lock numbers or require formal LOA forms. Call forwarding from the old system to a new provider can route calls immediately while the port is processed, avoiding downtime.

Which cloud phone system is best for HIPAA-compliant healthcare calls?

Nextiva, RingCentral, and Retell AI all offer HIPAA BAAs. Retell AI includes a self-service BAA portal and HIPAA compliance in its base tier, while most UCaaS vendors require a specific plan or enterprise agreement. Pine Park Health uses Retell AI for patient scheduling and reports a 38% increase in scheduling NPS.

What concurrent call capacity do cloud phone systems support?

Per-seat UCaaS plans scale concurrency with license count, one user typically supports one to two concurrent calls. Retell AI includes 20 free concurrent calls on every account with custom enterprise concurrency, which is why high-volume inbound operations and outbound campaigns end up pairing the two.

What is the difference between a cloud phone system and VoIP?

VoIP is the technology that carries a call over the internet. A cloud phone system is the full business service built on it: numbers, routing, voicemail, SMS, apps and admin, run by a provider in the cloud. For a side-by-side of business VoIP providers, see our VoIP guide.

How do I choose the best cloud phone system for my business?

Start with three questions:

1. What is your real bottleneck: routing calls or handling them? If your team needs better internal communication, video meetings, and CRM-connected calling, a UCaaS platform like RingCentral, Nextiva, or Dialpad fits. If your bottleneck is unanswered calls, after-hours coverage, or high call volume that exceeds your team's capacity, an AI voice agent platform like Retell AI solves a different problem entirely.

2. How many users and what is your monthly budget? Per-seat UCaaS pricing ranges from $15/user/mo (Nextiva NEXT Core, Dialpad Standard or Quo Starter) to $75/user/mo (Nextiva NEXT Scale). Multiply by your headcount and add 20-30% for hidden fees, SMS overages, and AI add-ons. If the number is uncomfortable, compare against pay-per-minute AI agents from $0.07 per minute.

3. What integrations are non-negotiable? If your team lives in Salesforce or HubSpot, CRM integration depth matters more than price, RingCentral leads here. If you need international calling, 8x8's 48-country unlimited plans are hard to beat. If you need HIPAA compliance, confirm BAA availability on your chosen tier, not just the enterprise plan. Avoid choosing based on the feature comparison chart alone. Test call quality on your actual internet connection, check the real invoice (not the advertised price), and confirm that the features you need aren't gated behind a higher tier or paid add-on.

What is the best cloud phone system for remote and distributed teams?

For remote teams, the most important factors are mobile app quality, call quality on variable internet connections, and collaboration features that work across time zones.

For under-10 remote teams: Quo (formerly OpenPhone) at $15/user/mo offers shared inboxes, the Sona AI agent for missed calls, and a clean mobile app, the simplest option for small distributed teams.

For 10-50 remote teams needing CRM integration: RingCentral's Advanced plan ($25/user/mo) connects to 300+ apps and provides 99.999% uptime. Dialpad ($15/user/mo Standard) offers superior AI transcription that helps remote managers review calls without listening to full recordings.

For international teams across 5+ countries: 8x8's advanced plans include unlimited calling to 48+ countries, eliminating per-minute international charges that can destroy remote team budgets. GoTo Connect includes free international calling to select regions on all plans.

For remote teams with high inbound volume: Retell AI handles overflow and after-hours calls without adding user seats, particularly valuable for remote teams spanning multiple time zones where someone always needs to be "on" but no one wants 3 AM shifts.

Which cloud phone system has the best AI features in 2026?

It depends on what you mean by "AI features":

Best AI call transcription and coaching: Dialpad. Real-time transcription is included on the $15/user/mo Standard plan with ~94% accuracy, live sentiment analysis, and AI-generated call summaries. No other UCaaS platform matches this at the base tier.

Best AI call handling (agent that answers and resolves calls): Retell AI. The AI voice agent handles entire conversations, qualifying leads, booking appointments, answering questions, processing requests. This is not an AI add-on bolted onto a phone system; it handles the call itself.

Best AI add-on to an existing phone system: RingCentral. RingCentral's AI Virtual Assistant (AVA, included in RingEX at no extra cost) provides real-time note-taking, call summaries, and translation. The AI Receptionist (AIR, $39+/mo add-on) handles 24/7 answering and routing for 8,300+ customers.

Important distinction: most UCaaS vendors charge $39-$60/user/mo extra for AI features. Retell AI and Dialpad include their core AI capabilities in the base product. Always check whether "AI-powered" in a vendor\'s marketing means included or add-on.

Can I connect an AI voice agent to my existing cloud phone system?

Yes. Most teams forward overflow or after-hours calls to the agent, or connect it over SIP trunking so they keep their numbers. Retell AI works with Twilio, Vonage, Telnyx or any SIP carrier and does not charge for telephony on your own SIP trunk.
