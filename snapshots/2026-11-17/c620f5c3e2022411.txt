EZLynx Delivers Real-Time Carrier Quotes Inside the AMS Submission Workflow

June 30, 2026

New solution reimagines commercial quoting as an AMS-embedded experience, making it easier for agents to diversify and expand their book of business

LEWISVILLE, TX – June 30, 2026 – EZLynx®, an Applied company, today announced Instant Commercial Quoting, a new capability built directly into the EZLynx agency management system that surfaces real-time carrier quotes inside the submission workflow as agents complete commercial applications, with no separate rater or portal required. Instant Commercial Quoting eliminates the disconnect between completing a commercial lines application and receiving carrier quotes, giving independent agencies a clear path to expand beyond personal lines and grow a profitable commercial book.

Instant Commercial Quoting is powered by the Ivans distribution network, connecting insurance carriers directly to agency partners via agency management and commercial quoting platforms.

This new capability launches with:

Core line of business coverage: Agents writing General Liability, Business Owners Policy, and Workers' Compensation policies can quote today, covering the three lines most agencies write first when placing commercial business.

Leading carrier markets for standard commercial lines: Agents get immediate access to their most widely written carrier markets, giving them the coverage they need to get started, with the lineup expanding to +20 markets.

Risk questions built for multi-market quoting: Agents complete a commercial application built around the risk questions carriers ask most, making it straightforward to capture the data needed to generate quotes across multiple carriers in a single workflow.

"As an EZLynx user who has relied on the personal lines rater for years, Instant Commercial Quoting felt immediately familiar — integrated directly into the submission from the start, just like the rater I already use every day," said Schelley Bedell, insurance agent, LGE Insurance Services. "The built-in tools like NAICS code lookup and suggested related lines mean I can walk through coverage options with a client while I'm still on the phone with them, without ever leaving the submission."

"More home and auto agencies are expanding into commercial lines to unlock new growth and diversify their book, but agents consistently tell us that commercial quoting feels like piecing together separate workflows," said Brenna Johnson, senior vice president, product management, EZLynx. "Instant commercial quoting delivers exactly what independent agents have been asking for: a fully integrated workflow that makes it possible to pursue commercial lines profitably and grow."

About EZLynx

EZLynx pioneered personal lines real-time comparative rating, enabling agents to generate quotes from multiple insurance carriers with a single data entry point. Today, over 37,000 agencies rely on EZLynx to provide more than 14 million home, auto, and package rating transactions every month. EZLynx has also expanded beyond personal lines comparative rating by creating innovative software solutions that transform every facet of agency life, including agency management, client self-servicing, sales pipeline management, marketing and communications, accounting, eSignature, and more, all available on a unified, one platform solution.
