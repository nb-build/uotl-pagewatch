How Truckstop’s freight broker TMS, ITS Dispatch, works.

TMS systems for freight brokers integrate various functions, such as load tracking, order management, invoicing, and reporting, into a single platform. The ITS Dispatch software can quickly interpret and organize data to streamline and optimize your operations. Brokers use ITS Dispatch to efficiently manage the entire logistics process, from sourcing carriers to tracking shipments and handling payments.

Our broker TMS enables better decision-making, enhances operational efficiency, and ultimately helps maximize profits.

Transform your business with ITS Dispatch, our TMS software for brokers.
