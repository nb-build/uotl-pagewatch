PrintReleaf and CEO Juice Launch ERP Integration for e-automate Dealers

Monday, October 05, 2026

Press release from the issuing company

DENVER, CO – PrintReleaf today announced a new integration with CEO Juice that connects PrintReleaf directly to e-automate, the most widely used service management system among North American office technology dealers. The integration runs through CEO Juice's ID136 API and is available now for any e-automate dealer that subscribes to ID136.

Most PrintReleaf use by MPS dealers draws page volume from Device Collection Agents (DCAs) or spreadsheet uploads. A DCA can miss devices that aren't on a network or record rogue meter reads, so the volume in PrintReleaf sometimes comes in different from the volume on the client's bill.

Connecting to e-automate billing data gives dealers:

Complete coverage: devices that were never connected to a DCA are included.

Accuracy: pages reforested or offset in PrintReleaf match the pages on the client's invoice.

Easier management: switching DCAs has no effect on PrintReleaf, so there's no customer-by-customer migration.

Easier scaling: when a dealer acquires an MPS provider, moving those clients into its e-automate database brings them into PrintReleaf too.

Billing model options: dealers can align PrintReleaf with actuals only, or with base allowances plus actuals.

"When a client asks how their pages were counted, a dealer should be able to point to the invoice," said Jordan Darragh, CEO of PrintReleaf. "Now they can, and the integration takes much of the friction out of the acquisitions and DCA changes that are so common in the MPS Dealer channel right now."

"We are always happy to connect e-automate to the tools our clients’ businesses depend on," said Gary Lavin, Founder at CEO Juice. "More of our clients are hearing sustainability questions from their customers, and the PrintReleaf integration lets them answer with data they already trust."

Availability

Dealers subscribe to ID136 through CEO Juice, receive their API credentials, and register their CEO Juice server in PrintReleaf. To get started, contact PrintReleaf or visit the ID136 page on the CEO Juice website and subscribe.
