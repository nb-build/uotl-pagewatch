Frequently Asked Questions

What size landscaping company is LMN built for?

LMN is landscape business management software built for operators who are serious about growth. LMN Starter fits companies with 5-10 employees that want to understand their numbers at a deeper level. LMN Professional fits companies with 10 or more employees that want clarity on job profitability and tighter communication between the office and the field. LMN Enterprise supports multi-location companies, starting at 100 users. Every plan connects estimating, scheduling, time tracking, and invoicing in one system.

How does LMN show whether a landscaping job is profitable?

Every estimate built in LMN ties back to your own operating budget, which accounts for all costs, including labor, equipment, materials, overhead, and your profit target. With LMN you see your estimated profit margin before you send the proposal giving estimators confidence and relying less on gut-feel. Once the job starts, real-time job costing compares estimated and actual hours and costs (e.g., materials) while your crew is still working on site. You catch an overrun while you can still do something about it.

How much does LMN landscaping software cost?

LMN Starter starts at $297 per month and includes one office or crew lead license and five crew member licenses. LMN Professional starts at $648 per month and includes three office or crew lead licenses and 15 crew member licenses. LMN Enterprise starts at 100 users with custom pricing. Prices are in USD, additional licenses cost extra, and plans include a one-time onboarding fee. Every plan includes budgeting, estimating, scheduling, LMN Crew, and invoicing and payments.

How long does LMN onboarding take, and can I bring over my existing data?

We pair every LMN customer with an onboarding specialist who works with you to set up your budget, price list, estimates, jobs, and invoicing in one-on-one training calls at your pace. If you want to be up and running in a week, we’ll get you there, but 30-45 days is typical. You can import your client list from QuickBooks or a CSV file, and we show you how to load vendor material price lists so your estimates start from your real costs.

Does LMN work with QuickBooks?

LMN syncs with QuickBooks, so your invoices and payments stay in step with your accounting. LMN Starter connects to QuickBooks Online. LMN Professional and Enterprise connect to both QuickBooks Online and Desktop, so you can keep the version you already use. Your estimates, invoices, and payments stay in sync, which cuts double entry. For card and ACH payments, see our LMN Pay FAQs.
