Mosaic Loan Servicing is now

Solar Servicing

Important Stakeholder Information

On September 22, 2025, Solar Servicing, announced that it completed its transaction to acquire the loan servicing operations of Solar Mosaic following the completion of Mosaic’s court-approved Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization.

Mosaic’s loan servicing operations have been successfully transitioned to Solar Servicing. Solar Servicing plans to continue Mosaic’s loan servicing operations as usual to ensure borrowers can continue to pay their loans as planned and that collections will be remitted to loan owners.

BORROWER FAQs

What does this mean for me? Collapse

Loan servicing operations will continue as expected under Solar Servicing for most customers.

There are no changes to payment terms or schedules for borrowers.

The same payment options are available and you should continue to make payments as you have done prior to this announcement unless you hear from us otherwise. If you would like to make a payment, please log into your account here, or mail your payment to P.O. Box 78080, Phoenix, AZ 85062-8080.

Borrowers are obligated to pay their loans under the same terms and schedule as their loan agreement indicates.

Do I still have to make my loan payments? Expand

Yes, you need to continue making your monthly payment on or before your due date to avoid any penalties per your loan agreement. If you would like to make a payment, please log into your account here or mail your payment to P.O. Box 78080, Phoenix, AZ 85062-8080.

What happens if I received a notice that says Solar Servicing is not my loan servicer? Expand

If you are notified that Solar Servicing is not servicing your loan, please reach out to the contact information contained within your Servicing Transition letter. Your loan was previously sold by Mosaic to an investor who has chosen an alternative loan servicing provider to service your loan moving forward. We apologize for the inconvenience.

Who can I contact if I have any other questions about my loan? Expand

You can reach the customer service phone line at (866) 493-6367 or email loanservicing@solarservicingllc.com.

INSTALLER/CONTRACTOR FAQs

What does this mean for unfunded projects?Collapse

Unless otherwise agreed with Mosaic, opportunities that were unfunded have been closed. Installation partners are encouraged to work with their customers to explore alternate financing options for these projects. We understand this is disappointing news, and we sincerely apologize for the disruption.

What does this mean for partially and fully funded projects?Expand

For loans that were partially funded as of the bankruptcy filing, a dedicated pipeline team has been hired to support system installers and loan owners as they work towards a resolution. For more questions related to open pipeline, please contact support@joinmosaic.com.

Will Mosaic continue to accept new loan applications? Expand

No, Mosaic no longer originates new loans. Solar Servicing is only a loan servicing company and is not in the loan origination business.

Will Mosaic pay installers the money they’re owed? Expand

Any amounts owed or obligations otherwise existing prior to June 6, 2025, will be addressed through the court process. For more information, please contact Mosaic’s claims agent, Kroll, toll-free by calling (833) 953-7040 in the U.S. or +1 (646) 974-5614 for International, or visiting this link: https://cases.ra.kroll.com/MOSAIC.
