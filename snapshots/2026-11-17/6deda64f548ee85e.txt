Podium Release 26.1

January 2026

Answer FAQs faster with Voice AI

Podium Voice AI users can now set up frequently asked questions so your voice agent can answer quickly and consistently across all locations.

What’s new?

FAQ Management

Add your business hours, service area, pricing information, and common policy questions to your FAQ. Your AI voice agent will use this information to answer caller questions quickly and consistently across all your locations.

Available to all businesses with Podium AI Voice, contact your CSM for set up help.

AI Call Coaching trains your reps

Get AI-powered insights on your team’s phone performance.

What’s new?

Call Reviews

See strengths and coaching opportunities on every call. Review call metrics including speaking time, silence time, and words per minute.

Team Performance Dashboard

Review your team’s call performance dashboard to identify coaching opportunities. See which reps have the best customer engagement and share their techniques with the team.

Metrics

See metrics like talk time, silence patterns, and conversation quality to help your team improve their customer interactions and close more deals.

Available in beta to businesses with Podium Premium Phones.

Sunbit Financing Integration

Offer customers flexible financing options through Sunbit, directly integrated with Podium.

What’s new?

Immediate & Consolidated Financing Option

Customers can apply for financing and get approved in seconds without leaving your business. Close more high-value sales by removing the price barrier.

Textable Financing Flow

Send customers a financing application link via text. They can apply, get approved, and complete payment all from their phone.

For more information on Sunbit financing visit our resource center. Available to MedSpa, HVAC, and Automotive businesses.

Payments Surcharging

Save on credit card processing fees by passing the cost to customers who choose to pay with credit cards. Fully compliant surcharging that helps you keep more of what you earn.

What’s new?

Automatic Surcharge Application

When enabled, surcharges are automatically calculated and applied to credit card transactions. Customers see the fee clearly before completing their payment.

Available to all businesses with Podium Payments in eligible states.

Podium Aesthetics EMR Updates

What’s new?

Expanded Client Profile

See a comprehensive view of each client including their treatment history, preferences, notes, product purchases, membership status, package affiliation and upcoming appointments all in one place. Better understand your clients to provide an exceptional experience.

ePrescribe

Prescribe medications electronically directly from Podium EMR. Send prescriptions directly to pharmacies from patient charts. Streamline your prescription workflow and improve patient safety with integrated e-prescribing capabilities. Includes drug interaction checking and prescription history tracking.

Packages

Create and sell custom treatment packages and memberships directly in Podium EMR. Bundle services together for better value and increased client retention. Track package usage and remaining treatments for each client.

Available to businesses with Podium EMR.

Avery Follows up at the right time

Avery now intelligently skips follow-up texts for patients who have already booked appointments.

What’s new?

Automatic Follow-Up Skip for Booked Patients

Avery will no longer send follow-up texts to patients who have already scheduled an appointment. This prevents unnecessary outreach and creates a better patient experience.

Patient Questions Still Allowed

Even after booking, patients can still text in with questions and get helpful responses from AI Voice – they just won’t receive automated follow-up texts.

Direct Hard Booking into Service Titan and HouseCall Pro

What’s new?

AI customer qualification and scheduling directly

Now your AI Employee can take calls, texts, emails, and third party leads from outreach all the way to booked into your calendar. See appointments appear in Service Titan and HouseCall Pro without lifting a finger, optimized for your tech schedules.

Available to businesses using Podium AI and Service Titan or Housecall Pro.

HVAC Voice AI Enhancements

Three new capabilities for HVAC businesses using AI Voice to streamline customer interactions and scheduling.

What’s new?

Contact Information Updates During Calls

AI Voice can now update customer contact information in real-time during calls. When a customer mentions a new phone number or address, the AI automatically captures and updates their record.

Route-Optimized Scheduling

When scheduling service appointments, Larry now considers job location to optimize technician routes. This helps reduce drive time and allows you to fit more jobs into each day.

Property Details Capture

Voice AI can now collect and store property metadata during conversations – such as property type, size, or equipment details – making future service calls more efficient.

Automotive Onboarding Improvements

Enhanced AI onboarding experience for automotive dealerships with comprehensive question flows.

What’s new?

Comprehensive Onboarding Questions

New structured question schema for automotive onboarding that captures all essential business information including hours of operation, service types, and location details in a conversational flow. So you know your AI employee is privy to all the important stuff

Structured Hours & Address Collection

AI now asks for business hours and address information in a structured format, reducing errors and ensuring accurate setup for scheduling and routing.

Playbook Intelligence Improvements

AI Playbooks are now smarter about when to take action and easier to manage.

What’s new?

Conditional Tool Execution

AI Employee can now automatically execute actions based on conversation context, even without explicit procedure steps. For example, if a customer says “my new number is 555-1234,” the AI will automatically update their contact info based on your policies – no manual procedure needed.

Playbook Enable/Disable Toggle

Easily turn playbooks on or off without modifying or deleting them. This makes it simple to test new playbooks, temporarily disable seasonal procedures, or quickly respond to changing business needs.

Version History & Audit Trail

All playbook changes now include version tracking and user attribution, so you can see who made changes and when.

New Auto Integrations

What’s new?

Tekion scheduling

Now Jerry can take calls and texts and book appointments directly into Tekion, with no oversight required! Jerry can fill the calendar you already use.

Tekion + Reynolds & Reynolds Service Marketing

Now you can trigger service marketing from Tekion and Reynolds & Reynolds based off triggers from your data in these systems, in addition to what was already available with Xtime.

Service AI Marketing Automations

What’s New?

Service Marketing drives more customers to your dealership

Now you can increase your show rate with automated appointment reminders, follow up with abandon customers, and drive more customers in for their first or next service. Set up in Automations.

Service Marketing Reporting

Now you can see all the messages Jerry is sending out to customers inviting them to make a service appointment. Click in deeper to see response rate and booking rate.
