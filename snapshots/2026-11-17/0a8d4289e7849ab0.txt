How Lusha’s B2B data foundation is built

Two layers do the work. Lusha Search gives you verified contacts and companies. Deep Intelligence scores them against your ICP and learns from your pipeline to predict which accounts are likely to buy. You get your own list, with the likeliest buyers first and a reason to reach out to each one.
Learn more about the Lusha Data Framework

Search layer

Lusha Search covers 515M+ verified contacts and 29M+ companies, with 77 fields on every record. Every email comes with a confidence score, and every phone number and email shows when it was last updated. Lusha also tracks 30 types of buying signals, like job changes, funding rounds, hiring surges and new leaders, each one dated and tied to a named company.

Intelligence layer

Deep Intelligence scores every account against your ICP and uses your CRM history to predict which ones are likely to buy. Recent signals give you the reason to reach out: a new VP, a funding round, a hiring push. For each account, it maps the buying committee: who decides, who champions, who uses it. It also finds lookalikes of your best customers and shows which companies are visiting your website.

FAQs

What is Lusha?

Lusha is the B2B data and intelligence layer for GTM teams and AI agents. Lusha scores accounts against your ICP, flags dated buying signals, maps the buying committee and returns verified contact details, from 515M+ contacts and 29M+ companies, with 132M+ contacts in the United States. Teams use it in Lusha Workspace, inside Claude and ChatGPT, through the Chrome extension and the API, and sync it with Salesforce, HubSpot and other CRMs.

Is Lusha an AI prospecting tool?

Yes, and prospecting is one of several jobs it runs. Lusha combines a verified B2B database with an intelligence layer: AI recommendations for accounts and contacts, dated buying signals, ICP scoring and lookalikes, available in Claude, ChatGPT and any MCP client.

Is Lusha's B2B contact data GDPR and CCPA compliant?

Yes. Lusha is GDPR and CCPA compliant, and independently certified under ISO 27001, ISO 27701, ISO 31700, ISO 42001 and SOC 2 Type II, with a TRUSTe seal. Certificates are published on the trust center.

How accurate is Lusha's contact data?

Lusha states 98% email accuracy and 85% US / 86% global phone accuracy across the full database, published on lusha.com/data. Sources are checked daily and contact fields refresh weekly. Lusha also publishes how fast that data goes stale: in a September 2026 measurement of 140,284 records, 12.25% of US sales leaders changed roles within twelve months. Both figures are published and linked, so you can check them rather than take them on trust. How Lusha verifies emails and phone numbers →

How fast does B2B contact data go stale?

Lusha measured it, twice: 12.25% of US sales leaders changed roles in twelve months in the latest run, September 2026. Contact details usually survive a job change; titles and employers do not. The measurement is published on the Lusha blog.

Can I use Lusha inside Claude or ChatGPT?

Yes. Lusha connects directly to Claude and ChatGPT, and through MCP to any compatible AI agent, so search, enrichment and buying signals run inside the conversation.

What buying signals does Lusha track?

26 signal types, each a named, dated event tied to a specific company. Funding, hiring, leadership and job change signals refresh weekly. IT spend and website traffic refresh monthly.

Does Lusha integrate with Salesforce and HubSpot?

Yes, with native integrations for both, plus Zoho, Pipedrive, Bullhorn, Dynamics and Capsule. See who’s already in your CRM while you search, import records to enrich and score them, push verified data back with duplicate matching and field mapping, and keep records current on a schedule with CRM Auto-Sync. CRM sync. Comparisons of enrichment tools for Salesforce and HubSpot users are on the blog.

Can Lusha help with recruiting, not just sales?

Yes. Recruiting teams use Lusha to re-verify past candidate pools and source new ones with verified direct dials and emails.

Is there a free way to try Lusha?

Yes. The free plan needs no credit card. Limits are on the pricing page.
