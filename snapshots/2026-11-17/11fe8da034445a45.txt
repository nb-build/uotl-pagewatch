Have Questions? We’re Here to Help!

What is a Mortgage CRM?

A mortgage CRM (customer relationship management) platform helps mortgage professionals manage their client database and interactions. When you purchase Jungo, you get a robust mortgage CRM that is optimized for the mortgage industry and completely customizable for your internal processes. This means your mortgage CRM will help you close more loans and delight more borrowers. Plus, we’ll be with you every step of the way.

What is Salesforce?

Salesforce is one of the top cloud-based CRM systems available and Jungo is built on this powerful platform. Jungo’s relationship with Salesforce means that you will have access to Salesforce’s powerful AppExchange and mobile app.

Does Jungo integrate with the other software I use?

Yes! With hundreds of integrations with top apps, as well as close partnerships with industry leaders, Jungo works seamlessly with the other programs you already love. We integrate with Ellie Mae’s Encompass, Calyx Point, Floify, Optimal Blue, Mortgage Coach and many other tools.

How will Jungo help me and my business?

With Jungo’s client contact database, automated marketing campaigns, post-close concierge service, co-marketing resources, automatic loan status updates, and many other tools, you’ll shoot to the front of the pack.

Your borrowers will be delighted by your consistent communication. Your referral partners will love how quickly and efficiently you close their client’s loans. And your past customers will be thrilled to recommend you to their friends and family.
