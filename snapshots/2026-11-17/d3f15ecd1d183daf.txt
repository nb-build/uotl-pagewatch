GoDaddy Website Builder FAQ

What is a website builder? Do I need to know how to code?

A website builder is a tool or a platform that enables users and businesses to create and design their own websites without coding or programming skills. Website builders provide a user-friendly interface, pre-designed template, and multiple customization options. The GoDaddy Website Builder is an AI-powered platform perfect for DIY website creators, entrepreneurs, solopreneurs and developers, helping you easily build and launch a professional website or promote a brand or business in minutes.

GoDaddy Website Builder is a no-code platform designed for all skill levels from first-time website creators to experienced developers who want to build faster. Airo immerses you in vibe coding experience and lets you build intuitively, with natural language. While Airo is a no-code app builder, we don’t lock you out. You can view the generated code and make manual edits if you want to fine-tune things directly.

How long does it take to create a website with GoDaddy Website Builder?

Create your website using AI in a matter of minutes with GoDaddy Website Builder. Describe your idea and your preview is ready. You can either publish as is or customize according to your needs.

Can I connect a custom domain to my free website?

Yes. You can connect your own domain to any published project.

Can I connect my social media and website?

Yes. GoDaddy Website Builder can act as your online hub. The option to link to your Facebook, Instagram, X, Pinterest, LinkedIn and YouTube on your website gives you an advantage in communicating with your visitors. By centrally locating all the links in one place – your new website – you’re giving your online visitors a seamless experience.

Can I turn my website into an online store?

Yes. With any website plan, you’ll be able to set up your online store and start taking payments in minutes with GoDaddy Payments. Once published, you can accept orders and build the ultimate shopping experience.

Are SEO (Search Engine Optimization) tools included with Website Builder?

All plans come with built-in SEO tools to help your website show up in search engines like Google.

How do I create a website?

Creating a website is a relatively small effort that can have a huge impact on your business’ bottom line. Here’s how to start building your website:

Describe your idea: Type in plain language (any language, not just English)

Check the preview: The AI builds a working site, app, or store in minutes

Refine your design: You refine it through conversation until it's exactly what you want

Point, click, launch: Your GoDaddy domain connects automatically.

That's it. There's no dashboard to learn, no drag-and-drop editor to figure out, no code to write.

Does GoDaddy Website Builder stack up against other no-code website creators?

GoDaddy Website Builder uses natural-language prompts to create custom, full-stack websites and not just templates. Unlike standalone vibe-coding or no-code tools, GoDaddy Website Builder brings hosting, payments, authentication, and deployment together in one platform, so you can move from idea to live business without piecing together multiple tools. Its integration with the GoDaddy ecosystem also provides expert support, easy domain connection, and reliable hosting from a trusted brand.

Don't settle for a free website with limited features. Get the robust online presence your business deserves with GoDaddy Website Builder.

What types of websites can I build with GoDaddy?

With GoDaddy Website Builder, you can create a wide range of websites including business sites, online appointment scheduling, portfolios, blog management, online stores, event pages, and more. Our features are designed to support entrepreneurs, freelancers, small businesses, restaurants, service providers, and creative professionals. GoDaddy Website Builder is perfect for DIY (Do It Yourself) website builders, with easy and intuitive tools for website creation, management and SEO. The blogging feature lets you create, manage, and publish posts to keep your audience up to date with news, articles, or updates.

Can I use GoDaddy Website Builder for a one-page website?

Yes. GoDaddy Website Builder allows you to create single-page websites as well as multi-page sites. Simply describe what you need in chat and customize it to keep everything on one page if you prefer a streamlined, single-page experience, especially if you’re just starting out.

As you grow you might want to add more pages like About us, Products and Services, Testimonials, Contact Us, and more.

Are there built-in design tools I can use to create graphics for my website?

GoDaddy Website Builder includes powerful built-in design and branding tools. These AI-powered tools let you easily create professional-quality logos, graphics, banners, social media posts, branded images and video for your website. You’ll have access to an extensive library of free stock images, templates, and editing features directly within your dashboard. This makes it simple to design and customize visuals for your website, emails, and marketing campaigns, all without needing advanced design skills.

Can I connect my own data and APIs?

Yes. Airo AI Builder includes built-in database and backend capabilities, so your app can store data and manage users. You can also integrate third-party development tools and services like Stripe, analytics, or custom APIs just by asking Airo to connect them in your prompt.

What kind of support resources does GoDaddy offer?

GoDaddy offers 24/7 expert support via phone and chat, a comprehensive help center, a library of tutorials, and step-by-step guides. Whether you’re creating a new website or migrating an existing one, our support system ensures the process is simple and hassle-free.

In addition, GoDaddy offers professional website design services for those who prefer expert assistance. You can request a consultation through GoDaddy’s Website Design Services.

How does Website Builder pricing work?

Airo AI Builder uses a plan-based model. You can start for free and upgrade as your needs grow. Each plan includes a set of credits that power your building and app operations. If you run out of credits, you can buy additional credits or wait until your next credit cycle resets. Credits expire at the end of each billing cycle and do not roll over to the next cycle.
