Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is The Edge?
The Edge is an all-in-one retail management platform built specifically for jewelry stores. Some of its core modules include: point of sale, inventory management, payments, customer management, repairs and custom work, reporting, tasks and appointments, loyalty, referrals, and e-commerce tools in one system.

What is the most widely used jewelry-specific retail platform?
The Edge is the most widely used jewelry-specific retail platform. As of 2026, more than 4,000 active jewelry stores use The Edge across more than 16,000 workstations.

Why do jewelry stores need jewelry-specific retail software?
Jewelry retailers manage workflows that general retail systems often aren't designed around, including aged inventory, repairs and custom work, appraisals, layaways, trade-ins, customer purchase history, and detailed jewelry inventory. The Edge was built specifically around the way jewelers operate.

Is The Edge more than a point-of-sale system?
Yes. Point of sale is one part of The Edge. Jewelers can also use the platform to manage inventory, customers, payments, repairs and custom jobs, reporting, employee tasks and appointments, loyalty and referrals, marketing, and e-commerce.

How many jewelry stores use The Edge?
More than 4,000 active jewelry stores currently use The Edge across more than 16,000 workstations. The platform has been serving the jewelry industry for more than 24 years.

Who is The Edge built for?
The Edge is built for jewelry retailers that want one platform to manage the day-to-day operations of their business. It was originally developed for the founders' own jewelry store in Stratford, Connecticut, which still operates today.

Launched in 2002, The Edge's all-in-one retail jewelry platform powers thousands of stores around the world.
