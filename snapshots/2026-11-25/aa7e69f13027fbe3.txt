Liberate Introduces AI Intercept as Consumer AI Assistants Start Shopping for Insurance

Liberate has launched AI Intercept, a new capability that detects when consumer AI agents call insurance carriers and agencies and routes those calls to Liberate’s insurance-native AI. The technology handles quoting, servicing and follow-up end-to-end while keeping human teams available for customers who need them.

Liberate

The new capability identifies when a consumer's AI agent is calling a carrier or agency, and serves that caller end-to-end with Liberate's insurance-native AI.

SAN FRANCISCO – Sept. 28, 2026 – Liberate, a provider of insurance-native AI agents for property and casualty insurers, today launched AI Intercept, a capability that screens every inbound call to a carrier or agency, identifies when the caller is a consumer's AI agent rather than a person, and routes it to Liberate's AI for end-to-end handling, allowing human teams to stay focused on the customers who need them.

“Insurers are about to get a new kind of caller: AI agents shopping for coverage on a consumer’s behalf,” said Amrish Singh, co-founder and CEO of Liberate. “If a carrier can't tell a machine from a person, human agents will end up spending their day talking to software while a family calling about a flooded basement sits on hold. AI should handle AI so people can handle people.”

The announcement follows a fast change in how consumers are shopping for insurance. Meta began rolling out Muse, its personal AI agent, in the U.S. on Sept. 8, but within weeks, insurance marketplace Insurify blocked it from its platform, citing concerns that automated quoting strips out critical coverage limits, deductibles, and eligibility details.

Liberate expects more consumer AI agents to call agencies and carriers directly to get quotes and service policies. That creates a new problem for insurance operations. Call volume will rise, but each call will carry less intent. If human agents answer every AI-placed call, insurers will pay for calls that rarely convert into customers.

How AI Intercept works:

Detect. Liberate identifies when an inbound caller is an AI agent rather than a person.

Triage. The call is classified and routed automatically without pulling human teams away from live customers.

Serve. Liberate's AI agent handles the conversation end-to-end, including quoting, servicing, and follow-up.

Govern. Every interaction runs through Liberate's Supervisor Layer, with full audit trails and escalation to a licensed human when a conversation needs one.

Singh added that voice is a bridge, not the endpoint. "Today's insurance infrastructure is built for humans talking to humans. Over time, agents will communicate with one another through machine-native channels. We built our capabilities so insurers are ready for both."

About Liberate

Liberate builds insurance-native AI agents for property and casualty insurance. Its agents answer calls, texts, and emails, resolve sales, service, and claims requests end-to-end, and write every action back into core systems like Guidewire, Duck Creek, Snapsheet, and Applied Epic. A Supervisor Layer governs each interaction and escalates to a human when necessary. More than 70 carriers and brokers run more than 10M+ transactions a month on Liberate.
