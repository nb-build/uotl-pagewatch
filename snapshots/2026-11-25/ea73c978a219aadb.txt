Jobber Unveils Jobber Teammate, the First AI Agent for Home and Commercial Service Businesses to Plan, Act, and Learn Like a Real Team Member

News provided by

Jobber

Sep 23, 2026, 12:00 ET

Share this article

Share this article

Jobber Teammate headlines announcements from Jobber Now that make growing businesses easier to run—from managing complex operations and finances to putting AI to work

TORONTO, Sept. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Jobber, the leading provider of software for home and commercial service businesses, today announced Jobber Teammate, marking a shift from software that business owners have to operate to AI that proactively works for them. Built directly into Jobber, Jobber Teammate uses the context it already has about a service pro's business to provide a personalized morning briefing, take on work across the business, and adapt over time to a service pro's preferences.

"AI is changing how service businesses run, and our job is to make that change easy and make it make sense," said Sam Pillar, CEO and co-founder of Jobber. "Jobber Teammate brings that approach to life by understanding each business, helping service pros get ahead of what needs attention and get work done, while ensuring they always remain in control."

Jobber Teammate headlines a broader set of announcements from Jobber Now including new tools for managing larger teams and a more complete view of business finances. It's the latest expansion of Jobber's role as the operating system for businesses running at scale.

"I'm proud of the work our teams have done this year to make Jobber the complete platform for small and growing home service businesses," said Matt Kaplan, Chief Product Officer at Jobber. "As your business grows, running it shouldn't get harder. Jobber is taking on more of the day-to-day work behind the scenes, helping you stay in control while giving you more time to focus on your customers, your team, and what's next."

Putting AI to Work: Jobber Teammate

Jobber Teammate proactively identifies what needs attention, prepares the work, and brings it to a service pro ready for review and approval. The service pro stays in control while spending significantly less time on admin.

Jobber Teammate's growing list of capabilities include:

Daily Digest: A personalized morning briefing pulling together everything a service pro wants to know, such as money collected, expected, and outstanding; jobs scheduled; and crew status.

Action Board: Throughout the day, Jobber Teammate triages what needs attention and gets a head start on it, such as drafting quotes to speed up reply rates, or building marketing campaigns to fill in upcoming gaps in the schedule—ready and waiting for a service pro's review.

Customizable Routines: Service pros show Jobber Teammate how they want a recurring situation handled once, like rescheduling the day due to inclement weather or a sick employee, and Jobber Teammate handles it that way going forward.

With use, Jobber Teammate continues to learn a service pro's preferences, becoming a more trusted team member over time.

"I built my business so it can run without me, and tools like Jobber Teammate are what make that possible," said Ryaan Tuttle, Founder, Best Handyman Boston. "Seeing it reschedule jobs based on the weather shows what AI can actually do for a service business, not in theory, but on a Tuesday when it starts raining."

Jobber Teammate is in beta, with early access opening to Jobber customers beginning today.

Jobber continues to embed purpose-built AI across its platform to address the specific needs of home and commercial service businesses. Additional AI-powered capabilities announced at Jobber Now include:

Custom Report Builder: Service pros can describe the reports they need, and Jobber AI builds them on demand, making it easier to get business insights that matter to them.

Phone Calls in Jobber: Service pros will be able to make and receive calls directly through Jobber, with Receptionist handling follow-up actions for them.

Marketing Suite updates: New Marketing Plan and Brand Builder capabilities help service pros build marketing campaigns and create custom social media content.

Simplifying Complex Operations: Projects and Crew Scheduling

Jobber is making its biggest investment yet in helping larger, growing service businesses manage more complex operations with new and enhanced features, including:

Projects: Designed for larger, longer and more complex work. Businesses can organize work into phases, milestones, tasks and visits, creating a structure that both the office and field teams can follow throughout the life of a project.

Crew Scheduling: Lets businesses organize employees and subcontractors into crews and assign the right crew to a job, instead of scheduling people one at a time.

This builds on dozens of updates Jobber has shipped this year for larger businesses, including automated job costing, improved time tracking, Jobber Voice for field teams, a more robust files and media library, and team @mentions to keep employees aligned throughout a fast-moving day.

Bringing the Financial Picture Together: Jobber Bookkeeping

Jobber already captures much of a service business's financial activity including quotes, invoices, and payments. Newly launched capabilities make it easier to capture the rest—employee time, expenses, receipts, and supplier invoices—automatically.

With Jobber Bookkeeping, for the first time, a business's books are now professionally managed directly by Jobber alongside its operational data. No more nights spent reconciling spreadsheets and bank statements. By bringing operational and financial data together, Jobber gives owners a clearer, more actionable view of their finances to make better decisions every day and improve profitability.

To learn more about all the features announced at Jobber Now, visit: jobber.com/jobbernow.

About Jobber

Jobber is the award-winning platform that puts blue collar first. Built for service pros, Jobber streamlines and automates operations so crews can run more efficiently, increase profitability, and scale with confidence. More than 100,000 businesses and 400,000 service pros in over 50 industries—including landscaping, plumbing, cleaning, and contracting—use Jobber to serve over 34 million properties in more than 70 countries. Jobber continually ranks as one of Canada's fastest-growing and most innovative companies by The Globe and Mail, Fast Company, and Deloitte. For more information visit: jobber.com.
