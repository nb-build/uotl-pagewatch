Fast BOM & Routing imports and builds

Import BOMs from drawings or CAD software and easily add routing steps. Production costs automatically update based on labor, material, and machine details, helping you have the information you need to send fast and accurate quotes.
Explore the BOM & Routing

Quickly nest for accurate estimates

Upload a DXF file directly on a quote to generate a nest and automatically update material and machine time cost estimates without leaving Fulcrum.
Explore Nesting Workflows

Set your team up for success

Attach files, leave notes, and live chat with the team. All details automatically sync to the rest of Fulcrum, keeping everyone in the loop.

Live order progress visibility

Your customers have options, stand out

Updates from the shop floor flow automatically to a secure Customer Portal. Customers can log in and view the details and statuses of their orders and invoices. Increase visibility and build relationships without any extra phone calls or emails.

Not winning enough work?

Ensure that your quoting process is spot-on, from estimates for completion to your pricing structure. Fulcrum’s job costing and seamless workflows help you to drive more business with higher margins.
