How We Picked the Top 10 Fencing Estimating Software

We’re QuoteIQ. We made this list. We also picked our own platform as #1 — here’s exactly why, with the trade-offs each tool brings to the table.

The U.S. fence construction industry generated an estimated $20.4 billion in revenue in 2026, according to IBISWorld, with 315,000+ businesses competing in a highly fragmented market. For most of those businesses, the gap between a 30% close rate and a 55% close rate comes down to quoting speed and professionalism. We built this list to help fence contractors find software that actually moves that needle.

We evaluated each platform against five criteria. First, pricing transparency — does the vendor publish real prices, or do you need to sit through a sales demo to find out what you’ll pay? Second, feature depth for fencing specifically — does the platform handle per-linear-foot pricing by material type, satellite measurement for property lines, and fence material inventory? Third, mobile usability — can an estimator generate a professional quote in the driveway without going back to the office? Fourth, customer review aggregate — we pulled ratings from the App Store, Google Play, Capterra, and G2 across thousands of reviews. Fifth, onboarding and support quality — a platform that takes six months to implement is a liability for a fence shop that needs to bill jobs next week.

Pricing was verified directly against each vendor’s published pricing page where available. Where vendors don’t publish pricing (ServiceTitan, Buildertrend), we used verified user reports from G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, and contractor forums. Sources for all pricing claims are cited in the competitor entries below.

The fencing industry is one where the estimating workflow is unusually distinct from generic field service management. A fence contractor quoting a 200-foot residential privacy job needs to calculate posts, rails, pickets, gates, and concrete — line-by-line — within minutes of a site visit or satellite measurement session. Generic CRMs leave that calculation entirely to the contractor. The best fencing estimating software builds the fence-industry math directly into the quoting flow. That distinction drove our rankings.

Jobber is the platform most fence contractors encounter when they first start looking for software beyond spreadsheets, and for good reason. Pros

It supports customizable assemblies for chain link, wood, and vinyl, and real-time collaboration between estimators and project managers reviewing plans simultaneously.

The base price is genuinely low, the interface is designed for non-technical operators, and the platform includes built-in marketing automation — email drip campaigns, automated review requests, and follow-up sequences — that most competitors charge separately for or gate behind higher tiers. For a solo operator doing 5–10 fence quotes per month and primarily needing a professional way to send estimates and invoices, Markate covers the basics.

The limitations are significant once you scale past one or two employees. Markate’s dispatching workflow is simpler than Jobber’s or QuoteIQ’s, integrations are narrower, and the platform lacks satellite measurement, AI estimating, and per-linear-foot fence pricing calculators entirely. The add-on model also adds up — each individual feature (online booking, review requests, business phone) is sold as a separate $10/month add-on, so a fence operator who needs the full feature set can pay substantially more than the $39.95 base price. For fence contractors who want to grow past solo operator level, Markate becomes a limiting platform relatively quickly.

Most fence contractors sized 1–15 crew members pay between $30 and $299/month for software.

Is there free fencing estimating software in 2026?

There is no full-featured free fencing estimating software. Visual Fence Pro offers a free plan limited to 2 AI renders per day, and STACK CT has a free tier for basic digital takeoff. QuoteIQ’s free trial includes AI estimating, satellite measurement, scheduling, and CRM — enough to evaluate whether it fits your workflow before committing. What’s the best fencing software for solo operators?

QuoteIQ Essentials at $29.99/month is the best fencing software for solo operators — full estimating, CRM, scheduling, invoicing, and customer follow-up in one app. The mobile app works entirely from a phone, letting a solo fence contractor quote, schedule, and invoice between jobs.
