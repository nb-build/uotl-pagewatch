CSR AI Chat Answering

July 13, 2026

Table of contents

Overview

CSR AI Chat Answering is a new feature designed to help save time by letting CSR AI independently and efficiently handle customer communications of incoming website chats on your website.

⚙️ Feature Requirement: To have CSR AI Chat, you should have the website chat bubble on your website. Click here to learn more.

What Does CSR AI Chat Answering Do?

Handles Website Chat Conversations: CSR AI responds to customer inquiries in real time, saving you time and ensuring consistent, high-quality service.

Works with your CSR AI settings: Define how you want CSR AI to handle your customers in CSR AI settings (learn more here).

How it works

Enablement: Go to Team → AI Team → CSR AI in your Housecall Pro account to enable CSR AI Chat.

📌 Note: You must have a website chat bubble on your website. Learn more here.

Customer Initiates Chat: When a customer starts a chat on your website chat widget, CSR AI responds and manages the conversation.

Escalation: If the customer requests a human, the chat is escalated, and you are notified.

Outcome Visibility: After the chat, you can see a summary of the conversation directly in your inbox.

What’s New!

Support for booking jobs, managing appointments, and more channels (SMS, email, or third-party integrations).

Settings & Customization: Pros will be able to enable/disable, as well as configure CSR AI skills and knowledge base directly in their settings.

FAQs

Can I turn this feature on or off myself?

Yes, go to your CSR AI settings to turn CSR AI Chat on or off.

Can the AI book jobs or send estimates?

Yes, now CSR AI Chat can book jobs, manage existing appointments, use custom scripts, and more.

What if a customer wants to talk to a real person?

The chat can be escalated to the org POC at any time, and they will be notified to join the chat and help the customer.

Where can I see the chat outcomes?

After the chat is completed or escalated, you’ll see a summary and notes in your inbox.
