Generate repeat business with Campaigns. Turn emails and text messages into paid jobs with Campaigns, our powerful marketing solution.

Campaigns is your built-in marketing engine, designed to turn emails, texts, and postcards into booked jobs and repeat business. With automated sends, dynamic personalization, AI-powered content, and professionally designed templates, you can engage the right customers at the right time, without juggling third-party tools. All housed in the Marketing Center, Campaigns makes it easy to save time, cut costs, and turn one-time customers into loyal advocates.

To access Campaigns:

Go to Marketing > Campaigns

How to Send an Automated Campaign

To send an automated campaign:

Go to Marketing > Campaigns

Click Create a campaign

Scroll down on the page and select Automated Campaigns

Choose the targeting you’d like to have for your automated campaign. You can:

Click New list to create a list from scratch

Select an existing list, or

Click the More icon (⋮) next to an existing list to edit list criteria

When creating a list, you can create a list based on:

Customer tags

Location

Lead source

Lifetime value

Creation date

Last service date

Customer type

Notification

The default customer exclusions that you cannot edit:

Are unsubscribed/opted-out

Are marked as Do not service

Have invalid contact info

Were last serviced more than three years ago

When you’re ready to review your list, click Save & preview in the top right corner of the page

Now you can choose a campaign flow template or build your own.

When building your own Campaign, you can start a campaign based on either a Job or an estimate. Edit the triggers and add filters based on what is best for your campaign.

Choose your Early exit rule by tapping on the “Early exit” button. You can choose when customers exit the campaign, and stop receiving comms from the Campaign based on:

New job is scheduled

New estimate is scheduled

Estimate is won or lost

Edit your time delays or messages by tapping on the card you want to edit.

📌 Note: Your custom text message number must be set up before sending automated text messages

To add a new step, tap the Add new button, and you can add an email, text message, or time delay.

When you’re done editing your campaign, tap Next and preview your campaign.

Understanding Delays

In a multi-step flow, each delay in your campaign counts from the completion of the previous step, not from the original trigger.

Example:

If your flow is:

Trigger: Job Completed

Delay: 1 week → Email sends

Delay: 2 weeks → Text message sends

The text message will be sent 3 weeks after the job is completed (1 week + 2 weeks), not 2 weeks. Think of each delay as the gap between that step and the one before it.
