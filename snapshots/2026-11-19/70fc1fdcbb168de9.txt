ClientbookWebsite design meets clienteling. The perfect match.

Punchmark and Clientbook have partnered to bring retail jewelers a unique new solution – integrating beautifully designed websites that attract new customers and drive more revenue with automated and scalable personal clienteling interactions.

All the Bells and Whistles

Punchmark and Clientbook have you covered throughout the entire customer journey, with the following features for jewelers that check all the boxes.

With Clientbook, you and your sales associates will be able to:

Automatically create task and life event reminders, receive alerts of upcoming life events for each client

Send text messages to clients without using their own phone or phone number

Gain visibility into your sales process and measure staff effectiveness

So, how do you turn a customer into a client?

Clientbook helps you and your salespeople become trusted advisors by turning first-time customers into lifelong clients. Whether you’re an owner, sales manager, or sales associate, Clientbook’s robust feature set will enable you to sell more. Period.

Owners

Offer a consistent clienteling experience for your customers and team. Gain visibility into sales interactions with your customers and sales team. Increase sales with a frictionless tool your sales associates will enjoy using.

Sales Managers

Get powerful data on your team’s clienteling activities. Easy-to-use interface and mobile app make for painless adoption. Increase sales through a consistent and responsive sales experience

Sales Associates

Make more sales through automation and improved outreach. Never forget a customer with follow-ups and life events. Engage with more clients by sharing products anytime, anywhere.
