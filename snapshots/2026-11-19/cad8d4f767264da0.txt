What We Do

Law Ruler relies on data-driven techniques and tools to help law firms of any size grow their practice. Our legal CRM empowers your law firm with effective marketing techniques and eliminates redundant manual tasks, so you can spend time making high-level strategic decisions for growth. By offering the best legal CRM software on the market today, we make marketing your firm and attracting new clients easier than ever before!

Legal CRM

Our advanced legal CRM system is designed to enhance existing workflows and eliminate client communication pain points. It lets you organize your leads, prospects, and existing clients, then automate critical communications with them so you can streamline your process and ensure you don’t miss out on prospective business.

Marketing Automation

Never lose touch with leads or clients again. With our marketing automation solutions, you can send personalized and automated texts and emails to ensure a positive experience and build better relationships with your clients.

Client Intake

With customizable logic-based forms and streamlined client intake tools like document assembly and eSignature, you can streamline the client intake process while still delivering a five-star experience to your clients.

Dashboard Analytics
