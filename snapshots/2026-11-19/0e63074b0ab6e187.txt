BisTrack Elevates User Experience with 2024.2

Every six months, the BisTrack team delivers strategic product releases, incorporating valuable customer feedback from the Ideas Portal.

Our latest release, 2024.2, is a significant step forward, focusing on expanded browser functionality and feature enhancements to improve the overall BisTrack user experience while driving significant business impact.

With a range of new capabilities and enhancements, 2024.2 is designed to streamline your workflows, boost productivity, and deliver unparalleled results.

Explore the full range of enhancements and new capabilities in 2024.2 below:

BisTrack Business Applications and Integrations

New business applications (add-on modules) are now available for BisTrack, and existing integrations have been enhanced to include exciting new capabilities:

Advanced Demand Planning and Inventory Management

BisTrack's new Epicor IP&O Integration leverages AI to optimize demand planning, inventory management, and supply chain analytics, empowering users to boost sales, reduce costs, and enhance forecast accuracy.

Intuitive Dashboards and Interactive Visualizations

BisTrack's new integration with Epicor Grow Business Intelligence empowers users to make data-driven decisions through easy-to-build, easy-to-use dashboards and interactive visualizations. [Currently in Controlled Release].

Enhanced Epicor Financials Functionality and Migration Path

BisTrack's enhanced integration with Epicor Financials helps ensure a smooth chart of accounts transition, reducing blockers and saving significant time for our users. An Epicor Financials migration path has been developed for Microsoft Dynamics GP users.

Optimized Connected Storefronts

BisTrack's enhanced integration with Epicor Commerce improves item sync data accuracy and the end-user experience, optimizing connected storefronts.

Expand Your Payment Options

BisTrack's integration with Epicor Payment Application has been enhanced with a new EPA in the Cloud offering that allows you to make use of EPA without providing and maintaining your own EPA servers.

AI-Powered Fleet Routing

BisTrack’s integration with Trimble has been enhanced to provide Multi-Vehicle Routing, which integrates with Journey Planner and leverages AI to save users multiple hours per week by reducing or eliminating manual route creation for their fleets. Users have full control over the recommended route, allowing them to adjust it to fit their unique business requirements.

BisTrack Web: A New Era of Browser-Based Productivity

BisTrack Web is a browser-based user experience of BisTrack. This interface offers an enhanced user experience, more personalization, and better extensibility. The development team has made significant progress in migrating desktop features to the web, enabling a richer user experience, more capabilities, and greater customization to help you scale.

Epicor Identity

Epicor Identity is now available for hosted users as a centralized security solution that simplifies user access across multiple Epicor systems, including BisTrack. Epicor Identity strengthens security by reducing the risk of unauthorized access, improves efficiency by streamlining user onboarding and offboarding processes, and enhances the user experience by allowing users to log in once to access multiple Epicor systems.

Upgrade today to unlock the full potential of BisTrack

Already a BisTrack user? The Epicor Services and Support teams are ready to help you upgrade today. Contact your Customer Account Manager to get started.
