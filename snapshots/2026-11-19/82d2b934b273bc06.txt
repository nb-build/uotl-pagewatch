Release Notes

RouteOne Release Notes

We add monthly enhancements to the RouteOne platform to help our dealers create optimal F&I experiences that can lead to additional sales and increased customer satisfaction.

Reach out to your RouteOne Business Development Manager or the RouteOne Service Desk at 866.768.8301 with any release-related questions.

November 2026 Release Notes

eContracting

Updated Forms

CT and IL dealers only - The updated CT and IL Reynolds and Reynolds LAW® forms will become active and available in the system beginning December 1, 2026.

Please note that these update forms support a second trade-in vehicle; however, the corresponding UI will not be available until December 2, 2026.

October 2026 Release Notes

Credit

Enhanced ID Management for Group DSAs

Group DSAs can now add and manage Captive IDs, Third Party IDs, and Menu IDs within the administration tool, making it easier to configure and maintain user profiles.

Updated RouteOne Vehicle Classification Service

We’ve recently upgraded our Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) decoding platform. Now when you enter a VIN in the RouteOne Credit Application or Vehicle Valuation tool, you may notice Improved style-level detail for some makes and models, requiring fewer manual selections and helping improve accuracy.

In addition, commercial medium-duty VINs are now supported. Learn more about our Vehicle Values tool.

Menu Tab Renamed

To provide greater clarity, the ‘Menu’ tab in the RouteOne platform has been renamed ‘F&I Menu.’ The updated name better reflects the functionality available within the tab and helps users more easily locate Menu-related features.

eContracting

Updated Forms

KS dealers only - The Reynolds and Reynolds LAW® Forms for Exchange Bank & Trust have been updated.

CA dealers only- The Reynolds and Reynolds LAW® Forms for Bankers Systems® have been updated.

PA dealers only - The Reynolds and Reynolds LAW® forms have been updated to support a new Delivery and Handling Charge field.

On-Demand

Dealers may notice a change as we transition to a new Dealer System Provider (DSP) screen accessed by a button on the existing page. Over the next several months, DSPs will be migrated from the existing PARTNERAPI module to three separate modules for RouteOne On-Demand Credit Automation, Document Exchange, and Automated eContracting. If you have questions or changes to your services with the DSP, please contact them directly.

September 2026 Release Notes

CARS Compliance Related

Product Updates for California CARS Act Compliance

We’ve made the following updates below to help our dealers manage compliance with the California CARS Act, which becomes effective on October 1, 2026.

Digital Retail

Email Retention

Retention of consumer-facing emails to the Deal Jacket to support the document retention requirement. For SecureDocOne subscribers, these communications would then be included in long-term storage.

Fusion Updates

Updates to our enhanced Digital Retail solution, Fusion, include clearer pricing disclosures, consumer disclaimers, aftermarket product disclosures, and regulatory and legal compliance improvements.

eContracting

Dealer configurable settings are now available that allow dealers to make the eSign Consent Form eSignable.

Updated Forms

Updated CA Reynolds and Reynolds LAW® Forms - Effective September 16, 2026, the "Used Vehicle Contract Cancellation Option Agreement" amount will no longer populate automatically from the DMS and must be entered manually on the Contract Worksheet. Beginning October 1, 2026, this field will no longer be available on the Contract Worksheet.

California dealers will have access to the latest version of the Volvo, GMF, and HCA Lease forms.

The CA Stellantis Lease Worksheet has been updated and will now display "N/A" in the Trade-In, Turn-In, and Other Agreements field. This field will be automatically populated and disabled from editing.

Menu

Updated disclosure text and placement for the print menu.

Updated confirmation and disclosure page for the digital menu.

August 2026 Release Notes

Credit

New Controls for Credit Bureau Preferences in RouteOne

Dealership System Administrators (DSAs) now have additional control over credit bureau selection and usage in RouteOne.

These new preferences allow DSAs to set a default credit bureau for users and prevent users running multiple credit bureaus at a time.

These settings can also help guide user behavior and support cost-management efforts.

July 2026 Release Notes

Credit

Enhanced Customer Search Performance

A date filter has been added to the Customer Search Page for faster page loading. It defaults to the last 30 days of customer activity, with options to view 60 or 90 days and save your preference for future searches.

This update helps improve page loading performance while making it easier to focus on recent business activity.

Updated Signature Fields on Review Documents

On August 5, 2026, the Date and Title fields in the Black Signature Block will no longer display "N/A" on review copies of contracts, ancillary and deal form documents when signatures or initials are required. "N/A" will continue to appear only in situations where it is applicable, such as when no co-buyer exists.

Digital Retail

Enhanced Third-Party Lead Notification Controls

Dealers can now customize Digital Retail–initiated third-party lead notifications with more precise settings, including configuration by sub-product (Pre-Qual, Lead, Fusion Quote Leads) and Online Credit Application lead identifiers.

January 2026 Release Notes

Credit

More Ways to Access RouteOne Menu from the Credit Platform

There are several ways RouteOne Menu subscribers can access RouteOne Menu from the RouteOne Credit platform.

In Deal Manager, App Manager, and Credit Card Manager, click the Edit/More button on a deal to open up RouteOne Menu in a new tab by navigating to Deals>Deal Manager> Edit/More.

You can also access Menu directly from the Credit Decision Summary page, from the Deal Jacket/Deal Structure, or click on the Menu tab at the top of your RouteOne platform.
