Work together, seamlessly

Fin and your team work from the same inbox and customer record. When Fin needs an approval or a piece of information, it asks a teammate and finishes the conversation itself.

Self-improving system

Fin constantly improves by learning from your best human reps, and suggestions and assistance from Fin make your human team better. With everything in one system, managers get recommendations on what to fix, from missing content to data integrations, and can act on them with one click.

Centralized Knowledge Hub

Centralize, manage, and optimize all of the information that powers Fin, human, and self-serve support. See how your content is performing across every touchpoint including Fin, Copilot and on your Help Center with detailed reporting.
