Product · Services

One AI Agent for Intake, Quoting and Proposals.

One AI Agent executes every stage of distribution, taking an account from raw lead to a proposal in your client's hands, with every data point synced to Applied along the way.

01 · Intake

Hand-off Client Discovery to an AI Agent.

Upload a dec page or renewal document and the agent extracts the data instantly. It researches the same verified sources your team uses, analyzes property imagery, and completes the submission, with everything saved for E&O documentation.

02 · Quoting

Hand-off Quoting to an AI Agent.

The agent opens its own browser, logs into your carrier portals, and crawls every screen the way your best CSR would, returning accurate, bindable rates for every market in your appetite.

03 · Proposing

Hand-off Proposals to an AI Agent.
