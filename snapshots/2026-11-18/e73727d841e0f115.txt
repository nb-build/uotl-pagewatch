What is a Talent Intelligence Platform?

In short? The end to both the legacy ATS and the point solutions that have sustained them for so long.

One system that unifies the entire recruiting lifecycle — sourcing, CRM, ATS, outreach, people data, Agents and AI — and learns as you use it.

Legacy ATS

A database that stores your work and nothing more — candidates sit in a system that never learns.

Relationships live in your head, a spreadsheet, or a recruiter's inbox — and walk out the door when they do.

Sourcing means manually toggling between social platforms, job boards, and data sources.

Outreach is manual, inconsistent, and only as good as whoever sent the last email.

Business development and recruiting live in separate tools, so BD context never reaches the recruiting team.

Contact data is stale, third-party, and wrong half the time — so your best outreach still bounces.

AI is merely bolted onto your workflow — and trained on someone else's data.

Automation requires a separate tool, a Zapier workaround, or an engineer — and breaks when the API changes.

Talent Intelligence Platform

An enterprise-grade ATS that moves every candidate through your pipeline, connected to everything else.

A true Recruiting CRM that builds and nurtures talent communities that compound over time.

Native sourcing with 850 million people and millions of companies built in, not bolted on.

Multi-channel outreach sequences that mirror your best recruiter — at scale.

A unified Sales CRM and prospecting platform that manages BD and client relationships in one place.

People and company data Loxo owns — verified mobile, personal, and work contact details.

Proprietary AI across the whole platform, with models that get smarter with every successful search.

Proprietary AI Agents are at work across the platform, handling the work so your team focuses on relationships.

OUR POINT OF VIEW

AI-native is a label. 2013 is a date.

If AI-native means built around artificial intelligence from the first day, Loxo has been AI-native since 2013. The 2013 loxo.co homepage described the next generation ATS built around artificial intelligence and machine intelligence, and the first Loxo AI shipped in 2015.

An AI-native ATS and CRM is where a Talent Intelligence Platform starts: two of its fourteen pillars. Loxo started there in 2013. The other twelve are the thirteen years since.

A firm that only wants AI on top of its ATS and CRM records does not need a new ATS/CRM for that. Loxo’s API and MCP already connect a firm’s record to the AI assistant it uses every day.

The step up from an AI-native ATS is a Talent Intelligence Platform. Loxo has been building that one around artificial intelligence since 2013.

A REPEATABLE WORKFLOW

One workflow to rule them all

At Loxo, we believe that your workflow is your competitive advantage — and a repeatable workflow leads to repeatable quality.

When your entire team is working on a shared, end-to-end workflow, leaders can identify (and double-down on) winning behaviors, spot bottlenecks early, and forecast confidently.

That’s why we built our platform from first-principles, with the ideal recruitment workflow at the very center — so you can keep getting better, with every search.

AN END-TO-END PLATFORM

See everything in one place

Consolidation isn’t just about saving clicks (although that’s nice, too). It’s about gaining the visibility you need to know what’s working, what isn’t, and why.

With all of your data in one source-of-truth platform, you not only save time but gain confidence.

By consolidating your ATS/CRM, sourcing, outreach, and more in one place, Loxo eliminates busywork and helps teams move faster — without sacrificing quality.
