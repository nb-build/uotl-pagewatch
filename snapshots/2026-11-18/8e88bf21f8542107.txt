How it works

From dormant lead to live phone call

One coordinated team of AI agents acquires your leads, works them with AI, and hands warm, ready-to-talk prospects directly to you. Here's the relay:

01

Social & email pull them back

Dynamic listing alerts and retargeting ads keep your brand in front of every lead — and drive them back to your IDX site instead of the portals.

02

The platform spots intent

Repeat views, favorites, shares, new saved searches — real signals that someone's getting ready to move.

03

AI Text starts the conversation

It opens with context it already has, replies in under 60 seconds, and surfaces the ones who are actually ready to talk — at a 48% response rate, 3x the industry average.

04

AI Voice takes over

As the conversation heats up, Voice calls as your assistant, qualifies on timeline and budget, and arranges a warm transfer — persisting for up to 90 days per lead.

05

You take the baton and close

A ready-to-talk buyer rings your phone or lands on your calendar. That's the only step that's yours — answer the call and close the deal.

Straight answers

The questions everyone asks

Why do I need a demo to get pricing?

Because your number depends on your market, your ad budget, your team size, and the size of your database. Generic tiers would either overcharge small operations or undersell what a big database can return. The demo exists to give you a real, defensible number — not to pressure you. Most clients get a full quote inside a single 30-minute call.

Are Ylopo leads exclusive?

Yes. Every lead your campaigns generate is 100% yours — never sold, shared, or recycled to another agent. Your brand is on every ad, every text, and every listing alert.

I already have a CRM. Do I have to switch?

No. Keep your CRM. The agents work alongside what you already use — keep your CRM, and replace the Frankenstein stack of point tools bolted around it.

I don't trust AI talking to my leads.

It's trained by seven Realtors-in-Residence on millions of real conversations — so it sounds natural, not robotic. And it doesn't replace you: it warm-transfers a live, ready-to-talk person straight to your phone. You still close every deal.

How is this different from BoldTrail, CINC, Lofty, or Zillow Flex?

Ylopo replaces the bolted-together stack of point tools with one orchestrated AI team — acquisition, AI Text, AI Voice, re-engagement, and dynamic listing alerts working together, feeding your existing CRM. Your strategist can walk through a side-by-side for whichever platform you're comparing.

I'm a solo agent — is this overkill?

Solo agents are some of Ylopo's strongest results (see Johnny Apple's $9K → $21K in his first 90 days above). The AI does the follow-up a team of ISAs would — which matters most when the team is just you.

I'm not technical. Is this complicated to run?

It's done for you. There's nothing to learn and nothing to build. The agents arrive optimized and trained on how to convert real estate leads, and a dedicated campaign management team is included.

What happens in the first 30–60–90 days?

Campaigns launch and your site goes live in the first weeks; AI begins working new leads immediately and can re-engage your existing database from day one. Leads typically start flowing within the first month, with appointment volume building as the AI's 90-day follow-up cycles compound.

Do I keep my leads if I ever cancel?

Yes — your leads are yours, full stop. They live in your CRM and stay with you.
