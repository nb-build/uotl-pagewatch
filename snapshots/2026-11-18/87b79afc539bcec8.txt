Dealertrack NADA FAQs

What is Dealertrack showcasing at NADA 2026 in Las Vegas?

Dealertrack is showcasing its seamlessly integrated, industry-leading ecosystem of lenders, OEMs, and aftermarket providers—designed to drive faster deals, smarter workflows, and greater profitability. Now enhanced with AI-driven fraud detection and intelligent automations, Dealertrack empowers dealers to operate more quickly and cost-effectively.

Where can I find Dealertrack at the NADA EXPO in Las Vegas?

Dealertrack will be featured at the Cox Automotive booths inside the Las Vegas Convention Center.

What’s new from Dealertrack this year at NADA?

Innovations built to help you move business forward with speed, flexibility, and confidence:

AI-powered fraud detection integrated into deal compliance

Access to 500+ lenders on the largest automotive digital contracting network

Award-winning DMS with a growing network of 220+ certified third-party integrations to work your deals your way

Enhanced F&I platform for fast, flexible, and compliant dealmaking

Can I schedule a demo with Dealertrack at NADA 2026?

Yes! Use the form on this page to book a personalized demo.

Are there exclusive Dealertrack promotions at NADA Las Vegas?

Yes, attendees can access special offers and early access to new tools by visiting the Dealertrack booth or registering online.

What can I expect during a demo of Dealertrack at the NADA event?

Product experts will be available at the booth to consult with you about your goals, answer questions, provide demos, and guide you through new features.

Is Cox Automotive participating in any panels or speaking sessions at NADA 2026?

Yes, Jessica Stafford, Senior Vice President of Consumer Solutions at Cox Automotive and Derek Hanson, Vice President of Operations at vAuto and Cox Automotive will lead a workshop, Master Digital Leadership: Defeat Disruption, Drive Value on Wednesday, February 4, 12:30 – 1:15 PM CT. Additional speakers will appear on the Cox Autmotive stage throughout the Expo.

Thursday, February 5 — 8:30am-5pm
Friday, February 6 — 8:30am-12:30pm
