Attract & Retain Clients

Online booking

Take the pressure off your front desk with an internal calendar and 24/7 online booking that keeps your days full without the constant phone calls.

Pet parent app

Give pet owners easy access to appointments, records, invoices, and messages in one simple app that keeps them connected to your clinic 24/7.

Marketing suite

Attract, engage, and retain clients with built-in marketing tools for campaigns, promotions, and targeted outreach.

SMS and chat

Follow up with clients instantly, share lab results, and request photos. Give pet parents direct access to your team when they need it.

Appointment reminders

Automatically remind clients about appointments and treatments via email, text, and push notifications. Reduce no-shows, improve compliance, and keep your schedule full.

Client notifications

Keep clients informed at every step with automated updates for appointments, invoices, care instructions, and follow-ups.

AI intake and forms

Let our AI handle the paperwork before the patient even arrives. Automatically collect and summarize patient history, freeing up your front desk and preparing your team for the visit.

Increase Revenue

Wellness plans

Build client loyalty and generate predictable, recurring revenue with customizable wellness plans. Bundle preventive care services to encourage compliance and improve patient health.

Treatment plans

Create, approve, and manage treatment plans effortlessly, with estimates, invoices, and client consent all connected in one smooth workflow.

AI Invoice automation

Generate and share invoices instantly with automatic charge capture, making checkout faster, cleaner, and stress-free for both staff and clients.

Automated billing

Reduce admin time and missed payments with automated billing workflows that handle invoices, recurring charges, and payment reminders for you.

Buy now–pay later

Offer flexible payment options that help clients get the care they need while ensuring your clinic gets paid.

Telemedicine

Provide care beyond the clinic walls with remote consultations that offer owners peace of mind and your team more flexibility.

Payment processing

Eliminate missed charges and streamline checkout with secure, integrated payments. Capture deposits, keep cards on file, and automate invoicing to ensure you get paid on time, every time.

Drive Clinic Efficiency

AI medical records

Automatically generate clear, structured medical records from your clinical data to save time, reduce errors, and improve consistency.

AI SOAP dictation

Dictate your notes naturally and let AI turn your voice into accurate, structured SOAP records — faster documentation with less typing.

AI invoice generation

Automatically create accurate invoices based on services and treatments, reducing missed charges and speeding up checkout.

Scheduling calendar

Manage appointments, resources, and staff schedules in one intuitive calendar that keeps your day running smoothly.

Flowboard

Visualize and manage your clinic’s entire workflow in real-time. Our intuitive flowboard gives your team at-a-glance visibility into patient status, from check-in to discharge.

Patient History

Get a complete view of every patient’s journey in a single, easy-to-read timeline. Instantly access medical records, communications, and past treatments for faster, more informed care.

Whiteboard

Manage hospitalized patients and complex cases with a digital whiteboard. Track treatments, monitor progress, and ensure seamless handoffs for optimal patient care.

Make better decisions

Financial reporting

Get real-time visibility into revenue, payments, and profitability with clear financial reports that support smarter decisions.

Operational reporting

Understand how your clinic runs day to day with insights into utilization, bottlenecks, and team efficiency.

Performance dashboards

Track key KPIs at a glance with customizable dashboards that turn complex data into clear, actionable insights.

Inventory analytics

Monitor usage, costs, and trends with inventory analytics that help you reduce waste, prevent stockouts, and control spend.

Charge-capture insights

Identify missed or under-captured charges with built-in insights that protect revenue and improve billing accuracy.

Audit logs

Maintain transparency and accountability with detailed audit logs that track system activity for compliance and peace of mind.
