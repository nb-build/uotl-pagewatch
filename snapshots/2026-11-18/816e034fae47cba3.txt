About Real Geeks

Technology that helps real estate professionals grow.

Real Geeks builds practical tools that help real estate professionals grow their business. Our goal is to give agents and teams a single system that makes lead generation, organization, and follow-up easier to manage.

Real Geeks started in 2007 on a beach in Hawaii.

Jeff, a successful real estate agent, and Kevin, a Purdue computer science graduate, set out to build a better website and CRM for Jeff’s business. They quickly realized how powerful it could be when a real estate website and CRM worked together as one system. That idea became Real Geeks.

As more agents discovered the platform, Real Geeks continued to grow. The company relocated to Dallas in 2016, and in 2017 Fidelity National Financial acquired Real Geeks. Today Kevin leads the company as the team continues to expand across the country, with employees in Hawaii and more than ten other states.

From the beginning, the goal has stayed the same: help real estate professionals grow their business. What started as a website and CRM has grown into a connected platform that helps agents attract leads, manage conversations, and stay organized as their business grows.

Even as the company has grown, Real Geeks has kept the spirit it started with in Hawaii. The team values collaboration, respects different perspectives, and enjoys building tools that make real estate professionals’ lives easier.
