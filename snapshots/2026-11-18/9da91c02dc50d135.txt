Run Your Agency on JenesisNow

JenesisNow is the agency management system at the center of everything Jenesis builds. It holds your clients, policies, carrier downloads, commissions, and daily workflow in one place, so your team stops working across six tools and a spreadsheet to answer one question.

Client & Policy Management

Every client, policy, document, and note lives in one record, so anyone on your team can answer a question without hunting through four systems.

Carrier Downloads

Policy data arrives from your carriers automatically and posts to the right client, which removes the rekeying that eats your service team’s mornings.

Communications & Automation

Send email and text from inside the client record, and let renewal and follow-up reminders go out on their own instead of depending on memory.

Commission Management

Track what each carrier owes you, reconcile it against what actually arrived, and find the gaps before they quietly become lost revenue.

Reporting & Insights

See your book by carrier, line, producer, and renewal month, so planning conversations start with real numbers instead of someone’s best guess.

Integrations

Connect the raters, accounting tools, and phone systems your agency already runs on, so the system fits your workflow rather than replacing it.
