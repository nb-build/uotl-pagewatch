AI Receptionist Price Tracker: Every Price Change Since 2024

Every published price change at seven AI receptionist and answering services since January 2024, dated from archived pricing pages, plus UK prices in pounds.

Key points

Seven US AI receptionist and answering services changed their published prices 23 times between January 2024 and October 2026: 11 price rises, 11 plan redesigns and 1 cut.

Human answering services rose steadily: Ruby and Smith.ai about 5% to 8% on the same plans, PATLive 22% to 42% before it moved to minute bundles in September 2026.

AI receptionist apps change more often and in bigger steps. Entry prices rose 34% at Goodcall and 80% at My AI Front Desk, and both unlimited plans we saw were later replaced or capped.

Every change links to the archived pricing page before and after it, and the data is free to download.

Three patterns stand out:

"Unlimited" doesn't last. Goodcall's $59 unlimited-minutes plan was replaced by tiers billed per unique caller in late 2024. Rosie moved every plan to unlimited minutes in October 2025, then capped them at 250, 1,000 and 2,000 minutes in March 2026 at the same prices.

Overage rates rise quietly. My AI Front Desk's extra minutes went from about $0.12 to $0.25 between October 2025 and March 2026. Smith.ai AI kept its plan prices in summer 2026 but raised the extra-call rate on each plan by 50c to 60c.

Plans get redesigned every year or so. My AI Front Desk went from one plan, to two, to four, to a single plan with credits in 30 months. Smith.ai AI restructured twice in 2026.

The one cut came from Smith.ai AI in September 2025, which lowered the cost of an extra call from $4.25 to $2.40 on its smallest plan and included more calls in every plan.

What does this mean if you're choosing one?

Price the plan on your own call volume, then add a rise. Human services have gone up most years. Budgeting a few percent more for next year is realistic for the services we track. Our calculator prices your volume on today's published plans.

Read the overage rate, not just the plan price. Several of the changes above left the headline price alone and raised the price of each extra minute, call or caller.

Don't plan around "unlimited". Both unlimited plans we tracked were replaced or capped, one within five months.

Ask whether your price is protected. Some providers keep existing customers on their old rate: Answer.co.uk says customers of its human pay-as-you-go service who joined before 23 June 2025 stay on £1.75 a call plus VAT, against £2.20 for new customers.

Check what you're billed by. Minutes, calls, unique callers and flat fees behave very differently as your volume grows. Our guide explains how each one works out.

How we build this tracker

Providers: the seven US providers in our cost guide. AnswerConnect is also in that guide, but answerconnect.com is excluded from the Wayback Machine, so it has no US history here.

Captures: for each pricing page we listed every archived capture from January 2024 to October 2026 and read roughly one a month, more than 130 in all. A price only counts if it's in the archived page itself. Where a page loads its prices with JavaScript that the archive didn't keep, we leave it out: Rosie's overage rate after March 2026 is one.

Dates: a change is dated as a window between two captures, never a single day, because we can't see what happened between them.

What's not counted: promotions, annual-billing discounts and add-ons. We compare monthly prices in US dollars, or pounds for UK providers.

Updates: we recheck every provider each quarter. The next update is due in January 2027.
