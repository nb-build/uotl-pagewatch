FAQs

Got questions? We’ve got answers.

What is webinar software?

Webinar software is a digital platform that enables organizations to host one-to-many broadcast presentations, connecting presenters with large audiences through video, audio, and content sharing. Unlike regular video conferencing, webinar software is specifically designed for structured presentations where hosts control the experience and attendees primarily listen and engage through interactive tools like polls, Q&A, and chat.

What types of events is Zoom's webinar software best for?

Zoom's webinar software is ideal for a wide range of events including marketing webinars, product demonstrations, training sessions, town halls, virtual events, and educational seminars. It's particularly effective for both internal communications like company-wide announcements and external events such as lead generation webinars, customer onboarding, and thought leadership presentations. The platform scales from intimate team presentations to large-scale virtual conferences and hybrid events with thousands of attendees.

How many attendees can Zoom's webinar software support?

Zoom Webinars can support up to 100,000 attendees with our proven, reliable platform that delivers industry-leading HD video and audio quality. Our webinar software is built on Zoom's trusted infrastructure, providing consistent performance whether you're hosting a small team presentation or a stadium-sized virtual event.

Does Zoom's webinar software offer features for both live and pre-recorded events?

Yes, Zoom's webinar software supports both live and pre-recorded events through our Simulive feature. With Simulive presentations, you can pre-record your webinar content and broadcast it as if it's happening live, while still engaging with your audience in real-time through chat, polls, and Q&A. This gives you all the impact of a live event with significantly less pressure on presenters.

How does Zoom's webinar software make webinars interactive?

Zoom's webinar software includes comprehensive engagement tools designed to turn passive viewers into active participants. Features include real-time polls, live Q&A sessions, interactive chat, audience reactions, and breakout rooms for small-group discussions. Additionally, attendees can engage before they even join through branded registration pages and immersive event lobbies that elevate your brand experience.

Is a webinar the same as Zoom Meetings?

No, webinars and Zoom Meetings serve different purposes and audiences. Zoom Meetings are designed for collaborative, two-way conversations where all participants can share video, audio, and screen content freely. Zoom Webinars are structured for one-to-many broadcasting, where hosts and panelists present to an audience that primarily listens and engages through controlled interactive features like polls and Q&A rather than open video/audio participation.

How much is Zoom webinars?

Zoom Webinars pricing varies based on attendee capacity and features needed. Basic webinar licenses start at competitive rates for smaller audiences, while enterprise-grade solutions support thousands of attendees with advanced features like custom branding, detailed analytics, and premium support. For specific pricing tailored to your organization's needs and attendee requirements, we recommend contacting our sales team or exploring our pricing page for current rates.
