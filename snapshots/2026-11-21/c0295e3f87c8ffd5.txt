Frequently Asked Questions

What is practice management software for accounting firms?

Practice management software is the operating system accounting, bookkeeping, and tax firms use to manage every client engagement in one place: workflows, client communication, document collection, time tracking, billing, and reporting. Instead of stitching together 5-9 disconnected apps (spreadsheets, project tools, email, e-signature, payment platforms), a firm management software like Financial Cents gives your team one place to see what’s due, who’s doing it, and what every client needs next.

How does Financial Cents help with firm management?

Financial Cents brings every part of running a small firm into one platform: workflow management and recurring engagements, an accounting CRM with native QuickBooks Online sync, a client portal for document requests and e-signature, time tracking and billing, proposals, and team capacity and reporting. Instead of jumping between tools, your team works in one dashboard where every task is connected to a client, an engagement, and a deadline.

Is Financial Cents really built for small and growing firms?

Yes, small and growing firms are who we built this for, not an entry-level tier we serve reluctantly. From a 2-person bookkeeping shop to a 30-person CAS firm, you’re the customer. Financial Cents is practice management software for small and growing accounting firms, whether you choose to stay small or scale on your own terms. While many practice management platforms are moving upmarket, we’re going the other way, with pricing, onboarding, and a product designed for owners who still answer their own client emails.

What manual processes in my firm can Financial Cents automate?

Financial Cents automates the repetitive work that eats your week: recurring engagement setup (monthly bookkeeping, quarterly closes, annual tax prep create themselves on schedule), client document requests with auto-reminders until clients respond, status updates so your team always knows what’s next, and workflow templates that build themselves into projects with assignments and due dates. Manual chasing, copy-pasting between tools, and “did anyone do this yet?” disappears.

What can Financial Cents AI do?

Financial Cents AI works on your client documents, your communication, and your firm’s data. Three AI agents handle every client upload: AI File Validator checks it against what you requested and flags the wrong year, wrong bank, or wrong document before it reaches your team; AI File Renaming renames it to your firm’s naming convention; and AI File Routing files a copy in the right client folder. Financial Cents AI also drafts client emails and generates workflow checklist templates from a single prompt. The three file agents are available on every plan, and on the Scale plan, the Financial Cents MCP connects your firm’s data to AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT.

Is Financial Cents secure and SOC 2 certified?

Yes. Financial Cents is SOC 2 Type 1 certified by AICPA, with TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, encryption at rest, multi-factor authentication, audit logging, and SOC 2 / SOC 3 / GDPR / HIPAA compliant infrastructure through Digital Ocean and AWS. Canadian firms can also choose Canadian data residency to keep client data inside Canadian borders. Full disclosure of our security practices is on our security page.

Can I use Financial Cents for tax practice management?

Yes, Financial Cents is built for tax, bookkeeping, and accounting firms. Tax firms use it for client onboarding, document collection, workflow tracking, and delivery through the secure portal. Collect everything you need to start a return with Tax Organizers — smart questionnaires clients complete in the portal, with conditional logic so they only see what applies and automatic reminders that chase for you. Get documents signed with built-in e-signatures — included at no extra cost on every paid plan, with optional KBA identity verification for IRS forms like the 8879. On the Scale plan, the Google Drive integration lets you print tax returns from Drake Tax, UltraTax, Lacerte, ProSeries, ProConnect, CCH Axcess, and TaxAct directly into the client’s Financial Cents folder.

Does Financial Cents include e-signatures and tax organizers?

Yes. Tax Organizers are included on every plan — build a reusable questionnaire, send it from any project, and collect answers and documents in the client portal. Built-in e-signatures are included at no extra cost on every paid plan (free trials include up to 5), so you can get engagement letters, authorizations, and other PDFs signed inside a client task, with optional KBA identity verification for documents that need it.

Is Financial Cents easy for my team to learn?

Yes, Financial Cents was voted the easiest-to-use accounting practice management software on Capterra by verified customer reviews (2026 Best Ease of Use award). Most teams are productive on day one, with built-in tutorials and a dashboard designed for accountants and bookkeepers, not project managers. Onboarding support is included on every plan, and the typical team is fully running on Financial Cents within two weeks.

Do I need a credit card or talk to sales to start the free trial?

No, start a 14-day free trial with just your details and company info. No credit card required, no demo required, no sales call required. Most practice platforms make you book a sales call and sit through a demo before they’ll even let you try the product. Financial Cents lets you sign up, import your clients, run real workflows, and decide for yourself before you talk to anyone. Or if you’d rather see it walked through first, book a free demo and a real person on our team will give you a personalized tour.
