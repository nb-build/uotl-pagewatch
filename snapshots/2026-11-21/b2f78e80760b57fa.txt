Our Story

We ran an MSP for 15 years and managed strategy in spreadsheets. Strategy Overview is the vCIO and QBR automation software we wished we had, built by the brothers who lived the problem.

Born inside a working MSP

We started an MSP in 2002, Red Key Solutions, and grew it into one of the best managed IT companies across New York City, Connecticut, Los Angeles and Florida. When we moved to a full managed model in 2009, one thing became obvious fast: we had to standardize our stack and give every client a real digital transformation roadmap.

We ran that process on spreadsheets for eight years. It worked, but it did not scale. So we turned the spreadsheet into a QBR app. The first beta saved Red Key’s own vCIOs hundreds of hours a year, and we knew every MSP shared the same frustration.

Founded by brothers Alex and Dimitri Markov, Strategy Overview became its own company in 2017. We are just getting started.

Alex and Dimitri Markov

2002

Founded Red Key Solutions

Our own MSP in New York, where the whole playbook was learned client by client.

2015

The spreadsheet becomes an app

First proof of concept: converting our assessment spreadsheet into software.

2017

Launched at IT Nation

Version 1 released to the community, and Strategy Overview becomes its own company.

2021

Rebuilt from the ground up

Two years of work ships as version 3: an entirely new platform built for the long term.

2023

Warranty updating and the client portal

The warranty engine launches, Halo and Syncro integrations ship, and the white labeled portal gets custom domains.

2024

The Plan module, and Arya is born

Plans with owners and dates ship in September. In November, Arya, the world's first vCIO assistant, says hello.

2025

QBR by Arya and a European datacenter

Arya starts building report content, grades and risk write-ups. EU partners get their own datacenter.

2026

Tickets, 30+ integrations and the open platform

The portal Tickets module ships, the integration family passes 30 apps, Strategy Academy live training launches, and the Open API + MCP server arrive in September.
