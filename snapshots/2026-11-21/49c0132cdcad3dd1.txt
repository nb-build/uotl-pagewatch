By Victor Raessen, Founder & CEO·Updated 31 October 2026

ConnectWise CPQ (formerly Sell, and before that Quosal) is the quoting module in the ConnectWise suite. If you are moving off it, the hard part is rarely the new tool. It is your catalog, your templates and the quotes still open. Here is how Salesbuildr compares, why MSPs switch, and how the switch runs without a gap in quoting.

Where the difference is

ConnectWise CPQ's integration docs need a ConnectWise sign-in, so the public pages don't show field by field what it writes into ConnectWise.

Salesbuildr documents Halo next to ConnectWise. Company and contact changes arrive in real time, so reps quote the right customer and contact without re-keying. The won quote can write the Halo sales order, the ConnectWise agreement and the fulfilment ticket, so nobody retypes the order and billing lines are not missed or mistyped.

Salesbuildr reads ConnectWise agreements, Autotask recurring service contracts and Halo recurring invoices back in. Whitespace shows what each client already has, so upsell, cross-sell and renewal proposals start from the contract instead of a spreadsheet.

Storefront, a Premium add-on, lets clients order standard items themselves without a rep building a quote.

Why MSPs switch from ConnectWise CPQ

Your ConnectWise opportunity stays current. Within 1 to 2 minutes of approval it shows the quote lines and the signed PDF. You then create the agreement and fulfilment ticket from the approved quote, so nobody retypes the order and billing lines are not missed or mistyped.

Distributor products without manual setup. Search Pax8 and your other connected distributors from inside the quote and add products at distributor pricing and stock. A new distributor product is created in ConnectWise the first time it is added to a proposal.

Room to grow beyond the quote. Whitespace analysis (Advanced plan and up) reads the agreements in ConnectWise Manage and shows which clients are missing which services, so upsell, cross-sell and renewal proposals start from accurate contract data instead of spreadsheets. Salesbuildr also syncs with Autotask and Halo, so your quoting is not tied to one PSA vendor.

How switching from ConnectWise CPQ works

Connect your PSA. Companies, contacts and opportunities come from your PSA, so you do not re-enter them. Your catalog, templates and bundles are set up in Salesbuildr during onboarding.

Import your catalog. Import your catalog and set up your first templates on your branding during onboarding.

Run both tools side by side. Pick a cutover date. Open quotes finish in the old tool.

Go live in 2 to 4 weeks. Most MSPs are live within 2 to 4 weeks of kickoff, with a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Questions about switching

Can I keep ConnectWise CPQ running while I switch?

Yes. Salesbuildr runs alongside your current quoting tool while you set up, so you switch on a date your team picks and cancel the old tool once its last open quotes are closed.

What happens to my open ConnectWise CPQ quotes?

Let open quotes finish in ConnectWise CPQ and start every new quote in Salesbuildr from a fixed date. Quotes expire, so the overlap ends by itself.

What happens in ConnectWise when a client accepts a quote?

Within 1 to 2 minutes of approval, the ConnectWise opportunity shows the quote lines and the signed PDF. You then create the agreement and fulfilment ticket from the approved quote.
