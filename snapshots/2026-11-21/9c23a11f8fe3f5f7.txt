Top Chiropractic EHR Softwares: 2026 List

ClinicMind Editorial Team

Jun 15, 2026
Fights Office Chaos

Choosing the right chiropractic EHR software takes more than comparing templates, scheduling tools, or billing features. Chiropractic practices need systems that support efficient documentation, smoother patient flow, stronger collections, and better coordination across the front desk, clinical care, and billing. The right platform can reduce administrative burden, improve the patient experience, and help clinics operate more efficiently as they grow.

We analyzed eight leading chiropractic EHR software options and ranked them based on the factors that matter most for this specialty.

How We Ranked the Top Chiropractic EHR Softwares

Our ranking methodology focused on five factors that most directly affect operational efficiency, collections, provider workload, and long-term growth for chiropractic practices:

Documentation and Clinical Workflow (30%): Ease of charting, SOAP note efficiency, compliance support, and workflow design tailored to chiropractic care.

Billing and Revenue Performance (25%): Ability to reduce billing errors, support claims management, improve reimbursement, and prevent revenue leakage.

Client Experience and Support (20%): Quality of onboarding, responsiveness, day-to-day usability, and the level of support practices receive after implementation.

Platform Breadth and Integration (15%): Strength of the broader system, including scheduling, patient engagement, billing, and how well tools work together.

Reputation and Trust Indicators (10%): Market presence, customer trust, years in business, and overall brand credibility.

ClinicMind

ClinicMind stands out in the chiropractic EHR market by delivering what many practices actually need but few systems fully offer: one connected platform that helps reduce documentation time, tighten billing performance, and improve patient flow without forcing teams to manage a fragmented tech stack. Rather than piecing together separate tools for charting, claims, communication, and credentialing, practices can manage everything in one place.

Key Features:

Documentation and Clinical Workflow: Chiropractic-specific EHR workflows, AI-driven documentation support, and tools designed to reduce charting burden.

Billing and Revenue Performance: Integrated revenue cycle management aimed at reducing claim errors, denials, and missed reimbursement opportunities.

Client Experience and Support: One login, one support team, and a more personalized service model than many larger healthcare software vendors.

Platform Breadth and Integration: Unified platform that includes EHR, billing, credentialing, and patient engagement tools in a single system.

Reputation and Trust Indicators: Family-owned, 25 years in business, and differentiated by its long-term operator focus rather than private equity ownership.

Summary of Online Reviews

ClinicMind users often highlight the platform's comprehensive approach, with positive feedback around support, training, and the value of having key operational functions connected in one system. Reviews commonly reflect appreciation for a more guided implementation experience and a team that stays engaged throughout the transition.

ChiroTouch

ChiroTouch remains one of the most recognized names in chiropractic EHR software, with a long-standing presence in the market and a workflow structure familiar to many established practices. For clinics seeking a known chiropractic-specific platform with charting, scheduling, and billing tools in one ecosystem, ChiroTouch continues to be a common consideration.

Key Features:

Documentation and Clinical Workflow: Chiropractic-focused charting, SOAP notes, and office workflow tools.

Billing and Revenue Performance: Integrated billing and practice management functionality for collections and claims workflows.

Client Experience and Support: Familiar system for many chiropractic offices with an established customer base.

Platform Breadth and Integration: Includes key practice management functions in one chiropractic-focused environment.

Reputation and Trust Indicators: One of the best-known chiropractic EHR software brands.

Summary of Online Reviews

ChiroTouch users often praise its chiropractic-specific workflows, ease of use, and broad feature set for managing documentation and daily office operations.

Jane App

Jane App has grown in popularity by offering a clean, modern user experience that appeals to practices looking for simple scheduling, documentation, and communication workflows. While it serves a broader range of private practices beyond chiropractic, its usability and patient-facing tools make it an attractive option for clinics that prioritize ease of use.

Key Features:

Documentation and Clinical Workflow: Streamlined charting and scheduling experience with user-friendly design.

Billing and Revenue Performance: Payment and front-office tools that support smoother collections and administrative workflows.

Client Experience and Support: Known for approachable onboarding and an easy-to-navigate interface.

Platform Breadth and Integration: Combines charting, reminders, online booking, and scheduling in one system.

Reputation and Trust Indicators: Strong reputation across private practice software markets.

Summary of Online Reviews

Jane users frequently highlight easy scheduling, appointment reminders, a clean interface, and strong patient self-booking features. Some reviewers note that clinics with more chiropractic-specific workflow needs may require additional customization, and costs can increase with add-ons or additional providers.

ChiroHD

ChiroHD is a cloud-based chiropractic EHR software designed to help practices manage charting, scheduling, and billing through a web-based platform. Its cloud delivery model appeals to clinics looking for accessibility and flexibility without relying on older, more rigid systems.

Key Features:

Documentation and Clinical Workflow: Cloud-based charting and chiropractic documentation tools.

Billing and Revenue Performance: Built-in billing and scheduling support for streamlined operations.

Client Experience and Support: Accessible interface and web-based convenience for day-to-day use.

Platform Breadth and Integration: Combines key chiropractic office functions within one system.

Reputation and Trust Indicators: Known cloud-based brand in the chiropractic software market.

Summary of Online Reviews

ChiroHD reviews commonly mention ease of use, helpful support, and the convenience of a cloud-based system designed for chiropractic practices. Some users also note opportunities for improvement in areas like reporting or insurance-related workflows.

AdvancedMD

AdvancedMD is not exclusively chiropractic-focused, but it remains a relevant option for larger or more complex practices that want broader healthcare IT capabilities combined with strong billing infrastructure. Its more expansive platform can appeal to clinics prioritizing administrative depth and scalability.

Key Features:

Documentation and Clinical Workflow: Broad EHR functionality adaptable to varying clinic needs.

Billing and Revenue Performance: Strong billing and revenue cycle management capabilities.

Client Experience and Support: Backed by a larger healthcare technology infrastructure.

Platform Breadth and Integration: Broad platform supporting multiple administrative and clinical workflows.

Reputation and Trust Indicators: Established and widely recognized healthcare technology brand.

Summary of Online Reviews

Users often point to AdvancedMD's strong billing and practice management capabilities, along with a broad feature set that can support more complex operational needs.

Tebra

Tebra is a widely recognized practice automation and healthcare operations platform designed to help independent practices manage scheduling, billing, patient communication, and front-office workflows in one system. While it is not chiropractic-specific, its broader practice management capabilities can appeal to clinics looking for an established platform with strong administrative functionality.

Key Features:

Documentation and Clinical Workflow: Practice management and operational tools designed to support daily clinical and administrative workflows.

Billing and Revenue Performance: Includes billing and revenue-related functionality to help streamline collections and front-office processes.

Client Experience and Support: Known for accessible workflows, patient communication features, and usability for independent practices.

Platform Breadth and Integration: Combines scheduling, billing, patient engagement, and practice operations within one connected platform.

Reputation and Trust Indicators: Established healthcare software brand with broad recognition among independent medical practices.

Summary of Online Reviews

Tebra reviews often highlight ease of use, scheduling and communication tools, billing functionality, and its value as an all-in-one platform for independent practices.

NextGen Healthcare

NextGen Healthcare brings a more comprehensive healthcare IT platform to the market, with capabilities that can support larger organizations and more layered operational structures. It is not a chiropractic-first solution, but it may still be evaluated by practices with more advanced scalability requirements.

Key Features:

Documentation and Clinical Workflow: Comprehensive clinical and operational functionality.

Billing and Revenue Performance: Enterprise-grade support for reimbursement and revenue cycle operations.

Client Experience and Support: Built to serve more complex organizations with broader infrastructure needs.

Platform Breadth and Integration: Extensive healthcare workflow coverage across departments and functions.

Reputation and Trust Indicators: Long-standing healthcare technology brand with broad market recognition.

Summary of Online Reviews

Users often associate NextGen Healthcare with enterprise-level capability and a broad operational footprint particularly for larger healthcare organizations.

DrChrono

DrChrono is a flexible, mobile-friendly EHR and practice management platform that appeals to independent practices looking for adaptable workflows and modern usability. While not chiropractic-specific, it can be a consideration for smaller clinics that value convenience and ease of access.

Key Features:

Documentation and Clinical Workflow: Mobile-friendly charting and practice management tools.

Billing and Revenue Performance: Supports billing and administrative workflow efficiency.

Client Experience and Support: Flexible experience designed for usability and accessibility.

Platform Breadth and Integration: Includes a mix of EHR and front-office capabilities.

Reputation and Trust Indicators: Recognized brand in ambulatory software.

Summary of Online Reviews

DrChrono users often highlight ease of use, flexibility, and a modern interface that appeals to smaller independent practices.
