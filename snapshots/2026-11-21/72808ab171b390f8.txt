Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Let's talk through it live.
Book a call→

Will my reps actually use it?

Yes. Your reps will use Proton because it makes their day easier and helps them find more to sell. Compared to other CRMs and platforms, reps get value without much typing. They can ask Pronto, our AI chat agent, for a call plan, log notes by voice, and use the mobile app in the field to plan their sales routes and talk tracks. Each account shows proactive wallet-share opportunities and suggests opportunities to sell more. Every record comes straight from your ERP, so the numbers match what reps already see there. Several of our case studies describe how reps ranging from veterans to brand new adopt Proton because it helps them sell more.

Which ERPs does Proton integrate with?

Proton connects directly with any ERP, including Epicor P21, Epicor Eclipse, Infor SX.e, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics Business Central, and more. See more on our integrations page.

How long does implementation take?

Most companies are live in about 12 to 16 weeks, depending on size and the number of products. Adding a new branch or business unit at a company already using Proton is much faster.

Our data is messy, or we’re changing ERPs. Should we wait?

No. Most distributors say their data isn’t clean, and waiting until it’s perfect usually means waiting a long time. AI can clean it up faster than your team can by hand, so wherever your data stands today, you can start.

A benchmark shows you where work is needed. If you belong to a buying group like Affiliated Distributors (AD) or already have a defined product taxonomy, it tells you which fields to fill first. If you don’t have one, Proton can help you build it.

Proton’s real-time, two-way connection sends fixes made in Proton back into your ERP, so your data gets better the longer you use it. It works best when the person who knows your ERP data works closely with your product experts, since they’re the ones who can define what good data looks like.

Changing ERPs? You can start on the system you have today. Proton’s data layer can connect to more than one ERP at a time, which is how acquired companies keep their own systems.

Do I have to switch CRMs to use Proton?

No. Proton connects directly to your ERP, so it works whether or not you already have a CRM. Some customers move their whole sales team to Proton. Others keep their current CRM and use both. If you need call notes or other activity to show up in your other CRM, we can set up a data sync during implementation. Your reps will use Proton itself to see their recommendations, because Proton doesn’t run inside another CRM.

Each product also works on its own, so you can start with product information management (PIM), order and quote automation, or eCommerce AI and keep your current CRM.

How is Proton different from Salesforce?

Salesforce was made to be generic to fit the use cases of many industries. Proton is set up for distribution out of the box: accounts, branches, order history, quotes, and rebates. You don’t need a team of admins to build and maintain it. Proton’s AI also uses your ERP data to show reps which accounts are slipping and what to sell next, without anyone building reports first. There are many ways we’re just a flat-out better fit for distribution companies, because that’s all we’re built for.

Can Proton handle project business and long sales cycles?

Yes. You can set up pipeline stages to match how you sell, track several contractors’ bids under one parent opportunity, and store drawings and spec books with each project.

Does order and quote automation work if we sell custom or configured products?

Proton reads the request, matches what it can to your catalog, and suggests substitutes. A rep reviews every line before anything reaches the ERP, so your experts still decide what the customer gets.

Can Pronto answer questions from our own documents?

Yes. You can load spec books, warranty terms, safety standards, and internal guides into a knowledge base, and Pronto uses them alongside your ERP data when it answers.

Where is our data hosted, and how is it secured?

We have a SOC 2 Type II report. We’re hosted on Google Cloud, encrypt customer data at rest and in transit, keep each customer’s data logically separated, and run quarterly vulnerability scans plus annual third-party penetration tests. In our Trust Center you’ll find our controls, certifications, subprocessors and AI model providers, and the documents a security review typically asks for.

Does Proton integrate with our phone system?

Proton integrates with popular phone systems like RingCentral, Five9 and more. Learn more on our integrations page.
