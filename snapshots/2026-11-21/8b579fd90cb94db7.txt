Reynolds and Reynolds

Reynolds and Reynolds supplies dealership operating and retail software. Its current Retail Management System includes ERA-IGNITE for dealership operations and FOCUS for CRM, with accounting, sales, parts and service functions described in the product family.

A changed vehicle deal needs a traceable accounting record

Use a synthetic customer who starts a deal online and changes the selected vehicle at the store. Inspect the customer identity, vehicle, trade assumptions and approved deal version. Reynolds describes carrying deal information among online work, FOCUS and ERA-IGNITE; the actual configured handoff should be demonstrated.

Follow the accepted transaction into the accounting record, then correct a permitted allocation under the controller’s procedure. Keep the original entry, adjustment and report identifiable. A common customer view is different from evidence that the financial posting and required statement agree.

For a dealership retaining an outside application, name the actual sending product, receiving record, support owner and commercial scope. Inspect the employee access and the handling of an incomplete transfer. A platform-wide description does not establish every field exchanged by the purchased interface.

Reynolds and Keyloop Serti: establish the Canadian store’s requirements

Keyloop’s Canadian catalog identifies Serti DMS, while Reynolds publishes a Canadian ERA-IGNITE offer. Identify the regional products, required manufacturer reports and selected CRM/service functions in the proposals. Neither a shared DMS category nor the supplier’s global footprint establishes every local requirement.

Use a synthetic parts receipt attached to an open service job. Follow the receipt, stock movement and approved customer charge into the receiving financial records. Keep the store, transaction owner and permitted correction identifiable in each proposed configuration.

Have the responsible staff identify the required documents, language support, permissions and outside connections for the actual location. Reconcile conversion balances and an amended transaction under the agreed scope. Obtain support and historical access for that named deployment instead of infer them from another dealership’s package.

Rey’s demonstration separates business insight from a support instruction

Reynolds introduces Rey as an assistant powered by Spark AI. Its public product demonstration shows queries about acquisition candidates, pending service tickets in parts and contracts-in-transit time by lender.

The parts example offers links to the underlying pending-ticket view and an advanced service-cycle report, while the contracts-in-transit example displays a lender comparison. A separate question about voiding a repair order returns an instructional video and an option to connect with technical support.

That last interaction demonstrates help, not an assistant silently voiding the repair order. A dealership evaluating the offer can distinguish access to business information from permission to execute a transaction.

Ask which data sources and employee roles support the proposed assistant, then compare a permitted question with the underlying report for the same period. When testing help with a correction, establish whether the assistant supplies instructions, starts a support conversation or is authorized to change a record.

Preserve the responsible employee's approval and the actual transaction history. The demonstration's sample values are illustrative product content, not operating results for the dealership evaluating Reynolds.

Rey also connects staff conversations and support

The Rey product page describes team chat across a dealership and dealer group, with a toolbar connecting Reynolds functions. Its named access points include the Consumer Profile from FOCUS, internal communications and the employee's direct-message queue.

This adds a collaboration question to the reporting demonstration: which permitted customer context can an employee bring into a conversation with the person responsible for the next task? The same page distinguishes system questions, help videos and documentation from a live connection to the Technical Assistance Center.

A manager can ask to see a staff question, its underlying customer context and the resulting colleague or support conversation under the proposed employee permissions. The operating record should still identify who made an authorized change.

Access to an assistant, an internal discussion and a technical-support interaction are different parts of the purchased workflow.
