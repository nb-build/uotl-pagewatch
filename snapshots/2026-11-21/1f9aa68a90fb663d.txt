1mind is the Exclusive AI Successor to Drift

Chatbots had a good run. Time to reimagine. Time for a delightful buyer experience.We’re partnering with Clari + Salesloft to supercharge GTM teams everywhere. 1mind is now the exclusive AI successor to Drift — and we’re integrating directly into the Clari + Salesloft Predictive Revenue System.All Clari + Salesloft users can now choose buyer delight — with 24/7 Superhuman teammates sitting on their site, in their product, and in video calls, who qualify, demo, and move deals forward. Every signal flows right back into Salesloft Cadences and Clari forecasts.One connected loop from first touch to closed-won.Better buyer experience. Better pipeline intelligence. Better together.This is not a chatbot upgrade. It’s a completely different approach to buyer engagement. And it’s being powered by 1mind Superhumans.

‍

What this means

Clari + Salesloft is sunsetting Drift and naming 1mind as its exclusive AI successor.Drift changed this industry. It pioneered conversational marketing and gave thousands of companies a better way to engage buyers online. We have deep respect for that foundation.But buyers in 2026 don’t want to navigate decision trees. They want real conversations, real answers, and real momentum on their deals. That’s what 1mind delivers — Superhumans that engage buyers the way your best rep would, 24/7, at scale, and in every place your buyers are — on your site, in your product, and over video calls.

What Superhumans actually do

They qualify. They demo. They handle objections. They solution. They move deals forward 24/7 — on your website, in your product, and on video calls.Superhumans work across the entire buyer journey: engaging inbound traffic, joining live sales calls as a ride-along SE, guiding product adoption in-app, and providing human-like customer support around the clock.Every interaction generates structured signals that feed directly into Clari forecasts and Salesloft Cadences. No data silos. No tab-switching. Your AI-led engagement contributes to your revenue intelligence, not apart from it.

Better Together

1mind Superhumans will be integrated directly into the Clari + Salesloft Predictive Revenue System. That's not just a partnership announcement. It's a closed loop.Your Superhuman sits on your site, in your product, on video calls — qualifying, demoing, moving deals forward. Every one of those buyer signals flows right back into Salesloft Cadences and Clari forecasts. No handoffs between systems. No intelligence lost between tools.Clari + Salesloft built the system that tells you where your revenue is going. 1mind built the Superhumans that drive relentlessly towards a close. Now they work as one.And if you're curious what that looks like, talk to Superhuman Mindy at 1mind.com
