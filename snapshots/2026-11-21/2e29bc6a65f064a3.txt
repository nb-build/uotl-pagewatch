PALADIN DATA ACQUIRES CYBERBOS POS PLATFORM

Paladin Data Corp. acquired CyberBOS, point-of-sale and business management platform for the lumber industry

Paladin Data Corp., Mesa, Az., a leading provider of software solutions for independent retail hardware stores, has acquired CyberBOS, Omaha, Ne., a long-established point-of-sale (POS) and business management platform specializing in the lumber, hardware and building materials industries. The acquisition strengthens Paladin’s position in specialty retail and expands its footprint among independently owned stores across North America.

“This is an exciting step for Paladin. CyberBOS has built a loyal following by solving real operational challenges for independent retailers,” said Mike Williamson, CEO of Paladin. “Paladin will continue to provide retailers with powerful tools to streamline operations, improve customer experiences, and grow their businesses. This acquisition is about continuity, investment, and growth.”

CyberBOS currently serves a customer base that includes independent lumberyards and hardware stores, feed and farm supply retailers, and other specialty merchants. Likewise, since 1980, Paladin has provided independent hardware, lumber and building materials retailers point of sale and business management solutions that require robust inventory control and supplier integrations.

Under Paladin, CyberBOS customers will gain the benefits of Paladin’s unsurpassed support while maintaining the trusted workflows they rely on today.

“This transaction represents a tremendous opportunity for CyberBOS customers,” said Charles Agulla, owner of CyberBOS. “Paladin’s deep industry expertise and long-standing customer relationships align perfectly with our vision. We’re excited that Paladin will continue delivering innovative technology that helps our retailers succeed in an increasingly competitive landscape.”

Paladin will continue supporting CyberBOS solutions with a focus on maintaining continuity and service quality. Both companies share a strong customer-first philosophy, ensuring a seamless transition and continued excellence in service.

Paladin has been a trusted provider of retail management software and point-of-sale solutions for independent retailers across North America since 1980. Paladin has provided independent retail businesses with robust, easy-to-use systems that integrate inventory management, customer engagement, reporting, and e-commerce capabilities. Known for its commitment to customer success, support, and continuous innovation, Paladin helps retailers operate more efficiently and compete effectively in a dynamic retail environment.

CyberBOS is a modern point-of-sale platform designed to provide flexible, intuitive solutions for retailers seeking streamlined operations and scalable technology. Built with a focus on usability and performance, CyberBOS enables businesses to manage sales, inventory, and customer interactions with ease. The platform has earned a reputation for adaptability and responsive development, helping retailers stay ahead in a rapidly evolving marketplace.
