Questions?

Is Cut+Dry a Marketplace?

No. Cut+Dry is not a marketplace. You can only order from your existing contracted distributors — we simply make the ordering and payment process digital and easier to manage.

Is Cut+Dry free for restaurants?

Restaurants can order digitally at no cost.

Can multiple team members access the account?

Yes. You can add users with custom permissions so owners, managers, and staff can order and manage payments.

Does Cut+Dry replace my sales rep?

No. Your distributor relationships stay the same. Cut+Dry enhances communication and makes ordering more efficient — it doesn’t replace people.

Will pricing or contract terms change?

No. Cut+Dry is free for restaurants forever.
