The Best Virtual Receptionist Services in 2026: Abby, Ruby, Smith.ai and AnswerConnect Compared

September 2, 2026

The best virtual receptionist service is the one whose pricing unit, receptionist team and AI option match the way your phone actually rings. We sell one of the four services below, so read this as an informed comparison rather than a neutral review: every price and feature comes from the providers’ own public pages on September 2, 2026 and is dated, so you can check the fit for yourself.

Quick take
Abby Connect: human and AI receptionists on one plan, the most flexible AI-first option of the four (AI-first, human-first or any mix, changed at any time), the only one whose receptionists all work in the office (none from home), 1,000+ integrations, a free trial on every plan, from $164 a month.

Ruby: human-only receptionist service; AI limited to transcripts and assisted call flows; the highest per-minute prices of the four.

Smith.ai: per-call pricing with per-call add-ons; AI-first or human-first chosen per plan.

AnswerConnect: the lowest published price per human minute; no AI by policy; a setup fee on two of three plans.

How We Compared Them

We scored each service on what you can verify before you sign: the pricing unit and effective rate per minute or call, hours, who answers and where they work, whether an AI receptionist is available and how it is priced, what is included versus billed as an add-on, trial and guarantee terms, contract length and integrations. We did not score call quality or answer speed; only a trial on your own calls can do that, which is why we weight a real free trial heavily.

Where the Receptionists Work

Of the four, only Abby answers every call from an office: 100 percent of its receptionists work together in Las Vegas, none from home. Ruby recruits its receptionists for remote roles (its job board lists “Remote Receptionist” positions), Smith.ai recruits virtual receptionists to work “from the comfort of your home office”, and AnswerConnect describes its receptionists as working from “distraction-free home environments”. The office matters for four reasons: supervision and coaching happen in real time, call quality and equipment are consistent, client information is handled on office systems rather than home networks, and the team that answers for you trains together on your account with the Client Success Manager alongside.

1. Abby Connect: Human and AI Receptionists on One Plan

Abby has answered calls for small businesses for more than 20 years and now serves over 6,200 of them with U.S.-based receptionists who all work in the office in Las Vegas (none from home), a dedicated team per client and a Client Success Manager. What sets it apart in 2026 is the plan structure. Abby is one subscription used three ways: just human receptionists, just the AI Receptionist, or a mix you can change at any time. Usage is measured in Abby Minutes: a minute with a human receptionist uses one Abby Minute, a minute with the AI Receptionist uses half, so AI-handled calls make the same plan go twice as far. That makes Abby the most flexible AI-first option of the four: you never choose an AI plan or a human plan, you set the mix and change it whenever you like. Under the hood it runs Deepgram Flux for speech recognition, the latest OpenAI models with automatic failover to Anthropic, and ElevenLabs for voice, trained in-house on more than 20 years of receptionist calls.

Plans are $164 a month for 50 Abby Minutes (Starter), $329 for 100 (Essential), $599 for 200 (Professional) and $1,380 for 500 (Growth), with Enterprise plans on request. Every plan is month-to-month with a free trial and no setup fee, 24/7 coverage carries no after-hours or weekend upcharge, and bilingual English and Spanish answering is included for both human and AI. Booking, intake, Spanish, call summaries and transcripts in the AbbyGO app are included rather than billed per call, and Abby publishes 1,000+ integrations, including Clio, Lawmatics, HubSpot, Salesforce and Calendly. Two things to know: pricing is per Abby Minute rather than per call, and plans above 500 Abby Minutes are Enterprise quotes. Read Abby’s pricing or how the free trial works.

2. Ruby: Human-Only Receptionist Service

Ruby has two decades of its own, U.S.-based receptionists recruited for remote roles (its job board lists “Remote Receptionist” positions), and the same complete feature set on every plan: 24/7 answering, bilingual handling, scheduling, intake, payment collection, HIPAA-compliant services, phone number hosting (Abby hosts your business number too), robocall filtering, and integrations through its platform. AI appears only as an enhancement (transcripts, sentiment analysis and AI-assisted call flows) at no extra cost; there is no AI receptionist answering calls, which is a feature or a limitation depending on what you want. Plans run $250 for 50 minutes to $1,725 for 500, the highest per-minute prices in this comparison, and the pricing page lists neither an overage rate nor a free trial. Ruby’s live chat is $143 to $520 a month, or 20 percent off when bundled with a receptionist plan (Abby sells Live Web Chat as a separate product too). Read Abby vs Ruby for the detail.

3. Smith.ai: Per-Call Pricing with AI-First or Human-First Plans

Smith.ai bills per call: $300 a month for 30, $810 for 90, $2,100 for 300, with overage of $8.50 to $11.50 a call. Every human-first plan includes 24/7 live staffing by work-from-home receptionists, lead screening and intake, a dedicated number and one CRM integration, no setup fee, no charge for spam when caller ID passes, and a 30-day money-back guarantee that refunds up to $1,000. Appointment booking ($1.50), a dedicated Spanish line ($1.00), text and email follow-up ($0.50), call recording ($0.25) and payment collection ($1.00) are per-call add-ons, so the real price of a booked call is several dollars above the plan rate. Smith.ai also offers AI-first plans (a toggle on its pricing page, with their own prices), so it is the other provider here with an AI receptionist, chosen per plan rather than mixed on one plan. Read Abby vs Smith.ai.

4. AnswerConnect: Human-Only Answering at a Low Per-Minute Rate

AnswerConnect publishes three plans: 200 minutes for $350 plus a $49.99 setup fee, 300 minutes for $395 with no setup fee, and 400 minutes for $575 plus $49.99, with $1.85 to $2.50 per additional minute and larger plans on request. That is $1.32 to $1.75 per human minute, the lowest published human rate of the four. Every plan includes 24/7 answering by home-based receptionists, apps and CRM integrations; the two larger plans add live chat, and the company plants a tree for every customer every month. Read the billing rules before you compare: minutes include after-call work and every interaction is rounded up to the next minute, though the first 30 interactions under 30 seconds each cycle are free. AnswerConnect offers no AI answering by policy and lists no free trial. Read Abby vs AnswerConnect.

How to Choose a Virtual Receptionist Service

Start from your calls, not the price list. Count last month’s inbound calls, estimate three minutes each, and note how many need scheduling, intake or a Spanish speaker.

Convert every plan to your cost per month. Add setup fees, the add-ons you will use and the overage you expect. Per-call plans favour long calls; per-minute plans favour short ones.

Decide how you feel about AI. If you want a person on every call, Ruby and AnswerConnect are built for that. If you want AI on routine and after-hours calls with a person available, Abby prices both on one plan; Smith.ai offers them as separate AI-first and human-first plans.

Ask who answers and where. In-house or home-based, a dedicated team or a shared pool, U.S.-based or not, and who owns your account after onboarding.

Check the billing rules. Rounding, after-call time, hold time, spam calls and the notice period for cancelling.

Insist on a trial with your real calls. A free trial before you pay tells you more than any comparison, including this one. See what a virtual receptionist costs for the plan math.

Best Virtual Receptionist Services: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best virtual receptionist service for a small business?

For most small businesses in 2026 it is the service that lets you put a person on the calls that need one and an AI receptionist on the rest without buying two products, which is how Abby is priced from $164 a month with a free trial. Ruby is human-only, Smith.ai bills per call with AI-first or human-first plans, and AnswerConnect has the lowest published price per human minute but no AI.

How much do the best virtual receptionist services cost?

Published plans run from $164 to $2,100 a month: Abby $164 to $1,380 per month for 50 to 500 Abby Minutes, Ruby $250 to $1,725 for 50 to 500 minutes, Smith.ai $300 to $2,100 for 30 to 300 calls, and AnswerConnect $350 to $575 for 200 to 400 minutes plus a $49.99 setup fee on two plans, as published on September 2, 2026.

Which virtual receptionist services offer a free trial?

Of the four compared here, only Abby lists a free trial (14 days or 500 Abby Minutes on every plan, no setup fee). Smith.ai offers a 30-day money-back guarantee after payment; Ruby and AnswerConnect list neither on their sites.

Which virtual receptionist services use AI?

Abby includes an AI Receptionist in every plan at half-rate minutes alongside human receptionists. Smith.ai offers AI-first and human-first plans with their own prices. Ruby uses AI only for transcripts, sentiment and assisted call flows, and AnswerConnect does not use AI to answer calls as a matter of policy.

Is a virtual receptionist the same as an answering service?

They overlap. An answering service traditionally takes messages and relays them; a virtual receptionist also schedules, qualifies leads, runs intake, transfers calls and represents your business by name. All four services here do the latter. See Abby’s answering service and virtual receptionist service pages for the difference in detail.

Do these virtual receptionist services use work-from-home receptionists?

Ruby, Smith.ai and AnswerConnect do: Ruby’s job board lists remote receptionist roles, Smith.ai’s careers page recruits receptionists to work from a home office, and AnswerConnect describes distraction-free home environments. Abby is the exception: 100 percent of its receptionists work in the office in Las Vegas, none from home.

Do the best virtual receptionist services answer in Spanish?

Yes, with different pricing. Abby includes English and Spanish answering for both human and AI receptionists on every plan, Ruby offers 24/7 bilingual answering, Smith.ai charges $1.00 per call for a dedicated Spanish line, and AnswerConnect offers a bilingual answering service.
