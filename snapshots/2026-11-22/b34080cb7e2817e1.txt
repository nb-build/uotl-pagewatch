FAQs

Everything you want to know before taking the next step.

What is Smokeball legal practice management software?

Smokeball is powering people-law. Our legal practice management software lightens the administrative burden, so legal professionals have more time to serve the people who need them most. With automatic time tracking, tailored workflows, and rapid innovation through AI, Smokeball is the industry standard in people-law.

Does Smokeball work for my practice area?

Smokeball is designed for solo lawyers, small law firms, and growing legal teams across a wide range of practice areas. Firms can choose a plan based on their size, workflows and required capabilities, from legal billing and case management to advanced automation, reporting, and firm-wide productivity tools.

What makes Smokeball different from other legal practice management software?

Smokeball combines legal practice management, automatic time tracking, document automation, legal billing, trust accounting and business reporting in one platform. It also integrates closely with Microsoft Word and Outlook and provides practice-area-specific workflows and an extensive library of automated legal forms. You can see how Smokeball compares to other providers on our comparison page.

What can law firms do with Smokeball AI?

Smokeball AI helps legal professionals review matter information, summarise documents, compare files, draft correspondence and find relevant details more quickly. Archie, Smokeball’s AI matter assistant, works within existing legal workflows to help firms complete routine work more efficiently while keeping legal professionals in control.

How does Smokeball protect law firm and client data?

Smokeball is ISO 27001 certified and hosts its solutions on Amazon Web Services. Sensitive data is protected with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2 or higher in transit, supported by multi-factor authentication, role-based access controls and continuous security monitoring. Smokeball AI limits access according to firm, matter and user permissions, and Smokeball does not provide customer data to third parties for AI model training.

How much does Smokeball cost?

Smokeball pricing depends on the selected plan, the size of the firm and the features or services required. Available plans support different needs, ranging from legal billing and browser-based case management to advanced automation, reporting and firm management. Firms can request personalised pricing based on their requirements.
