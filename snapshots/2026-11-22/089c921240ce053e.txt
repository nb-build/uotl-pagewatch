Key Takeaways

MSP PSA tools split into two camps in 2026: standalone PSAs (ConnectWise PSA, Datto Autotask PSA, HaloPSA) and platforms that include RMM and PSA together (Syncro, SuperOps, Atera).

Published per-technician prices for unified platforms start at $129 per month (Syncro Core and Atera Pro, billed annually) and $149 per month (SuperOps Pro, billed monthly). ConnectWise PSA and Autotask PSA are quote-only.

Endpoint limits matter as much as the sticker price. Syncro and Atera include unlimited endpoints per technician; SuperOps Pro and Super include 150 endpoints per technician.

A standalone PSA still needs a separate RMM, so the true cost is the PSA license plus the RMM license plus the integration work between them.

Syncro fits MSPs that want RMM and PSA in one console with per-technician pricing. A standalone PSA is still the better call if you have an RMM you can’t displace.

Methodology and Disclosure

How we evaluated these tools: we picked the PSAs MSPs shortlist most often, based on G2, Capterra, MSP community threads (r/msp) and direct buyer evaluations. Each tool was scored on PSA depth, RMM coverage, pricing transparency, endpoint limits, integrations, and fit for MSPs.

Pricing was checked against each vendor’s public pricing page in October 2026. Ratings and prices change, so confirm them on the vendor’s page before you buy.

Disclosure: Syncro is our platform. We included it on this list because we believe it fits the use cases covered here, and we applied the same evaluation criteria to Syncro as to every other tool.

PSA Software: The Business End of Running an MSP

Most MSPs don’t go shopping for a PSA because they want new software. They go shopping because invoices are going out late, billable time is slipping through the cracks, or the RMM and the PSA have stopped agreeing on how many endpoints each client has.

PSA software (professional services automation) runs the business side of an MSP: ticketing, time tracking, contracts, billing, and client reporting. The best PSA software for MSPs ties those workflows together and connects them to RMM, so billable work gets captured and invoiced without manual reconciliation.

It’s also one of the most expensive decisions you’ll make. The PSA touches every workflow that drives revenue, and if you get it wrong, the migration cost is measured in years, not quarters.

The category has consolidated. Kaseya owns Datto Autotask, ConnectWise PSA remains the long-standing enterprise option, and HaloPSA has become the go-to standalone alternative. Meanwhile, platforms that include RMM and PSA together (Syncro, SuperOps, Atera) have pulled share from standalone buyers, especially MSPs with fewer than 30 technicians.

This guide compares the six PSA tools MSPs evaluate most often in 2026. For each one, you’ll see what it does well, where it falls short, who it fits, current published pricing, and the limits that change the real cost.

The 6 Best MSP PSA Software Tools for MSPs (And How to Choose the Right Platform)

1. ConnectWise PSA

ConnectWise PSA (formerly ConnectWise Manage) is the long-standing leader among established MSPs. It covers ticketing, contracts, time tracking, billing, sales pipeline and project management as a deeply configurable suite, and it’s the reference point everyone else gets compared to.

What we like: deep configurability for complex contract structures and multi-tier service catalogs. Strong reporting and project management for MSPs doing project-based work, and the largest integration ecosystem in the MSP category.

What we don’t like: pricing is quote-only, so you negotiate before you see a rate card, and multi-year terms with annual uplifts are common. The UI feels dated next to newer tools, and full implementations routinely take months.

Best for: MSPs with 30+ technicians, mature service delivery processes, and the patience to configure the platform to match their workflow.

Pricing: custom quote. RMM is a separate purchase.

G2 rating: 4.0/5 (582 reviews), as of October 2026

User perspective: long-tenured users on Reddit and G2 consistently describe it as powerful but complex, with nearly everything needing configuration. It rewards investment and punishes inattention.

Weighing a move off ConnectWise? Our ConnectWise alternatives guide covers the options in more detail.

2. Syncro (Our Pick)

Syncro is the all-in-one platform for MSPs that don’t want to stitch together a separate ticketing system, billing system, scripting tool, and reporting layer. RMM, PSA, and remote access live in one console with one data model.

What we like: tickets, billing, contracts, RMM alerts, and reporting share the same data, so remediation actions land in the same queue as client communication. Syncro’s PSA includes help desk ticketing, a branded client portal, live chat, estimates, invoicing and payments, recurring billing automation, and customizable reports with Power BI templates. Per-technician pricing with unlimited endpoints means landing new clients doesn’t raise your software bill.

What we don’t like: contract customization is good but doesn’t match ConnectWise PSA’s depth for highly bespoke contract structures. Real-time ticket automations, the live metrics dashboard, and M365 multi-tenant security sit on the Team plan, not Core.

Best for: MSPs consolidating RMM and PSA into one platform, or new MSPs building a stack from scratch. A strong fit at any size if you want predictable per-technician pricing.

Pricing and limits:

Core: $129 per technician per month, billed annually. Includes RMM and PSA with unlimited endpoints.

Team: $179 per technician per month, billed annually. Adds Microsoft 365 multi-tenant management (Entra ID sync and identity actions, CIS-aligned security baselines, automated license billing), real-time ticket automations, and session recording.

Enterprise / Custom: multi-year terms, PO billing, volume pricing, or custom start dates for larger MSP operations (contact sales).

Every plan: 14-day free trial with no credit card, 24/5 live support, SOC 2 Type II certification.

Integrations:

Accounting: QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, and Xero.

Payments: Syncro Payments, Stripe, PayPal, Worldpay, and Authorize.net.

MSP stack: Pax8 (procurement and license data into billing), Hudu (IT documentation), Domotz (network monitoring), and Zapier.

Remote access: Splashtop remote desktop is included, plus background tools for event viewer, registry, services, and PowerShell.

Permission-scoped API access for everything else. See our guide to PSA integrations and automation for how these connect.

Compliance: Syncro signs a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement for active, paying subscribers who complete the self-serve HIPAA setup and agree not to store PHI on the platform.

G2 rating: 4.5/5 (557 reviews), as of October 2026

User perspective: G2 reviewers consistently cite the combined RMM and PSA workflow and pricing transparency as the main reasons they switched from multi-tool stacks.

3. Datto Autotask PSA

Autotask PSA, now owned by Kaseya, has been a top PSA for MSPs for two decades. It covers ticketing, time tracking, billing, contracts, CRM and project management, and it’s most common at MSPs running other Kaseya tools.

What we like: a mature feature set, strong contract and SLA management and a broad integration library. Integration with the rest of the Kaseya stack (VSA, Datto RMM, Datto BCDR) is tight if you’re already invested.

What we don’t like: quote-only pricing with multi-year commitments. Kaseya’s pricing and contract practices have drawn visible community pushback, and UI modernization has lagged newer platforms.

Best for: MSPs already standardized on the Kaseya or Datto stack who want alignment across tools.

Pricing: custom quote. RMM is a separate purchase.

G2 rating: 4.3/5 (636 reviews), as of October 2026

User perspective: long-tenured Autotask shops are loyal to its workflow depth. New evaluators often cite contract terms and support experience as deal-breakers.

Thinking about leaving the Datto stack? Our Datto alternatives guide compares the options.

4. HaloPSA

HaloPSA is a UK-rooted PSA that has gained real share with MSPs since 2022. It’s known for a modern interface, deep workflow customization, and strong billing.

What we like: a modern interface compared with legacy PSAs, and customizable workflows and ticket categories without developer help. Pricing is published and simple: one per-agent price with every feature included.

What we don’t like: customization depth comes with a learning curve. RMM isn’t part of HaloPSA, so you still buy and integrate a separate RMM. Support is centered on UK hours, which matters for North American MSPs.

Best for: MSPs that want a standalone PSA with deep customization and plan to keep their current RMM.

Pricing: £66 per agent per month, billed annually, on Halo’s UK pricing page, with no tiers or add-ons. Confirm US dollar pricing with Halo.

G2 rating: 4.6/5 (47 reviews), as of October 2026

User perspective: HaloPSA shows up often on MSP Reddit threads as the tool teams replaced ConnectWise with. Customization and modern UX are the reasons cited most.

5. SuperOps

SuperOps is a newer platform built in the 2020s that combines PSA and RMM. The pitch is a modern interface, AI-assisted automation and unified pricing.

What we like: a modern UI built without legacy assumptions. AI-assisted ticket triage and automation are part of the product rather than bolted on, and combining PSA and RMM avoids integration work.

What we don’t like: a younger platform means a smaller community, fewer third-party integrations and less proven depth for complex MSP workflows. The Pro and Super plans cap coverage at 150 endpoints per technician, so dense client fleets push you toward a per-endpoint plan.

Best for: newer MSPs, or MSPs willing to trade some workflow depth for UX and AI features.

Pricing: Pro at $149 and Super at $179 per technician per month, billed monthly, each including 150 endpoints per technician. Super Plus is priced per endpoint, starting at $2.50 per endpoint per month with a 150-endpoint minimum. Annual billing saves 15%.

G2 rating: 4.4/5 (205 reviews), as of October 2026

User perspective: reviewers consistently praise the UI and AI features. The most common critique is platform maturity for complex MSP workflows.

Weighing SuperOps against other options? See our SuperOps alternatives guide.

6. Atera

Atera is a unified IT management platform with ticketing, billing and RMM, sold per technician with unlimited endpoints. It’s most popular with smaller IT shops and lean MSPs.

What we like: unlimited endpoints per technician keeps pricing predictable as client fleets grow. The built-in AI Copilot helps with ticket triage and scripting, and onboarding is fast.

What we don’t like: PSA depth trails dedicated PSAs, and reporting is functional but not deep. It’s a better fit for ticketing-and-RMM workflows than for MSPs running complex multi-project contracts.

Best for: lean MSPs and small IT teams that want unified ticketing and RMM with simple pricing.

Pricing: Pro at $129, Growth at $159 and Power at $209 per technician per month, billed annually. Monthly billing costs more (Pro is $139, Growth $189). The Superpower tier is quote-based.

G2 rating: 4.6/5 (1,274 reviews), as of October 2026

User perspective: customers consistently praise the pricing model and onboarding speed. The most common gap cited is PSA depth for established MSPs with complex billing and contracts.

Weighing Atera against other options? See our Atera alternatives guide.

How to Choose the Right PSA for Your MSP

Five questions narrow this decision faster than any feature checklist.

Do you want one platform or a best-of-breed stack? If you want one platform, the tools that include RMM (Syncro, SuperOps, Atera) are your shortlist. If you want best-of-breed, evaluate the standalone PSAs (ConnectWise, Autotask, HaloPSA) alongside your existing RMM, or see our roundup of the best RMM software for MSPs.

How complex is your billing? If you run multi-tier contracts, project work and time-and-materials engagements alongside fixed-fee managed services, ConnectWise PSA and HaloPSA have the deepest contract structures. If your billing is mostly fixed-fee per endpoint or per user, simpler platforms cover it.

How much configuration time can your team absorb? ConnectWise and Autotask reward investment but punish neglect, while unified platforms get running faster. Map the implementation effort against your client onboarding schedule before deciding.

How will your bill change as you grow? Price out your current endpoint count and where you expect to be in two years. Unlimited-endpoint plans hold flat as clients grow; capped and per-endpoint plans rise with every new device, and quote-only tools may not show you the curve until renewal.

How much migration can you tolerate? PSA migrations are expensive. If you’re 18 months from renewal, optimizing what you have may be the right answer; if you’re 6 months out, evaluating now gives you leverage with your current vendor.

Choose the PSA That Matches Your Tooling Strategy

Every PSA on this list can run a competent MSP service desk. The differences that matter are structural: whether RMM comes with it, how pricing scales as you add endpoints, and how much configuration you’re signing up for.

If you’re happy with your RMM and need maximum contract depth, a standalone PSA like ConnectWise PSA or HaloPSA still earns its place. If you’re building from scratch or tired of reconciling two systems, a platform that includes RMM and PSA will save you the integration work and keep your costs predictable.

Start with your tooling strategy, price it at the endpoint count you expect in two years, and the shortlist usually narrows itself.

Frequently Asked Questions About PSA Software

What is the best PSA software for MSPs?

It depends on your tooling strategy. For RMM and PSA in one platform, Syncro, SuperOps and Atera are the leading options. For a standalone PSA with maximum depth, ConnectWise PSA is the long-standing leader, and HaloPSA is the strongest choice for modern UX with deep customization.

How much does PSA software cost for an MSP?

Published 2026 prices for platforms that include RMM start at $129 per technician per month (Syncro Core and Atera Pro, billed annually) and $149 per technician per month (SuperOps Pro, billed monthly). HaloPSA lists £66 per agent per month on its UK pricing page, while ConnectWise PSA and Autotask PSA are quote-only and need a separate RMM.

Which PSA tools include RMM?

Syncro, SuperOps and Atera include RMM and PSA in the same platform. ConnectWise PSA, Datto Autotask PSA and HaloPSA are standalone PSAs that integrate with a separately licensed RMM.

What integrations are available with PSA software?

Most MSPs need accounting (QuickBooks, Xero), payment processing, RMM, documentation and distributor integrations. Syncro, for example, integrates with QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, Stripe, PayPal, Worldpay, Authorize.net, Pax8, Hudu, Domotz and Zapier, and includes Splashtop remote access.

Is per-technician or per-endpoint pricing better for an MSP?

Per-technician pricing with unlimited endpoints usually favors growing MSPs, because adding clients doesn’t raise your software bill. Per-endpoint or endpoint-capped pricing can be cheaper for very small fleets but rises with every device you add.

How long does PSA implementation typically take?

Platforms that include RMM and PSA usually get running in a few weeks. Standalone PSAs like ConnectWise PSA and Autotask PSA often take several months for full implementation, longer if you’re migrating data from a previous PSA.

Can a PSA support HIPAA compliance for MSPs?

Yes, if the vendor will sign a Business Associate Agreement. Syncro signs a BAA for active, paying subscribers who complete its self-serve HIPAA setup and agree not to store PHI on the platform; confirm the equivalent terms with any other vendor before you commit.

How does Syncro compare to ConnectWise PSA?

Syncro includes RMM and PSA in one platform with published per-technician pricing and unlimited endpoints. ConnectWise PSA is a standalone PSA with deeper contract customization, quote-only pricing and a separate RMM. For other options, see our ConnectWise alternatives guide.
