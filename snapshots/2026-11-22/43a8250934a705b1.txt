SendGrid Free Plan Ended - Cheapest Next Step

BulkEmailSetup Team

November 6, 2026

6 min read

SendGrid no longer has a free plan. Twilio retired the Free Email API and Free Marketing Campaigns plans starting May 27, 2025, with a 60-day transition, after which free accounts stopped sending. New accounts now get a 60-day trial at 100 emails a day, then the cheapest paid plan is Essentials from $19.95 a month. If you send under about 6,000 emails a month, a free tier elsewhere (Mailjet, Brevo, Resend) is the cheapest next step. If you send far more, skip the free tiers entirely.

Above roughly 100,000 a month, per-email pricing and shared IPs start working against you. That is where SendGrid alternatives and a server you own come in. If you just hit the ceiling of a free tier, outgrew the free SMTP tier walks through the jump.

Choose by volume, not by brand

Before you switch

Export what you can. Suppression lists, unsubscribes and bounces from SendGrid, even on a paused account.

Re-check DNS. Remove SendGrid's CNAMEs for DKIM if you leave, and add the new provider's records. Two half-working DKIM setups are worse than one.

Do not import unsubscribes as active. Load them into the new suppression list first.

Start small. Even on a free tier, send to your most engaged contacts first.

Frequently asked questions

Does SendGrid still have a free plan?

No. Twilio's changelog says the Free Email API and Free Marketing Campaigns plans were retired starting May 27, 2025, with a 60-day transition. After that, sending stopped on free accounts. New accounts now get a 60-day free trial capped at 100 emails a day with no credit card required, and then need a paid plan to keep sending.

What is the cheapest SendGrid plan now?

Essentials, listed from $19.95 a month on Twilio's Email API pricing page. It sends from shared IP pools and does not include a dedicated IP. The next step, Pro, starts at $89.95 a month and includes one dedicated IP. Marketing Campaigns, the newsletter editor and contact list tool, is priced separately from the Email API.

Which providers still offer a free SMTP tier?

Several, as of October 2026: Mailjet allows 6,000 emails a month (200 a day), Brevo 300 a day, Resend 3,000 a month (100 a day), SMTP2GO 1,000 a month, Mailgun 100 a day, and Postmark 100 a month. Free tiers change often, so check each provider's own pricing page before you build around one.

What is cheapest for 10K emails a month?

Amazon SES is cheapest on paper at about $1 for 10,000 emails ($0.10 per 1,000), but new accounts start in a sandbox and need approval. Without that hassle, Mailjet's or Brevo's free tier covers most of 10,000 a month, and SMTP2GO Starter covers 10,000 for $10. At this volume a dedicated server is not worth it yet.

What happened to my contacts when the SendGrid free plan ended?

Twilio's changelog says free accounts with more than 100 stored contacts would lose all of them at the end of the transition, and that this could not be restored by upgrading later. If you still have the account, export what is left now. If the contacts are gone, rebuild from your own signup database or CRM, not from old campaign exports.

When does a paid plan or own server beat free tiers?

Free tiers stop working around 6,000 to 9,000 emails a month. Cheap paid plans cover roughly 10,000 to 100,000 a month for $10 to $90. Past about 100,000 a month, or once you need dedicated IPs, a server you own gets cheaper: BulkEmailSetup's Basic setup is $549 one-time for 25,000 emails a day, with about $11 a month in hosting.
