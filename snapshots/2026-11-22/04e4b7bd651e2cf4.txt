About Sungage Financial

Sungage Financial is a leading fintech platform that provides technology-driven and customer-focused solutions for the residential solar financing industry. Aurora and Sungage have been partnered since 2022 and offer an integrated solution that allows Aurora customers to take their residential solar proposal to the next level and go from proposal to financing application, fast!Sungage’s commitment to best-in-class products, an outstanding homeowner experience, and streamlined processes support the success of our installer partners.

Solution Overview:

You can now configure your Sungage account and connect it directly with your Aurora account within Sales Mode. Once the integration has been configured in Aurora, all of your Sungage loan products will appear in Sales Mode as Financing options. With this integration you can:

Conduct a soft credit check on behalf of the homeowner and apply for a loan from within Aurora’s Sales Mode

Show the homeowner Sungage loan products in approved language

Say goodbye to manual data entry into the Sungage Portal, and the need to manually manage your Sungage products in Aurora
