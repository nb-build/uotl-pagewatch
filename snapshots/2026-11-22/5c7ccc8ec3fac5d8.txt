Wix FAQ

What is Wix?

Show more

Wix is an all-in-one platform for creating a website and running your business online. Build with AI and drag and drop, register a domain, sell products, take bookings and market what you build. Free to start, with 300M+ sites built on it.

Is Wix only for websites?

Show more

No. Wix is an all-in-one platform for building your website and running your business with AI. Beyond the Wix Harmony editor, it includes Wix Studio for agencies and freelancers, and Wix Headless, which connects sites and apps built in any AI tool or framework to Wix’s business solutions.

How do I get started on a site with Wix?

Show more

Describe your site in a prompt, and Wix Harmony, our AI-powered editor, lays out a business-ready site for you. Or start from one of our 2,000+ Wix templates instead. Refine either by chatting with Aria, your personal AI agent, or by hand. Most people go from idea to published site in a matter of minutes.

Can I create a site from ChatGPT, Claude or another AI tool?

Show more

Yes, with Wix Headless. Build your site or app in ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Codex or another AI tool, then connect it to Wix in one prompt. Wix adds payments, bookings, a CMS and more, and puts it live on Wix hosting. You keep your code and can keep prompting to make changes. It's free to connect and you can upgrade when you’re ready to sell.

What is the difference between Wix Harmony, Wix Studio and Wix Headless?

Show more

Wix Harmony is Wix’s flagship AI website builder where small business owners can generate a business-ready site in minutes, then customize any detail with AI or by hand. Wix Studio is the professional platform for agencies and freelancers building client sites. Wix Headless connects a site or app built in any AI tool or framework to Wix’s business solutions in one prompt. Host it on Wix or on your own setup, like Vercel, Netlify or AWS. All three run on the same enterprise-grade infrastructure and provide all business and marketing tools.

Is Wix free, and when do you need a paid plan?

Show more

Yes, you can start on Wix for free. Build and publish a site without a credit card, and stay on your free plan for as long as you like. Every Wix site, free or paid, includes hosting, so you never set up a server or pay a separate provider. Free plans come with a Wix-branded domain; paid plans start affordably and add a custom domain, payments and more business features. Compare all Wix plans to find what fits.

How can I connect or register a domain?

Show more

Register a new domain with Wix, or connect one you already own, directly from your Wix dashboard — no technical setup or DNS knowledge needed. Once connected, your domain goes live automatically. Annual website plans include a custom domain free for the first year.

Can I build an online store on Wix?

Show more

Yes, with the Wix online store builder. Add a product catalog, inventory and checkout, and accept payments by card or digital wallet through Wix Payments or other providers — online and in person with POS. Building is free; you only need a paid plan to start accepting payments.
