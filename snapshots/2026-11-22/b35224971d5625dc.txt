Frequently asked questions

How much does The New Flat Rate cost?

Pricing starts at $99/month per tech. Plans scale depending on how many trades you're in and the level of training and support your team needs.

How do contractors usually implement it?

Most companies start with a trusted tech—someone in the field who’s open to trying it for a week. Once you see the results, we help roll it out to your whole team using live or remote training, tech support, and even ride-alongs.

Does it work with our current dispatch or FSM software?

Yes. The New Flat Rate works with over 250 field service platforms and integrates with ServiceTitan and Housecall Pro.

What kind of training and support is included?

You get full access to our online training library, live implementation classes, a tech support line, and optional ride-alongs. Whether you're just getting started or scaling across multiple techs, we're here to support you.

What if my techs don’t want to sell?

That’s exactly who this is built for. With The New Flat Rate, your techs don’t have to sell—they just show the menu. The customer picks what’s right for them.

How is this different from other pricing systems?

Most systems expect you to build everything from scratch. With The New Flat Rate, we build the menu pricing system for you—and train your team to use it. Plus, we’re the only system that gives your techs five ready-to-use service options for every job, so they never have to wing it or guess the price again.
