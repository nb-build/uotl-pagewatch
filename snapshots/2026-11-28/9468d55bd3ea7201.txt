Total Expert Declares the End of the CRM Era, Introduces the Customer Operating System

Total Expert’s Customer Operating System combines a system of context with a system of action where humans and AI agents leverage always-on intelligence to help lenders drive growth in any market.

5 mins read

Read now

Total Expert’s Customer Operating System combines a system of context with a system of action where humans and AI agents leverage always-on intelligence to help lenders drive growth in any market.

MINNEAPOLIS — August 4, 2026 — Total Expert today announced the Customer Operating System and has declared the CRM era officially over. The capabilities Total Expert has delivered over the past 18 months, including Customer IQ, Lead Management, and its industry leading AI Sales Assistants, were key innovations leading to the creation of a new platform category. A platform that no longer depends on loan officers and customer-facing users logging in and completing tasks as the sole way to generate ROI from technology.

Financial services institutions face a critical choice: Customer for life strategies built on individual effort and inconsistent execution, or strategies powered by critical context with always-on intelligence and action. The former fails at both scale and speed. The latter, powered by a Customer Operating System that combines context awareness with coordinated execution, doesn't.

“After seeing billions of dollars in new loans generated for our customers via Customer IQ and AI Sales Assistant doing the heavy lifting and then bringing the loan officer into the loop in the precise moments the consumer needs expert advice, it became clear the future of how customer-facing teams leverage technology is going to look dramatically different moving forward,“ said Joe Welu, Founder and CEO of Total Expert, “As an example, a top 10 lender drove over 1,000 new HELOC originations in just six weeks using Total Expert’s Customer IQ and AI Sales Assistant.”

The customer operating system is built around a core thesis: Elevate people to the highest and best use of their time.

The Customer Operating System is built around a single organizing question: What is the highest and best use of each person’s time? The platform absorbs the work people don’t like and won’t do consistently—following up with every lead, nurturing past customers over years, watching for the signals that a customer is moving back into the market—and brings humans in at exactly the moments customers need high-quality advice and education on their options.

At its core, the Customer Operating System is an enterprise-grade system of context and system action.

‍

System of Context

Customer IQ: Know every customer, in every moment. The system of context is the intelligence foundation of the platform. Always on and continuously enriching every customer and lead with zero-, first-, and second-party data, it surfaces behavioral signals, financial milestones, intent, and relationship history to identify the moments that matter most. For mortgage and consumer lenders, that means knowing when a customer has tappable equity, a favorable rate opportunity, or a life event signaling a new need. For banks and credit unions, it means identifying the inflection points that represent the highest-value opportunities for deepened relationships and cross-sell.

‍

System of Action

Orchestration and purpose-built apps: Enterprise-grade capabilities to execute consistently, at scale, with humans and AI assistants. The system of action coordinates human and AI-enabled engagement in response to Customer IQ-generated intelligence—through the AI Sales Assistant, AI Scenario Assistant, Journey Orchestration, and connected sales-focused apps like Lead management and customizable pipeline views, loan officers and customer-facing teams can leverage AI assistants to complete tasks they don’t want to do or won’t do consistently, while relying on the Customer Operating System to bring them into the loop in the moments that matter most to their customers.

Together, these systems create compounding value: context informs action, action drives outcomes, and outcomes sharpen the intelligence that powers the next engagement. A CRM depreciates from the day it’s deployed; a Customer Operating System gets smarter with every interaction.

“We work closely with over 200 CEOs and C-suite executives across the industry, which informs our approach on how we partner, build, and deliver the capabilities our customers need to win as they reimagine their businesses for the AI age,” said Welu. “Our new capabilities and Customer Operating System approach to delivering these capabilities help us closely align with our customers and focus on outcomes over logins.”

‍

Built for a regulated industry

In financial services, the barrier to deploying AI is not capability—it’s trust. The Customer Operating System embeds consent awareness, compliance controls, and auditability into the platform itself, so institutions can govern how AI assistants engage, within what parameters, and prove what happened and why. That governance-first design is what makes AI execution deployable at enterprise scale in banking and lending.

Purpose-built for banks, credit unions, mortgage lenders, and consumer lenders, it enables institutions to operationalize and scale a Customer for Life Strategy—from acquisition and conversion to retention and growth—powered by a unified combination of AI and human execution.

“Traditional CRM is dead. It was built to manage transactions and tasks, not maximize customer lifetime value. To drive important business outcomes like improving top of funnel conversion, recapture, and cross-sell, you must have deep context on every customer and lead across the entire customer journey,” said Welu. “The golden opportunity has always been the lifetime relationship. A Customer Operating System is built to make earning customers for life repeatable and scalable. It brings together a system of context that surfaces the right moments with a system of action that ensures nothing is ever missed.”

‍

Realizing the industry’s first Customer Operating System

Total Expert’s Customer Operating System is the industry’s first realization of this vision. Having assembled its foundational components, the company is executing an aggressive roadmap to expand capabilities across consumer lending and depository markets through 2026 and beyond.

For the 200+ financial enterprises already on Total Expert, this is not a migration. Customer IQ, Journey Orchestration, and Lead Management are the components they use today, now operating as one system. Existing customers activate AI assistants on top of the context and governance already in place.

To learn more, visit TotalExpert.com.
