Frequently asked questions

What is PatientNow?

PatientNow is the complete practice platform built only for aesthetic medicine: EMR, online booking, marketing, payments, photo documentation, and business intelligence in one system. More than 5,000 active locations run on PatientNow worldwide.

What specialties does PatientNow serve?

PatientNow is built for aesthetic medicine: med spas, plastic surgery, dermatology, weight loss, IV therapy, and wellness practices. The templates, workflows, and reporting already speak your specialty's language.

What makes PatientNow different from general medical EMRs?

PatientNow is built exclusively for aesthetic practices, with before and after photo management, cosmetic procedure templates, and aesthetic inventory tracking that generic systems bolt on or skip.

Can PatientNow handle both surgical and non-surgical work?

Yes. The platform manages everything from injectables to major surgical procedures, with the documentation, scheduling, and billing workflows each side needs, in one system.

How long does it take to get started?

Most practices are up and running within weeks, not months. Our onboarding team handles data migration, staff training, and setup.

How hard is it to switch from our current EMR?

Most practices are fully up and running within weeks. Our onboarding team handles data migration, staff training, and setup. You keep seeing patients while we do the heavy lifting

Do we own our data if we ever leave?

Yes. Your patient records are yours. Our team will help you export your data if you ever decide to move on.

Is PatientNow HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Records are encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and audit trails track every action. PatientNow also holds Meaningful Use certification.

What support is included?

Every practice gets access to our U.S.-based support team, plus onboarding, training resources, and a knowledge base. Real humans who know aesthetic medicine, not a ticket queue in another time zone.
