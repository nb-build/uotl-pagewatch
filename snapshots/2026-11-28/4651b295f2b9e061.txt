Questions teams ask before a demo

What is Vidyard?

Vidyard is an AI-powered video platform that helps businesses create, host, share, and personalize videos across the customer journey. From marketing campaigns and sales outreach to customer onboarding, Vidyard brings video, engagement insights, and automation together in one platform.

Who uses Vidyard?

Vidyard is built for marketing, sales, and customer success teams. Marketers use it to engage audiences and generate pipeline, sellers use it to build relationships and move deals forward, and customer teams use it to improve onboarding, adoption, and ongoing engagement.

What does pricing look like for my company?

Vidyard has four plans: Free, Starter, Teams, and Enterprise. Visit our pricing page or talk to our team for more information.

How does Video Agent personalize each video, and who approves it?

When a buyer takes an action, like downloading content, booking a meeting, or engaging with your brand, Video Agent pulls their details from your CRM into your script. Each viewer gets their own personalized AI Avatar video. You write the script, choose the avatar and voice, and approve everything before it goes live.

Does Vidyard work with Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Salesloft and other tools our team uses?

Yes. Vidyard integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo, Outreach, Salesloft, and Gong. Engagement data flows back to your CRM, so reps know who watched and what to do next. Vidyard MCP also brings video into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI tools.

How is Vidyard different from a basic screen recorder?

Vidyard goes beyond recording and sharing videos. It combines video hosting, branded viewing experiences, viewer analytics, CRM integrations, and AI-powered personalization so teams can understand engagement and take action.

Is Vidyard secure?

Yes. Vidyard completes an annual SOC 2 Type 2 audit and complies with GDPR. Admins control access with permissions, password protection, and SSO on certain plans.
