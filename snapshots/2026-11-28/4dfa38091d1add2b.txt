Questions practices ask us

Who owns Lighthouse 360?

Henry Schein One. It is the software joint venture of Henry Schein and Internet Brands, and it bought Lighthouse 360 from Web.com in March 2019. Henry Schein One also makes Dentrix, Dentrix Ascend and Demandforce. Henry Schein got a new CEO in March 2026 and replaced its executive committee in August 2026.

Is Lighthouse 360 the same as Lighthouse 360+?

Lighthouse 360+ is the version launched in September 2023. It added online scheduling, digital forms, a phone add-on and payment reminders. It is still a reminder system first. Practices still use a separate forms vendor and a separate payment processor alongside it.

What does Lighthouse 360 cost, and is there a contract?

There is no price on their site. Practices pay $250 to $450 a month, often as a line inside a Dentrix contract. Many are on 1 to 3 year terms with a fee to leave early, and one practice owed $440 a month until its term ran out.

Is Lighthouse 360 part of my Dentrix bill?

Often, yes. Henry Schein One sells it in a bundle with Dentrix, eClaims and the practice website. Dropping Lighthouse alone does not lower the Dentrix bill and can cost you the claims discount. Some waited out the contract or left the bundle entirely.

Does Lighthouse 360 work with my practice management system?

Henry Schein One says Lighthouse 360+ works with any practice management system. It reads your system every 10 minutes. Forms write back on Dentrix, Dentrix Ascend and some others. Sync problems are common, and one practice needed a stopgap because its Dentrix version and Lighthouse 360 did not work together.

Is Lighthouse 360 dental only?

Yes. Lighthouse 360 is built for dental offices. NexHealth serves dental and medical practices on the same platform, with the same sync, forms, scheduling and payments.

Who owns NexHealth?

Its founders, backed by Silicon Valley investors. The roadmap comes from what practices ask for, and new product updates ship most weeks: 880+ releases in the past five years.

How do I switch from Lighthouse to NexHealth?

A NexHealth person installs the sync, sets up your reminders, forms and online booking, and walks your front desk through it. 7 days from signed to first feature live.

Does NexHealth work with my practice management system?

15 systems, dental and medical, cloud and on premise: Dentrix, Dentrix Ascend, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Denticon, Curve, Cloud9, Dolphin, eClinicalWorks, athenahealth, NextGen, ModMed and more. The full list is at nexhealth.com/integrations.

Is NexHealth for medical practices too?

Yes. 19 specialties: dental, orthodontics, primary care, dermatology, physical therapy and more.
