Frequently Asked Questions

Telecom Software Solutions: What to Know

What is telecom billing software and why do communications service providers need it?

Telecom billing software is a platform that enables communications service providers (CSPs) to accurately rate, invoice, and collect revenue for voice, data, VoIP, UCaaS, IoT, and other communications services. Unlike more generic billing tools, Rev.io’s telecom billing software is purpose-built to handle the complexity of usage-based billing, real-time CDR rating, hybrid pricing models, multi-carrier settlements, and telecom-specific tax compliance.Without a dedicated solution, service providers face billing errors, delayed cash flow, regulatory risk, and customer dissatisfaction. Rev.io's telecom billing platform automates the entire quote-to-cash lifecycle so CSPs can bill accurately, collect faster, and scale without adding manual overhead.

How does usage-based billing work for VoIP and UCaaS providers?

Usage-based billing charges customers based on their actual consumption of services rather than a flat fee. For VoIP and UCaaS providers, this means rating and invoicing for call minutes, SIP trunk sessions, data consumption, SMS messages, and other variable usage metrics. A telecom billing platform like Rev.io ingests call detail records (CDRs) and other usage data in real time, applies the correct rate plans and pricing tiers, and generates accurate invoices automatically. Rev.io also supports hybrid billing models that combine usage-based charges with recurring monthly subscriptions on a single invoice, giving providers the flexibility to bundle services and launch new offerings quickly.

How does Rev.io handle telecom tax compliance and regulatory fees?

Telecom tax compliance is one of the most complex challenges facing communications service providers, with taxes, surcharges, and regulatory fees varying across federal, state, and local jurisdictions. Rev.io integrates with leading telecom tax engines to automatically calculate and apply the correct taxes on every invoice based on the type of product sold and the service location. This automation reduces the risk of audit exposure, eliminates manual tax calculations, and ensures every invoice is compliant. Rev.io also partners with tax classification and remittance specialists to help providers navigate the full scope of telecom regulatory requirements.

Can telecom providers use Rev.io to launch managed IT services like security and cloud backup?

Yes. Rev.io Cyber Protect, powered by Acronis, gives telecom and communications service providers a turnkey way to expand into high-margin managed IT services without building a separate MSP practice. The platform includes integrated remote monitoring and management (RMM), endpoint detection and response (EDR), and cloud backup, all managed from a single pane of glass and billed through Rev.io. This allows CSPs to bundle managed security and backup services alongside their existing VoIP, UCaaS, and connectivity offerings, deepening customer relationships and generating new recurring revenue streams.

What makes Rev.io different from other telecom billing and BSS platforms?

Rev.io is the only AI-native platform that unifies telecom-grade communications billing, professional services automation(PSA), integrated payments, and cloud security (RMM, EDR, and backup) in a single solution. Most BSS and billing platforms address only one piece of the puzzle, forcing providers to stitch together disconnected tools that create data silos, manual work, and revenue leakage. Rev.io was born in telecom over 20 years ago and is purpose-built for the operational complexity that VoIP resellers, UCaaS providers, CLECs, and communications service providers face every day. With Rev.io, everything from quoting and CRM through billing, payments, and endpoint security lives in one connected platform.
