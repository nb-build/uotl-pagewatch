FAQs

Everything you need to know about Synup, how it works, and getting started.

What is Synup?

Synup is a software platform that helps small businesses, multi location brands and agencies manage local listings, reviews, SEO presence and AEO (AI Optimization) paired with an agent, Sydekick which runs Local SEO & marketing on autopilot.

How is Synup different from a traditional local marketing platform?

Traditional tools give you dashboards and make you click through every task. Synup's platform includes AI features and it's agent, Sydekick capable of handling full tasks including research, content generation, campaign planning and execution.

What can Sydekick actually do?

It fixes and syncs business listings, monitors and responds to reviews, publishes local social posts, tracks and improves local rankings, and checks your visibility in AI search engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity. It also runs recurring skills and multi-step playbooks across all your locations.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial that includes $50 in AI credits and one location to test. Cancel anytime — no long-term contracts, no cancellation fees. You can add a business and watch Sydekick complete your marketing tasks in real time.

How does Sydekick keep me in control?

You set the guardrails. Sydekick proposes actions and can run approved skills automatically, but anything sensitive like publishing a review response or changing core business info requires your sign-off. You see everything it does and can pause, edit, or reverse any action.

Can it manage multiple locations and clients?

Yes. Synup is built to scale from a single storefront to thousands of locations. You can run playbooks across every location at once, segment by brand or region, and manage separate client workspaces from one place.

Which directories and tools does it connect to?

Sydekick syncs listings to Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing, Facebook and 100+ publishers, voice assistants and data aggregators, plus the review and social channels where your customers already are.

Is it built for agencies?

Absolutely. Agencies use Synup to onboard clients quickly, run marketing at scale, and prove results with white-labeled reporting. Sydekick does the repetitive work so your team can focus on strategy and growth.

How does AI-search visibility (AEO) work?

Answer Engine Optimization makes sure your business shows up accurately when people ask AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI overviews for recommendations. Synup checks how you appear, flags gaps, and strengthens the structured data and content that these engines rely on.

How much does it cost?

Pricing scales with the number of locations and the capabilities you turn on. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, and you can see transparent pricing before you ever talk to sales.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Synup follows enterprise-grade security practices, encrypts your data in transit and at rest, and only connects to the platforms you authorize. You stay in control of what Sydekick can access and act on.
