Hey there,

If you're reading this, you probably spend the day in driveways and on jobsites, measuring, selling and keeping crews moving. Then the other job starts at night: typing up quotes, counting materials and ordering extra just in case. On top of that, you're tracking permits and 811 tickets, sending invoices and remembering who's waiting on what.

That all has to happen somewhere. Right now it's probably a notepad, a spreadsheet, a whiteboard, your camera roll and a few apps that don't talk to each other. And something always slips: a short material order, a quote redone from scratch because they want vinyl, an invoice that never went out.

I know because I lived it. I'm a fence guy, and the job was never as clear as it should have been. Customer expectations didn't always match our scope of work, and they were always asking when their install date was. The crew showed up without a detailed layout, notes or site photos, and changes made between the signed quote and install day didn't always reach them.

Nothing out there was built for how fence companies actually work. So I built FenceCRM: draw the fence once, and the materials, the proposal, the schedule and the invoice all come from that one drawing.

And you don't set it up alone. When you sign up, we load your materials and prices, build your presets the way your crews build, and set up your proposals with your photos, reviews and contract. If something isn't right, you reach me directly, not a ticket queue.

Build your next quote in FenceCRM. If it's not faster and more accurate than how you do it now, email me and tell me why.
