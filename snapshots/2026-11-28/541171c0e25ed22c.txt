Outreach Corp (Outreach) is a company that specializes in providing artificial intelligence-powered sales execution platforms. The company's main activities include developing solutions that enhance sales engagement, deal management, and pipeline management. Outreach offers a range of products and services, including artificial intelligence-driven forecasting, conversation intelligence, and rep coaching tools. The company's platform is utilized by sales, customer success, and revenue operations teams across various industries such as information technology services, financial services, and telecommunications. Outreach collaborates with channel partners to distribute its solutions effectively. Outreach is headquartered in Seattle, Washington, the US.
