We believe local should go
wherever your customers are.

We started Nearby Now in 2011 with a simple idea: the great work a local team already does every day should be the foundation of their rankings and reputation — not an afterthought. Fifteen years later, we still build with that single conviction.

The five beliefs that run the company.

1/

We believe local for local businesses should extend everywhere you have customers — not just where you have a building.

2/

We believe you should get ranking and reputation credit for every single job, and every satisfied customer.

3/

We believe your reviews should drive traffic and leads directly to you — not to someone else's website.

4/

We believe your online reputation should be your business asset, not ours. You keep everything. Even after you leave.

5/

We believe nothing puts your business in a better light than the great work you already do every day, and rave reviews from your customers.

—

Our mission: turn today's customer service and goodwill into tomorrow's leads, opportunities, and sales. Privately held. Client-funded. Same goal since 2011: deliver value, daily.
