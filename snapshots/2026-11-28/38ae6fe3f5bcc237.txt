Frequently asked questions

What is production management software?

Production management software plans, schedules, and tracks manufacturing in one system, connecting orders, materials, and shop floor work in real time. Katana’s production management software replaces the spreadsheets, emails, and disconnected tools manufacturers use to coordinate production.

What is the difference between MRP and ERP?

MRP (material requirements planning) calculates which materials you need, when, and how many. ERP covers the whole business, including finance and HR. Katana handles MRP and production, and connects to your accounting, ecommerce, and shipping tools rather than replacing them.

How much does manufacturing software cost?

Katana’s pricing is custom, based on your products, order volume, and locations. It covers the build, setup, and support in one contract.

What types of manufacturers use Katana?

Job shops, batch producers, contract manufacturers, and lean operations use Katana across industries including food and beverage, apparel, electronics, cosmetics, and pharmaceuticals. These are growing manufacturers that have outgrown spreadsheets and entry-level tools, and need a system built around their process without the cost and rigidity of an enterprise system.

Do I need a consultant to get started?

No. Katana’s team scopes your setup, moves your data in, and trains your team. A dedicated CSM stays with you before and after go-live.
