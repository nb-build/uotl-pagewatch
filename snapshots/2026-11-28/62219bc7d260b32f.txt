shopVOX Joins the Fullsteam Family

A New Chapter for shopVOX

After more than 15 years of building shopVOX, our co-founders Rao Meka and Ravi Duvvuri have sold the company to Fullsteam , a family of software companies.

This is a big milestone. From the very beginning, shopVOX has been about helping sign, print, and apparel businesses run better. That mission hasn’t changed – and by joining Fullsteam, we’ll have even more resources to keep improving the tools you rely on every day. Here’s what you can expect:

Rao and Ravi poured their passion into building shopVOX, and none of this would have been possible without you – our customers. Your trust, feedback, and support have shaped shopVOX into what it is today. Thank you for being part of the shopVOX journey. We’re excited for what’s ahead — and we’re looking forward to building it together.
