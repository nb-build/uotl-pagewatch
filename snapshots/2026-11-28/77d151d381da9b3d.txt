Jewelry store POS software features you'll actually use

Jewel360 was thoughtfully designed for jewelry retailers like you. Our POS software has all of the features you need — plus added flexibility to customize your solution to your unique business' needs.

Cloud-based POS

Run your store from anywhere on Mac, PC, or tablet, with your data securely backed up on AWS servers so your business is always protected.

Inventory management

Keep in-store and online inventory perfectly synced with built-in vendor options and the flexibility to handle special orders without the extra tools.

Repairs and custom work

Automatic text notifications keep customers updated on every repair, while job templates make ticket creation fast and consistent for your whole team.

Special orders

Special orders tie directly into purchase orders with built-in deposit management, and customers get automatic text updates every step of the way.

Reporting and analytics

Track marketing performance, monitor inventory turn, and identify your top customers — all in one place so you can make smarter business decisions.

Clientbook integration

Sell on social, market across channels, and use in-store insights to fine-tune your retailing strategy and deepen customer relationships.

Frequently asked questions

Will switching to Jewel360 affect my current POS system setup?

Jewel360 is designed to be highly compatible with existing POS systems. We offer a smooth transition process and support to help you integrate Jewel360 without disrupting your current operations.

Can Jewel360 support my business growth and scalability?

Yes, Jewel360 is designed to grow with your business. It provides you with analytics and reporting tools to help you make informed decisions and optimize your operations as your business expands.

What kind of support can I expect during and after the transition to Jewel360?

Our team offers comprehensive support during the integration process and ongoing customer service. You will have access to a dedicated support team ready to assist you with any questions or issues that may arise.

How can Jewel360 help me manage my inventory more efficiently?

Jewel360 has advanced inventory management that syncs in-store and online sales. Inventory reports give valuable insights into sales trends, and stock tracking ensures you are never over- or under-stocked on products. Additionally, it supports real-time e-commerce integration, which helps in syncing inventory across online and physical stores.

Does Jewel360 integrate with accounting software like QuickBooks?

Yes, Jewel360 integrates seamlessly with both QuickBooks online and desktop versions, allowing you to streamline your financial management processes by ensuring all your sales and expenses are accurately recorded.

What kind of marketing and customer engagement features does Jewel360 offer?

Jewel360 includes built-in marketing tools such as email and text notifications for promotions and events. It also includes customer loyalty and online marketing features. This helps maintain customer engagement and encourages repeat business.
