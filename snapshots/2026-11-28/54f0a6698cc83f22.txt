97% of people learn about a local business online Let's make sure they can find you

Most customers look into a business’s online presence before they interact with the business itself. Moz Local ensures your business stands out online with accurate listings and data-driven insights. Manage everything from one simple platform to build visibility and drive traffic.

Ensure the accuracy of your listing data, wherever it’s found

Accuracy matters for local SEO. 52% of consumers say they have left a negative business review after encountering false or incorrect information on assets like local business listings.

Moz Local helps you easily create and manage your local business listings across multiple directories, all in one place.

Auto-sync listings — Keep your listings actively updated across search engines, online directories, social media, apps, and data aggregators with minimal time and effort

Monitor listing health — See what fields your listings are missing, which listings require action, and which listings have already been updated.

Gain insight into Google Business Profile performance — Analyze key data around GBP interactions and rankings with the local pack.

One central dashboard to monitor and respond to reviews

60% of consumers expect a response within 2 days — meet expectations with Moz Local’s streamlined review management.

With Moz Local, you no longer need to keep track of multiple review platforms and logins. You can view and respond to reviews within the same tool you use for social media and listings management.

Centralized review management — Monitor, read, and respond to reviews on multiple sites from a single dashboard. Respond quickly to negative feedback to create a positive customer experience and retain customers.

Analyze and understand customer sentiment — Identify trends and common themes in reviews to get a better understanding of how customers interact with your business. Spot areas for improvement and see where you are exceeding expectations.

Never miss a review — Get alerts and track response times to stay on top of your reputation management. See the average time it takes you and your team to respond to reviews to better meet customer expectations.

Looking to manage more than 50 locations with Moz Local?

Whether you're an agency, a franchise, or a multi-location business, build citations and monitor reviews at scale with our comprehensive suite of tools.

Moz Local is the complete toolkit to boost visibility, engagement, & local rankings

Track your true local ranking with Local Grid

Manage all channels from one place with our social platform integration

Monitor your presence and growth across Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and more — without switching between platforms.

Streamlined social media management

Plan, schedule, and manage social content across multiple locations and platforms with our social calendar. Set up post templates across your locations.

Save time and stay on-brand with a review response templates

Create customizable templates and leverage AI suggestions to quickly respond to reviews while maintaining consistent messaging.

Never miss an interaction with engagement monitoring

Track engagement metrics, direct messages, and new reviews in one platform. Instantly spot and respond to pending activity.

Comprehensive reporting made easy

Build engaging reports for clients, stakeholders, or business partners from an extensive library of pre-made templates. Alternatively, create custom reports to showcase results.
